要知道兩顆細胞相遇後發生了什麼，不能只分別看它們。基因被讀取時會產生 RNA，研究者可以讀取這些分子，觀察哪些基因變得活躍或沉寂。但逐顆讀取 RNA 的單細胞定序通常先把細胞拆散，於是原本誰碰過誰、接觸多久，就難以追溯。

作者因此建立 Cell-Cell-seq，把有凹穴的水凝膠微粒（Nanovial）當作容納細胞的小空間。先放入前列腺癌細胞（PC3），再加入經改造、能辨認癌細胞表面特定目標的免疫 T 細胞。凹穴讓兩顆細胞維持接觸，形成固定的細胞對（dyad），也保護它們不在搬運時散開。T 細胞裝載後再共同培養三小時，研究者把每一對連同微粒送進小液滴，為同一對的 RNA 資料加上共同識別碼，再讀取混合 RNA。這樣便能一次分析數千對互動，同時保留配對身分。

作者另外測量 T 細胞釋放的殺傷相關蛋白質（granzyme B），確認配對後仍有功能反應，但不能直接證明癌細胞已死亡。辨認目標後結合較牢的 T 細胞，較容易形成配對，也較常出現可測得的分泌訊號。不過，各個細胞對的分泌量差異超過十倍，顯示即使用同類細胞配對，反應仍很不一致。

分析 RNA 時，還有一道難題：讀數變了，不一定是細胞互動的結果。每顆細胞原本的基因活動就不同，把兩份 RNA 混在一起，也會改變讀數。為了分清這兩種情況，作者先各自測量未曾互動的癌細胞與 T 細胞，再由電腦隨機配對、混合它們的資料，產生許多「虛擬細胞對」（pseudo-dyad）。它們代表只有資料相加、沒有細胞互動時，可能出現的讀數範圍。真實細胞對的某個基因若反覆偏離這個範圍，才被列為互動相關變化。兩次獨立實驗也觀察到相近的變化方向。

接著，作者根據哪些基因原本只在其中一種細胞被測到，推定混合訊號較可能來自哪一方。另一組對照在微粒表面放上相同的辨認目標，單獨啟動 T 細胞，再把其資料與未接觸癌細胞的資料混成虛擬細胞對，比較真配對多出的變化。但人工刺激未必等同真接觸，癌細胞相關候選基因仍須在單獨癌細胞中測到。相較把細胞混在培養盤裡自由相遇，固定配對更清楚地捕捉到一批短暫升高、很快又減弱的早期反應。這與接觸時間較一致、短暫訊號較不容易被不同階段的細胞稀釋相符。

更重要的是，作者能把同一對細胞的反應對起來看。某些細胞對中，雙方有一批基因的讀數會一起升高；這些基因參與製造吸引其他細胞前來的訊號分子。另一種趨勢則相反：癌細胞中，曾被發現與逃避免疫攻擊有關的 TM4SF1 基因讀數越高，T 細胞的啟動相關讀數往往越低。這些關係在虛擬細胞對中並不明顯，不能單靠兩份資料相加來解釋；但它們仍是相關性，尚不能證明是哪個基因造成另一方的反應。

最後，癌細胞通常提供較多 RNA，可能讓 T 細胞的訊號在混合讀數中顯得偏弱。作者另用 ccRepair 校正這種比例偏差：以單獨細胞中表現較穩定的基因作為參照，只調整已推定來源的基因讀數，不能補回未量到的 RNA。校正後，各方的反應更容易分辨，雙方一起升高的反應也仍然存在。這項研究驗證的是特定癌細胞與改造 T 細胞的早期互動。訊號來源仍依賴參照資料推定，不能當成直接分別量到兩顆細胞的全部 RNA，也不能據此判定患者的治療成效。

作者把腫瘤細胞與 T 細胞保留成對測量，再用未接觸細胞組成的模擬對照辨認接觸相關變化，揭露會被拆散量測與不同接觸時間掩蓋的早期反應。

當免疫細胞遇到腫瘤細胞，雙方會改變哪些基因的活動？常見的單細胞定序會拆開細胞，讀取基因被抄寫出的訊息分子 RNA，卻也失去「誰曾和誰接觸」的紀錄。作者提出 Cell-Cell-seq，把一個腫瘤細胞和一個能辨認它的免疫細胞——T 細胞——保留成對，再測量接觸後的變化。

這套方法先把前列腺癌細胞放進帶有凹槽的微小凝膠顆粒 Nanovials，再加入經改造、能辨認腫瘤上特定標記的 T 細胞。凹槽限制兩個細胞的移動，讓它們維持接觸，也保護這一對細胞不在後續處理中分開。作者把保留下來的細胞對一起送入定序液滴，用同一個識別碼記錄雙方的 RNA。這樣能同時處理數千對細胞，但每對得到的仍是兩個細胞混在一起的讀數。

細胞被留在一起後，是否仍能作出反應？作者在顆粒上捕捉 T 細胞釋出的殺傷相關蛋白 granzyme B。作者比較 T 細胞表面的辨識蛋白（受體）：與腫瘤標記結合較久的版本，有約 11% 的細胞對分泌訊號達標；結合較短的版本約為 5%。這不是腫瘤死亡率。不同細胞對的訊號強度相差超過十倍，顯示相同配對方式並不保證相同反應。

不過，兩份 RNA 混在一起，本來就可能看起來不同，不能把所有差異都算成接觸的效果。作者因此將未接觸的腫瘤細胞與 T 細胞資料隨機配對，在電腦中建立大量「模擬細胞對」（pseudo-dyads）。這些對照保留細胞原有的個別差異，卻沒有雙方接觸的經歷。只有真實細胞對相較於模擬對照，在反覆抽樣比較中仍顯著改變的基因，才列為接觸相關的變化。

接著，作者以單獨培養時只在一方測到的基因，推定改變的來源。他們也把辨識標記固定在凝膠顆粒上，讓 T 細胞接觸後啟動，再將其資料與未接觸的腫瘤細胞資料合成第二組模擬對照，分辨啟動本身與腫瘤參與後的反應。不過，單獨刺激未必重現接觸時的狀態；這仍是依對照推定來源，並非逐一追蹤每個 RNA 分子原屬哪個細胞。

兩次獨立實驗找到可重現的接觸相關變化。尤其是受到刺激後迅速出現、又會消退的早期基因反應，在這種控制配對的實驗中更清楚；接觸起點較集中，但並非完全同步。相較之下，把大量細胞放在同一培養孔、讓它們自行相遇時，這些訊號較弱或沒有被測出。這與各對細胞接觸起點不一、短暫反應被混在一起的解釋一致。

成對紀錄也揭露雙方反應如何一起變動。例如，趨化因子是吸引其他細胞靠近的訊號蛋白；雙方用來製造這類蛋白的基因，其 RNA 讀數往往一起升高。另一些細胞對則呈現 T 細胞啟動相關基因較高、腫瘤的免疫調節相關基因較低。這些關係在模擬對照中不明顯，但相關性本身尚不能證明是哪個分子促成或壓制了另一方。

最後，作者用稱為 ccRepair 的校正方法，處理一方 RNA 較多、使另一方訊號在混合讀數中顯得較弱的問題。它以單獨細胞中較穩定的基因作參考，只調整已推定來源的基因讀數尺度，讓各自的反應更清楚，同時保留跨細胞的共同變化。這篇研究因此把「細胞遇見誰」和「遇見後改變什麼」連了起來；目前驗證的是工程化腫瘤與 T 細胞的早期體外反應，尚未證明能據此預測長期治療效果。

Cell-Cell-seq 把一個腫瘤細胞與一個 T 細胞保存在同一個微小凹槽中。研究者讀取每一對細胞的混合基因活動記錄，再與未接觸細胞拼成的電腦對照比較，找出接觸相關的反應及兩個細胞之間的協同變化。

研究想知道細胞接觸後，雙方的基因活動如何改變。一般逐個細胞讀取的方法會先把細胞分開，因而丟失『誰曾與誰接觸』的關係。作者用經過改造的前列腺癌細胞，搭配能辨認並攻擊目標的免疫細胞，也就是 T 細胞。T 細胞靠表面的辨認蛋白（T 細胞受體），認出癌細胞展示的分子片段（抗原）。為了保住這種接觸，研究者先把癌細胞放進像微型杯子的水凝膠微粒（Nanovial），再加入 T 細胞。凹槽讓兩者維持接觸，也在搬運時保護這一對細胞；研究者再依螢光標記挑出配對。

這套保留細胞配對、再讀取基因活動的方法叫 Cell-Cell-seq。作者先讓 T 細胞進入杯中與癌細胞配對，再共同培養。每個杯子接著進入小液滴，雙方反映基因活動的分子記錄（RNA）會被標上同一個辨識碼。因此，一份讀數對應一對細胞，裡面仍混有雙方的記錄。

另做的分泌實驗中，作者在杯內捕捉 T 細胞分泌、參與殺傷的蛋白（granzyme B），再用螢光讀出累積量。受體結合較穩定的那一組，約 11% 的配對超過分泌門檻，較弱受體組約為 5%。沒有捕捉材料的背景對照低於 1%。不同配對的訊號可相差十倍以上，顯示相同設計的細胞也會有不同反應。這項讀數證明了分泌反應，不能直接當成癌細胞被殺死的比例。

不過，兩份基因活動記錄混在一起，本來就會與任一單獨細胞不同。作者把未接觸的癌細胞與 T 細胞逐個測量，再隨機配成許多電腦中的『假配對』（pseudo-dyad）。每個假配對按比例合併兩份讀數，模擬只有混合、沒有接觸時可能看到的範圍。因為每個細胞原本就不同，對照必須涵蓋多種組合。作者反覆抽取樣本，保留在 50 次比較中都符合差異標準的基因，作為接觸相關變化。兩次獨立實驗都找到了相近的接觸相關基因變化；合理改變假配對中雙方的混合比例，也保留了主要結果。

為了判斷變化來自哪一方，作者先利用單獨測量時只在一方出現的基因。作者另把辨認目標固定在微粒表面，讓 T 細胞在沒有癌細胞時受刺激。再把這些 T 細胞的記錄與癌細胞記錄組成假配對。若真配對仍有額外變化，而且該基因原本可在癌細胞測到，就把它列為癌細胞相關反應。這仍是依對照推估來源：接觸可能讓另一方開始產生原本測不到的記錄，而抗原刺激也未必完全重現真實接觸時的 T 細胞狀態。

在數千對細胞中，有些 T 細胞的啟動反應與癌細胞的應答都很強，有些只偏向一方，另有少數反應很弱。與普通培養盤共同培養相比，Cell-Cell-seq 更容易看見刺激後很快升起、又很快消退的早期基因反應。作者的解釋是，普通培養盤中的細胞碰面時間不同，短暫反應容易被其他接觸階段的讀數沖淡。T 細胞進入杯中先花約 1.5 小時，再共同培養 3 小時；時程較一致，但各對接觸仍非同時開始。

混合讀數還有另一個陷阱：若癌細胞貢獻較多 RNA，T 細胞的記錄占比就會下降，看起來像反應變弱。作者為此設計 ccRepair，從單獨測量的細胞挑出表現穩定、可辨識來源的參考基因。比較這些基因在單獨與配對測量中的讀數，便能估計混合造成的比例偏差，再調整雙方的讀數尺度。校正後，原本被沖淡的 T 細胞反應較清楚，兩個細胞一起變動的關係仍在。ccRepair 只調整已判定來源的基因讀數，並不補造新的 RNA 記錄。

保住配對關係後，研究者看見雙方的趨化因子相關基因常一起升高；趨化因子是傳遞訊息、招引細胞的蛋白。另一種關係則方向相反：癌細胞上一個與免疫抑制相關的基因 TM4SF1 較高時，T 細胞的啟動相關基因往往較低。相同關係在假配對中不明顯，支持它們與真實接觸有關。可是，共同升降仍不能證明哪個分子造成了哪個反應。這項研究驗證的是一套工程化癌細胞與 T 細胞模型，未證明改變這些基因能改善治療，也未驗證早期讀數能預測長期殺癌效果。

主題一：Nanovial 凹腔載體製備與表面功能化
製備能容納腫瘤與 T 細胞配對、保留接觸並可裝配分泌捕捉或抗原呈現配體的凹腔水凝膠載體。

最佳版本vote 1

Nanovial 的成形先從小液滴開始。作者用油流擠細水流，再把水流切成小滴；這種裝置稱為流體聚焦微流體液滴產生器。三個入口分別通入明膠水溶液、含四臂聚乙二醇（4-Arm-PEG，分子量 5 kDa，即 5 千道爾頓）的水溶液，以及油相。第二股水相還含受光後啟動反應的光起始劑（LAP）。油相由穩定液滴界面的 Pico-surf 與作為載液的 Novec 7500 組成。兩種水相在滴內分成不同區域，稱為相分離。接著在產生器下游、收集前，以紫外光把可反應的分子接成網（UV 交聯），再洗去未聚合的部分，留下帶凹腔的水凝膠載體。成形流程見本論文方法節 §4.1，沿用 Langerman 等人（2025；文獻 6）的製備操作。

Nanovial 的尺寸要同時看外徑與凹腔內徑。本文製得的外徑為 48 ± \(2 \mu m\)，凹腔內徑為 35 ± \(1 \mu m\)。作者將載體滅菌後，放在 4°C 的洗滌液中保存。洗滌液以維持酸鹼度與鹽分的磷酸鹽緩衝液（PBS）為底，加入抑制細菌與真菌的 1% Antibiotic-Antimycotic、0.05% Pluronic F-127，以及 0.05% 牛血清白蛋白（BSA）。Pluronic F-127 是界面活性劑，BSA 是常用來減少非特異吸附的蛋白；這是配方成分的功能說明，本文沒有逐一比較移除它們的效果。這些百分比與低溫條件均按方法節 §4.1 記錄，原文未報告可替換範圍或保存期限。

Nanovial 的功能化是在凹腔內建立可更換的分子接頭。作者先用帶有可反應接頭的生物素（NHS-conjugated biotin）修飾凹腔，再加入可結合多個生物素的蛋白（streptavidin）。在室溫慢速旋轉 30 分鐘後，以過量洗滌液洗三次。接著加入帶生物素、能抓住 granzyme B 的捕捉抗體（biotinylated anti-human granzyme B），或供 T 細胞辨認的抗原片段與呈現蛋白所組成的單一複合體（PAP pMHC monomer）。第二次接合也在室溫慢速旋轉 30 分鐘，再洗三次。兩輪 30 分鐘是原文使用的接合條件，並非經本文證明的最短時間。接合流程見本論文方法節 §4.1 及 Langerman 等人（2025；文獻 6）。

Nanovial 的凹腔形狀來自相分離與交聯的分工。相分離先讓滴內不同材料各占一塊空間，交聯再把其中的聚合物網路固定下來。洗掉未聚合相後，原本被它占據的空間就能形成開口凹腔。這是對方法節 §4.1 成形流程的物理解釋。明膠留在凹腔的表面層，後續可供 PC3 黏附，並不是把兩顆細胞完全封死的外殼。開口讓細胞與培養液接觸，凹腔則限制細胞移動；因此局部捕捉分泌物不等於將每一對與外界完全隔離。

表面接頭要把捕捉能力留在 Nanovial 上。凹腔的生物素與 streptavidin 結合後，streptavidin 仍可接住第二批帶生物素的材料，於是形成表面、接合蛋白與功能材料之間的連結。若抗體只漂在液體裡，抓到蛋白也未必仍跟著原先那個 Nanovial。每輪洗三次的作用，是移除沒有固定住的材料，讓最後的局部訊號較能對應載體。這是接合設計的功能解釋；方法節 §4.1 未量化各輪接合效率，也未逐一測試省略洗滌的後果。

Nanovial 的尺寸還要配合後面的液滴晶片。凹腔須容納 PC3 與 T 細胞，外徑與軟硬度則會影響載體能否通過晶片並穩定形成液滴。補圖 3 比較外徑 35、47、48、\(50 \mu m\) 的配方，以及 45、49、50、\(51 \mu m\) 的軟配方；圖說把軟配方定義為 PEG 交聯密度較低。各配方外徑的變異係數為 2.8%–7.1%，也就是尺寸標準差占平均值的比例。作者以不會在測試中溶解的膠珠，觀察載體能否與條碼珠一起被包進膠珠乳化液滴（gel beads-in-emulsion，GEM）。這項篩查支持封裝相容性，不能推成所有尺寸都有相同的定序品質。

Nanovial 製備出問題，首先會影響載體能否保住細胞與捕捉分泌物。依成形原理推論，未形成穩定網路時，洗滌後可能留不住預期的杯形；尺寸不合適時，可能裝不下細胞或干擾液滴生成。若表面接頭不足，固定的捕捉材料就可能較少。此時訊號偏低，可能是蛋白沒被充分捕捉，不能直接判定細胞分泌較少。這些是流程機制預期的失敗方向，本文沒有提供相分離或交聯失敗的判定門檻。是否確實得到能用的凹腔，須回到尺寸、形貌與共封裝證據，不能只憑加過試劑判定成功。

Nanovial 製備的關鍵缺口是定量配方與曝光條件。本文未列微流體通道尺寸、各相流速、明膠／PEG／LAP 濃度，以及紫外光波長、強度和曝光時間。滅菌方法、streptavidin 與功能材料濃度、型號，以及軟配方的定量組成都未交代。用來捕捉 T 細胞表面 CD45 蛋白的 anti-CD45 塗層出現在方法節 §4.3–4.4，完整接合條件也未提供。製備細節可查連結單細胞分泌與轉錄體資料的 SEC-seq 流程（Langerman 等人，2025；文獻 6）。de Rutte 等人（2022；文獻 7）及 Udani 等人（2024；文獻 8）則提供平台背景。不能把這些引用直接當作本文已報告的配方。

主題二：腫瘤與 T 細胞的順序裝載及接觸時窗控制
先以明膠保留 PC3 腫瘤細胞，再由腫瘤抗原辨識捕捉 T 細胞，以建立可經清洗且接觸時窗受控的細胞配對。

最佳版本vote 1

作者先裝入 PC3，再加入 T 細胞，稱為順序裝載（sequential cell loading；操作見本論文方法節 §4.1）。第一步利用凹腔內既有的明膠層讓 PC3 黏附。作者把 PC3 與外徑 \(48 \mu m\) 的 Nanovial 按細胞對載體 3:1 混合，放進已有 1.5 mL 腫瘤細胞培養液的 24 孔盤。於 37°C、5% CO₂ 培養 2 小時，每隔 30 分鐘用 1 mL 移液器重懸 10 次。接著以孔徑 \(20 \mu m\)、可反向回收載體的可逆濾網（CellTrics reversible strainer）過濾，並用過量洗滌液洗去未結合 PC3。定期重懸可讓細胞與載體繼續有碰面的機會；這是操作的功能解釋，原文未比較其他混勻次數。

T 細胞的裝載以已留住 PC3 的 Nanovial 為起點。作者將這些載體回收到另一個裝有 1.5 mL 腫瘤細胞培養液的 24 孔盤，再加入事先以追蹤染劑（CellTracker Deep Red）標記的 T 細胞。T 細胞對已載 PC3 的 Nanovial 比例為 1.5:1。於 37°C、5% CO₂ 培養 1.5 小時，在中點重新吹打混勻，最後再次用 \(20 \mu m\) 可逆濾網與過量洗滌液除去未結合 T 細胞。這次留下 T 細胞的是凹腔內的 PC3，而非替 T 細胞另外加一層通用黏著劑。第二階段的操作也見本論文方法節 §4.1。

細胞對載體比例是投入細胞數除以載體數（cell-to-Nanovial ratio）。3:1 不表示每個 Nanovial 最後都裝三顆 PC3，1.5:1 也不表示每個凹腔都有一顆半 T 細胞；實際裝載結果要靠分選與影像確認。兩次裝載使用的腫瘤細胞培養液，是 Ham’s F-12K 基礎培養液加入 10% 胎牛血清（FBS）與 5% GlutaMAX 補充物。FBS 提供血清成分，GlutaMAX 是培養用的穩定麩醯胺酸補充物。5% 按方法節 §4.1 原文保留，本文沒有提供替換比例的比較。

PC3 與 T 細胞的特定結合靠抗原辨識。本文的工程細胞株（PC3-A2-PAP）表現前列腺酸性磷酸酶抗原（PAP），並以表面呈現蛋白版本 HLA-A*02:01 展示其中的 TLMSAMTNL 片段。抗原片段與呈現蛋白合在一起稱為 pMHC，T 細胞則靠表面辨識蛋白（T cell receptor，TCR）結合它。PC3 另外帶有綠色與紅色螢光蛋白（GFP／RFP），用來追蹤腫瘤細胞，並不負責辨認 T 細胞。抗原模型源自 Mao 等人（2022；文獻 9），本文操作見方法節 §4.1。

TCR156-WT 與 TCR156-45 都辨認相同的 PAP pMHC，但兩個受體版本相差兩個胺基酸。TCR156-45 採用讓結合在特定受力條件下更持久的設計（catch-bond engineering）。這裡的重點是結合不容易立即解離，而不是增加另一個辨識目標。本文結果節段落 46–47 報告 TCR156-45 的配對效率較高，與較穩定的抗原接觸一致。受體設計源自 Chen 等人（2025；文獻 10）。本文沒有提供兩個突變位置，也未量化受力與結合持續時間的關係。

裝載比例是先經測試才選定。PC3 對 Nanovial 的投入比例測過 0.5、1.5、2、3、4.5，裝有 PC3 的載體比例到 3 後趨於平臺。T 細胞測試則固定使用 20 萬個已載 PC3 的 Nanovial，分別測 0.5、1、1.5、2、2.5 的投入比例；雙細胞載體比例到 1.5 後，繼續增加 T 細胞未見明顯改善。因此作者後續選 3:1 與 1.5:1，讓投入細胞增加時仍能換得有效裝載，而不是把細胞越加越多。這是方法節 §4.1 與補圖 1 的經驗選擇，原文未報完整重複數與統計檢定；20 萬個是最佳化測試的載體數，不能直接當作每個正式樣本的用量。

轉錄體實驗的接觸時窗從 T 細胞加入後才開始計算。T 細胞先有 1.5 小時的裝載期，第二次過濾後再共同培養 3 小時；結果節與補圖 12 因此描述約 4.5 小時的互作時窗。PC3 先前單獨裝載的 2 小時不屬於雙細胞接觸。這種安排把相遇限制在較集中的時段，也讓定序聚焦早期反應，但每一對何時首次碰面仍可能不同。本文未量到各對的首次接觸時間，不能把它寫成所有細胞都精確同步地接觸 3 小時。時間依據為方法節 §4.1、§4.4 與結果節段落 61。

順序裝載的兩次洗滌負責去掉尚未固定的細胞。依流程推論，省略洗滌會讓游離 PC3 或 T 細胞跟著樣本進入後續步驟，增加測到非預期細胞組合的機會。若 TCR 不辨認 PC3 展示的抗原，則不能預設它能在相同清洗條件下保留相同的配對。作者另加入不辨認此目標的 TCR T 細胞檢驗捕捉特異性，但未提供該受體的具體名稱與序列。凹腔提供幾何限制，抗原辨識提供選擇性的結合，兩者的作用不能互相替代；這是對方法節 §4.1 對照設計的解釋。

順序裝載的缺漏集中在染色與個別樣本的實際投入。方法節 §4.1 未交代 CellTracker Deep Red 的濃度、染色時間，以及每個正式 PC3 與 T 細胞樣本的絕對細胞數。本文也沒有提供裝載期內各配對接觸起點的分布。因此目前可確定的是操作次序、投入比例和處理時窗，不能再推成每次實驗都有同樣的配對數或完全一致的接觸歷史。Nanovial 的製備則沿用能連結單細胞分泌與轉錄體資料的 SEC-seq 流程（Langerman 等人，2025；文獻 6）。該引用不補足本文未載明的細胞裝載參數。

主題三：雙細胞 Nanovial 的流式分選與顯微驗證
依載體散射與兩種細胞的螢光訊號富集配對 Nanovial，並以顯微影像確認分選事件確實保留兩個接觸細胞。

最佳版本vote 1

作者依光訊號逐一挑出 Nanovial，這種操作稱為螢光細胞分選（FACS；流程見本論文方法節 §4.2）。Sony SH800S 分選儀先依散射訊號圈出載體，再依 PC3 的綠色螢光圈出載有腫瘤細胞的載體，最後依 T 細胞標記保留同時帶兩種細胞訊號的事件，稱為雙陽性事件。圖上每個圈定的保留區域稱為 gate。一個事件指一次通過光束、被儀器記錄的物體，可能是完整 Nanovial，也可能是游離細胞或聚集體，因此不能一開始就把所有事件叫作細胞對。

分選圖的座標包含散射方向與訊號脈衝兩部分。側向散射（side scatter，SSC）與前向散射（forward scatter，FSC）記錄不同方向的散射光；物體通過光束時，儀器會記錄訊號強度隨時間變化的曲線。A 是曲線下的面積，表示這段時間累積的訊號；H 是訊號的最高強度，W 是訊號持續的時間。第一層用 SSC-A 對 FSC-H 選出 Nanovial。第二層用綠光偵測通道的 FITC-A 對 FITC-W，保留載有 PC3 的 Nanovial；綠光來自 PC3 表現的綠色螢光蛋白（GFP）。FITC 在這裡是通道名稱，並不是說作者另外用 FITC 抗體染 PC3。方法節 §4.2 將游離細胞記在這個 gate 左側、Nanovial 聚集體記在右側，空載體也要排除；圖 2B 可對照整個順序。

T 細胞的最後一層分選需依實際標記辨認。方法節 §4.2 寫的是遠紅光通道的 APC-A 對 APC-W，圖 2B 與補圖 2B 卻標為 APC-Cy7-A/W。第一次實驗使用追蹤染劑 CellTracker Deep Red。第二次改以 T 細胞表面的神經生長因子受體作標記（NGFR，亦稱 CD271）。辨認這個標記的抗體稱為 anti-CD271；抗體攜帶的遠紅螢光染料 APC/Fire750 讓儀器能讀到訊號。APC、APC-Cy7、APC/Fire750 在此分別表示原文記錄的通道或螢光標記，不能默認它們使用同一套濾光片設定。本文沒有交代如何逐一對應這些命名差異，重現前必須核對實際染劑與儀器設定。

面積與寬度搭配使用，是因為相同總螢光可以來自不同物體。較小的游離細胞與帶細胞的 Nanovial 通過光束時，訊號持續型態可能不同；多個載體黏在一起也可能產生不同的脈衝。這是方法節 §4.2 使用面積對寬度的量測原理解釋。只挑最亮的事件，無法保證得到恰好一顆 PC3 與一顆 T 細胞。脈衝形狀有助排除不合適物體，細胞數與接觸位置仍要靠影像確認。

顯微驗證直接檢查分選事件是不是預期的細胞對。作者將目標 Nanovial 分選進 96 孔盤，用 Nikon ECLIPSE Ti 倒立螢光顯微鏡觀察。明場影像顯示凹腔外形，兩種細胞的螢光則標出它們的位置，因而能確認兩顆細胞位在同一凹腔中接觸。這項驗證見方法節 §4.2 與圖 2B。雙陽性只代表兩種訊號同時被記錄，影像才補上空間關係；本文沒有提供完整定量的雙細胞純度，也不能由一張代表影像推成每個事件都正確。

正式上機濃度依樣本的物理單位記錄。Nanovial 樣本以每 \(\mu L 500\) 個載體送入 Sony SH800S；一般共培養樣本則是每 \(\mu L 1000\) 顆細胞。方法節 §4.4 還要求從一般共培養組分選等數量的 GFP⁺ PC3 與 NGFR⁺ T 細胞到同一管。這是在控制細胞種類的數量，並沒有恢復普通共培養中原先誰與誰配對。兩種濃度均是原文操作值，本文未提供流速或濃度最佳化結果，不能把 500 個載體直接改寫成 500 顆細胞，也不能據此推算定序回收數。

分選失敗最直接的後果，是把空載體、游離細胞或聚集體當作雙細胞載體送入後續測定。依量測原理推論，若通道、染劑或兩色互相溢出的校正不匹配，雙陽性區域也可能被錯估。本文未提供 gate 數值邊界、用於校正色彩溢出的補償矩陣、雷射與濾光片設定、噴嘴或晶片尺寸、分選壓力及速度。原文也未列影像分析軟體，且沒有另加存活染劑的 gate 描述。因此方法節 §4.2 提供的是分選次序與驗證思路，不能憑圖面替作者補出完整儀器設定。

配對建庫把分選後的完整 Nanovial 當作一個投入單位，接入形成條碼珠液滴的系統（10x Chromium GEM-X）。作者使用讀取 RNA 3′ 端來量基因表現的文庫流程（single-cell 3′ gene expression，3′ GEX）。每個樣本分別依製造商的單細胞操作流程建庫，將投入單位換成 Nanovial（本論文方法 §4.4；未列廠商文件編號）。

共同條碼建立於含凝膠珠的乳化液滴（gel beads-in-emulsion，GEM）。圖 1 與補圖 3 顯示一個 Nanovial 與條碼珠同處液滴，珠提供辨認該液滴的序列（barcode）。兩細胞釋出的 RNA 取得同一 dyad 層級 barcode，之後被讀成混合轉錄體。這保留一起被測量的關係，沒有直接為兩個細胞建立分開的 barcode。共同 barcode 不保證兩方 RNA 各半。本文指出 PC3 通常較大、總 RNA 較多，因此可主導混合文庫。補圖 4 比較各組每筆轉錄體資料的總計數，也就是文庫大小。這反映取得的資料量；圖說未交代是否已合併同一 RNA 的重複讀取。兩方來源仍要依單細胞對照與後續計算推定，不能把共同封裝寫成已分離兩份完整轉錄體。

第一次定序實驗（run 1）包含四組：未活化 T 細胞單獨存在的對照（singlet）、PC3 singlet、抗原刺激後的 T singlet，以及 PC3–T dyad。未活化 T 細胞用辨識白血球表面 CD45 的抗體（anti-CD45）捕捉；PC3 靠明膠黏附。刺激對照則用抗原片段與呈現分子組成的複合體（pMHC）。作者將單個複合體（monomer）固定在載體上，捕捉並活化 T 細胞。這四組讓後續比較有未互作及單獨刺激的參照。

Run 1 使用傳代第 11 次（passage 11）的工程 PC3-A2-PAP 細胞，以及製備第 9 天的 T 細胞。PC3-A2-PAP 是能展示指定腫瘤抗原的工程 PC3；T 細胞帶辨認同一目標的改造受體（TCR156-45，TCR 即 T 細胞受體）。T 細胞裝載前用追蹤染劑（CellTracker Deep Red）預染。移除未結合細胞後，各組放入 6 孔盤的 2 mL 培養液，在 37°C、5% 二氧化碳培養 3 小時。收集後以過量洗滌液洗一次，重懸為每 \(\mu L 500\) 個 Nanovial，再以流式分選儀（Sony SH800S）依螢光逐組分選。

第二次定序實驗（run 2）重複四組，另加一般共培養。該組在 24 孔盤的腫瘤細胞培養液中，按效應 T 細胞與標靶 PC3 的數量比（effector:target，E:T）1:1 混合，於 37°C、5% 二氧化碳培養 3 小時。五組都以神經生長因子受體（NGFR，又稱 CD271）作 T 細胞標記。收集清洗後，加入辨識這個標記的抗體（anti-human CD271），抗體上帶有 APC/Fire750 螢光標籤。在冰上染 30 分鐘，再洗一次。一般共培養以每 \(\mu L 1000\) 個細胞分析，將等數帶綠色螢光蛋白（GFP）的 PC3 與 NGFR 陽性 T 細胞分到同一管；Nanovial 四組仍用每 \(\mu L 500\) 個載體。

建庫在每組分選後分別進行，產出由 RNA 轉成的 DNA 複本（complementary DNA，cDNA）文庫。文庫用大量讀取序列的 NovaSeq X Plus 10B 平台定序：run 1 使用半條流動槽通道（0.5 lane），run 2 使用一條通道（1 lane）。兩次都記為 2 × 50，即雙端各讀 50 個鹼基。每個 dyad 取得的序列讀取數稱為定序深度，無法由上述總體配置推得固定值。

匹配對照讓單獨細胞與 dyad 經過相近的載體處理和建庫流程。未互作 singlet 提供細胞本來的表現，供後續合成 pseudo-dyad；抗原刺激 T singlet 則提供無腫瘤參與時的反應。一般共培養另檢查自由相遇條件的表現，但其中等數回收兩類細胞不會重建原本誰與誰接觸。建庫前的 3 小時培養用於觀察早期反應，並保留腫瘤細胞與完整轉錄體。這段時間在 T 細胞 1.5 小時裝載後開始，不能稱每對精確同步接觸 3 小時。補圖 3 的載體與凝膠珠共封裝則支持硬體相容性；它不提供各樣本的目標回收數或每筆細胞或 dyad 資料的定序深度。

抗原模型先確定 PC3 展示什麼。工程株 PC3-A2-PAP 表現前列腺酸性磷酸酶（PAP），並以呈現抗原的分子型別 HLA-A*02:01 展示 PAP 的 TLMSAMTNL 片段。抗原片段與呈現分子的複合體稱為 pMHC。PC3 另表現綠色與紅色螢光蛋白（GFP／RFP）供追蹤。T 細胞則導入辨認此 pMHC 的 T 細胞受體（T cell receptor，TCR），原型稱 TCR156-WT，改造版稱 TCR156-45。受體比較以相同 pMHC 為辨認目標。TCR156-WT 的 WT 指原型，TCR156-45 與它相差兩個胺基酸。作者調整受體在受力時的結合特性，讓改造受體與目標維持結合更久（catch-bond engineering）。相同目標、不同結合穩定性，使捕捉與功能差異有可比較的生物依據。本文引用先前工程研究，沒有提供兩個突變的位置或完整受體序列。

T 細胞製備從 HLA-A*02:01 健康供者的冷凍外周血單核細胞（peripheral blood mononuclear cells，PBMC）開始，由 AllCells 提供。PBMC 是含淋巴球等細胞的材料，不等於已純化的 T 細胞。作者用能刺激 T 細胞表面 CD3 與 CD28 的抗體磁珠（CD3/CD28 Dynabeads）過夜活化，用量為每 100 萬個細胞加入 \(25 \mu L\) 磁珠。

T 細胞培養液以 AIM V 基礎培養液加入 5% 人類 AB 型血清。另含支持 T 細胞培養的重組白細胞介素（interleukin）IL-2 50 U/mL 與 IL-15 1 ng/mL。U/mL 是每毫升的活性單位，不等於質量濃度。配方還加入 1× 穩定型麩醯胺酸補充劑（GlutaMAX），以及 \(50 \mu M\) 提供還原性環境的 \(\beta-mercaptoethanol\)。以上為本文採用配方，沒有逐成分濃度的最佳化比較。細胞培養與工程流程見本論文方法 §4.5，沿用 Chen 等（2025，文獻 10）。

基因導入使用攜帶目標 TCR 的反轉錄病毒載體（MSGV retrovirus；MSGV 為原文載體名稱，本文未展開全稱）。第 1、2 天各做一次以離心促進病毒與細胞接觸的導入（spin infection），條件為 1350 × g、90 分鐘、30°C。這裡 × g 是相對地球重力的離心加速度，不能直接當轉速使用。第 3 天用磷酸鹽緩衝生理食鹽水（PBS）洗一次，再換新鮮 T 細胞培養液；第 5 天移除 Dynabeads。病毒製備流程沿用 Mao 等（2022，文獻 9）。前處理與基因導入各有用途。CD3/CD28 磁珠提供 T 細胞的活化刺激；病毒載體則把編碼指定 TCR 的遺傳訊息帶入細胞。離心提高病毒與細胞接觸的機會，是對 spin infection 的機制解釋。本文未量測各步的獨立貢獻，也未證明兩次導入或這組離心參數優於所有替代條件。

第 7 天以流式細胞分析逐細胞檢查標記。人類 CD3 用來確認 T 細胞身分，CD8 則用來辨認其中通常負責殺傷的一類 T 細胞。截短的神經生長因子受體標記（truncated NGFR）用來估計基因導入效率。小鼠來源的 TCR \(\beta\) 鏈（murine \(TCR\beta\)）則用來檢查導入受體是否呈現在細胞表面。第一次定序實驗使用第 9 天的 TCR156-45 T 細胞（流程見本論文方法 §4.5 與 §4.4）。單有導入標記不等於受體已到細胞表面；即使受體已在表面，也不能直接推出遇到腫瘤後的效應強度。這就是為何本研究還需要後續抗原匹配、配對與功能量測。

培養配方和細胞時程是本研究的樣本條件。磁珠用量、兩次導入、第 5 天去磁珠與第 7 天檢查，使後續使用的細胞有明確處理歷程。第 9 天只明確對應第一次定序實驗，本文未完整交代兩次定序的供者與批次關係。不能把這些採用條件宣稱為所有供者與 TCR 都最佳的設定。

品質篩選從四組 Nanovial 實測資料開始：PC3、未活化 T、活化 T 的單細胞，以及真實 dyad。每份資料來自一個單細胞或 dyad，記錄各基因的 RNA 表現量（轉錄體資料）。作者只保留總分子計數至少 500、粒線體基因表現比例低於 10% 的資料。分子計數依能區分原始分子與擴增副本的標記計算（unique molecular identifier，UMI），所以 500 不是「500 種基因」。基因本身還須在至少 10 個細胞中被偵測，才進入後續分析（操作見本文方法 §4.6，段落 122）。

品質門檻與統一表現尺度的處理（normalization）各有用途。從一般量測原理看，分子太少時，偶然漏掉幾個分子就可能大幅改變表現比例。粒線體讀數偏高則常是細胞受損或細胞質 RNA 流失的警訊，不能單憑它判定死亡。統一尺度能減少各份資料定序讀數多寡（定序深度）的影響，卻不能把缺少的 RNA 補回來。原文沒有宣稱 500 與 10% 是適用所有細胞的最佳界線。

雙來源篩選使用基線單細胞中只在一方測到的基因，稱為私有基因（private genes）。作者要求兩方的私有基因表現各占至少 10%，支持同一份讀數含有 T 細胞與 PC3 的來源訊號。原文未交代這個比例的分母如何計算。因此，不能自行指定一組基因，再用其表現量的總和當作分母（方法 §4.6，段落 122）。雙來源條件無法單獨證明恰好一顆 T 細胞和一顆 PC3 正在接觸。細胞偶然被包入同一液滴，或樣本混入外來 RNA，都可能提供另一側讀數，仍須結合前段分選與成像。相反地，若一方 RNA 極少，真實配對也可能被比例門檻排除。這是依混合量測原理指出的失敗模式；本文未報告此門檻的誤收率或漏收率。

主題八：基因關聯模組發現與功能特徵評分
從實測表現網路萃取細胞身分與互作反應的基因模組，再用模組分數描述每一配對的功能狀態。

最佳版本vote 1

作者用尋找共同變動基因的軟體（GeneChorus v0.12.5），分析已調整讀數尺度的單細胞與 dyad 表現。若直接用未調整尺度的計數建網，可能把讀數總量差異當成基因關係，也不符合原文指定的輸入。原文指定先轉換已分群細胞的資料，再進行多輪計算。轉換後的資料稱為 scaled-squared space，但原文沒有交代轉換式或輪數。操作見本論文方法第 4.6 節，段落 125。

建立基因關聯網路時，軟體以相關係數（Pearson correlation）衡量兩基因表現的線性關係：一個基因的表現每增加相同幅度，另一個的增減幅度是否也大致固定。網路中的每個基因是一個節點，保留的關係則是一條連線。只有平方相關係數超過 0.1 才保留連線，也就是 \(r^2\)>0.1。本文沒有提供不同門檻的選取比較，因此不能將 0.1 說成已證明的最佳值。Pearson 的 r 為正表示兩基因傾向同升同降，為負表示一高一低。平方後保留關係強弱，卻不保留正負方向，因此不能把所有入選連線都說成「共同上調」。連線也不能證明某基因直接控制另一個。

相關網路整理出一起變動的基因群（gene module）後，作者查找其中哪些功能標籤特別集中。標籤來自整理基因功能的詞彙系統（Gene Ontology，GO）。檢查某種標籤在模組內是否比用來比較的背景基因更常出現，稱為富集分析（enrichment）。操作見方法第 4.6 節，段落 125。每個模組以最富集的詞彙命名，再按各細胞群的平均模組表現，優先展示相對其他群最突出的模組。這種命名依據的是功能標籤的集中程度，因此 GO 富集名稱不能替代功能實驗。

代表性基因群（signature）有兩種選法。第一種先比較 PC3 與未活化 T 細胞的基因表現。作者使用單細胞分析套件 Seurat v4.4.3，以比較兩組表現排序的檢定（Wilcoxon test）找出差異。接著，將表現倍數取以二為底的對數（log₂ fold-change），選換算值絕對值最大的前 25 個基因。第二種從選定模組中取網路連線數（degree）最高的前 25 個基因。第一種名單有助區分 PC3 與 T 細胞的貢獻，第二種則用模組內連線較多的基因概括共同變動。兩種選法必須按對應圖說選用，不能合併成同一條排序規則；若交換名單，分數所代表的狀態也會改變。25 是本文採用的數目，原文沒有證明它是最佳值。

為了替每個 dyad 計算分數，作者使用 Seurat 中為基因群評分的函式（AddModuleScore()）。操作見方法第 4.6 節，段落 137。圖 5D 把圖 3C 的四組基因作為輸入：T 細胞身分、PC3 身分、T 細胞激素反應，以及干擾素反應特徵（IRF response，沿用本文圖名）。細胞激素是細胞用來傳訊的蛋白，干擾素屬於其中一類。IRF response 概括回應干擾素刺激的基因表現；這裡量的是相關基因分數，不是蛋白分泌量。

模組分數概括每筆單細胞或 dyad 資料中一組基因的表現，不必只憑一個基因判斷狀態。依 Seurat 官方說明與原始碼，AddModuleScore() 的一般計算方式是先算出每個可抽選基因在全部待評分資料中的平均表現，再依高低分成區間（bins）。軟體為每個指定基因，從其所在區間隨機抽選作比較基準的參照基因。這些參照基因合併並去除重複後，軟體對每筆單細胞或 dyad 資料，分別計算指定基因群與參照基因群的平均表現。以前者減去後者，就是該筆資料中指定基因群的分數（函式說明：https://satijalab.org/seurat/reference/addmodulescore）。

模組評分的背景比較有助於分辨一組基因是否表現得特別突出。即使指定基因的平均表現很高，若同筆資料中的參照平均值也同樣高，扣除後分數仍可能接近零。這可減少把共同的高表現背景誤讀為模組特有反應。正分表示指定基因平均值高於參照，負分表示低於參照，不表示基因完全沒有表現。這是計算關係的解讀，不能直接當成路徑活性的測量。

知道一般計算方式，仍不足以重現本文的分數。本文只明示 AddModuleScore() 與輸入組別，沒有提供完整輸入及參照基因名單，也未交代如何劃分表現區間。讓隨機抽選可按相同順序重做的起始設定，稱為亂數種子，本文也未提供，因此不能推定作者使用官方預設值。至於分數的展示方式，圖 3D 以群集 0、5、1、3、18 呈現相對特徵強度，但原文未交代將分數換成比例、排列單細胞與 dyad 資料的完整公式。

主題九：虛擬配對零分布與互作相關基因檢定
以獨立單細胞表現的反覆隨機配對建立無互作零分布，找出無法由單純混合解釋且對抽樣穩健的互作相關基因。

最佳版本vote 1

虛擬配對先將每個基因的原始分子計數除以該細胞總計數，得到相對表現。計數依區分原始分子與擴增副本的標記取得（unique molecular identifier，UMI）。作者把一個 PC3 與一個 T 細胞的相對表現等權平均，形成 pseudo-dyad。這項虛擬混合法（pseudo-mixing）的操作見本文方法 §4.6，段落 127–128 與方程 1。若各細胞相對表現記為 x，則 \(x_{\mathrm{pseudo}}=0.5x_{\mathrm{PC3}}+0.5x_{\mathrm{T}}\)。這裡的等權是計算配方，不表示每個真實 dyad 都由雙方提供相同 RNA 量。混合必須先在線性相對表現尺度進行，再做對數轉換。從數學原理看，先取對數再平均，通常不等於先平均再取對數，因為對數會壓縮高值。若把順序倒過來，得到的背景就不再是本文假設的兩份相對表現相加。

經驗零分布（empirical null distribution）描述無互作假設下可能出現的讀數範圍。單一平均值只給一個中心，會隱藏同類細胞本來就不同的表現。反覆混合實測單細胞，才能保留這些個體差異對混合結果的影響。本文與用合成混合偵測意外雙細胞的概念相通，引用 McGinnis、Murrow 與 Gartner 的 DoubletFinder 論文（Cell Systems，2019；文獻 11）。這是概念溯源，本文沒有報告用 DoubletFinder 套件執行此項分析。重抽樣（resampling）反覆從單細胞資料選取新的配對組合。作者建立未活化與活化兩種 pseudo-dyad：前者混合 PC3 與未活化 T，後者混合 PC3 與受到抗原呈現複合物（pMHC）刺激的 T 細胞。每次各抽 895 個 PC3 與 T，形成 895 對；895 是三組單細胞與真實 dyad 四組中最小的樣本數。每種對照各產生 50 組資料，同時抽取 50 組、每組 895 個真實 dyad 配合比較（方法 §4.6，段落 128–129）。

每輪差異比較把一組未活化 pseudo-dyad 與等數真實 dyad 合併，再用單細胞分析工具 Seurat 統一尺度。原文用統一事件表現尺度的 NormalizeData，將尺度參數 scale.factor 設為 10,000，並描述先加一再取對數的處理（log1p）。接著按兩組表現排序比較差異（Wilcoxon rank-sum test），透過差異比較函式 FindMarkers 執行，設定 test.use="wilcox"（操作見本文方法 §4.6，段落 130；Seurat 版本為 4.4.3）。同時檢查許多基因時，即使沒有真正差異，也可能偶然挑出看似有差異的基因。檢定的 p 值衡量在沒有差異的假設下出現這類結果有多罕見。作者再校正各基因的 p 值，限制顯著名單中這類誤判的預期比例（Benjamini–Hochberg correction）。校正後的 p 值（adjusted p）低於 0.05，才把該基因記為該輪顯著。10,000 是統一數值尺度的設定，不是每個樣本實際測得 10,000 個 RNA 分子。

穩健集合要求某基因在全部 50 輪未活化比較都達顯著，才列為互作相關基因（emergent genes）。等數比較降低樣本數不同對檢定的影響，取交集則排除只靠少數抽樣組合才顯著的基因。這是本文的保守規則；原文未要求 50 輪方向一致，也未另訂固定倍數變化門檻。50 組重抽樣沿用原有細胞資料，不能當成 50 次獨立生物實驗。

圖面摘要與集合判定必須分開。圖 4D/E 的另一項讀數是對數倍數變化（log₂ fold-change）：把真實 dyad 相對未活化 pseudo-dyad 的表現倍數取以二為底的對數。正值表示真實 dyad 較高，負值表示較低，絕對值越大表示倍數差異越大。圖中 adjusted p 與對數倍數變化各取 50 次比較的平均；平均 adjusted p 不是新算出的合併 p 值。圖中標示上、下調各 10 個 adjusted p 最小的基因，再加上各 5 個對數倍數變化絕對值最大的基因。這些排名用來展示，不替代「全部 50 次皆顯著」的集合定義（方法 §4.6，段落 149）。

虛擬配對仍依賴所選單細胞能代表沒有互作時的狀態，以及混合權重足以描述背景。補圖 8 展示比率敏感度，圖面與 E–F 圖說列出 1:1、1:4、1:5、1:8，但 A–D 圖說把第三組寫成 1:6。這項來源不一致須查原始分析釐清，不能擅選一個數字。本文方法的方程仍是等權平均；原文未提供逐真實 dyad 估計配比的演算法。原文也未提供重現抽樣序列所需的起始值（隨機種子）。抽出細胞後是否放回、讓同一細胞能在同輪再次被抽到（有無放回抽樣），也未交代。完整 FindMarkers 設定，以及不同實驗是否均使用 895，同樣缺少說明。

主題十：私有基因與活化對照的細胞來源歸屬
結合單細胞基線的私有表現與活化態虛擬配對對照，將互作相關基因保守歸屬到 PC3 或 T 細胞區室。

最佳版本vote 1

私有基因歸屬先檢查未互作單細胞的基線表現（private-gene mapping，操作見本文方法 §4.6，段落 138；結果段落 68）。若已列為 T 細胞互作相關的基因在單獨 PC3 中測不到，便視為 T-private；反過來，PC3 互作相關基因在單獨 T 中測不到，便視為 PC3-private。原文用基因 CCL4、XCL1 示範 T 側來源，用 CXCL1、CYR61 示範 PC3 側來源。

活化態虛擬混合（activated-state pseudo-mixing）把 PC3 與活化 T 的單細胞資料合成 pseudo-dyad，再以本文的差異表現框架和真實 dyad 比較（操作見本文方法 §4.6，段落 131）。活化 T 對照接觸的是 Nanovial 上呈現抗原的胜肽與主要組織相容性分子複合物（peptide–major histocompatibility complex，pMHC）。它讓比較背景包含 T 細胞活化時本來就可能出現的變化，因而幫助找出真實配對額外的反應。PC3 基線過濾針對的是兩種 T 細胞活化狀態不完全相同的問題。單靠 pMHC 刺激的 T，未必等同和 PC3 接觸的 T。若省略過濾，兩種 T 狀態的差異也可能被誤當成 PC3 的反應。要求 PC3 基線可測得表現，讓腫瘤側歸屬多一項支持，但原文未比較不同基準的表現。

PC3 基線過濾要求候選基因在單獨 PC3 中有足夠表現（PC3-expression filter，操作見本文方法 §4.6，段落 131）。作者用只檢查平均值是否高於指定基準的單尾 t 檢定，測試統一尺度後的表現是否高於 0.05。同時檢查許多基因，可能偶然挑出看似高於表現基準的基因。作者因此校正檢定結果，限制通過名單中這類誤判的預期比例（Benjamini–Hochberg correction）。校正後的顯著性指標（adjusted p）低於 0.05，才保留該基因。前一個 0.05 是表現尺度上的基準，後一個是統計顯著門檻；兩者不是同一種數值。

最終歸屬先取兩張名單的交集，也就是共同成員。令 \(E_{\mathrm{rest}}\) 代表所有 50 次未活化 pseudo-dyad 比較都顯著的集合，\(D_{\mathrm{act,PC3}}\) 代表活化對照差異名單中通過 PC3 基線過濾的集合，則 \(E_{\mathrm{PC3}}=E_{\mathrm{rest}}\cap D_{\mathrm{act,PC3}}\)。剩下的差集歸為 T 細胞相關，即 \(E_{\mathrm{T}}=E_{\mathrm{rest}}\setminus E_{\mathrm{PC3}}\)（方法 §4.6，段落 132）。因此，T 細胞相關名單的每個成員，不一定都符合嚴格的 T-private 條件。

基線測不到不等於另一側永遠不表現。補圖 7 的來源推定假設互作主要改變原本就在表現的基因，而不會讓另一種細胞開始表現原先在其基線測不到的基因。若這個假設失效，私有基因便可能被誤歸屬。PC3 基線過濾也有對應代價：真正因互作才從零開始表現的 PC3 基因，可能被排除。這些名單是依對照條件推定的來源，不是逐分子直接定位。

來源展示的比較基準各有用途。圖 4B 熱圖包含未活化 T、PC3、活化 T、一組隨機未活化 pseudo-dyad、一組隨機活化 pseudo-dyad，以及真實 dyad 六組；圖 4C 的分布圖則整合 50 組 pseudo-dyad。圖 4F 以每個點代表一個 PC3 相關基因，同時呈現變化大小與顯著程度（火山圖）。橫軸是 50 次真實 dyad 對活化 pseudo-dyad 比較的平均對數倍數變化：先把表現倍數取以二為底的對數，正值表示真實 dyad 較高，負值表示較低。縱軸將 50 次比較的平均 adjusted p 取以十為底的負對數，越高表示 p 值越小。圖 4D/E 則使用未活化對照。因此並排圖上的數值，不能當作都來自同一個比較（方法 §4.6，段落 136、146、149）。

主題十一：ccRepair 區室錨點與混合稀釋校正
用單細胞對照中的穩定區室錨點為每個真實配對計算縮放係數，恢復區室基因的可比較表現尺度。

最佳版本vote 1

ccRepair 針對真實 dyad 中已推定來源的基因，調整因 RNA 混合而改變的表現尺度（操作見本文方法 §4.6，段落 134）。來源分為 PC3 與 T 細胞兩個區室（compartment）。PC3 基因只乘 PC3 的係數，T 基因只乘 T 的係數；未歸屬基因和所有單細胞對照不改。調整後再放回統一尺度的表現矩陣，供可視化、功能特徵比較與基因相關分析。

錨點基因（anchor genes）是用來提供穩定表現參照的一組基因。作者從 PC3 單細胞，以及受到 Nanovial 上抗原呈現複合物（pMHC）刺激的 T 單細胞選取錨點。候選須是偏向某一來源的私有區室基因（private compartment genes）。將目標側相對另一側的表現倍數取以二為底的對數（log₂ fold-change），結果須大於 1.5；統一尺度後的標準化表現則須在目標區室大於 1、相反區室小於 0.2。方法段落記載約 100 個一致的基因，但未明確交代是每側約 100 個或兩側合計（段落 134）。這些錨點條件要求目標側可測得表現，並把候選集中於來源差異較大的基因。T 側參照明確採用 pMHC 活化單細胞，讓參照至少包含活化狀態；不能默認換成未活化 T。原文未證明這些閾值最優，也未提供定量的穩定性評估規則，因此「區室特異」本身不等於「互作時不會改變」。

縮放係數（scaling factor）以未混合單細胞的錨點平均表現，除以該 dyad 同一區室的錨點平均表現。若區室 c 的錨點集合為 \(A_c\)、單細胞目標平均值為 \(\mu_c\)，可將方法段落的文字整理為 \(s_{d,c}=\mu_c/\operatorname{mean}_{g\in A_c}(x_{d,g})\)。接著只對屬於區室 c 的基因套用 \(x'_{d,g}=s_{d,c}x_{d,g}\)。其中 d 表示某一個 dyad，g 表示基因；這是將原文規則記號化，並非作者另列的一條編號方程。組成稀釋（compositional dilution）是指一方 RNA 在混合總量中占比較低，使其基因相對讀數一起偏小。ccRepair 的工作假設是同一區室的穩定錨點能反映這種整體尺度偏移。若錨點在 dyad 中只達目標的一半，按原文比值規則，該區室乘數便為二。這是公式的示意例子，不是本文額外測量值；乘數也不能直接當成實測 RNA 貢獻比例。

錨點若在互作中升高，分母可能變大，校正便可能把該區室真正的升高壓低。錨點若降低，乘數可能放大，讓其他基因看似升高。這是由比值規則推得的失敗模式，不是本文已量得的錯誤率。若 dyad 的錨點平均接近零，乘數還可能不穩定；原文未交代零分母處理或乘數上下限，不能自行補入小常數或裁切規則。

校正效果主要看表現尺度與相關結構。圖 6B/C 對照基因 GZMB、XCL1、DKK1 的分布，也比較整組相關基因（模組）的表現概括值，也就是模組分數。圖 6F 與補圖 10 檢查跨細胞的共同變化在校正後是否仍存在。這些基因是圖中的檢視例子，不代表完整錨點清單。ccRepair 接在互作相關基因判定之後，不能倒寫成建立 pseudo-dyad 所代表的無互作讀數範圍（零分布）的必要前置步驟。

完整重現仍缺少錨點清單、標準化尺度是否已含對數的程式細節，以及共享基因的分配方式。不同數值尺度會改變平均值與乘數，因此不能自行選一種尺度後宣稱重現原分析。ccRepair 也不補回未量到的 RNA，不把混合資料變成兩個獨立實測細胞的完整轉錄體。它提供的是已歸屬基因的尺度調整，效用取決於來源與錨點假設。

模組相關先為每個真實 dyad 的四組相關基因（模組）計算分數，再比較分數在不同 dyad 中是否一起變動。作者用單細胞分析工具 Seurat 的 AddModuleScore()，把指定基因集轉成逐事件相對表現分數。前三組描述 T 細胞身分、PC3 身分，以及 T 細胞訊息與效應分子反應。第四組收錄對干擾素這類免疫訊息蛋白作出反應的基因（本文標為 IRF response）。接著量化兩組分數的線性同向或反向變動（Pearson correlation；操作見本文方法 §4.6，段落 137）。圖 5D 的 T 對 PC3 分數為 −0.11，T 訊息反應對干擾素反應為 0.36；這是觀察到的相關強度，不是篩選閾值。

基因層級分析限於來源較可辨的私有基因（private genes）。T 側候選是已歸為 T 相關、且在 PC3 單細胞中測不到的互作相關基因；PC3 側則反過來定義。作者以真實 dyad 的標準化表現，計算所有候選基因兩兩之間的相關矩陣（方法 §4.6，段落 138–140）。每個矩陣格子是一對基因跨多個 dyad 的相關，並不是在單一 dyad 裡算一次相關。

候選篩選先排除基因對自己的對角格子，再只取矩陣上三角，使每對基因只算一次。作者把這些相關值排序，保留高於第 99.95 百分位或低於第 0.05 百分位的基因對。接著將 50 組未活化 pseudo-dyad 的相對表現矩陣串接，用 Seurat 標準化後，計算同一組私有基因的背景相關矩陣。真實減去虛擬的相關差值為 \(\Delta r=r_{\mathrm{real}}-r_{\mathrm{pseudo}}\)。

第二層門檻取自整張差值矩陣。先排除基因和自己比較的格子，再將其餘格子（非對角元素）的差值排序，取第 99.5 百分位。作者再檢查第一層入選基因對所涉及的基因，按原文規則保留相關差值超過此門檻的基因（操作見本文方法 §4.6，段落 140–144）。相關差值必須保留正負號。真實相關比虛擬相關更正，差值為正；若真實相關變得更負，差值便可能為負。原文最後的第 99.5 百分位條件取有號差值的高端，不能改寫成絕對差值的雙尾篩選。圖中另有負相關示例，也不能據此倒推作者使用了原文未寫的絕對值規則。

相同基因與相同排序，讓圖 5E 和補圖 9 的相關熱圖能直接比較。從方法原理看，兩個基因可能本來就在同一細胞裡一起變動；混合總量的限制也可能讓兩側訊號反向變化。Pseudo-dyad 提供獨立混合時的背景，讓這些既有結構不被自動當成互作證據。圖 5I 再以每個基因對為一點，比較真實與虛擬相關，分成 T–T、T–PC3、PC3–PC3 三類，並各標示相關差異最大的前五對（方法 §4.6，段落 152）。

分層檢視用 T 細胞相關基因 CD3E 的表現切組，再比較 TM4SF1 分布。作者以真實 dyad 中 CD3E 標準化表現的第 95 百分位為界；高於者為高組，等於或低於者為低組。圖 5F 顯示兩組真實 dyad 的 TM4SF1 分布，圖 5G 用合併的未活化 pseudo-dyad 作對照（方法 §4.6，段落 147）。原文未說明虛擬組沿用真實門檻或重新取自身百分位，也未明示兩圖的平均數比較檢定，因此不能補寫成某個已知檢定。

真實與虛擬相關不同，支持這種共變超出所建立的獨立混合背景。原文展示 T 側與 PC3 側製造吸引其他細胞之訊號蛋白的基因（趨化因子基因）一起升高，也展示 TM4SF1 與 T 細胞相關基因的反向關係（結果段落 80–81）。這些是跨配對的協調或反向表現線索。即使同一趨勢在 ccRepair 校正後仍存在，也不能單靠相關決定訊號由哪一方發起，更不能直接證明某個基因抑制另一方。

候選相關仍有抽樣與篩選限制。同一單細胞可在不同虛擬資料集中反覆出現，因此拼接 50 組不會增加獨立供者數。本文未提供每對相關差值在重新抽樣時可能波動多大的估計範圍（抽樣信賴區間）。也未交代如何逐對檢查差異是否超出偶然，以及同時檢查許多基因關係時如何降低誤判風險（多重比較校正）。因此，按百分位挑出的極端關係，不能當成已通過校正、用來判斷差異是否超出偶然的顯著性檢定結果。混合比例仍可能影響相關，宜合看虛擬背景與 ccRepair 前後結果（圖 6F、補圖 10）。將同一批資料挑出的極端關係直接解讀成已確認機制，會高估其證據強度。

主題十三：共培養與配體受體推論的增益比較
將配對解析的互作基因與一般共培養及 CellChat 推論結果比較，界定受控配對在早期反應和下游程式偵測上的增益。

作者先將真實 dyad 與未活化 pseudo-dyad 比較，找出互作相關基因（emergent genes）。它們相對只有資料混合的對照仍反覆出現變化，不一定原本完全沒有表現。圖 4G 比較這些基因在受控配對與孔盤共培養中的差異檢定結果。作者另用依訊號分子表現推測通訊的工具（CellChat）取得基因名單，再與互作相關基因比對（補圖 11）。 共培養基準讓 T 細胞與 PC3 在孔盤裡自由相遇（standard co-culture，操作見本文方法 §4.4）。第二次實驗使用 24 孔盤。效應 T 細胞對目標 PC3 的投入數量比（effector-to-target ratio，E:T）設為 1:1，表示投入等數量的兩類細胞。作者在 37°C、5% 二氧化碳下培養 3 小時，再分選等數量的兩類細胞做單細胞 RNA 定序。原文的「bulk co-culture」指大量細胞共同培養；此處的定序仍逐顆讀取，未保留原本的配對。

圖 4G 先用分析單細胞基因表現的軟體（Seurat），比較共培養後 T 細胞與未活化 T 細胞的基因表現分布（差異表現檢定，differential expression，DE；操作見本文方法 §4.6「散點圖」）。兩組的差異不是直接由共培養 T 細胞和 dyad 互比得到。本文整體分析列出 Seurat v4.4.3；這段方法只說採用差異表現檢定的預設設定，沒有逐項列出。 圖 4G 從互作相關基因中依調整後 p 值，各取最顯著的前 100 個上調與前 100 個下調基因，再加上已知早期反應基因。調整後 p 值（adjusted p-value）考慮同時檢驗許多基因所造成的誤判風險。圖說把展示範圍稱為 T 細胞歸屬基因。每一點代表同一基因：橫軸取共培養檢定的調整後 p 值，縱軸取 50 次真實 dyad 對未活化 pseudo-dyad 檢定的平均調整後 p 值。兩軸再轉成以 10 為底的負對數，並標出一般共培養未達顯著的基因。

選取上、下調各 100 個基因，是作者的展示規則，讓兩個變化方向都能被檢視；原文沒有證明 100 是最佳數目。50 次則承接上游的重抽樣比較，不是 50 位供者或 50 次獨立實驗。上游先保留在全部 50 次未活化 pseudo-dyad 比較中調整後 p 值都小於 0.05 的基因，再供圖 4G 排序與取平均。這裡的平均 p 值是展示摘要，原文未把它定義為另一項合併檢定。 圖 4G 的負對數座標越大，代表 p 值越小。點偏向縱軸高處，表示該基因在 dyad 比較中的統計證據較強，不能換算成 RNA 增加相同倍數。p 值也受樣本數、變異與對照選擇影響；此處兩項檢定的樣本單位和對照不同。加上圖中基因先按 Cell-Cell-seq 結果挑選，這張圖適合檢視指定反應，不能單獨證明某方法在所有條件下更準確。

早期訊號的增益與反應持續時間有關。刺激後迅速升高又會消退的基因，稱為即時早期反應基因（immediate-early genes），本文例子包括 IER3、EGR1、EGR3 與 NR4A3。孔盤中尚未相遇、剛相遇與較早相遇的細胞一起進入群體比較，短暫高峰可能被其他階段沖淡。作者在受控配對中看到這些基因更顯著，與接觸時窗較集中的解釋相符。早期反應的背景依據見 Odagiu 等人（2020；文獻 27）與 Shao 等人（1997；文獻 28）；兩者不是本比較的操作方法。 接觸時窗不能只看共同培養的 3 小時。Nanovial 配對還有前面的 1.5 小時 T 細胞裝載，接觸可能在此期間開始；孔盤組則明確記錄混合後培養 3 小時。E:T = 1:1 只固定初始數量，不能保證每顆 T 細胞恰好碰到一顆 PC3。原文未逐對量測接觸起點，也沒有只改變同步程度的對照，因此不能把增益全歸因於完全同步接觸。

CellChat 依訊號分子的基因表現推測可能的通訊。傳遞訊號的分子稱為配體，辨認它的蛋白稱為受體，兩者構成配體—受體配對（ligand–receptor pair）。依已知配對及其在細胞群中的表現推論候選通訊的工具是 CellChat（方法來源：Jin et al., Nature Communications 12, 1088，2021；文獻 4）。作者分別分析「Nanovial 表面抗原活化的 T 細胞與 PC3」及「一般共培養後的游離細胞」，取得兩份候選基因名單。 兩種分析的觀測範圍不同。CellChat 從已知配體與受體的表現提出候選訊號傳遞。真實 dyad 對 pseudo-dyad 的差異表現檢定，則檢查相遇後哪些基因改變，也能包含接收訊號後才啟動或降低的下游反應。這些基因未必就是配體或受體，因此名單不完全重疊是可預期的。差異反映兩者回答的問題不同，不能直接當作 CellChat 的漏報率。

補圖 11 用區域重疊表示基因名單的交集（Venn diagram；比較規則見本論文補圖 11）。三個集合是兩組 CellChat 候選基因與真實 dyad 對 pseudo-dyad 找出的互作相關基因。Cell-Cell-seq 專有區有 344 個基因，三者交集有 79 個。Cell-Cell-seq 僅與活化 T 細胞那組 CellChat 重疊的區域有 24 個，僅與一般共培養那組重疊的區域有 9 個。這些都是基因數，不是細胞間通訊的連線數，也不是預測準確率。

補圖 11 的 344 只能確定是該圖的 Cell-Cell-seq 專有區基因數。圖說把計數對象寫成 CellChat 資料庫中的配體與受體基因。討論段落卻把 Cell-Cell-seq 專有區解釋為超出配體與受體範圍的互作相關基因。兩處未完整交代比較的基因範圍。缺少輸入名單與資料庫版本，就不能斷言這 344 個基因都不在任何版本的 CellChat 資料庫，也不能計算偵測率或準確率。 重現 CellChat 比較仍缺少軟體與資料庫版本、細胞分組、篩選閾值，以及重新打亂分組以估計偶然訊號的置換次數。本文也沒交代如何把顯著的通訊連線轉成基因名單，不能自行補上預設值。CellChat 的候選配對與 dyad 的下游反應都不能單獨指定致因分子。這項比較沒有逐一阻斷候選訊號，因此提供互補證據，尚未驗證特定配體—受體造成了哪個反應。

方法組合策略

(Synergy & Pipeline)

1. 以不同保留機制完成可操作的配對。 明膠凹腔先固定較大的 PC3，再讓 TCR–pMHC 辨識捕捉 T 細胞，減少兩種細胞都靠隨機同時落入的要求；兩次過濾去除游離細胞，尺寸／螢光 gating 再富集候選 dyads。顯微成像補上流式訊號無法單獨證明的「同腔兩細胞接觸」，使後續 mixed barcode 具有配對依據。

2. 把物理時窗控制與參考樣本共同設計。 1.5 小時 T-cell loading 後的 3 小時培養為早期反應設定一致的操作時窗；同平台的 PC3、resting T 及 pMHC-activated T singlets 同時保留載體處理背景。前兩者供無互作混合零分布，活化 T 對照則幫助區分單純 T 活化與真正需要腫瘤參與的變化。由於接觸可能發生在 loading 中，各 pair 的起點仍有變異，且 pMHC 活化並不保證與 dyad T 的狀態等同。

3. 用分布處理混合，再用狀態對照推定來源。 直接扣掉「平均 PC3 + 平均 T」會忽略各細胞自身異質性；反覆相對表現混合產生 50 組零分布並配平每次樣本量，可以評估特定抽樣組合是否驅動 DE。所有 resting 比較皆顯著的交集先定義 emergent 集合，activated 比較再加 PC3 基線 filter，降低把活化強弱差異錯認成腫瘤反應的風險。這一搭配解決的是可檢驗的混合背景與條件性來源推定，不會提供逐轉錄本的確定來源。

4. 將反應偵測與尺度恢復放在各自的位置。 先以 pseudo-mixing 分析互作相關基因，後以 ccRepair 的 activated-T／PC3 錨點校正已歸屬基因。前者回答混合對照下是否有變化，後者使 RNA 較少的一側不致在可視化與模組比較中被稀釋掩蓋。回頭比較 real/pseudo 相關及校正前後相關結構，可檢視候選跨細胞關係是否主要來自組成差異；它不是因果驗證的替代品。

5. 把功能讀出與方法比較當成不同的驗證層。 Granzyme B 捕捉確認配對能伴隨可測效應分泌；一般共培養檢視時序不受控時哪些早期訊號較難解析；CellChat 比較則檢視新生基因是否超出配體／受體推論的觀測範圍。各層的時間、資料單位與對照不同，必須保留這些區別，才不會把功能可測、基因相關與直接殺傷／通訊因果混為一談。

借鑑價值

(Takeaways for your research)

  • 如果你的細胞接觸在移液或分選時容易丟失， 可以考慮先用可功能化凹腔固定其中一方，再用生物辨識捕捉第二方；並把尺寸相容性、雙陽性 gating 與顯微接觸驗證作為同一條準備流程。不同細胞大小與黏附方式仍需重測，48 μm 和 3:1／1.5:1 是本文模型的參數。
  • 如果你想觀察容易被共培養時間差掩蓋的早期程式， 可以考慮限制配對形成與後續培養時窗，並把 loading 時間列入實驗紀錄；本文 1.5 小時 loading + 3 小時培養的設計提供做法，不能代替每個 pair 的實際接觸計時。
  • 如果你的測量單位本來就包含兩個細胞， 可以考慮以匹配 singlet 的反覆線性混合建立經驗零分布，並讓 real/pseudo 每次比較使用相同樣本量；同時檢查混合權重、抽樣種子與重複資料的相依性，避免把重抽樣次數當生物重複。
  • 如果雙細胞混合使你難以判斷新生基因來自哪一方， 可以考慮同時採用基線 private genes 與「只刺激其中一方」的實驗對照；本文的 PC3 基線 filter 說明如何限制活化狀態不匹配造成的假歸屬，但對基線零表現或雙側共享基因仍要保留不確定性。
  • 如果某一夥伴的 RNA 較多、遮蔽另一方的反應， 可以考慮以穩定區室錨點進行每事件縮放，並明確限定改動的基因、樣本與分析用途。借用 ccRepair 前應先驗證錨點穩定性與小分母處理，因本文未提供這些完整實作細節。
  • 如果你希望從跨細胞相關中挑選可測試機制， 可以考慮將真實 pair 的相關與 pseudo-pair 背景相比，再檢查混合校正後是否仍存在。候選 chemokine 共變或 TM4SF1–T activation 反向關係仍需擾動、阻斷或直接蛋白／功能驗證，才能建立作用方向與因果。

Abstract

Cell–cell interactions drive rapid and heterogeneous changes in gene expression, yet most transcriptomic methods either dissociate cells, losing pair identity and interaction timing, or infer communication indirectly from ligand–receptor co-expression. Here we present Cell-Cell-seq, a scalable workflow for profiling defined cell pairs (“dyads”) with single-cell transcriptomic resolution. Cell-Cell-seq uses cavity-containing hydrogel microparticles (Nanovials) to confine two cells, synchronize contact onset, protect fragile conjugates during handling and sorting, and interface directly with droplet-based RNA sequencing. Using antigen-matched prostate tumor cells and engineered T cells as a model system, Cell-Cell-seq captured thousands of tumor–T cell dyads and revealed broad functional and transcriptional heterogeneity across interactions. Dyads unmasked transient activation programs that were obscured in standard well-plate co-culture, consistent with asynchronous contact in bulk assays. To distinguish interaction-induced programs from the composite nature of dyad transcriptomes, we developed a pseudo-mixing framework that generates in silico pseudo-dyads to construct an empirical null distribution under “no interaction,” enabling statistically robust identification of emergent genes and partner-resolved attribution of responses. Dyad-resolved analysis further revealed coordinated cross-cell programs, including coupled chemokine expression consistent with bidirectional paracrine signaling and inverse coupling between tumor immunoregulatory programs and T cell activation. Finally, we introduce ccRepair to correct compositional dilution in mixed transcriptomes, improving interpretability while preserving genuine cross-cell coordination. Together, Cell-Cell-seq provides a generalizable platform for dissecting immune synapse biology and mapping interaction-dependent programs across heterogeneous cell populations, with applications in profiling tumor–immune communication and functionally screening immunotherapies.

1. Introduction

Cell–cell communication is the foundation of multicellular life. From the earliest developmental programs to the dynamics of immune defense, neural signaling, and host–microbe symbioses, cells continuously exchange information that reshapes their gene expression and behavior. These reciprocal interactions enable tissues to coordinate complex functions, while their dysregulation contributes to disorders ranging from cancer to autoimmunity and neurodegeneration. Understanding how transcriptional programs are induced by specific cellular interactions is therefore a central challenge across all of biology1.

Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) has transformed biology by providing transcriptome-wide profiles of individual cells, uncovering heterogeneity across tissues and disease states2. Yet, by requiring dissociation of cells into suspensions, scRNA-seq largely eliminates spatial and relational context, making it difficult to determine which cells were directly communicating3. Computational frameworks that infer interactions from ligand–receptor co-expression provide valuable hypotheses4 but remain indirect, describing potential rather than realized communication, and rarely are validated by direct analysis of interacting cell dyads. To recover this missing relational context, recent efforts have turned to spatial transcriptomics and other in situ profiling methods.

Spatial transcriptomics preserves tissue organization and provides powerful maps of gene expression across microenvironments5. However, most platforms still measure transcripts from regions that contain multiple cells, limiting unambiguous assignment of expression changes to a defined cell pair. Even in emerging higher-resolution approaches, transcript localization relies on computational segmentation and boundary assignment that remain imperfect, and the measured signal can reflect partial-volume contributions from neighboring cells. Moreover, adjacent cells are embedded within broader tissue microenvironments, so observed expression patterns integrate both direct contact-dependent responses and indirect influences (e.g., paracrine or longer-range signaling). As a result, spatial methods provide rich contextual snapshots of gene expression in tissues, but they are not designed to isolate the causal transcriptional programs induced specifically by a single cell–cell interaction.

To disentangle contact-dependent programs from broader microenvironmental influences, approaches are needed that capture transcriptional changes from single, physically interacting cell pairs isolated as defined units (hereafter termed dyads) and scale to hundreds to thousands of interactions to resolve response heterogeneity. Such methodologies would provide a causal framework for mapping interaction-induced programs across biological systems and offer a foundation for systematically linking defined cell-cell communication nodes to functional outcomes in health and disease.

Creating stable dyads at scale and deconvolving their mixed transcriptomes are two key challenges to study cell-cell interactions. In conventional suspension or well-based co-cultures, cells can migrate, detach, or re-partner, making it difficult to control interaction timing and to preserve fragile contacts during handling and enrichment. These limitations motivate pairing strategies that physically constrain two cells as a defined unit, reducing degrees of freedom for cell motion and enforcing sustained proximity, so that contact duration can be controlled and measured across large numbers of interactions. However, even when intact dyads are captured for sequencing, the resulting composite transcriptomes obscure which changes arise in each cell, necessitating methods that can assign interaction-induced programs to the appropriate partner cell. Guided by these constraints, we sought a pairing format that physically stabilizes dyads during handling and is directly compatible with droplet-based sequencing. We hypothesized that Nanovials, cavity-containing hydrogel microparticles, could serve as stable and protective compartments for profiling defined cell-cell interactions, while remaining compatible with droplet-based single-cell transcriptomic workflows6. The inner cavity confines two cells within a microscale volume, limiting lateral motion and reducing opportunities for additional cells to join the pair. The cavity surface can be functionalized to retain diverse adherent and suspension cell types in sustained proximity for controlled interaction durations7,8. Here, we operationally define an ‘interacting dyad’ as a two-cell pair in direct physical contact within the cavity, validated experimentally. This physical confinement also buffers fragile contacts from disruptive shear during routine handling and, when used, flow-based enrichment/sorting, preserving transient or weak interactions that would otherwise be lost. In addition, cavity-immobilized capture reagents can retain secreted proteins, enabling linkage of dyad transcriptional states to functional outputs such as cytokine secretion.

Main Figures and Captions:

By integrating these features, we established a generalized Cell-Cell-seq workflow that enables systematic capture of interacting cell dyads (Figure 1). We applied this workflow to profile and investigate a previously characterized cancer–T cell interaction: a prostate cancer cell line expressing prostatic acid phosphatase (PAP) and engineered T cells with cognate T cell receptors (TCRs)9,10. Recognition of cancer cells by T cells is primarily initiated through TCR–pMHC binding and further facilitated by multiple surface ligand–receptor pairs as well as secreted cytokines. While patterns of T cell activation have been relatively well annotated, the heterogeneity of cancer–T cell interactions remains largely unexplored, making this model an ideal system for evaluation by Cell-Cell-seq.

To resolve the challenge of composite transcriptomes, we developed a computational framework for Cell-Cell-seq that rigorously distinguishes interaction-driven signals from background (Figure 1). First, by comparing true dyads with a library of pseudo-mixed controls generated from independently sequenced single cells, we identify genes that are uniquely perturbed upon direct interaction. Second, transcripts are assigned to each interacting cell type leveraging baseline controls, a generalizable feature for studying diverse pairings. Third, by incorporating control conditions, such as peptide major histocompatibility complex I (pMHC)-coated Nanovials that activate T cells in the absence of tumor cells, we isolate induced genes that require reciprocal communication. Together, these strategies uncover emergent gene expression networks that cannot be inferred from single-cell datasets alone. For example, Cell-Cell-seq revealed consistent negative correlations between T cell activation markers and tumor-derived immunosuppressive factors, such as TM4SF1 and DKK1, across independent experiments, highlighting the ability to identify coupled gene programs and clinically actionable axes of immune evasion. More broadly, this framework enables systematic identification of interaction-specific targets for drug discovery and provides a foundation for building predictive models of cell–cell communication in health and disease.

2. Results

Nanovials enable stable co-loading of tumor and T cells

Supplementary Figures and Figure Captions

To establish a platform for studying defined cell–cell interactions, we first evaluated whether Nanovials could stably capture dyads of tumor and immune cells. PC3 prostate cancer cells engineered to express prostatic acid phosphatase (PAP) and the MHC-I allele HLA-A*02:01 (PC3-A2-PAP; dual-labeled by co-expression of GFP and RFP for fluorescence tracking) efficiently adhered to Nanovial cavities. These cells are able to present a PAP-derived peptide to antigen-specific T cells creating a stable foundation for subsequent T cell loading (Figure 2A, Supplementary Figure 1). Unless otherwise noted, we use “PC3” to refer to this engineered line (PC3‑ A2‑PAP) throughout the manuscript.

To assess whether co-capture of T cells is primarily and specifically driven by TCR–pMHC recognition, we used two previously developed TCRs10, TCR156-WT and TCR156-45, both targeting the same PAP epitope (TLMSAMTNL) presented by HLA-A*02:01 but differing in their dwell time on pMHCs upon binding. These TCRs are structurally highly similar, differing by only two amino acids. In TCR156-45, selected mutations were created to introduce “catch bond” interactions that prolong dissociation, thereby stabilizing cancer–T cell interactions. Prior studies showed that T cells expressing TCR156-45 exhibit strong cytotoxicity against PC3 cells, whereas those expressing TCR156-WT do not.

Brightfield and fluorescence microscopy confirmed stable confinement of a PC3 cell together with a T cell within individual Nanovial cavities (Figure 2B, D), with both cell types fitting within the cavity while maintaining direct cell–cell contact. We next asked whether co-capture reflected the biophysical strength of the cognate pMHC–TCR interaction. T cells engineered with TCR156-45 bound to PC3-loaded Nanovials with higher efficiency than T cells expressing the wild-type receptor (Figure 2E), consistent with preferential stabilization of antigen-specific contacts. Finally, flow cytometry enabled robust enrichment of double-positive Nanovials containing both PC3 and T cells (Figure 2B), supporting scalable recovery of defined dyads for downstream handling and analysis.

Functional tumor–T cell interactions captured within Nanovials

We next asked whether co-loading preserved functional responses associated with tumor cell recognition by T cells. To quantify productive cytotoxic activation, we functionalized Nanovials with granzyme B capture antibodies (Figure 2A) and measured accumulated granzyme B secretion from individual tumor–T cell dyads. Fluorescence microscopy confirmed granzyme B capture on a subset of TCR156-45 dyads with PC3 cells (Figure 2C,D), and across hundreds of dyads granzyme B signal spanned more than an order of magnitude, revealing marked functional heterogeneity (Figure 2F). Approximately 11% of tumor–T cell dyads containing TCR156-45 T cells exceeded the granzyme B secretion threshold, compared with ~5% for TCR156-WT dyads and <1% in no-capture-antibody controls (Figure 2C). As positive benchmarks, pMHC-coated Nanovials and PMA/ionomycin stimulation yielded 24% and 39% of dyads above threshold, respectively (Figure 2C), supporting that granzyme B capture reports productive T cell activation rather than nonspecific retention. Granzyme B accumulation increased with interaction time (Supplementary Figure 2): in antigen-matched TCR156-45 dyads, the fraction above threshold rose from ~0.7% at 3 h to ~15% at 7 h and ~21% at 11 h, whereas TCR156-WT dyads remained near baseline (~1–2%) over the same interval. Together, these results show that Nanovials preserve tumor–T cell dyads and enable functional readouts of activation-dependent effector responses.

Integration with single-cell transcriptomics

To link defined cell-cell interactions with transcriptional state, we introduced sorted Nanovials containing tumor–T cell dyads into a droplet microfluidics scRNA-seq workflow (10x Genomics Chromium GEM-X). Because epithelial tumor cells are substantially larger than T cells and typically contain more total RNA, we empirically optimized Nanovial dimensions to (i) reliably accommodate a tumor–T cell dyad within the cavity and (ii) maintain robust emulsion generation without clogging, while also understanding the influence of the more RNA-rich partner on dyad libraries (Supplementary Figure 3). In parallel, we profiled matched PC3-only and T cell-only singlets (including activated T cell controls) to provide reference states and to build an additive ‘pseudo-dyad’ null model used later to identify interaction-induced programs. We successfully obtained transcriptomes from dyads, as well as from PC3 cells alone, T cells bound via anti-CD45 without tumor exposure, and T cells activated on pMHC-coated Nanovials (Supplementary Figure 4). To focus on early transcriptional responses while maintaining tumor cell viability and intact transcriptomes, dyad interactions were limited to 3 hours prior to enrichment and library preparation.

Interaction-specific transcriptional states and heterogeneity

Across all Nanovial events, a UMAP embedding separated PC3 singlets, unactivated T cell singlets, pMHC-activated T cell controls, and PC3–T cell dyads into distinct clusters (Figure 3A). Consistent with this interpretation, dyads showed intermediate expression of canonical lineage markers (T cell: PTPRC, TRAC; PC3: EPCAM, HOXB13), while a subset of genes was selectively elevated in dyads relative to either parent population (e.g., IRF1, TNF) (Figure 3B).

To interpret these dyad-associated responses, we overlaid gene-module scores corresponding to canonical T cell and PC3 signatures, as well as an interferon response (IRF) program and a T cell cytokine/effector program (Figure 3C). IRF and T cell cytokine/effector programs reflected a subset of gene modules most strongly associated with dyad responses (Supplementary Figure 5). Module-score overlays revealed substantial dyad-to-dyad heterogeneity: some dyads strongly induced both IRF and cytokine/effector programs, others preferentially induced one program, and a minority showed minimal induction (Figure 3C). The cytokine/effector program observed in dyads partially overlapped with the transcriptional program in pMHC-activated T cell controls (Figure 3C), and within dyads, memory-associated genes (e.g., IL7R, CCR7) were reduced while activation/effector genes (e.g., GZMB, TNFRSF9/CD137) were increased, consistent with emergence of an activated T cell phenotype (Figure 3C).

Notably, interaction timing was held constant (1.5 h loading and 3 h co-culture), yet dyads occupied a continuum of composite states. Figure 3D summarizes dyad-associated clusters by ordering events along a continuum of relative cell-type signature strength and response programs. In the leftmost state (PC-3 singlets; cluster 0), transcriptomes are dominated by the PC3 signature (green) with minimal T cell contribution (blue). In dyad clusters, events span graded composite states: cluster 5 (“PC3–T cell exposed”) shows modest T cell signal with limited cytokine induction; cluster 1 (“PC3–T cell contact”) exhibits stronger T cell contribution accompanied by increased cytokine-response scoring (red); and cluster 3 (“PC3 loss–T cell contact”) is characterized by high cytokine-response scores alongside markedly reduced PC3 signature. The rightmost state (T cell singlets; cluster 18) is dominated by the T cell signature with minimal PC3 contribution. Across these states, IRF-response scoring (yellow) is elevated in dyad states and varies independently of the cytokine program; representative images aligned to each column illustrate these corresponding Nanovial contexts (Figure 3D). To test robustness, we performed an independent Cell-Cell-seq run and observed similar dyad-associated subpopulations (Supplementary Figure 6). In parallel, bulk co-culture captured a broad interferon response in PC3 cells but did not resolve the coordinated high-IRF/high-cytokine dyad states or the tumor-low/T cell–dominant endpoint states, consistent with averaging and loss of pair identity in standard workflows (Supplementary Figure 6). Because each dyad transcriptome is a composite mixture of tumor and T cell RNA, identifying interaction-induced changes requires a statistically grounded baseline for expression under ‘no interaction’ that reflects realistic variability in both partners. We therefore developed a computational pseudo-mixing framework that constructs an empirical null distribution from a large library of in silico pseudo-dyads, enabling quantitative testing of interaction-dependent gene programs.

Pseudo-mixing identifies emergent genes induced upon interaction

A central challenge in analyzing transcriptomes of cell dyads is distinguishing true interaction-induced programs from the background signal that arises simply from combining two independent cell transcriptomes. A naïve approach, subtracting an “average” PC3 profile plus an “average” T cell profile, fails because both cell types exhibit substantial cell-to-cell variability (and, for tumor cells in particular, broad transcriptional dispersion), such that many genes can appear “changed” simply due to which two cells happened to be paired.

To address this, we developed a computational pseudo-mixing framework (Supplementary Figure 7) that constructs an empirical null distribution for mixed dyad transcriptomes by generating a large library of pseudo-dyads. Specifically, we randomly sampled transcriptomes from non-interacting PC3 cells and non-interacting T cells and summed them to form many in silico dyads spanning the full range of plausible PC3×T transcriptional combinations (Figure 4A, Supplementary Figure 7). This strategy is conceptually related to approaches used to model technical artifacts such as scRNA-seq doublets, where synthetic mixtures are generated to represent expected mixed profiles11. However, rather than using pseudo-mixtures solely to flag artifacts, we purposefully use them to define the expected distribution of expression for each gene under “no interaction”, conditioned on realistic variability in both partners.

These pseudo-dyads therefore provide a rigorous baseline for what a mixed transcriptome should look like in the absence of communication. By comparing each observed dyad to this library (rather than to a single composite expectation), we identify emergent gene expression as genes whose expression in true dyads deviates significantly from the pseudo-dyad distribution—i.e., changes that cannot be explained by random pairing of independent cells and instead reflect transcriptional programs induced by direct interaction.

To attribute emergent genes to the cell-of-origin within the dyad (tumor versus T cell), we implemented two complementary strategies. First, private gene mapping leveraged transcripts detectably expressed in only one parent population under non-interacting reference conditions. For example, CCL4 and XCL1 were observed in T cells but not PC3 cells, while CXCL1 and CYR61 were restricted to PC3 cells (Figure 4B). Emergent expression of these genes in dyads was therefore assigned to the most likely originating partner. Because interaction could, in principle, induce low-level expression of some transcripts in the opposite cell type, this ‘private gene’ criterion is intentionally conservative and primarily supports assignment for strongly lineage-biased genes.

We next expanded gene assignment using activated-state pseudo-mixing (Supplementary Figure 7), which distinguishes dyad-specific programs from transcriptional changes expected from T cell activation alone. Specifically, we compared emergent genes in true dyads to pseudo-dyads generated from non-interacting PC3 cells paired in silico with T cells activated on cognate PAP pMHC-coated Nanovials (Figure 4B-C). Genes induced in true dyads but absent from these activated-state pseudo-dyads were attributed to PC3-specific interaction responses (e.g., IRF1, NFKBIA), whereas genes amplified in both were attributed to the T cell activation program (e.g. CSF2 and IER3). Together these approaches expanded the assignable gene set to include context-dependent responses such as CSF2, IER3, and NR4A3 in T cells, and stress-associated genes in PC3 cells (Figure 4B-C).

Having established these gene-attribution categories (private T cell, private PC3, and shared/interaction-induced), we next visualized how each class localizes across the joint embedding of singlets, activated controls, and dyads. UMAP feature plots and associated violin plots (Figure 4C) revealed distinct enrichment patterns for genes in each attribution category. UMAP overlays illustrate expression across experimentally measured singlet and dyad transcriptomes, whereas pseudo-dyad states are included in the violin plots only; violins quantify expression across unactivated T cell, activated T cell, PC3 cell, resting pseudo-dyad, activated pseudo-dyad, and interacting dyad conditions. Private T cell–biased genes such as CCL4 and XCL1 were highly expressed in subpopulations of activated T cells and interacting dyads but were not detected in PC3 singlets. Conversely, CXCL1 and CYR61 showed low basal expression in PC3 cells that was strongly increased in a subset of dyads. In contrast, shared or interaction-induced genes such as IER3, IRF1, and NFKBIA were low in resting pseudo-dyads but became selectively concentrated within the dyad-associated cluster, consistent with programs induced by cell–cell engagement. Comparing activated pseudo-dyads to true interacting dyads further distinguished tumor-sided interaction responses from activation-linked genes: IRF1 remained substantially lower in activated pseudo-dyads than in interacting dyads, whereas IER3 exhibited similar distributions in both conditions, suggesting a predominantly T cell–sided response rather than PC3 induction.

We next performed genome-wide differential expression comparing measured dyads to computational pseudo-dyads to define an empirical-null-based set of interaction-associated (‘emergent’) genes (Figure 4D). Whereas Figure 4C highlights representative examples, this analysis systematically identifies transcripts whose expression deviates from the distribution expected under additive mixing of independently profiled PC3 and T cell transcriptomes. Across two independent Cell-Cell-seq experiments (runs 1 and 2), we observed a reproducible emergent gene set, with a larger fraction of significant genes downregulated in dyads relative to pseudo-dyads than upregulated (Figure 4D). Concordance across runs was supported by overlap among the most significant emergent genes and by correlation of per-gene effect sizes (Pearson r=0.63; Figure 4D). Importantly, the number and identity of emergent genes were robust to reasonable changes in pseudo-dyad mixing weights (e.g., equal versus PC3-weighted mixtures; Supplementary Figure 8). Together, these results show that pseudo-mixing provides a statistically grounded baseline for isolating interaction-associated transcriptional signals, which we next assign to the tumor or T cell compartment.

To further resolve the cellular origin of emergent transcriptional programs, we applied our deconvolution framework to assign the larger set of emergent dyad genes to either the T cell or PC3 compartment, yielding compartment-specific volcano plots (Figure 4E,F). Representative downregulated transcripts included BCL11B, GIMAP5-812, and CXCR413, while upregulated transcripts included immediate-early and activation-associated genes such as IER314, CCL415, and EGR116,17 (Figure 4E). Notably, BCL11B has been reported to decrease upon engineered T cell activation and its perturbation has been linked to enhanced anti-tumor activity in adoptive T cell contexts18,19.

For PC3-assigned genes, upregulated transcripts were enriched for inflammatory/stress-adaptive programs and included chemokines implicated in tumor–immune crosstalk (e.g., CXCL1/2/3/8), whereas downregulated genes were enriched for cytokine-related signaling pathways (Figure 4F). Together, these compartment-resolved programs align with known biology: T cell activation and effector signaling alongside tumor inflammatory and stress responses, supporting the robustness of deconvolution-based gene attribution. Because Cell-Cell-seq profiles defined dyads at early time points, these signatures emphasize immediate-early and contact-proximal responses that may be obscured or remodeled in longer-term co-culture or in vivo settings.

Early and transient interaction programs are enriched in defined dyads

To test whether defined, time-synchronized dyads reveal early and transient transcriptional responses that are diluted in conventional assays, we compared emergent gene signatures from Nanovial-confined interactions to those measured in standard well-plate bulk co-culture (effector to target ratio, E:T of 1:1). While some overlap was observed, immediate-early response genes (e.g., IER3, EGR1, EGR3, NR4A3) were among the most strongly enriched in the Cell-Cell-seq condition (Figure 4G(i)). These transcripts are rapidly and transiently induced upon stimulation, consistent with Cell-Cell-seq preserving defined pairing and synchronized interaction timing. In contrast, these signals were reduced or absent in well-plate population-averaged co-culture, where interaction onset is asynchronous and pairwise resolution is lost. Additional transcripts were downregulated upon interaction and were weakly reflected in bulk co-culture (Figure 4G(ii)), suggesting further interaction-associated programs that are obscured by population averaging. Together, these analyses show that Cell-Cell-seq enriches early, contact-proximal transcriptional programs by maintaining defined dyads under controlled timing, while the pseudo-mixing framework provides a statistically grounded baseline to identify emergent genes, assign them to the appropriate cellular source, and compare interaction programs across experimental contexts.

Reciprocal correlations reveal coordinated cell-cell interaction programs

Before examining gene–gene relationships, we asked whether tumor–T cell dyads exhibit coordinated responses at the level of transcriptional programs. We scored each dyad for the same gene modules defined in Figure 3 (T cell signature, PC3 signature, T cell activation/cytokine response and IRF response) and compared module activities across thousands of dyads (Figure 5A–C). At this program level, the strongest positive relationship was observed between the T cell cytokine-response module and the IRF-response module (Pearson r = 0.36; Figure 5D), consistent with coordinated immune activation and tumor-associated interferon signaling in a subset of dyads. In contrast, cell-type–intrinsic modules showed weak negative correlations (e.g., T cell vs PC3 signature; r = −0.11), consistent with compositional tradeoffs in composite dyad transcriptomes. Guided by these program-level relationships, we next quantified pairwise gene–gene correlations across dyads after assigning transcript origin to the T cell or PC3 compartment using private-gene mapping (Figure 5E). This analysis revealed coordinated intercellular gene programs that were not apparent in pseudo-mixed controls (Supplementary Figure 9). Among the most prominent features was a coordinated chemokine axis, marked by positive correlations between T cell–derived chemokines (e.g., CCL3/4, XCL1/2) and tumor-derived chemokines (e.g., CXCL1/3/8), consistent with synchronized paracrine signaling across the immune synapse. Similar intercellular correlation structure was observed across independent runs. In contrast, a distinct block of negatively correlated gene pairs emerged, including a robust inverse relationship between the tumor-associated gene TM4SF1 and multiple T cell activation genes (CD3D, CD3E, CD3G, CD2, CYBA, CD53)20–23, a signature that was absent in pseudo-dyads (Supplementary Figure 9). To further validate this relationship, we stratified dyads by CD3E expression and examined TM4SF1 levels. Violin plots confirmed that dyads with high CD3E expression were associated with significantly lower TM4SF1 expression in the tumor compartment (Figure 5F), consistent with stronger T cell activation programs. This coupling was not observed in pseudo-mixed controls (Figure 5G), indicating that it cannot be explained by additive mixing alone. Notably, these dyads were not confined to a single UMAP cluster (Figure 5H), suggesting that intercellular communication states span a continuum that is not fully captured by discrete clustering.

To globally assess which gene–gene correlations are specifically associated with cell–cell interaction, we compared pairwise correlation coefficients measured in real dyads (y-axis) to those in pseudo-dyads (x-axis) (Figure 5I). This analysis stratifies gene pairs into three regimes: (i) interaction-associated correlations that are absent or weak in pseudo-dyads but strong in real dyads (e.g., CCL4–CXCL3, XCL1–CXCL8), which lie high along the y-axis; (ii) interaction-associated negative correlations that are strengthened in real dyads (e.g., CD3E–TM4SF1) which fall low on the y-axis; and (iii) correlations that are similar in real and pseudo conditions, clustering near the y = x diagonal. Together, this landscape highlights coordinated intercellular gene programs that emerge during defined tumor–T cell engagement and are not explained by additive mixing alone.

Correcting gene expression bias from mixed transcriptomes

A key challenge in analyzing dyad RNA-seq is compositional dilution: the measured transcriptome is a mixture of two cells, and when one partner contributes more RNA (or is more transcriptionally active), it can mask changes in the other. In our data, this manifests as a negative correlation between overall T cell and PC3 signature strength across dyads (Figure 5A), indicating that variation in partner RNA contribution can compress cell-type-specific signals and complicate interpretation of interaction-induced programs. Without correction, dyad-to-dyad differences can reflect how much RNA each partner contributed rather than how each partner responded to interaction, obscuring true interaction-dependent programs. Notably, the pseudo-mixing framework used to define emergent genes compares true dyads to composition-matched pseudo-dyads and is therefore largely robust to this effect; ccRepair is introduced here to improve partner-resolved interpretation and state separation across dyads.

To mitigate the compositional dilution effect, we developed ccRepair, a normalization strategy that restores each partner’s gene expression scale toward singlet-derived baselines. ccRepair (i) identifies stable “anchor” genes for T cells and PC3 from non-interacting singlets, (ii) uses these anchors to estimate the expected contribution of each cell type in an unmixed state, and (iii) rescales cell-type compartment genes within each dyad to match these expectations (Figure 6A). Conceptually, ccRepair does not “create” new expression; it corrects for unequal mixing so that biological differences among dyads are not dominated by fluctuating RNA contribution.

This correction yields practical improvements. First, cell-type markers and functional genes that were attenuated by dilution become sharply resolved, including T cell effector genes (e.g., GZMB, XCL1) and tumor immunoregulatory genes (e.g., DKK1), enabling clearer attribution of programs to the appropriate partner (Figure 6B–C). Second, ccRepair enables cleaner program-level comparisons across conditions: using corrected signatures, we distinguish programs favored by pMHC-only activation from programs preferentially enriched in real dyads, including a PC3-specific dyad response containing adhesion/synapse-associated genes (e.g., ICAM1) that is not recapitulated by antigen stimulation alone (Figure 6D–E, Supplementary Figure 10).

Importantly, ccRepair preserves genuine cross-cell coordination while reducing technical distortion. After correction, coordinated multicellular modules, such as the chemokine bloom signature linking PC3 chemokines (CXCL1/3/8) with T cell inflammatory/effector genes (XCL1/2, CSF2), remain evident and become more robustly detectable (Figure 6F). Together, ccRepair improves interpretability of dyad transcriptomes by separating biology (interaction state) from mixing (RNA contribution), thereby exposing reciprocal, partner-dependent programs that would otherwise be diluted or obscured.

3. Discussion

Understanding how individual cells communicate in multicellular environments remains a central challenge across the life sciences from immunology and oncology to developmental biology. Here, we introduce Cell-Cell-seq, a scalable platform that physically encapsulates and transcriptionally profiles defined interacting cell dyads in Nanovial hydrogel cavities. By preserving dyad identity, controlling the timing and duration of contact, and enabling downstream multiomic readouts, Cell-Cell-seq overcomes key limitations of both dissociative single-cell methods and conventional co-culture systems. This platform provides a unique lens into the transcriptional and functional consequences of direct cell–cell interaction, capturing emergent, contact-dependent programs with single-dyad resolution.

Unlike ligand–receptor inference frameworks (e.g., CellPhoneDB24 and CellChat4), which infer putative communication by pairing the expression of ligands in one cell population with cognate receptors in another, Cell-Cell-seq directly measures the transcriptional consequences of realized cell–cell interactions and links these states to functional outcomes. Ligand–receptor methods are well-suited for constructing tissue-scale interaction networks and prioritizing candidate signaling axes, but because they operate on expression snapshots cannot on their own establish whether two cells were interacting, over what time period, or whether ligand–receptor co-expression produced a downstream response in either partner. Cell-Cell-seq is conceptually related to approaches that directly capture physical cell–cell conjugates, including PIC-seq25 and more recently cytometry-based interaction mapping frameworks such as Interact-omics26. However, these methods typically recover contacts retrospectively (e.g., from dissociated samples or after bulk co-incubation), such that interaction onset and duration are heterogeneous and cannot be experimentally synchronized; moreover, sample handling can disrupt fragile synapses and/or introduce technical aggregates that blur truly native contacts. In contrast, by engineering and preserving physical pairing, Cell-Cell-seq synchronizes the onset of interaction and maintains dyad identity through handling and sorting. We further isolate interaction-driven programs by benchmarking true dyads against pseudo-dyad controls, that define the expected distribution under simple mixing, and then assign emergent transcripts to their cellular origin to enable partner-resolved interpretation of bidirectional responses within each dyad.

Notably, comparison to ligand–receptor predictions highlights that interaction-induced transcription extends well beyond canonical “communication axes.” In a supplemental Venn analysis (Supplementary Figure 11), a subset of emergent Cell-Cell-seq genes overlaps with the union of ligands and receptors prioritized by CellChat from either co-cultured T cells + PC3 cells or pMHC-activated T cells + PC3 cells (on the order of tens of genes), whereas >300 emergent genes are detected exclusively by Cell-Cell-seq and are not represented among curated ligands/receptors in either baseline. This divergence is expected: ligand–receptor inference prioritizes potential signaling based on expression of known ligand/receptor pairs, while Cell-Cell-seq captures the downstream, context-dependent transcriptional programs that arise from contact, synapse formation, and reciprocal feedback.

When benchmarked against standard well-plate co-culture, Cell-Cell-seq captured transient early response genes such as IER3, EGR1, EGR3, and NR4A3 more consistently, transcripts known to be induced rapidly following TCR engagement and to decay within hours as activation programs progress27. Classical descriptions of these immediate-early waves often rely on synchronized stimulation (e.g., plate-bound anti-CD3/anti-TCR antibodies) to impose a defined onset of signaling across a population28. In contrast, target-cell co-cultures initiate asynchronously across many pairs, averaging pre-contact, early-contact, and late-contact states and thereby diluting the short-lived transcripts that best report response initiation. While in vivo timing has been inferred using engineered genetically encoded timer reporters29, Cell-Cell-seq synchronizes interaction onset directly by pairing cells and constraining proximity, making these fleeting programs observable under target-recognition conditions and enabling a more mechanistic view of immune response initiation.

At the biological level, Cell-Cell-seq revealed a rich repertoire of emergent interaction programs. Notably, we uncovered a coordinated chemokine bloom, a dyad-level signature in which T cell–derived chemokines (XCL1, CCL3, CCL4) were correlated with tumor-derived chemokines (CXCL1, CXCL3, CXCL8), consistent with bidirectional paracrine signaling. In parallel, we identified a negative correlation module between tumor-expressed TM4SF1, associated with immune evasion/immunosuppression in several tumor settings, and multiple T cell activation genes including CD3D, CD3E, and CD2. These compartment-resolved, dyad-linked programs and their putative communication modes are summarized schematically in Supplementary Figure 12. These interactions were absent in pseudo-dyad controls and emerged only in real dyads, indicating that they represent true, dyad-linked cross-cell coupling rather than confounding cell state effects. Given the druggability of factors such as DKK1 and TM4SF1, these dyad-linked signatures suggest opportunities to target immune suppression at the level of individual communication events. Prior work has implicated DKK1 in tumor immune evasion largely through remodeling of suppressive myeloid compartments, with additional evidence for direct inhibition of CD8 T cell programs in some settings30. For TM4SF1, recent studies in hepatocellular carcinoma connect tumor TM4SF1 to immune evasion and report impaired CD8 T cell function consistent with a more direct tumor–T cell axis, but dyad-resolved transcriptional coupling has remained difficult to demonstrate with conventional assays31.

Comparison to in vivo T cell trajectories further demonstrates the utility of Cell-Cell-seq. In an adoptive transfer model using the same TCRs profiled here, single-cell RNA-seq of tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes recovered a pseudo-temporal progression from quiescence to early effector, late effector, and terminal ISG-high states. Cell-Cell-seq recapitulates and compresses features of these trajectories into a rapid, experimentally controlled window, capturing within hours chemokine-rich (CCL3, CCL4) and interferon-stimulated (IRF1, NFKBIA) modules that in vivo emerge over days of tumor residence. These results suggest that early dyad-level transcriptional programs measured by Cell-Cell-seq can provide proximal correlates of longer-term in vivo states (e.g., persistence versus dysfunction), motivating future studies that explicitly link early dyadic signatures to downstream fate.

Technically, Cell-Cell-seq builds on and significantly expands prior Nanovial-based approaches, which have been used to profile secretory phenotypes8,32, cytokine output33, and EV production34. The platform uniquely integrates high-throughput dyad formation, functional readouts, and dyad single-cell transcriptomics, offering an experimentally tractable and generalizable system for studying immune-tumor, immune-immune, or other intercellular interactions. While other platforms such as DEP-based pairing or microfluidic chips offer control over cell pairing, they often lack scalability, single-cell transcriptomic resolution, modularity for suspension and adherent cells, or compatibility with droplet-based sequencing35–39. Cell-Cell-seq directly fills this gap.

Finally, we addressed a core technical limitation of dyad sequencing, the compositional dilution in mixed transcriptomes, using ccRepair. By rebalancing dyad contributions based on anchor genes learned from singlets, ccRepair mitigates systematic skew in apparent cell-type dominance, restores suppressed effector transcripts, and improves quantitative comparisons of activation intensity across dyads. Together, these experimental and computational advances position Cell-Cell-seq as a complementary approach to inference methods: it retains their hypothesis-generating value while providing a direct, paired readout of interaction-induced programs, including large modules that are invisible to ligand–receptor catalogs alone.

Beyond T cell–tumor interactions, the platform is readily extendable to other systems including APC–T cell, stromal–immune, B–T cell, or stem–niche interactions by altering capture ligands on the Nanovials40. Integration with other modalities such as CITE-seq41, ATAC-seq42, or CUT&Tag43 will further enrich the platform’s multiomic depth. For example, combining Cell-Cell-seq with surface proteomics would allow tracking of immune checkpoint receptor engagement and downstream transcriptional response within a dyad. Similarly, linking chromatin accessibility profiles to interaction timing could reveal early regulatory events driving fate decisions such as exhaustion or memory formation.

From a translational perspective, Cell-Cell-seq offers a framework for functional screening of cell-cell interaction therapies, such as TCRs, chimeric antigen receptors (CARs), bispecific antibodies, or checkpoint inhibitor antibodies. By linking transcriptional responses and cytokine output to specific interactions, it enables both discovery and prioritization of potent therapeutic candidates in a native cell–cell engagement context with all of the associated heterogeneity of each of the interacting partners. Furthermore, by correlating early dyadic signatures with long-term outcomes (e.g., persistence, killing, or exhaustion), Cell-Cell-seq could uncover predictive biomarkers of functional potency, guiding the rational design of cell-cell interaction therapies.

In summary, Cell-Cell-seq introduces a foundational technology for dissecting cell–cell communication with temporal, functional, and transcriptional resolution. It bridges the gap between single-cell sequencing and spatially contextualized interaction analysis, providing a scalable and versatile approach for understanding how cells recognize, respond to, and influence each other. As the field advances toward personalized cell-cell interaction therapies, Cell-Cell-seq offers a unique and timely tool to chart the molecular choreography of immune recognition, therapeutic response, and intercellular signaling, one dyad at a time.

4. Methods

4.1. Nanovial Preparation and Cell Loading Optimization

Nanovials were fabricated utilizing previously described protocols6. Aqueous gelatin solution was flowed into a three-inlet flow-focusing microfluidics droplet generator where it was mixed with a second aqueous solution consisting of 4-Arm-PEG 5KDa and Lithium phenyl-2,4,6-trimethylbenzoylphosphinate) photoinitiator (LAP, Sigma Aldrich). The mixture was then introduced to an oil-phase composed of Pico-surf (Sphere Fluidics) surfactant in Novec 7500 (3M) at the junction where the droplets were generated. A phase equilibrium resulted in separation of the two aqueous phases within the droplet. The phase-separated droplets were UV-crosslinked downstream the generator and immediately before the collection of the now-crosslinked particles in a tube. Next, the particles were washed to remove unpolymerized phases. The cavity of the resulting Nanovials was then specifically decorated with NHS-conjugated biotin (ApexBio) generating streptavidin binding sites for modular surface functionalization. Finally, the Nanovials were sterilized and stored in an aqueous buffer (washing buffer) consisting of Phosphate-buffered Saline solution (PBS, Gibco), 1% Antibiotic-Antimycotic (Gibco), 0.05% Pluronic F-127 (Sigma Aldrich), 0.05% Bovine Serum Albumin (BSA, Sigma Aldrich) at 4°C until usage. The inner cavity size for the 48µm ± 2µm Nanovials fabricated were 35µm ± 1µm. Nanovials were either utilized unmodified as described above or were functionalized in a two-step process as previously described6 to study the secretion profile of the interacting cells. Utilizing the pre-existing biotinylated sites, streptavidin solution was first added to the Nanovials, incubated at room temperature on a slow rotator for 30min and then washed three times with excess washing buffer. In the final step, Nanovials were incubated with biotinylated anti-human granzyme B antibody (R&D systems) or PAP pMHC monomer at room temperature on a slow rotator for 30min and then washed three times with excess washing buffer.

In order to maximize the efficiency of loading single PC3-T cell dyads on Nanovials, a few optimization experiments were performed. These experiments aimed to determine the optimum cell-to-Nanovial ratio for loading each cell sequentially. Nanovials were initially mixed and loaded with PC3 cells at various cell-to-Nanovial ratios (0.5, 1.5, 2, 3, and 4.5) and the resulting samples were analyzed with flow cytometry. The percentage of PC3-loaded Nanovials increased with increasing cell-to-Nanovial ratios, reaching a peak at a ratio of 3 and then plateauing at higher cell-to-Nanovial ratio (Supplementary Figure S1). Next, the efficiency of capturing T cells onto Nanovials pre-seeded with PC3 cells were accessed. A fixed number (0.2 million) of PC3-loaded Nanovials were mixed with varying numbers of T cells to achieve different T cell-to-Nanovial ratios (0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, and 2.5) and the efficiency of each sample was determined utilizing flow cytometry analysis. The percentage of Nanovials loaded with both cell types increased with higher T cell-to-Nanovial ratios up to 1.5 and exhibited no significant improvement at higher ratios of 2, and 2.5 (Supplementary Figure S1). The optimized sequential loading protocol resulted in efficient co-loading of PC3 and T cells onto Nanovials, maintaining stable cell attachment throughout subsequent handling and FACS processing steps. This stability is crucial to preserve accurate analysis and reliable downstream functional assessment of the interacting cell dyads. Therefore, the PC3 cell-to-Nanovial ratio of 3 and the T cell-to-PC3-loaded Nanovials ratio of 1.5 was selected for all future experiments.

Using the optimal conditions described above, cells were loaded into Nanovials sequentially by first mixing the GFP+/RFP+ expressing prostate cancer cells expressing PAP antigen (PC3-A2-PAP) with 48µm Nanovials at a 3:1 cell to Nanovial ratio in a 24-well plate pre-filled with 1.5ml target cell medium (comprised of 10% FBS (Thermo Fisher Scientific) and 5% GlutaMAX supplement (Thermo Fisher Scientific) in Ham’s F-12K base medium (Thermo Fisher Scientific). Next, the well plate was incubated for 2hrs in an incubator at 37°C and 5% CO₂. At 30min intervals, the plate was removed from the incubator and the cell-Nanovial mixture was re-suspended with a 1ml pipette 10 times. At the 2hr time point, the cell-Nanovial mixture was passed through a 20µm reversible strainer (CellTrics) and washed with an excess amount of washing buffer to remove unbound tumor cells. This resulted in loading the tumor cells into the Nanovial cavities leveraging the pre-existing gelatin layer (which served as the binding motif for the tumor cells). The strained Nanovial suspension was then recovered in another 24-well plate with 1.5ml target cell medium. Next, pre-stained (CellTracker deep red) T cells (expressing cognate TCR) were added to the well in a 1.5:1 cell to Nanovial ratio. The well plate was incubated for 1.5 hours in an incubator at 37°C and 5% CO₂ with a pipetting re-mix step at the intermediate time point. At the end of the incubation period, the cell-Nanovial mixture was passed through a 20µm reversible strainer (CellTrics) and washed with an excess amount of washing buffer to remove unbound T cells. This resulted in loading the cognate TCR T cells into the Nanovial cavity via their capture by the pre-seeded tumor cells within the Nanovial cavity. Finally, Nanovials pre-seeded with PC3 cells were loaded with non-cognate TCR T cells to test the specificity of the PC3-TCR T binding.

4.2 Flow Cytometry Gating of Nanovials with Cell Dyads.

PC3-T dyad-loaded Nanovial samples were analyzed to isolate the population of interest and sorted with the Sony SH800S flow sorter. In order to isolate the cell dyad-loaded Nanovials, first the SSC-A vs FSC-H plot was utilized to isolate the Nanovial population. Next, the FITC-A vs FITC-W signals plot was employed to isolate the GFP+ expressing PC3 cell-loaded Nanovials from the empty Nanovial population, and to exclude the undesired population of i) free cells (appearing on the left side of PC3-loaded Nanovials gate) and ii) Nanovial aggregates (appearing on the right side of the PC3-loaded Nanovials gate). Finally, the cell dyad-loaded Nanovial population was isolated via drawing a gate on the APC-A vs APC-W plot. 4.6 Computational Analysis

Data preprocessing and UMAP visualization

We analyzed transcriptomic profiles from four Nanovial sample types: PC3 cells alone, unactivated T cells alone, PC3–T cell interacting dyads, and activated T cells alone. Low-quality cells and lowly expressed genes were removed, so that the retained cells contained at least 500 detected unique molecular identifier (UMI) counts and less than ten percent mitochondrial gene expression, and the retained genes were detected in at least ten cells. PC3–T cell interacting dyads were retained if they contained at least 10% proportional expression of private genes from both cells, indicating a dyad contains one T cell and one PC3 cell.

UMAP visualization was performed using Seurat (version 4.4.3). After the above filtering steps, the data were normalized and scaled, two thousand highly variable genes were selected, and principal component analysis was conducted on these highly variable genes. The top twenty principal components were used to compute the UMAP embedding, which visualized the four cellular populations (differences in sequencing depth were accounted for by the prior normalization step).

Gene–gene correlation networks and signature gene selection

Gene–gene correlation networks were constructed with GeneChorus (v0.12.5) using normalized expression values across cells and interacting dyads. For each gene pair, Pearson correlation coefficients were computed across clustered cells in scaled-squared space over progressive rounds, and edges were retained if the squared correlation coefficient exceeded 0.1. GeneChorus modules were summarized as gene sets and annotated by Gene Ontology enrichment of the genes within each module; modules are referred to in the text by their top enriched gene ontology terms. To prioritize modules for visualization, we computed the mean expression of each module’s genes within each Seurat-defined cluster and selected modules showing the strongest cluster-specific enrichment (highest mean module expression relative to other clusters). For downstream visualization and scoring, we defined “signature genes” as the top 25 genes by absolute log2 fold-change from differential expression of PC3 versus resting T cells (Seurat Wilcoxon test), or alternatively the top 25 highest-degree genes within selected correlation modules (as specified in the relevant figure legends).

Pseudo-dyad construction and assignment of emergent genes to PC3 or T cells

To distinguish interaction-induced (“emergent”) transcriptional changes from compositional effects of mixing two transcriptomes, we generated pseudo-dyads by in silico combining single-cell profiles and compared them to measured PC3–T cell interacting dyads (Supplementary Figure 7).

Pseudo-dyad generation. We constructed two pseudo-dyad sets: resting pseudo-dyads (PC3 + unactivated T cells) and activated pseudo-dyads (PC3 + activated T cells). For each set, we first converted raw UMI counts to relative expression by dividing each gene count by the total UMI count per cell. We then repeatedly sampled equal numbers of PC3 and T cells (n = 895 each; 895 corresponds to the smallest group size among PC3 singlets, unactivated T singlets, activated T singlets, and interacting dyads) and formed pseudo-dyads by averaging paired profiles:

$$x_{\text{pseudo-dyad}} = 0.5\,x_{\mathrm{PC3}} + 0.5\,x_{\mathrm{T}}$$

Each resampling produced a dataset of 895 pseudo-dyads; we generated 50 independent pseudo-dyad datasets per condition (resting and activated). In parallel, we generated 50 matched interacting-dyad datasets by randomly subsampling 895 real dyads per dataset to maintain comparable sample sizes across comparisons.

Differential expression to identify emergent genes. For each of the 50 resting pseudo-dyad datasets, we concatenated the pseudo-dyads with one size-matched interacting-dyad dataset and applied Seurat normalization (NormalizeData, scale.factor = 10,000) followed by log transformation (log1p). We then performed differential expression (DE) testing between interacting dyads vs resting pseudo-dyads using Seurat’s Wilcoxon rank-sum test (FindMarkers, test.use = “wilcox”) with Benjamini–Hochberg correction. Genes with adjusted p < 0.05 were recorded for each comparison. Resting emergent genes were defined as those significant in all 50 resting comparisons, yielding a conservative set of interaction-associated genes robust to subsampling.

Filtering to attribute emergent genes to PC3 versus T cells. Genes identified from the resting comparison can reflect either (i) T cell activation programs or (ii) PC3 responses to T cell engagement. To help separate these sources, we performed an additional DE comparison between interacting dyads vs activated pseudo-dyads (both containing activated T cells). Because T cells in activated pseudo-dyads were stimulated by pMHC and may not match the activation state of T cells in interacting dyads, this comparison can introduce T-cell-state artifacts. We therefore restricted the resulting DE genes to those plausibly attributable to PC3 by retaining only genes with measurable baseline expression in PC3 cells. Specifically, we removed genes with minimal PC3 expression by testing whether normalized expression in PC3 singlets exceeded 0.05 (one-sided t-test with Benjamini–Hochberg correction) and retaining only genes passing this PC3-expression filter (adjusted p < 0.05).

Finally, we assigned resting emergent genes to cell type as follows: genes present in both (i) the conservative resting emergent set and (ii) the PC3-filtered DE list from interacting vs activated pseudo-dyads were classified as PC3-associated emergent genes. The remaining resting emergent genes were classified as T-cell-associated emergent genes.

ccRepair normalization for visualization and correlation analyses

Mixed dyad transcriptomes contain RNA from both cells, which can dilute compartment-specific expression when the two cells contribute unequal transcript abundance. To reduce this compositional bias for visualization and gene–gene correlation analyses, we applied ccRepair, a scaling procedure that rebalances cell-type–specific (“compartment”) genes within each dyad using anchor genes derived from singlet baselines. To account for the dilution effect of mixed transcriptomes in dyads, approximately 100 consistent private compartment genes were selected for pMHC-NV T cells and PC3s (log 2 FC DE > 1.5, normalized expression >1 in target compartment and <0.2 in reciprocal compartment) to serve as anchor genes. These genes were used to calculate a target mean anchor expression in unmixed pMHC-NV cells and PC3 cells, and a scaling factor for correction in interacting dyads. In interacting dyads, the ratio between the average single target anchor gene expression and the average dyad anchor gene value was used as a per-cell multiplier to adjust the diluted gene expression. Only compartment genes were adjusted, and only by their target ratio (PC3 genes with PC3 ratio, T cell genes with pMHC-NV T cell ratio). ccRepair does not affect genes not assigned to a compartment. Adjusted gene expression for interacting dyad cells only were merged back into a normalized count matrix with singlets and unaffected genes.
