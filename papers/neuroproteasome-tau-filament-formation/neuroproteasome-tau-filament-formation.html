阿茲海默症的腦中，有一個標誌性的變化：一種原本乖乖溶在神經細胞裡的蛋白質，叫做 tau，會突然變質。兩條 tau 會纏成一根扭曲的細絲，科學家稱為成對螺旋細絲（paired helical filament，PHF），也就是俗稱的神經纏結。奇怪的是，在最常見的阿茲海默症裡，這些 tau 既沒有基因突變、也沒有被製造得過量，卻自己走上了結塊這條路。到底是細胞裡的哪一個開關按下了這個轉變，過去一直沒有答案。

這篇論文指認出一個關鍵角色，是一台裝在神經元外膜上的蛋白質絞碎機。細胞本來就有一種叫蛋白酶體（proteasome）的機器，專門把用壞、該淘汰的蛋白質絞碎回收。而神經元很特別，會在自己的細胞膜上、朝著細胞外，多掛一台專屬版本，作者把它稱為神經蛋白酶體（neuroproteasome）。麻煩的是，這台表面的絞碎機和細胞內部的那些，用的是同一批零件。因此沒辦法用基因手段單獨關掉表面這一台。作者於是設計了一款絞碎機抑制劑，在藥物上掛了一條親水的長鏈（PEG）。這條長鏈讓藥物太親水而進不了細胞，只能卡住暴露在表面的神經蛋白酶體，碰不到細胞內部的（這個進不了細胞的抑制劑叫 iBEp）。他們再用多種滲透測試反覆確認：它真的進不去，卻確實把表面那台絞碎機關掉了。

結果很直接。只要關掉這台表面絞碎機，神經元裡原本可溶的 tau，就迅速變成不溶的結塊。無論是在培養皿裡的神經元，還是直接打進活體小鼠腦部的海馬迴，都是如此。而且這些結塊不是隨便的一團。在電子顯微鏡下，可以看到和人類阿茲海默症一模一樣的扭曲雙螺旋細絲。用會辨認 tau 的抗體接上金粒子去標定，也證實那真的是 tau。連細絲的寬度、螺旋交叉的間距，都和人腦纏結對得上。更關鍵的是，就算用的是帶正常人類 tau、完全沒有突變的小鼠，一樣會結塊。這說明正常的 tau 本身，就能被推上這條病變之路。

這些結塊還帶著阿茲海默症 tau 的招牌。健康的 tau 若在特定位置被貼上磷酸標記，就成了病態 tau 的指紋。作者發現，關掉神經蛋白酶體之後，tau 正好在人類阿茲海默症常見的那幾個位點被貼上這種標記。一種專門讓纏結發亮的染劑（Thioflavin S），也把這些結塊照得清清楚楚，形狀就像阿茲海默症腦切片裡火焰般的纏結。這裡還順帶解開了一個舊謎團。過去有人用能同時關掉表面和內部所有絞碎機的老藥，卻反而看不到 tau 結塊，一直令人費解。作者發現，一旦內部的絞碎機也被關掉，細胞會啟動另一套備援清道夫（自噬與溶酶體）把結塊清走。只有單獨關掉表面這一台、不驚動備援系統時，tau 的結塊才會留下來被看見。這也正是為什麼，非得特地做出只作用在細胞表面的工具不可。

接著，作者把這台絞碎機，和阿茲海默症最大的基因風險因子連了起來。ApoE 是一種由腦中膠細胞分泌、負責搬運脂質的蛋白質。人類有 E2、E3、E4 三種版本，其中 E4 是散發型阿茲海默症最強的遺傳風險，E2 則被認為有保護作用。作者發現，神經蛋白酶體上就抓得到 ApoE，連同幫 ApoE 接駁上細胞的一個受體蛋白（Lrp1）。更重要的是，不同版本的 ApoE 會決定有多少絞碎機留在細胞表面：E2 留下最多，E3 中等，E4 最少。

不只基因，年齡也會壓低這台絞碎機的數量。在小鼠身上，大約從一歲（十二個月）起，細胞表面的神經蛋白酶體就逐漸變少。把基因和機器功能擺在一起看，就浮現出一個儲備量的概念。帶 E4 的神經元，只要損失大約兩成的絞碎機活性，tau 就開始結塊。帶 E3 的神經元，要損失約六成才會跨過這道門檻。帶 E2 的神經元，則要到約八成五才守不住。換句話說，E4 神經元幾乎沒有緩衝餘裕，一點打擊或老化就跨過門檻。E2 神經元則有厚厚的儲備。作者也在過世病人的腦組織裡，看到互相呼應的結果：帶 E4/4 的人，腦中表面絞碎機明顯偏少，而 tau 病變嚴重的腦區又更少。

綜合起來，這台裝在神經元表面的絞碎機，像是一個把基因風險和老化風險收攏在一起的樞紐。只要它的功能掉到某個門檻以下，正常的 tau 就會翻轉成阿茲海默症式的纏結。不過作者也留了餘地。把抑制劑急性打進腦子，並不等於重現病人歷經數年、遍佈全腦的慢性病變，這比較像一個乾淨的概念驗證。而且有一個關鍵環節仍未解開：為什麼關掉細胞表面的絞碎機，會讓細胞內部的 tau 結塊？中間那條分子鏈到底是什麼，以及神經元為何不像面對一般壓力那樣啟動保護反應，都還等著後續研究回答。

阿茲海默症（AD）裡，本來溶在神經細胞中的 tau 蛋白會失控黏成扭曲的纖維纏結，但沒人知道細胞裡是什麼按下這個開關。作者發現神經細胞外壁上有一台專門絞碎蛋白的機器（神經蛋白酶體），只要用一種進不了細胞、只能堵住這台表面機器的抑制劑塞住它，神經元裡的 tau 就迅速凝結成和 AD 病人腦中一模一樣的纏結纖維；而 AD 最大的基因風險因子 ApoE 的三個版本（E2、E3、E4）以及老化，都會決定表面有多少台這種機器在崗位上，藉此解釋了為什麼帶 E4 的人和年長者更容易發病。

阿茲海默症（AD）有一個困擾學界多年的謎團。神經細胞裡有一種叫 tau 的蛋白，平常好端端地溶在細胞裡、幫忙撐住細胞結構；可是在阿茲海默症病人的腦中，這些 tau 會一根根黏成扭曲的長纖維、堆積成纏結，而且堆得越多、病情越重。這種扭成雙股螺旋的纏結纖維有個正式名字叫「成對螺旋纖維」（paired helical filaments, PHF），是判定阿茲海默症型 tau 病變的金標準。奇怪的是，這些病人的 tau 基因並沒有突變，卻自己走上了凝結這條路，一直沒人知道是細胞裡的什麼按下了這個開關。

這篇研究給出的答案，藏在神經細胞的外壁上。原來細胞裡本來就有一台把不要的蛋白絞碎回收的機器，叫做蛋白酶體（proteasome）；神經元還額外裝了一台特別版，嵌在細胞外壁、開口朝向細胞外，而且只有神經細胞才有，作者稱它為神經蛋白酶體（neuroproteasome）。這台表面絞碎機的功能過去幾乎是一片空白，而作者發現，它正是決定 tau 會不會凝結的關鍵。

要驗證這台表面絞碎機的功能，最直接的做法是把它關掉看看，但這裡有個麻煩：表面這台和細胞內部那台是用一模一樣的零件組成的，用基因手段一動就兩台一起壞，分不清是哪一台的功勞。作者於是想了個巧妙的辦法：他們拿一個本來就能卡死絞碎機的分子（epoxomicin），在它身上綁一條又長又親水的尾巴，讓整個分子大到再也擠不進細胞。這麼一來，它就只能碰到露在細胞外壁的那台表面絞碎機，碰不到躲在裡面的那台。這把「只堵外面」的鑰匙叫 iBEp。作者用了好幾種方法確認 iBEp 幾乎完全進不了細胞（滲透性比原版低兩千五百倍以上），也確認它只讓表面那台失效、裡面那台照常運作 — 因為細胞內部的絞碎機一旦被堵通常會拉警報（囤積垃圾蛋白、啟動備援清理），而 iBEp 完全不會引發這些反應。

堵住表面這台絞碎機後，結果很清楚。作者用一種強力去垢劑清洗神經元：正常可溶的 tau 會被洗掉，只有已經黏死凝結的 tau 才洗不掉、沉澱下來。堵住表面絞碎機後，這種「洗不掉的 tau」大量暴增，而把 tau 基因剃除的神經元完全不出現這種訊號，證明看到的確實是 tau。這裡還藏著一個看似矛盾、卻很關鍵的觀察：能同時關掉表面與內部兩台絞碎機的原版藥（它進得了細胞），反而不會長出凝結的 tau，照理說「關得更多」應該更糟才對。原因是，一旦內部那台也被關掉，細胞會緊急派出另一組備援清道夫（自噬-溶酶體系統）補位，順手把剛冒頭的 tau 凝結物清乾淨。作者把這組備援清道夫也一起用藥堵住，凝結的 tau 就又冒出來了。換句話說，只有在「單獨」關掉表面絞碎機、不驚動內部絞碎機、備援也按兵不動時，tau 凝結的真面目才會現形。

這些洗不掉的 tau，真的就是阿茲海默症那種纏結纖維嗎？作者把 iBEp 直接注射進活體小鼠掌管記憶的腦區（海馬迴），再用電子顯微鏡去看沉澱物，果然看到一根根扭成螺旋的纖維，長相與阿茲海默症病人腦中的 PHF 一致；用會辨認 tau 的金顆粒抗體去標，也確認這些纖維就是 tau。tau 基因被剃除的小鼠則怎麼找都找不到纖維。而且不只長相像 — 這些 tau 還被貼上與病人同款的磷酸標記，也能被專門染纏結的染劑（Thioflavin-S）染成火焰狀團塊。這些都是阿茲海默症型 tau 病變的招牌特徵。

那麼，這台表面絞碎機和阿茲海默症最大的基因風險因子有沒有關係？作者去釣出表面絞碎機平常抓著哪些夥伴，結果釣到了 ApoE — 一種在腦中運送脂質的蛋白，也正是阿茲海默症最大的基因風險因子 — 以及它的接收器 Lrp1。ApoE 有三個版本：E2 具保護性、E3 中性、E4 則大幅提高風險。進一步實驗發現，這三個版本會決定神經元表面有多少台絞碎機在崗位上：帶 E2 的表面絞碎機最多，E3 居中，E4 最少。也就是說，高風險的 E4 會讓神經元表面能清理垃圾的絞碎機變少。這個規律在人的死後腦組織裡同樣成立：帶兩個 E4 的人，表面絞碎機比帶 E3 的人明顯更少，而且在 tau 纏結越嚴重的腦區掉得越多。此外，隨著年齡增長，表面絞碎機也會自然減少 — 恰好呼應「老化是阿茲海默症最大的風險因子」這件事。

最後一個問題最關鍵：少了幾台絞碎機，就足以讓 tau 凝結嗎？作者用藥一點一點地關掉表面絞碎機，看每種 ApoE 版本要關到多少才會冒出凝結的 tau。三者的差距大得驚人：E4 神經元只要損失大約兩成的處理量就崩潰凝結，E3 要損失六成，E2 得一路撐到損失八成半才崩。完全沒下藥時，三種版本都風平浪靜。這代表每種版本的「容錯餘裕」天差地遠：E4 神經元幾乎沒有緩衝，稍有風吹草動就跨過凝結的門檻；E2 則餘裕很大、格外耐操。所以帶 E4 本身還不足以致病，但只要再加一記打擊（例如老化又讓絞碎機少了幾台），就很容易被推過門檻 — 這正好把「基因風險」與「老化風險」用同一台絞碎機串在了一起。要提醒的是，這是急性、局部的人為抑制，無法完整複製阿茲海默症歷時數十年、遍佈全腦的慢性病程；它證明的是「表面絞碎機失能足以點燃 tau 纏結」這個因果環節。也正因為抓到了這個環節，設法保住或增強這台表面絞碎機，就成了對抗阿茲海默症一個值得探索的新方向。

阿茲海默症裡，本來溶解在神經細胞內的 tau 蛋白會轉變成結塊的扭轉纖維（纏結），但沒人知道細胞裡是哪個環節先出錯把它推向結塊。作者發現一台鑲在神經細胞外層膜上、開口朝外的蛋白質絞碎機（神經蛋白酶體）是關鍵：只要專門把它卡住，tau 就會結成像真正阿茲海默症病人腦中的纏結。這台絞碎機又受 ApoE 基因版本（E2>E3>E4）與老化調控，因此成為把基因風險和老化風險接到 tau 病變的樞紐。

阿茲海默症的大腦裡，有一種本來乖乖溶在神經細胞裡、負責穩定細胞內部骨架的蛋白質，叫做 tau。在生病的過程中，這些原本分散的 tau 會彼此纏繞、疊成一束一束扭轉的纖維，也就是俗稱的神經纖維纏結（paired helical filament, PHF）。這種從「溶解」變成「結塊」的轉變，是阿茲海默症最核心的病理特徵之一。奇怪的是，在絕大多數病人身上，這些 tau 並沒有任何基因突變，也沒有被過量製造，卻還是走上了結塊這條路。到底是細胞裡的哪一個環節先出了差錯、把正常的 tau 推向結塊，長久以來一直是個謎。

這篇研究把矛頭指向細胞裡一台專門處理蛋白質的機器——蛋白酶體（proteasome），可以把它想成細胞的「蛋白質絞碎機」。每個細胞內部都漂浮著大量這種絞碎機，負責把老舊或多餘的蛋白質切碎回收。作者關注的是一台很特別的版本：它不是漂在細胞裡，而是鑲嵌在神經細胞的外層細胞膜上，把工作的開口朝向細胞外，一邊從細胞內拉進蛋白質切碎、一邊把碎片吐到細胞外。因為只有神經細胞才有這台「長在牆上的絞碎機」，作者稱它為神經蛋白酶體（neuroproteasome）。他們發現，只要專門把這一台卡住，神經細胞內的 tau 就會開始結塊。

難題在於，這台表面絞碎機和細胞內那成千上萬台用的是同一批零件，沒辦法用基因手段單獨關掉其中一台。作者的巧思是從化學下手。他們拿一種已知能精準毒殺絞碎機的小分子（epoxomicin），在它身上接一條又大又親水的長尾巴。這條尾巴讓整個分子重到、也「怕水」到無法穿過細胞膜，於是它只碰得到暴露在細胞表面的那台絞碎機，碰不到躲在細胞裡的其他絞碎機。這個工具叫 iBEp。作者用多種方法反覆確認它真的進不了細胞，也真的只讓細胞膜上的絞碎機失去活性，細胞內那些完全不受影響。

用這把「只鎖表面」的工具處理神經細胞後，作者先做了一個不預設答案的普查：把所有蛋白質分成「還溶得開」和「已經結塊」兩堆來比對。結果在成千上萬種蛋白質裡，只有極少數是「總量沒變、卻明顯跑進結塊堆」的，而 tau——阿茲海默症的招牌——正是其中最突出的一個。順著這條線索，他們證實無論在培養皿的神經細胞、還是活體小鼠的海馬迴（大腦最早出現阿茲海默症病變的區域之一）注射 iBEp，都會冒出大量結塊的 tau。更關鍵的是，這些結塊在電子顯微鏡下呈現扭轉的纖維形狀，和真正阿茲海默症病人腦中的 PHF 一模一樣；用金顆粒標記的抗體確認這些纖維的確是 tau；而且它們帶著阿茲海默症特有的幾個磷酸化標記（臨床上正是靠這些位點辨認病變的 tau），也能被專門染結塊的染劑染成火焰狀的纏結。換句話說，卡住這台絞碎機做出來的，不是隨便的蛋白垃圾，而是像病人腦中那種貨真價實的 tau 纏結。

這裡有個一直讓人困惑的地方：能同時關掉細胞內外所有絞碎機的原版老藥，反而不會讓 tau 結塊，為什麼只關表面這一台才會？作者發現，關鍵在細胞的備援機制。當細胞內部的絞碎機被關掉時，細胞會啟動另一套清垃圾的系統（自噬—溶酶體）來把結塊的 tau 清掉，於是看不到纏結；但單獨關掉表面那台，並不會觸發這套備援，結塊就留了下來。作者進一步證實：如果一邊用老藥關掉內部絞碎機、一邊再把溶酶體也堵住，被藏起來的 tau 纏結又會重新出現。這說明過去的實驗之所以沒看到這個現象，是因為備援系統把證據掃掉了。

接著作者追問：這台絞碎機是被誰調控的？他們把神經蛋白酶體釣出來看它旁邊黏著誰，結果釣到了 ApoE 和它在神經細胞上的接口 Lrp1。ApoE 是大腦裡由支持細胞（膠細胞）分泌、負責搬運脂質的一種蛋白質，人類有 E2、E3、E4 三種版本，其中帶 E4 的人罹患阿茲海默症的風險最高，E3 中性，E2 則有保護作用——這正是阿茲海默症最大的單一基因風險因子。作者發現，這三種版本會直接影響有多少絞碎機能停留在細胞表面：E2 讓表面的絞碎機變多，E4 則讓它大約掉到三分之一，E3 居中。而且要有這個效果，ApoE 必須帶著脂質；把脂質拿掉就沒有作用。

最後，作者把這條線和真正的患病風險接起來。他們在同時帶有人類 tau 和不同 ApoE 版本的神經細胞上，一點一點增加抑制程度，看要削掉多少絞碎機活性、tau 才開始結塊。結果差異驚人：E4 神經細胞只要損失約兩成活性就會結塊，E3 要到約六成，E2 則要到約八成五才會。也就是說，E4 神經細胞幾乎沒有緩衝餘裕，E2 則保留了很大的安全空間。

單是 E4 把絞碎機壓低，還不足以直接引爆結塊，但它讓神經細胞的「儲備」變薄，只要再來一記「第二擊」——例如老化——就會被推過門檻；而作者也確實觀察到，小鼠的神經蛋白酶體會隨年齡（大約一歲起）逐步減少。在人腦組織裡，這個推論同樣成立：帶 E4/4 的阿茲海默症患者，腦中神經蛋白酶體的量明顯低於 E3/3，而且在 tau 病變越嚴重的區域掉得越多。綜合起來，這台長在神經細胞牆上的絞碎機，就是把「基因」和「老化」這兩大風險因子接到 tau 病變上的關鍵樞紐，也因此成為未來可能的治療著力點。不過必須提醒，這些都來自急性、局部的用藥，並不等於重現病人歷時多年、遍佈全腦的慢性病程，比較像一個乾淨的原理驗證；至於為什麼關掉開口朝外的表面絞碎機，會讓細胞內部的 tau 結塊，中間的分子步驟其實也還沒完全釐清。

技術及材料

主題一：膜不可穿透型蛋白酶體抑制劑的化學合成
以固相胜肽合成搭配點擊化學把親水性大基團接到 epoxomicin 與 MG132 上，製造只能作用於細胞外露之神經蛋白酶體、無法進入細胞質的膜不可穿透抑制劑。

最佳版本vote 1

這個模塊要回答一個很實際的問題：那把只鎖表面的鑰匙 iBEp，到底是怎麼一步步做出來的。作者的起點是 epoxomicin，一種能卡死蛋白酶體的抑制劑。他們先用固相胜肽合成（Fmoc solid-phase peptide synthesis，SPPS）搭骨架：把胺基酸一顆顆接在固定的樹脂珠上，好處是每接一顆只要用溶劑沖掉多餘試劑就好，不必每步都純化。接完三顆胺基酸（一個 Thr 加兩個 Ile）後，再接上一段親水長鏈（PEG），並在鏈尾留一個叫疊氮基（azide）的化學把手。骨架用強酸從樹脂上切下來後，再在溶液中把負責卡死酵素的環氧酮彈頭接到分子另一端。最後用點擊化學把帶炔基的生物素（biotin）扣到尾端的疊氮基上，得到成品 iBEp（全名 Biotin-PEG24-Epox）。

為什麼一定要用 epoxomicin 這個彈頭？蛋白酶體切蛋白時，靠的是催化位點上一個帶羥基的蘇胺酸（threonine）當剪刀。epoxomicin 尾端的環氧酮彈頭會專門對準這個蘇胺酸，跟它形成一個穩固的環狀共價鍵，等於把剪刀焊死。因為這個彈頭需要蘇胺酸這種特定結構才反應得起來，而蛋白酶體剛好是少數用蘇胺酸當剪刀的酵素，所以它幾乎只認蛋白酶體、不會誤傷別的蛋白酶。這種鎖是共價、不可逆的，卡上去就不鬆開。作者另外還做了一款用 MG132 當彈頭的版本，MG132 的結合是可逆的、會卡住也會鬆開。一個焊死、一個活扣，兩種鎖法方便在後面互相驗證。

接著是這篇論文最核心的設計：為什麼掛上 PEG 跟 biotin，藥就進不了細胞？細胞膜是一層油脂做的牆，只有夠小、夠油溶的分子才鑽得過去。PEG 是一條很長又極度親水（愛水怕油）的鏈，biotin 也是個親水的大基團，兩個一起把整個分子變得又大又愛水，於是它在油牆前面卡住、擠不進細胞質（設計理據見 Matsson & Kihlberg 2017）。作者特別用最長的一款 PEG（24 個單元，PEG24），就是要把穿透性壓到最低。這樣一來，iBEp 只碰得到暴露在細胞表面、開口朝外的神經蛋白酶體，碰不到躲在細胞質裡的那一大群蛋白酶體。

把 biotin 接到骨架上為什麼用點擊化學（click chemistry），而不是隨便一種接法？點擊化學是一類「一拍即合」的反應：疊氮基（azide）跟炔基（alkyne）一碰就扣成一個穩固的三角形環，專一、乾淨、產率高，不會去動分子上其他部位。iBEp 用的是需要銅離子催化的版本（CuAAc）。而可逆的 SulfoMG-132 改用不需要銅的版本（無銅點擊 SPAAC，靠一種張力環結構 DBCO 直接跟疊氮基反應），避開銅可能帶來的干擾。

作者不只做一款，而是做出四款各有用途的膜不可穿透抑制劑。iBEp 是共價、不可逆的主力。SulfoMG-132 換成可逆的鎖，且用完全不同的化學骨架。IgG-Epox 把彈頭掛在一整顆抗體（IgG）上，體積龐大到絕無可能溜進細胞。JF647-Epox 再接上一個螢光染料（Janelia Fluor 647），方便在顯微鏡下直接看它停在哪。前三款鎖法與體積都不同，如果它們都誘發同樣的 tau 結塊，就能排除「只是某一支分子剛好有奇怪副作用」的可能，讓結論更硬。

這套設計最怕三件事出錯。第一，如果 PEG 太短、藥滲進了細胞，它就會連細胞質裡的蛋白酶體一起卡死，之後看到的 tau 結塊就分不清是表面還是內部那台造成的，整個歸因垮掉——這也是為什麼要一路把 PEG 加長到 24。第二，如果環氧酮彈頭在合成或保存中被破壞，藥就失去卡死酵素的能力，等於一把斷了齒的鑰匙。第三，如果點擊反應沒接完全，成品裡混著沒接上 biotin 的分子，純度和穿透性都不保。為了確認做出來的真的是設計的分子，作者最後用反相高效液相層析（HPLC）搭配質譜（LC–MS）驗明每一款產物的身分與純度。

工具與材料
  • epoxomicin：一種靠尾端環氧酮彈頭與蛋白酶體形成共價鍵、把它永久卡死的不可逆抑制劑。
  • 環氧酮彈頭 (epoxyketone warhead)：epoxomicin 尾端的反應基，會與蛋白酶體催化位點的蘇胺酸形成環狀共價鍵，是『卡死酵素』的關鍵。
  • MG132：另一種蛋白酶體抑制劑，與酵素可逆結合、會卡住也會鬆開；SulfoMG-132 以它為彈頭。
  • 固相胜肽合成 (Fmoc SPPS)：把胜肽一端黏在樹脂珠上、一顆顆往上接胺基酸的合成法，每步只需沖洗、不必純化。
  • 點擊化學 (click chemistry)：疊氮基與炔基一拍即合扣成三角形環的專一、高產率反應；iBEp 用銅催化 (CuAAc)，SulfoMG 用無銅版 (SPAAC)。
  • 疊氮基／炔基 (azide／alkyne)：點擊化學裡互相扣合的一對化學把手。
  • biotin (生物素)：又親水又佔體積的標籤，既增加分子體積阻擋穿膜，也能被 streptavidin 抓住供偵測。
  • iBEp：膜不可穿透的共價抑制劑，全名 Biotin-PEG24-Epox，是本研究的主力工具。
  • SulfoMG-132：可逆版膜不可穿透抑制劑，以無銅點擊化學把 biotin 接到 MG132-PEG24 上。
  • IgG-Epox：把 epoxomicin 掛在一整顆抗體 IgG 上的龐大版膜不可穿透抑制劑，體積大到絕無法入胞。
  • JF647-Epox：接上 Janelia Fluor 647 螢光染料的版本，供顯微鏡直接定位抑制劑停在哪。
  • HPLC／LC–MS：反相高效液相層析與液相層析質譜，用來驗明每款產物的身分與純度。

主題二：抑制劑膜不透性與代謝穩定性的多重正交驗證
以物理化學、細胞與體內多重正交測定嚴格證明抑制劑無法穿膜且不被水解，使任何 tau 表型都可歸因於神經蛋白酶體而非細胞質蛋白酶體。

這個子項要回答一個看似枝節、實則決定全篇成敗的問題：這款抑制劑到底進不進得了細胞？整篇論文的立論全押在「看到的 tau 聚集只可能來自表面那台神經蛋白酶體」，只要抑制劑偷偷漏進細胞、也把細胞質蛋白酶體關掉，這句話就報廢。單一種測定各有盲點，於是作者用一整套原理互不相干的方法反覆問同一件事，這叫正交驗證——原理彼此獨立，任一種若有偽陰性，其他幾種會補上，全數一致才敢下結論。第一層先問「這分子在純物理上過不過得了一層膜」：PAMPA 用一張沒有細胞、只有磷脂的人工膜，最乾淨地量分子靠擴散能滲過多少；MDCK 進一步用真的活細胞鋪成一層，量分子能不能穿過整層、還能看出細胞會不會主動把它泵出去。兩者都顯示 iBEp 的穿透性至少比能穿膜的原版藥 epoxomicin 低 2,500 倍。

第二層不再靠間接推論，而是直接把處理過的神經元打開、抽出裡面所有小分子，用質譜 (LC–MS) 去找有沒有 iBEp 或它的碎片。這台質譜靈敏到 1 pg 都測得到，作者再用一個專門秤 biotin 的顯色法 (HABA，靈敏到 1 pmol) 交叉確認，結果細胞裡什麼都沒找到。他們還堵上另一個漏洞：萬一 iBEp 在體內被酶剪斷，釋放出來的小片段就可能溜進細胞。於是把 iBEp 泡在腦裂解液裡，隔一段時間就量還剩多少、算出半衰期 (T1/2)，證實它穩定、不會被剪斷。

光證明「進不去」還不夠，還得證明它「確實在該作用的地方幹活」，否則可能只是個沒作用的廢分子。作者把神經元拆成兩堆——貼在膜上的蛋白、和漂在細胞質的蛋白，分別餵一段會被蛋白酶體切開而發亮的小片段受質 (Suc-LLVY-AMC) 來量各自的切割活性。結果乾淨俐落：iBEp 把膜相那堆的活性壓下去，細胞質那堆卻完全不動。這正是「只關表面、不碰內部」的正面鐵證。

最後一層借用細胞自己的反應來再確認一次。細胞質蛋白酶體一旦被堵，細胞會出現一整套典型的塞車警報：該被絞碎的垃圾蛋白排隊堆積（例如專門被蛋白酶體吃掉的報告蛋白 GFPu 累積、貼著泛素標記的垃圾蛋白與自噬接收器 p62 增多），細胞還會緊急趕造新酶來救急（反彈反應 bounceback），並叫來備援清道夫（自噬泡形成，可用 LC3-GFP 探針看到）。作者發現 iBEp 一個警報都沒觸發，等於從細胞的自發反應反過來證明內部那台沒被碰到。反過來想這件事就更清楚：假如 iBEp 真的漏進去，這套測定會整組亮紅燈——胞內質譜抓到訊號、細胞質活性下降、GFPu 與 p62 堆積、反彈反應啟動，而 tau 聚集也就再說不清是哪一台造成的。正因為全數呈陰性，作者才敢把後續所有 tau 表型算在神經蛋白酶體頭上。

工具與材料
  • PAMPA-BBB：用一張只有磷脂、沒有細胞的人工膜測分子被動擴散能滲過多少，最乾淨地反映分子本身的穿膜傾向；iBEp 在此至少比 epoxomicin 低 2,500 倍。
  • MDCK-MDR1：用真的活細胞鋪成單層測穿透性，並能看出細胞會不會主動把分子泵出去（外排），補上人工膜測不到的一面。
  • 胞內代謝物 LC–MS：把處理過的神經元打開、抽出所有小分子後用質譜尋找 iBEp 或其碎片，偵測極限低到 1 pg，直接證明細胞裡沒有它。
  • HABA biotin 定量：一種專門秤樣本中 biotin 含量的顯色法（偵測極限 1 pmol），與質譜交叉確認 iBEp 未進入細胞。
  • 腦裂解液穩定性 (T1/2)：把化合物泡在腦裂解液裡、隨時間用質譜量殘留百分比並算半衰期，證實 iBEp 不會被剪斷而釋放出可穿膜的碎片。
  • Suc-LLVY-AMC 活性測定：一段被蛋白酶體切開後會發亮的小片段受質；分別餵給膜相與細胞質相，量各自的切割活性，證明 iBEp 只壓低膜相、不動細胞質相。
  • GFPu (Ub-G76V-GFP)：一顆會被蛋白酶體持續絞碎的報告蛋白；細胞質蛋白酶體被堵時它會累積發亮，iBEp 不引起累積，反證未進入細胞。
  • LC3-GFP：標記自噬泡形成的螢光探針；細胞質蛋白酶體被抑制常誘導自噬，iBEp 不誘導，屬不透性的旁證。
  • proteasome bounceback：蛋白酶體被堵後細胞緊急趕造新酶的反彈反應（以 qPCR 偵測新酶基因表現）；iBEp 不觸發，說明內部蛋白酶體未受抑制。
  • ubiquitin conjugates / p62：泛素標記的待分解垃圾蛋白與自噬接收器 p62，是細胞質蛋白酶體塞車的標記；iBEp 不使其增加。

主題三：Sarkosyl 分級分離萃取內源性不可溶 tau
用 N-lauroylsarcosine 洗滌加超高速離心把神經元或海馬組織分成可溶與不可溶部分，定量並捕捉抑制所誘發的內源性不可溶 tau 聚集體。

最佳版本vote 1

要研究 tau 有沒有『結塊』，第一步得先把已經結塊的 tau 從一大鍋正常蛋白裡乾淨地撈出來、還要能秤重。作者用的手法叫 sarkosyl 分級分離。它利用一種溫和的去垢劑（N-lauroylsarcosine，簡稱 sarkosyl）：這種洗劑力道適中，能溶掉細胞裡大部分正常、鬆散的蛋白質，卻溶不掉已經纏成硬實纖維的 tau。作法是把神經元或海馬組織泡進 sarkosyl 裂解液後，再用超高速離心（186,000 g）狠狠一甩：溶掉的蛋白留在上層液（可溶部分），沒溶掉的纖維被甩到管底沉澱（不可溶部分）。這管沉澱，就是一份幾乎只含聚集態 tau 的乾淨樣本。

為什麼同樣是 tau，正常的溶得掉、聚集的溶不掉？關鍵在結構。正常的 tau 是單根、鬆散的，去垢劑一泡就散進水裡。而病變的 tau 彼此緊密堆疊成一種叫 \(\beta-\)摺疊的規則結構、纏成硬實的纖維，去垢劑鑽不進、拆不開。所以『能不能被 sarkosyl 溶掉』就成了一把好用的尺：溶不掉的，操作上就等於已經聚集的。撈出來的沉澱要做後續分析時，再用強力的變性劑（6 M 尿素）把它硬泡開回溶。

撈出來之後怎麼判讀？作者把可溶與不可溶兩部分分別跑蛋白質電泳、用抗 tau 抗體顯影，並用一台紅外線影像儀（LiCor）量帶子的強度，再以可溶部分裡穩定表現的看家蛋白（GAPDH）當基準做正規化，好跨樣本公平比較。一個關鍵讀數是：被抑制誘發出來的不可溶 tau，會出現在約 64 kDa 的位置——這正是聚集、過度磷酸化 tau 的分子量特徵。

這裡有兩個對照缺一不可。第一個是把 tau 基因整個剃除的神經元（Tau-KO）：如果不可溶那條帶子在 Tau-KO 裡完全消失，就證明它真的是 tau，而不是某個剛好被抗體誤認的別種蛋白。第二個是一款『沒有彈頭』的假抑制劑（inactive scaffold，即 biotin-PEG24-IleIleThr，把 iBEp 的環氧酮彈頭拿掉）：它體積、親水性都跟 iBEp 一樣，唯獨不會卡死酵素；如果只有真 iBEp 誘發聚集、這款假的不會，就證明聚集是抑制了蛋白酶體造成的，而不是那個又大又親水的分子本身戳出來的。反過來說，這套手法也很脆弱：離心不夠快，聚集態 tau 甩不到管底、就撈不乾淨；去垢劑太兇會把纖維也溶掉做出假陰性、太弱又讓正常蛋白共沉做出假陽性。

工具與材料
  • sarkosyl (N-lauroylsarcosine)：一種溫和去垢劑，能溶掉正常鬆散蛋白、卻溶不掉纏成纖維的聚集態 tau，是分離不可溶 tau 的關鍵試劑。
  • 超高速離心 (186,000 g)：高速甩沉，把又大又重的不可溶 tau 聚集體沉到管底、與上層可溶蛋白分開。
  • 可溶／不可溶部分：離心後的上層液與管底沉澱，分別代表正常鬆散蛋白與已聚集的 tau。
  • \(\beta-\)摺疊：tau 纖維裡緊密堆疊的規則結構，讓聚集體抵抗去垢劑溶解。
  • 6 M 尿素：強力變性劑，用來把撈出的不可溶沉澱硬泡開回溶，供後續分析。
  • LiCor 定量：用紅外線影像儀量蛋白電泳帶強度的定量法。
  • GAPDH：穩定表現的看家蛋白，當作跨樣本正規化的基準。
  • Tau-KO 神經元：剃除 tau 基因的對照，用來證明不可溶帶子確實是 tau、非抗體誤認。
  • 失活 scaffold (biotin-PEG24-IleIleThr)：拿掉環氧酮彈頭的假抑制劑，體積親水性同 iBEp 但不卡酵素，用來證明聚集來自抑制、非分子本身。
  • 64 kDa 位移：被誘發的不可溶 tau 在電泳上出現的分子量位置，是聚集、過度磷酸化 tau 的特徵。

主題四：電子顯微鏡、免疫金與 AT8／ThioS 原位染色鑑定 PHF 與 AD 特徵
結合負染／免疫金電鏡與 AT8 磷酸化 tau、ThioS \(\beta-\)摺疊原位染色，從超微結構到分子標記多角度證明所誘發的聚集體即為 AD 相關的 tau 雙股螺旋纖維（PHF）。

最佳版本vote 1

前一步撈出了『溶不掉的 tau』，但溶不掉不等於就是阿茲海默症那種纖維。這一步的任務，是像驗明本尊一樣、從好幾個獨立角度證明它就是 AD 的雙股螺旋纖維（paired helical filament，PHF）。作者用了三組互補的證據：一是用電子顯微鏡直接看纖維的立體長相、量它的尺寸；二是用金顆粒標記的抗體確認這些纖維的成分真的是 tau；三是用兩種染色分別檢查它帶不帶 AD 的分子招牌——特定位置的磷酸標記，以及聚集體特有的 \(\beta-\)摺疊結構。

先看形狀。電子顯微鏡本身只認得出『電子穿不穿得過』，而蛋白質由碳氫氧氮這些輕原子組成、散射很弱，幾乎沒有對比。作者的辦法是負染（negative staining）：滴上一種含重金屬的染劑（醋酸鈾醯），它會積在纖維周圍、把背景染得又暗又密，纖維本身反而透亮地襯出輪廓。這樣就能看清那一根根扭成雙螺旋的纖維，還能量出尺寸——最寬處約 25.9 nm、兩股交叉一次的間距約 87 nm，這兩個數字正好和人腦裡的 PHF 對得上。至於纖維成分是不是 tau，則靠免疫金（immunogold）：用一個會專門認 tau 的抗體去貼纖維，抗體上再接一顆 6 nm 的金顆粒；金這種重原子在電鏡下是明顯的黑點，黑點乖乖排在纖維上，就證明纖維確實是 tau。

光是形狀對、成分對還不夠，作者還要它帶著 AD tau 的『分子招牌』。第一個招牌是特定位置的磷酸標記。阿茲海默症裡的 tau 會被過度磷酸化——在一些平常不該加磷酸的位置被硬加上磷酸基；作者用一個專認這種磷酸化 tau 的抗體（AT8，認的是 Ser202/Thr205 這兩個位點）去染，切片就亮了起來。他們還用質譜確認被誘發的 tau 磷酸化位點，正好對應人類阿茲海默症常見的那幾個（Ser202、Thr205、Thr217、Ser404）。第二個招牌是聚集體特有的 \(\beta-\)摺疊結構，用 Thioflavin S（ThioS，一種會嵌進 \(\beta-\)摺疊並發亮的染劑）就能照出來，呈現阿茲海默症切片裡典型的火焰狀與絲狀團塊。更關鍵的是，AT8 與 ThioS 的訊號會在同一個影像切面上重疊（共定位），代表同一團東西既是磷酸化 tau、又有 \(\beta-\)摺疊——這正是 AD 纏結的雙重指紋。

為什麼要大費周章去量寬度和交叉間距、還要比對人腦數值？因為這些尺寸就是 PHF 的指紋：形狀像、數字也一致，才敢說它跟人腦的 PHF 是同一種東西。為了不讓期待影響判讀，拍照和數纏結都是盲化進行的。最硬的一個對照仍是 Tau-KO 小鼠：把牠的樣本濃縮到三倍去找，還是找不到任何螺旋纖維——這徹底排除了『纖維其實來自別種蛋白』的可能，坐實了纖維非 tau 不可。

這一步為什麼要三組證據齊發？因為單靠任何一種都有盲點。只看電鏡，看得到扭曲的纖維卻不知道它是什麼做的；只看 AT8 或 ThioS，知道有磷酸化、有 \(\beta-\)摺疊，卻看不到超微結構、也可能把別的東西誤染。還有一個隱憂是腦組織本身會發出背景螢光，可能被誤認成 ThioS 訊號，所以作者特地用一種染劑（Sudan Black B）把自體螢光壓下去。把形狀、成分、磷酸招牌、\(\beta-\)摺疊四個角度疊起來、每個角度又設好對照，才能結論說：被誘發的，就是貨真價實的 AD 型 tau PHF。

工具與材料
  • 負染電鏡 (negative staining)：用重金屬染劑把背景染暗、反襯出纖維透亮輪廓的電鏡法，用來看纖維形狀與量尺寸。
  • 醋酸鈾醯 (uranyl acetate)：負染用的重金屬染劑，積在纖維周圍製造電子密度對比。
  • 免疫金 (immunogold)：在抗 tau 抗體上接一顆金顆粒，金在電鏡下呈黑點，黑點排在纖維上即證明成分是 tau。
  • 纖維寬度與交叉間距：PHF 的尺寸指紋（寬約 25.9 nm、交叉間距約 87 nm），與人腦 PHF 一致。
  • AT8：專認特定位點（Ser202/Thr205）磷酸化 tau 的抗體，一亮即代表出現 AD 型的病態磷酸化 tau。
  • Thioflavin S (ThioS)：會嵌進聚集體 \(\beta-\)摺疊結構並發亮的染劑，照出火焰狀與絲狀的纏結團塊。
  • \(\beta-\)摺疊：聚集體特有的規則堆疊結構，是 ThioS 的染色標的。
  • 共定位：AT8 與 ThioS 訊號在同一影像切面重疊，證明同一團既是磷酸化 tau 又有 \(\beta-\)摺疊。
  • 質譜磷酸化位點鑑定：確認被誘發 tau 的磷酸化位點對應人類 AD（Ser202/Thr205/Thr217/Ser404）。
  • NeuN：神經元細胞核標記，共染以確認訊號位在神經元。
  • Sudan Black B：壓抑組織自體螢光的染劑，避免背景螢光被誤認為 ThioS 訊號。

主題五：立體定位海馬內注射的體內抑制模型
以立體定位把膜不可穿透抑制劑直接注入小鼠海馬 CA1，並以對側媒劑對照的內控設計，測試單靠神經蛋白酶體抑制是否足以在體內誘發 tau 病理。

最佳版本vote 1

培養皿的結果需要在活體腦裡再驗一次，這個子項就是把抑制劑精準送進小鼠腦深處的手術。海馬埋在腦子深處、肉眼看不到，得靠座標盲打：作者把小鼠頭固定在帶刻度的架子上，以頭骨表面一個固定地標 (bregma) 當座標原點，依「往旁、往後、往下」三個方向的座標把針尖送到海馬（立體定位 stereotaxic）。之所以能一次次命中看不見的目標，是因為同一品系小鼠的頭骨地標和腦內結構相對位置幾乎固定，剛性架子加上 bregma 這個共同原點，把每次手術校準到同一參考系，就像用經緯度定位一樣可重現。而他們特地挑海馬裡的 CA1 這個亞區，是因為它正是人類 AD 最早、也最嚴重出現 tau 病變的地方，在此下手最貼近真實疾病。

針到位後，作者不是用手推針筒，而是用一台能以固定慢速送藥的幫浦 (微注射泵) 緩緩打入 2 微升藥液，送完停留約 1 分鐘再把針拔出來。這個停留很關鍵：給藥液時間擴散進組織站穩，才不會在拔針時順著針留下的通道往上倒流、漏到上層皮質。反過來說，若送藥太快或送完立刻抽針，藥液會亂竄或回流，真正到達 CA1 的劑量就變得不確定、甚至打錯位置——那樣即使看到 tau 病理，也說不清是不是打在對的地方、對的劑量。

這套模型最聰明的地方在對照設計。作者在同一隻小鼠的左右兩側海馬各打一針：一側打抑制劑，另一側打不含藥的溶劑當對照，這叫同側／對側內控。比較的兩側來自同一隻動物，年齡、基因背景、手術當天狀態全都一樣，唯一差別只有「有沒有藥」，於是兩側差異就乾淨地只反映藥效。相比之下，若改用「一群動物打藥、另一群打對照」，兩群本來就有的個體差異會混進結果，很難分清是藥的功勞還是動物不同。左右對打把這層雜訊消掉，還能一對一配對比較、統計更靈敏。作者更把注射管盲化，讓執刀與判讀的人都不知道哪一管是藥，避免不自覺偏袒某一側。給藥後養 72 小時再取腦分析。

劑量則是在「太低沒效、太高有害」之間找甜蜜點。作者試過發現，濃度一旦超過約 12 µM 就會誘發小鼠癲癇；而癲癇是一場劇烈的神經風暴，會帶來大量繼發損傷與異常放電，之後看到的 tau 病理就再也分不清是神經蛋白酶體被抑制直接造成的、還是癲癇的副產物。於是他們往下調到 6.4 µM——這個劑量既能測到膜上蛋白酶體確實被抑制，又不引起明顯不良反應。寧可效果溫和一點，也要保住歸因的乾淨。

工具與材料
  • 立體定位 (stereotaxic)：把頭固定於帶刻度的剛性架、以三維座標將針尖精準送到看不見的腦深處目標的手術法。
  • bregma：頭骨表面一個固定地標，當作立體定位座標系的共同原點，是每次手術對齊同一參考系的關鍵。
  • 海馬 CA1：海馬中的一個亞區，也是人類 AD 最早、最嚴重出現 tau 病變之處，故選為體內注射的靶區。
  • 微注射泵：以固定慢速緩緩送藥的幫浦，避免手推造成的壓力波動；本實驗送入 2 微升。
  • 同側／對側內控 (ipsi/contra)：同一隻動物一側打抑制劑、對側打溶劑對照，使兩側只差在有無藥，去除動物間個體差異並允許配對統計。
  • 劑量最佳化 (6.4 µM)：在有效與安全間取捨的劑量；>12 µM 會誘發癲癇，故選 6.4 µM，既能抑制膜相蛋白酶體又無明顯不良反應。
  • 盲化：把注射管的身分對操作與判讀者隱藏，避免不自覺偏袒某一側造成的偏誤。

用 Sulfo-NHS-LC-Biotin 表面標定配合 GDN 質膜富集分級，選擇性量測質膜上神經蛋白酶體亞基的表面丰度，作為跨基因型、年齡與人腦組織的核心定量讀值。

整篇論文後半段（ApoE 基因型、老化、人腦）的核心數字，其實就是一個量：細胞表面到底停著多少台神經蛋白酶體。但這個量很難測——因為同一種零件，細胞裡也有一大堆。作者要的是『露在表面那一群』的丰度，得想辦法把它從內部那群裡分出來。這個模塊用了兩種各自獨立的辦法去量同一件事：一是只在細胞表面『噴漆做記號』再撈出來，二是把細胞打碎、用離心把最外層的質膜這一層物理地分出來。兩種方法都指向同一個數字，結論才可靠。

第一種辦法叫表面生物素標定。作者用一種特別的試劑 Sulfo-NHS-LC-Biotin：它會跟蛋白質上的某些位置反應、貼上一個叫 biotin 的小標籤，而它的關鍵特性是進不了細胞——所以只貼得到露在細胞表面的蛋白，碰不到躲在裡面的。貼完之後，再用一種會死死抓住 biotin 的蛋白（streptavidin）做成的珠子，把所有被貼過標籤的表面蛋白通通釣出來；biotin 與 streptavidin 之間是生物界最強的一種非共價結合，抓得又緊又專一，少有漏抓或亂抓。最後看釣出來的這一堆裡含有多少神經蛋白酶體的零件，就知道表面停了多少台。這整個標定要在低溫（4°C）下做，把細胞的膜運輸『凍住』，免得標定期間內部的蛋白被搬到表面、或表面的被吞進去，污染了『表面』這個定義。

第二種辦法叫質膜富集分級，走的是物理分離。作者先把細胞用低張裂解液加一種溫和的去垢劑（GDN）打開——GDN 溫和到能把膜溶開、卻不把膜上的蛋白複合體拆散。接著靠差速離心：把打碎的細胞用一連串越來越快的轉速去甩，越大越重的胞器越早沉下去。先用慢速（400 g）把細胞核甩掉，再用中速（5,000 g）沉掉較重的膜，最後用高速（75,000 g）把質膜這一層富集出來。這樣就得到一份以質膜為主的樣本，再去量裡面的神經蛋白酶體。

量出來的數字要怎麼公平比較？作者用一個比值：表面的 \(\beta5\)／全部的 \(\beta5\)（\(\beta5\) 是蛋白酶體的一個催化零件）。用比值的好處是，它問的是『在所有的酶裡，有多大比例停在表面』，跟細胞總共有多少酶無關，於是不同樣本、不同基因型才比得起來。在人腦樣本裡因為沒法測總量，就改用一個在阿茲海默症中含量不變的膜蛋白（Kv1.2）當基準來校正上樣量。正因為有了這個乾淨、可比的表面丰度讀數，作者才能把它一路量下去——比較 ApoE 的三種版本（E2/E3/E4）、追蹤 1 到 24 個月的老化曲線、對照人腦不同區域——把『表面神經蛋白酶體的多寡』變成串起整個 ApoE 與老化故事的共同貨幣。

這套量法的可靠性，完全建立在『只數到表面那群』上，所以幾個地方一鬆就全毀。如果那個 biotin 試劑其實能滲進細胞，它會連內部的蛋白一起貼標籤，釣出來的就不再是純表面的，表面與內部根本分不開。如果標定沒在低溫下做，細胞的吞吐運輸還在跑，會把內外攪混。分級離心的轉速一旦設錯，別的胞器會混進質膜層、污染讀數。而少了『表面／總量』的正規化，各樣本之間根本無從公平比較。這也是為什麼這一步跟前面驗證抑制劑不透性，本質上是同一種嚴謹：要量『表面』，就得先確保工具真的只碰得到表面。

工具與材料
  • 表面生物素標定 (surface biotinylation)：用膜不可穿透的 biotin 試劑只在細胞表面蛋白上貼標籤，再釣出來定量。
  • Sulfo-NHS-LC-Biotin：一種進不了細胞的 biotin 試劑，只在細胞表面露出的蛋白上貼 biotin 標籤。
  • streptavidin：會以生物界最強非共價鍵死死抓住 biotin 的蛋白，做成珠子把貼過標籤的表面蛋白釣出來。
  • 質膜富集分級 (plasma membrane fractionation)：把細胞打碎後用離心把最外層的質膜這一層分出來，供量測表面神經蛋白酶體。
  • 差速離心 (differential centrifugation)：用一連串越來越快的轉速（400 g→5,000 g→75,000 g），把不同大小密度的胞器分層沉降。
  • GDN：一種溫和去垢劑，能把膜溶開卻不拆散膜上的蛋白複合體。
  • 表面 \(\beta5\)／總 \(\beta5\) 正規化：表面酶佔全部酶的比例，作為與總量無關、可跨樣本比較的讀數（\(\beta5\) 是蛋白酶體的催化零件）。
  • Kv1.2：在阿茲海默症中含量不變的膜蛋白，用作人腦樣本的上樣量校正基準。
  • 低溫 (4°C) 標定：把膜運輸凍住，避免標定期間內部與表面蛋白互相污染『表面』定義。

神經蛋白酶體和細胞裡其他蛋白酶體幾乎一模一樣，很難單獨撈出來研究，於是作者替它裝了一個「標準掛勾」。他們用基因編輯技術 (CRISPR–Cas9) 在小鼠自己的一個蛋白酶體零件基因 (Psma3) 上，接一小段方便被抗體抓取的標籤 (3x-FLAG)；因為 Psma3 是每一台蛋白酶體核心都會用到的共用零件，掛牌焊在這裡就不會漏抓任何一台。這個核心是負責切碎蛋白的桶狀構造 (20S core particle)，兩端蓋上調節蓋後合稱 26S——掛牌裝在核心上，不論當下有沒有加蓋子都抓得到。之後只要用會認這個標籤的抗體，就能像磁鐵一樣把整台機器連同旁邊黏著的夥伴一起吊出來。

作者刻意在小鼠自己的 Psma3 基因原地改造，而不是外加大量表現帶掛牌的蛋白酶體：過量硬塞會逼機器和平常碰不到的蛋白硬湊，撈出的「互作體」多半是假象；原地改造讓帶掛牌的機器維持天然數量與比例，也經確認不影響切碎活性、帶掛牌小鼠與一般小鼠無異。更關鍵的是，Psma3 這零件連分泌 ApoE 的膠細胞等其他細胞也都有，所以掛牌預設關著 (條件式)：基因裡用可被剪切的辨識位點 (loxP) 夾住原片段，只有當專門剪 loxP 的酵素 (Cre 重組酶) 出現時才切換成帶掛牌版本，而 Cre 又只在神經元啟動 (BAF53b-Cre)。這保證撈到的每台機器都來自神經元，直接回應了「神經蛋白酶體與細胞質蛋白酶體共用零件、基因手段難以區分」這個貫穿全篇的難題。

撈取時，作者把腦組織分成兩堆——漂在細胞內部的細胞質，和富集細胞膜的膜相——各自用抓 FLAG 掛牌的珠子把蛋白酶體連同旁邊的蛋白一起沉澱 (共免疫沉澱 Co-IP)，再用質譜秤出每種蛋白的量。巧思在於把細胞質那一堆當成「背景基準線」：膜相和細胞質裡都很多的，是每台機器共通的背景；只有膜相明顯多、細胞質裡卻幾乎被清空的蛋白，才是專門停在細胞膜、靠著神經蛋白酶體的夥伴。作者靠這個差異篩出 64 個蛋白，鎖定了 ApoE 和它的受體 Lrp1；而其他已知的 ApoE 受體（ApoER2、VLDLR、LDLR）都沒撈到，反而說明這個抓取有選擇性、不是亂黏一通。

這個設計最怕兩件事，作者都設了把關。第一，若省掉「膜相 vs 細胞質」的差異比較、只看膜相撈到什麼，清單會被共通夥伴塞滿，ApoE、Lrp1 會被埋在幾百個常見蛋白裡，「神經蛋白酶體抓著 ApoE」的發現就失去說服力。第二，基因編輯可能把掛牌那段 DNA 塞錯位置，作者用能看 DNA 插對與否的檢測 (Southern blot) 確認沒有隨機亂插；再加上嚴格對照——沒有 Cre 的膜相、細胞質相與空白沉澱都抓不到 ApoE 與 Lrp1，只有「有 Cre 的神經元膜相」才撈得到——才排除了亂黏與外漏的假象。

工具與材料
  • CRISPR–Cas9：基因編輯技術，用來在小鼠自己的 Psma3 位點精準接上標籤。
  • Psma3 位點：編碼 20S 核心一個零件的基因，是每台蛋白酶體共用的零件，掛牌焊在此可不漏抓任何一台。
  • 3x-FLAG 標籤：一小段方便被市售抗體牢牢認住的胺基酸序列，當作蛋白酶體的親和純化「掛牌」。
  • 20S core particle / 26S：20S 是負責切碎蛋白的桶狀核心，兩端加上調節蓋 (19S) 即 26S；掛牌裝在核心上兩種形式都抓得到。
  • loxP / Cre 重組酶：loxP 是可被剪切的辨識位點，Cre 是專門剪它的酵素；兩者搭配讓掛牌只在收到指令時才裝上。
  • BAF53b-Cre：只在神經元啟動 Cre 的驅動子，使帶掛牌的蛋白酶體僅出現在神經元，撈取來源明確。
  • 共免疫沉澱 (Co-IP)：用抓 FLAG 掛牌的珠子把蛋白酶體連同旁邊黏著的夥伴一起沉澱下來。
  • 膜相／細胞質分級：把腦組織分成富集細胞膜的膜相與細胞內部的細胞質，作為差異比較的兩堆樣本。
  • 定量質譜 (TMT Co-IP–MS)：秤出兩堆樣本中每種蛋白的量，找出膜相富集、細胞質耗竭的專一互作蛋白。
  • Southern blot：檢測 DNA 是否插對位置的方法，確認掛牌沒有隨機亂插到基因組別處。

主題八：ApoE 亞型脂蛋白顆粒與受體調控實驗（PC-ApoE／RAP／抗體餵食）
用人源 ApoE 亞型敲入小鼠、體外脂化 PC-ApoE 顆粒、RAP 受體拮抗劑與活細胞抗體餵食，證明細胞外脂化 ApoE 亞型（E2 > E3 > E4）差異調控神經蛋白酶體的質膜定位。

已知帶不同版本 ApoE（E2、E3、E4）的人表面神經蛋白酶體多寡不同，這個子項要證明真正在調控的是「細胞外、帶著脂質的 ApoE」，而且效果強弱分明 (E2 > E3 > E4)。作者三管齊下——天生只帶不同人類 ApoE 版本的小鼠、體外做出的脂化 ApoE 顆粒、能擋住 ApoE 受體的拮抗劑——每一招都去量表面停了多少台神經蛋白酶體。量法是表面生物素標定 (surface biotinylation)：用一種只貼得到細胞外側的標籤試劑，在低溫下短暫處理細胞、進不去內部，再用珠子把貼到標籤的「表面那一群」拉下來，量其中的神經蛋白酶體零件 \((\beta5)\) 除以細胞總量，得到「表面佔比」這個核心讀數。

第一招用天生只帶單一人類 ApoE 版本的敲入小鼠：牠們的海馬做表面生物素標定後，帶 E4 的表面神經蛋白酶體明顯比帶 E3、E2 的少，證明基因型效應在受控小鼠上也成立。第二招把實驗室純化的 ApoE (重組 ApoE) 與一組脂質 (POPC 加膽固醇，簡稱 PC) 組裝成貌似腦內天然脂蛋白的脂化顆粒 (PC-ApoE)，加到神經元外面：PC-ApoE2 讓表面機器增加約 2 倍、PC-ApoE4 減少約 3 倍（相對 PC-ApoE3），而去脂化的 ApoE 沒作用。第三招用公認能擋住各種 ApoE 受體的拮抗劑 (RAP) 把碼頭佔住，表面的 Lrp1 與神經蛋白酶體一起變少、GluR1 不變；並用抗體餵食 (對活細胞餵會認 FLAG 掛牌的抗體、只標得到表面) 再驗一次表面變化。

把這些結果串起來，機制就清楚了：神經蛋白酶體旁邊黏著 ApoE 和它的受體 Lrp1，細胞外的脂化 ApoE 顆粒接到表面受體上，而受體又和神經蛋白酶體綁在一起，於是 ApoE 一接上，就能左右「機器留在表面、還是被收進細胞」的平衡；不同版本的 ApoE 抓受體的效果不同，表面機器數量就跟著不同。用 RAP 把受體整個擋住時，表面的 Lrp1 和神經蛋白酶體會一起變少，等於把 ApoE 的訊號源頭掐斷、機器守不住表面。脂質之所以必要，是因為 ApoE 要帶著脂質才抓得住受體，這也是去脂化 ApoE 完全沒作用的原因。

之所以要三管齊下，是因為只靠基因改造小鼠只能說「帶 E4 的動物表面機器較少」，那是天生的相關性；外加成分明確的 PC-ApoE 顆粒才證明「單是外加不同版本的 ApoE 就足以改變表面數量」，RAP 則證明這條路要經過 ApoE 受體——遺傳、生化、藥理三方收斂，才把相關推成因果。過程中的每個對照都在擋假陽性：去脂化 ApoE 證明脂質是必要的；不隨處理改變的膜蛋白 (GluN1／GluR1) 若一起變動就代表是全域效應或毒性、它們維持不變才說明變化專一；抗體餵食用 MAP2 當陰性對照，MAP2 標不到才代表膜完整、量到的確實是表面而非漏進去的內部訊號。

  • 表面生物素標定 (surface biotinylation)：用只貼細胞外側的標籤試劑加珠子沉澱，量出表面那一群蛋白，是本篇的核心表面讀數。
  • PC-ApoE：把純化 ApoE 與脂質 (POPC 加膽固醇) 組裝成、貌似腦內天然脂蛋白的脂化顆粒，用來測試細胞外 ApoE 的充分性。
  • 重組 ApoE / 去脂化對照：實驗室純化的 ApoE；把脂質拿掉的版本作對照，證明脂化是起作用的必要條件。
  • RAP：公認能擋住各種 ApoE 受體的拮抗劑，用來測試調控是否需經 ApoE 受體。
  • 抗體餵食 (antibody feeding)：對活著沒破膜的神經元餵入認 FLAG 掛牌的抗體，只標得到表面神經蛋白酶體，作為表面丰度的正交讀數。
  • GluN1／GluR1：處理後不變的膜蛋白對照，維持不變才說明對神經蛋白酶體的效果是專一的。
  • MAP2 對照：抗體餵食的陰性對照，標不到才代表細胞膜完整、量到的是表面而非漏入的內部訊號。

主題九：TMT 定量蛋白質體與磷酸化蛋白質體的質譜採集與資料庫檢索
以等重同位素 TMT 標記結合離線高 pH 反相分餾與 Orbitrap 質譜，並用 COMET／SEQUEST 對 Uniprot 資料庫檢索，定量可溶／不可溶蛋白質體、tau 磷酸化位點與神經蛋白酶體互作體。

這條流水線的目的，是「一次同時秤出很多個樣本裡、每一種蛋白各有多少」。作者先用一種剪刀酵素 (trypsin) 把每個樣本的蛋白剪成規則的小片段 (胜肽)——因為蛋白太大不好直接秤。質譜 (mass spectrometry) 是一台能極精準量分子重量的儀器：每段胜肽的重量、以及打碎後的碎片重量分布，合起來像一組指紋，拿去比對已知所有蛋白序列的資料庫，就能反推它來自哪一種蛋白。全篇的可溶／不可溶普查、tau 磷酸化位點與神經蛋白酶體互作體，都是靠這台儀器量出來的。

要「同時」比很多樣本，作者靠等重同位素標記 (TMT) 這組化學條碼。它的巧妙在「等重」：同一段胜肽不論來自哪個樣本，貼上不同 TMT 後總重仍相同，在儀器裡疊成同一個訊號、不會讓混合物更複雜；可是一旦打碎，每種 TMT 會露出一個重量獨特的小碎片 (報告離子)，碎片的訊號強度就代表這段胜肽在對應樣本裡有多少。因為各樣本的同一段胜肽是在同一時間、同一張圖裡一起被打碎比對的，等於在「完全相同條件」下較量，避開了分開跑時機器狀態每次不同的誤差。把一種蛋白所有胜肽的報告離子加總，就得到它在各樣本的相對量。作者總蛋白與磷酸化用一次貼 16 個樣本的版本 (TMTpro 16-plex)、互作體用貼 10 個樣本的版本 (TMT 10-plex)。

混成一管後仍有好幾萬段胜肽，一次跑分不完，所以先做一次預先分堆：用管柱在較鹼性條件下把胜肽沖散到很多管子裡 (離線高 pH 反相分餾，96 孔併成 24 或 12 份)，這分離原理和後面正式上機的酸性條件互補，能把胜肽盡量攤開讓儀器多秤到幾種。上機時質譜分三層掃描：MS1 偵測完整胜肽的重量、MS2 挑一段打碎讀序列、MS3 再把報告離子單獨隔出來精準量各樣本多寡，而即時檢索 (real-time search) 讓儀器邊跑邊認出胜肽、只對確認身分的才多做 MS3。作者總蛋白普查用能做 MS3 的方法 (Orbitrap Lumos，150 分鐘)，因為要準確比幾千種蛋白、最怕鄰近胜肽混進來稀釋比例；磷酸化與互作體樣本較少、量較低，改用更靈敏、也更能定磷酸位點的高解析 MS2 (Orbitrap Eclipse，180 分鐘)。

量完後，COMET 與 SEQUEST 兩套演算法把每一張碎片圖去對照資料庫裡所有已知蛋白「理論上該長什麼樣」的圖，挑出最吻合的胜肽；資料庫用 Uniprot（總蛋白與磷酸化對小鼠、互作體對人類蛋白酶體序列），並刻意混入一份把序列反過來寫的誘餌 (reverse decoy)，用來估算有多少比對其實是矇對的——少了誘餌就控不住假比對、清單會灌水。要找磷酸化位點時，把「絲胺酸／蘇胺酸／酪胺酸上多掛一個磷酸」設成差異修飾，演算法對每個這類位點試「有磷酸」和「沒磷酸」兩種重量，哪一種對得上就知道磷酸掛在哪。附帶一提，前面若省掉分餾，量少的互作蛋白會被漏掉；若省掉 MS3、任由鄰近胜肽混入，溫和但真實的不可溶 tau 升高可能被壓到看不出來——這也是作者謹守這些步驟的原因。

工具與材料
  • 質譜 (mass spectrometry)：極精準量分子重量的儀器，靠胜肽的重量與碎片指紋比對出蛋白身分。
  • trypsin：剪刀酵素，把太大的蛋白剪成規則的小片段 (胜肽) 才好上機秤。
  • 等重同位素標記 (TMT)：總重相同、打碎後露出不同報告離子的化學條碼；總蛋白／磷酸化用 TMTpro 16-plex，互作體用 TMT 10-plex。
  • 報告離子 (reporter ion)：TMT 打碎後釋出的小碎片，其訊號強度代表該胜肽在對應樣本的量。
  • 離線高 pH 反相分餾：上機前用較鹼性條件把胜肽預先分堆到多個管子，與上機的酸性分離互補，攤開胜肽讓儀器多採樣。
  • Orbitrap (Lumos／Eclipse)：高精密質譜儀；總蛋白用 Lumos 跑 MS3 法，磷酸化與互作體用 Eclipse 跑高解析 MS2 法。
  • MS1／MS2／MS3：三層掃描：偵測完整胜肽重量、打碎定序、再隔出報告離子精準定量。
  • real-time search：儀器邊跑邊認出胜肽身分，只對確認的才多做一步 MS3 定量以省時。
  • COMET／SEQUEST：比對演算法，把實測碎片圖對到資料庫的理論圖找出最吻合的胜肽。
  • Uniprot 資料庫 + reverse decoy：已知蛋白序列資料庫，混入一份反向誘餌以估算並控制矇對的假比對比例。
  • 差異修飾 (differential modification)：把 Ser／Thr／Tyr 加磷酸設為可選重量，逐位嘗試以定出磷酸掛在哪個殘基。

主題十：蛋白質體資料正規化、empirical Bayes 差異表現與熱圖特徵縮放
用對數轉換加中位數拋光正規化，再以 empirical Bayes／DEqMS 收縮變異數偵測差異蛋白丰度，並用特徵縮放產生熱圖，穩健地找出唯獨在不可溶部分升高的聚集受質。

最佳版本vote 1

質譜量出來的是一張大表：每一行是一種蛋白、每一欄是一個樣本，格子裡是原始訊號量；這串處理就是把它「洗乾淨、公平比較、穩健下結論」。在正規化前，作者先設三道品質關卡把不可靠讀值擋在門外：第一道用誘餌資料庫加一個分類方法 (線性判別分析) 把認錯身分的錯誤率壓到 1% 以下 (1% FDR)；第二道只採報告離子訊號夠強的胜肽（總蛋白／磷酸化 >150、互作體 >100），太弱量不準就不要；第三道看挑取夠不夠乾淨 (isolation specificity >0.7)，避免鄰近胜肽混入。過三關的數據才進入後續的正規化與統計。

正規化分兩步，讓不同樣本站在同一基準。第一步取以 2 為底的對數 \(\log_2(x+1)\)（加 1 是避免對 0 取對數）：蛋白量級差距極大、有的上萬有的個位數，取對數後把跨數量級的數字壓到相近尺度，倍數變化也變得對稱好比。第二步做中位數拋光 (median polish)：先減掉每一行（每種蛋白）的中位數、再減掉每一欄（每個樣本）的中位數，把「這種蛋白天生訊號就高」「這管樣本整體上樣多一點」這類與生物無關的系統偏差抵銷掉，各樣本才公平可比。

接著做差異統計，關鍵是「收縮變異數」。蛋白質體重複樣本通常很少（往往三個），每種蛋白自己的變異估不準，總有少數蛋白剛好三個重複很接近、變異小到不合理，就被誤判成顯著。判斷差異本質上是算一個比值：分子是兩組差多大、分母是量測波動多大；分母一旦被估得極小，比值就爆大、假顯著。empirical Bayes 的做法是借所有蛋白的力氣，先看整體的變異大概多大，再把每種蛋白那個不可靠的分母往這個共同水準拉回一點；不合理的小分母被拉大、比值回到現實，於是顯著與否反映「差異真的夠大」而非「運氣好變異超小」。作者用專為質譜設計的套件 (DEqMS) 比較 iBEp 與 DMSO 兩組，同時壓低假陽性又保住偵測力。

真正的巧思在最後一道「不可溶 vs 可溶」的雙堆差異篩。作者要的不是「哪些蛋白變多」，而是「哪些蛋白是因為沒被清掉才結塊」——這是單看一堆答不了的問題。對每種蛋白，他們同時看它在結塊（不可溶）和可溶兩堆各變了多少：若是細胞「做得更多」，兩堆會一起升；若是「該清除卻沒清掉、堆積成塊」，就只在結塊堆升、可溶堆維持原樣。於是只保留「不可溶顯著升高且超過門檻、可溶卻不變」的蛋白，把合成增加的雜訊濾掉、只留降解失敗的候選。這一篩只剩四個：Ckmt1、tau、Taf15、Hspb1，阿茲海默症的招牌 tau 正在其中。也因為質譜裡一種蛋白由幾段胜肽支持、可靠度差很多，作者才特地用把這件事納入考量的 DEqMS，而非假設每個量測一樣可靠的通用工具。

呈現時作者用特徵縮放 (feature scaling) 上色成熱圖：不同蛋白絕對量差很多，直接上色會讓量大的整排偏濃、量小的偏淡，看不出趨勢，所以把每種蛋白在所有條件中的最大值定為 1、最小值定為 0、其餘按 \(\frac{x-\min}{\max-\min}\) 落在中間，再統一對 DMSO 校準，讓每列都用滿色階、一眼比出哪個條件相對最高。回頭看，這套流程每一步都在擋一種錯誤：少了正規化，上樣多寡會被誤讀成生物差異；少了變異數收縮，偶然變異超小的蛋白會冒出假顯著；少了雙堆差異篩，凡總量上升的蛋白都被算進來，「合成變多」與「降解失敗聚集」混為一談，作者最想抓的四個聚集受質就會被埋沒。

工具與材料
  • 對數轉換 \(\log_2(x+1)\)：把跨數量級的訊號壓到相近尺度、使倍數變化對稱好比；加 1 是避免對 0 取對數。
  • 中位數拋光 (median polish)：先減每行（每種蛋白）中位數、再減每欄（每個樣本）中位數，抵銷與生物無關的系統性偏差。
  • target-decoy＋LDA (1% FDR)：用反向誘餌加線性判別分析把認錯身分的錯誤率壓到 1% 以下。
  • 品質關卡 (summed SN、isolation specificity)：只採報告離子訊號夠強 (>150／>100)、挑取夠乾淨 (>0.7) 的胜肽，擋掉量不準的讀值。
  • empirical Bayes 收縮變異數：借所有蛋白的整體變異，把單一蛋白不可靠的變異估計拉回現實，使差異檢定更穩、少假陽性。
  • DEqMS：專為質譜資料設計的差異統計套件，把「一種蛋白由幾段胜肽支持、可靠度不同」納入考量。
  • 不可溶 vs 可溶雙堆差異篩：只留「結塊堆升、可溶堆平」的蛋白，把合成增加濾掉、只留降解失敗導致聚集的候選。
  • 特徵縮放 (feature scaling)：把每種蛋白各自歸一化到 0–1（\(\frac{x-\min}{\max-\min}\)），讓熱圖顯示相對型態而不被絕對量主導。

主題十一：不可溶受質的聚集傾向與序列生物物理特性計算
對 iBEp 誘導升高的不可溶蛋白，用 TANGO 聚集傾向、Kyte–Doolittle 疏水性與 Metapredict V2 無序度預測，檢驗這些受質是否本質上更易聚集。

最佳版本vote 1

前一步篩出一批被 iBEp 推進不可溶堆的蛋白，作者想追問：它們是碰巧被抓來聚集，還是天生體質就容易黏成一團？這一步不做濕實驗，改純粹從胺基酸序列下手。他們先照 iBEp 造成的變化分兩組：相對對照增加超過 25% 的算「明顯增加 (increased)」、變化在正負 10% 以內的算「幾乎沒受影響 (unaffected)」，每個蛋白的完整序列都從蛋白序列資料庫 (UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot) 取得。設對照組是關鍵——單看一個蛋白分數高沒意義，必須拿它和沒受影響那組比，才知道差異蛋白是不是「相對而言」更容易聚集。

作者用三把尺量這些蛋白的天生體質，而它們正好各對應聚集的一種物理推力。第一把是 TANGO 聚集傾向：它讀序列、依二級結構的物理化學規則預測哪些片段愛彼此疊成 \(\beta-\)摺疊、黏成團（類澱粉樣聚集常見的結構），量的是「愛聚集的片段多不多」。第二把是 Kyte–Doolittle 疏水性：替每種胺基酸打「怕不怕水」的分數再平均成整體疏水程度 (GRAVY，以 Python 工具 Biopython 計算)，量的是「互相黏的推力強不強」——怕水的區段為了躲水會彼此貼合，正是聚集的推力。第三把是 Metapredict V2 無序度：預測一條蛋白有多少比例是沒有固定摺疊、鬆散飄動的區段，量的是「結構鬆不鬆、好不好被扳成愛聚集的姿態」（tau 正是典型的無序蛋白）。三者合起來，就能從序列本身估出一條蛋白與生俱來多容易聚集；但要強調這是電腦的傾向預測、是佐證而非鐵證。

之所以這樣設計，是為了省力又不被單一指標騙。用電腦從序列預測，不必再做一堆濕實驗，就能一次檢驗「這些受質是不是天生愛聚集」的假說。一次用三種指標，是因為任何單一分數都可能誤導——某蛋白 TANGO 高卻其實不疏水、或很疏水卻結構很硬，三把尺互相參照才穩。用全長序列而非隨手截的片段，避免「挑到哪段」影響結論。而 increased 對 unaffected 的分組比較，把「分數多少」這種絕對值，變成「差異蛋白相對於沒受影響的蛋白是否更高」這種站得住的相對結論。

反過來說，少了對照或偷懶都會讓結論垮掉。若沒有 unaffected 這組當基準，一個蛋白的 TANGO 或疏水分數再高也無從解讀，因為沒有比較對象、不能宣稱它「比較容易聚集」。若只信單一指標，某蛋白可能因為和聚集無關的原因分數偏高，害人下錯結論——三把尺互相牽制才安全。若拿隨手截的片段而非全長來算，分數會被「剛好挑到哪一段」帶偏。最後，這終究是傾向預測，當成鐵證就過度宣稱——它能說的只是這些受質「看起來本來就偏愛聚集」。

工具與材料
  • increased／unaffected 分組門檻：把 iBEp 下增加 >25% 的蛋白歸「明顯增加」、變化 <±10% 的歸「幾乎沒受影響」，後者作為比較基準。
  • UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot：蛋白序列資料庫，用來取得每個蛋白的完整全長序列供計算。
  • TANGO 聚集傾向：讀序列、依二級結構規則預測哪些片段愛疊成 \(\beta-\)摺疊聚集，量「愛聚集片段多不多」。
  • Kyte–Doolittle 疏水性 / GRAVY：替每種胺基酸打怕水分數再平均成整體疏水程度；越疏水越傾向互相貼合聚集。
  • Biopython：一套 Python 生物資訊工具，用來計算疏水性平均值 (GRAVY)。
  • Metapredict V2 無序度：預測一條蛋白多少比例是無固定摺疊、鬆散飄動的區段；越無序越易被扳成聚集構型。

主題十二：膜滲透性與外排比的定量公式
用一組 logPapp、Papp、外排比與回收率公式，把 PAMPA 與 MDCK 的原始供／受室濃度換算成可比較的滲透係數，客觀量化並排序化合物的膜穿透性。

最佳版本vote 1

前面用兩種試驗量了各化合物進不進得去膜，但量到的只是「上游池、下游池各剩多少濃度」這種原始數字，彼此不好直接比；這個子項就是一組換算公式，把原始濃度統一換成一個叫「表觀滲透係數 \((P_{\mathrm{app}})\)」的分數。兩種試驗角度互補：PAMPA 是純物理版，中間隔一層人工磷脂膜，一邊是加了藥的供室（上游池）、一邊是空白的受室（下游池），看藥被動滲過去多少；MDCK 把膜換成一層活細胞（帶有會把藥往外打的幫浦 P-gp），除了被動穿透，還能看細胞會不會主動把藥趕出去。有了統一的滲透係數，才能客觀排名，並精確講出「iBEp 的滲透性比原版 epoxomicin 低 2,500 倍以上」這種結論。

PAMPA 的換算公式核心很直白：藥從上游池滲走得越多，滲透性越高。它拿「上游池最後剩下的濃度佔一開始的比例」\((C_{\mathrm{don}}/C_{\mathrm{ref}})\) 當主要訊號，式中 \(V_D\) 是上游池體積 (0.3 ml)、A 是膜面積 (0.3 cm²)、\(\varepsilon_a\) 是膜的有效孔隙度 (0.76)、\(\tau_{\mathrm{ss}}\) 是藥開始穩定滲透前的延遲時間，完整式子為 \(\log P_{\mathrm{app}} = \log_{10}\left(-\dfrac{2.303\,V_D}{A\,(t-\tau_{\mathrm{ss}})\,\varepsilon_a}\times\log_{10}\left(\dfrac{1}{1-R_M/100\%}\times\dfrac{C_{\mathrm{don}}}{C_{\mathrm{ref}}}\right)\right)\)。其中 \(R_M\) 是「膜滯留」——有些藥沒滲過去、卡在膜裡，用「上游加下游合起來比一開始少了多少」算出：\(R_M = \left(1-\dfrac{C_{\mathrm{don}}+C_{\mathrm{acc}}}{C_{\mathrm{ref}}}\right)\times100\%\)；先扣掉這部分，滲透係數才不會被高估。

MDCK 的滲透係數算的是「藥累積到下游池的速率」\(：P_{\mathrm{app}} = \dfrac{V_A}{\mathrm{Area}\times\mathrm{Time}}\times\dfrac{[\mathrm{drug}]_{\mathrm{acc}}}{[\mathrm{drug}]_{\mathrm{initial},d}}\)（\(V_A\) 為下游池體積、Area 為膜面積 0.7 cm²），統一表示成 \(10^{-6}\,\mathrm{cm\,s^{-1}}\)。外排比比較「從上到下」與「從下到上」兩方向的滲透係數，\(P_{\mathrm{app}}(BA)/P_{\mathrm{app}}(AB)\) > 2 就代表細胞在主動把藥往外趕（P-gp 在作用）。回收率則是一道健檢：\(\,\mathrm{recovery} = \dfrac{C_{\mathrm{acc}}V_{\mathrm{acc}} + C_d V_d}{C_{\mathrm{initial},d}\,V_d}\times100\%\)，把最後上下游加起來和一開始加的量對帳，看藥有沒有莫名不見（黏管壁或被分解）。

這些公式背後的物理很單純：藥穿過膜的速率大致正比於「膜的滲透性 × 兩邊的濃度差」，所以量上游掉多少、下游多多少，再扣掉卡在膜裡沒過去的部分，就能反推出這層膜對這個藥的滲透係數。外排比之所以要比兩個方向，是因為單純的被動擴散兩邊來去應該對稱，但 P-gp 這種主動幫浦會專門把藥往某一方向（從細胞內側往外側）打，使「從下到上」比「從上到下」快出一截；兩方向一比不對稱，就抓得到被動擴散以外還有主動外排在作用。

這幾個設計都是為了讓結論客觀、站得住，少了任何一個都會失真。換算成統一的滲透係數，是因為不同化合物、不同試驗的原始濃度沒法直接比，只有同一把尺才能排名、才敢講「低 2,500 倍」這種倍數；不換算就講不出排名與倍數。算膜滯留 \(R_M\) 並先扣掉，是因為卡在膜裡沒真的滲過去的藥若被錯算，滲透係數會被高估、扭曲。算回收率是自我健檢：若最後上下游加起來遠少於一開始的量，代表藥被吸附或分解掉了，這時的 \(P_{\mathrm{app}}\) 不可信、得回頭檢查。至於外排比，若沒有搭配 P-gp 抑制劑（例如 cyclosporine A）當對照，就算看到兩方向不對稱也不能斷定是 P-gp——要加了抑制劑後不對稱消失，才確定是它在作用。

工具與材料
  • PAMPA：以人工磷脂膜隔開供室與受室的純物理滲透試驗，量化合物能否被動穿過脂膜。
  • MDCK / MDR1-MDCKII：用表現外排幫浦的活細胞單層做的滲透試驗，加入細胞層次與主動外排。
  • 供室／受室 (donor／acceptor)：加藥的上游池與空白的下游池，藥從供室滲到受室的量是核心原始讀值。
  • 表觀滲透係數 \((P_{\mathrm{app}})\)：把原始濃度換算成的統一滲透速率分數（\(10^{-6}\,\mathrm{cm\,s^{-1}}\)），供客觀排名與倍數比較。
  • 膜滯留 \((R_M)\)：卡在膜裡沒真的滲過去的藥所佔比例，須先扣掉以免高估滲透係數。
  • 外排比 (efflux ratio)：比較兩方向的滲透係數，>2 代表細胞在主動把藥往外趕。
  • P-gp：會把藥往外打的主動外排幫浦，造成雙向運輸不對稱。
  • cyclosporine A：P-gp 抑制劑，作對照；加入後不對稱消失才確定外排由 P-gp 造成。
  • 回收率 (% recovery)：把最後上下游總量與初始量對帳的健檢，過低代表藥被吸附或分解、數據不可信。

主題十三：螢光影像整合密度量化與統計檢定設計
用 ImageJ 整合密度量化配合標準化正規化與盲化，並以 ANOVA、配對 t 檢定與差異中差異設計進行統計推論，客觀量化影像螢光與跨區域／基因型的交互作用效應。

這個模塊的第一半，是把一張螢光顯微照片變成一個可以公平比大小的數字。作者用的量化工具是免費影像分析軟體 Fiji（等同 ImageJ）。顯微鏡存出來的原始檔是 ND2 格式，先用一個叫 Bio-Formats 的匯入外掛把它讀進 Fiji。顯微鏡其實是一層一層掃描、拍成好幾張不同深度的切面，作者把這疊切面壓成一張、每個位置只留最亮的那一層，這一步叫最大強度投影 (maximum intensity projection)，讓分散在不同深度的訊號集中到同一張圖上。接著在圖上圈出要量的範圍，讀出這個範圍的整合密度 (integrated density)——白話說就是把框內每一個像素的亮度全部加總起來，得到一個總亮度數字。這裡刻意用「加總」而不是「平均」：因為螢光亮度大致正比於被標記的分子多寡，把整個範圍的亮度加起來，總和才對應到範圍裡目標分子的總量；只取平均的話，範圍變大、分子變多時反而會被稀釋掉看不出來。

但這個總亮度數字還不能直接拿去比，因為它會被兩件與生物訊號無關的事情灌水。第一，不同照片框到的細胞或組織多寡本來就不一樣，細胞多的框自然總亮度高。第二，不同批染色、不同天拍攝的整體亮度也會漂移。作者的解法是正規化 (normalization)——把目標訊號除以一個代表「這張圖裡有多少細胞或組織」的基準量，抵銷掉這些干擾。在活體腦片上，他們把磷酸化 tau 的抗體訊號 (AT8) 除以標記神經元細胞核的訊號 (NeuN)；作者特別確認 NeuN 在各種處理下都不變、而且和數到的細胞核數目一致，所以 NeuN 剛好能當「這張圖有多少神經元」的量尺 (loading control)。在培養皿的神經元上，他們改用另一種基準：把標記神經元樹突的 MAP2 影像先從 16-bit 轉成 8-bit、再用 Default 這個自動門檻變成只有黑白兩色的圖 (二值化)，數出白色部分佔整張圖的面積比例 (面積分率)，用它代表畫面裡有多少神經元本體。要數細胞核數目時則是另一條路：把 DAPI 細胞核影像用 Huang 這個自動門檻挑出亮點，再用 Particle Analyzer 只把大小落在 8–15 µm 的亮點當成一顆核來數，順手濾掉太小的雜訊和黏成一團的團塊。最後所有數字再除以沒下藥的溶劑對照組 (DMSO) 的平均值，換算成「相對於對照是幾倍」的統一刻度。要是跳過這整套正規化、直接比原始總亮度，一個剛好框到比較多細胞、或那天染得比較亮的樣本，就會被誤判成訊號變多，做出一個根本不存在的效應。

量化裡還有一個容易被忽略、卻攸關結論可信度的環節：盲化 (blinding)。作者讓做影像分析的人事先不知道每張圖屬於哪一組（下藥還是對照），甚至由另一位技術員先把切片與試管的組別編號蓋掉。這對需要人工判讀的量測尤其重要——例如數海馬迴 CA1 區那種火焰狀的纏結染劑訊號 (ThioS)，是靠人眼一顆顆數的。人只要事先知道「這應該是有病的那組」，下手時就會不自覺地把模稜兩可的訊號也算進去，把差異放大。先蓋住組別再數，數出來的差異才會是訊號本身、而不是判讀者的期待。

模塊的第二半，是拿到這些數字之後，用統計判斷組間差異是真的、還是抽樣運氣。作者的統計都在商用軟體 (Origin、GraphPad Prism) 上跑，只有質譜資料改用專門的 DEqMS。要比較三組以上（例如 E2、E3、E4）時，他們用變異數分析 (ANOVA)，而不是把每兩組各做一次 t 檢定。原因是每多做一次比較，就多一次純屬巧合卻看起來有差的機會，比較做得越多、假陽性越容易累積；ANOVA 先用一個整體檢定問「這幾組裡到底有沒有任何一組不一樣」，確認有之後，才用事後檢定 (Tukey 或 Fisher LSD) 追是哪幾組之間有差，這樣才把整體的假陽性率壓在控制之內。遇到同一隻小鼠一側打 iBEp、另一側打對照這種同體對照的實驗，他們用配對 t 檢定 (paired t-test)：因為兩個數字來自同一隻動物，配對比較會先把「這隻鼠整體比較高或比較低」的個體差異抵銷掉，只留下左右兩側的差，比把兩堆數字當成不相干的兩群來比敏感得多。

整個模塊裡最巧妙的統計設計，是用在人腦組織上的差異中差異 (difference-of-differences)。作者想主張表面神經蛋白酶體變少是 ApoE 基因型 (E4) 造成的，但這裡有個難纏的干擾：帶 E4 的人腦子裡的 tau 病變本來通常就比較重，而病變重的區域絞碎機本來也會比較少，所以就算量到 E4 的人絞碎機少，也分不清是基因型的錯、還是那顆腦子單純病得比較重。作者的破解法是取同一個人身上兩個腦區來比：一個幾乎沒有 tau 病變 (BA4)、一個病變較明顯 (BA7)。先算出每個人這兩區的絞碎機落差，再去比不同基因型之間這個落差差多少——也就是比較 \((\mathrm{BA4}-\mathrm{BA7})_{\varepsilon 4/4}\) 與 \((\mathrm{BA4}-\mathrm{BA7})_{\varepsilon 3/3}\) 這兩個「落差的落差」。因為兩個腦區來自同一個人，每個人整體病得多重這個干擾在相減時就自動抵銷，剩下的才是純由基因型放大的區域差異。結果顯示 E4/4 的這個落差大約是 E3/3 的兩倍，作者因此能乾淨地把基因型效應從病理程度效應裡分離出來。

工具與材料
  • 整合密度 (integrated density)：把選取範圍內每個像素的亮度全部加總，得到一個代表螢光訊號總量的數字。
  • Fiji / ImageJ：免費的影像分析軟體，本研究用來圈範圍並量化螢光強度。
  • Bio-Formats Importer：Fiji 的匯入外掛，把顯微鏡的 ND2 原始檔讀進軟體。
  • 最大強度投影 (maximum intensity projection)：把不同深度的多張切面壓成一張、每個位置只留最亮那層，讓訊號集中到同一張圖。
  • Huang 閾值 (Huang threshold)：一種自動決定亮度門檻的方法，用來把 DAPI 細胞核從背景挑出來。
  • Particle Analyzer：Fiji 依物件大小計數的工具，本研究只把 8–15 µm 的亮點當成一顆細胞核來數。
  • 面積分率 (area fraction)：把 MAP2 影像轉成 8-bit 黑白二值圖後白色所佔的面積比例，用來代表畫面中神經元本體多寡。
  • DAPI：把細胞核整個染亮的染劑，用來計數畫面裡的核數目。
  • AT8：辨認阿茲海默症型磷酸化 tau 的抗體，其螢光訊號是活體量化的目標。
  • NeuN：標記神經元細胞核的訊號，因跨處理不變且對應核數，被當作正規化的量尺 (loading control)。
  • MAP2：標記神經元樹突的訊號，其覆蓋面積被當作培養皿實驗的正規化基準。
  • 正規化 (normalization)：把目標訊號除以細胞或組織量的基準、再除以對照組 (DMSO) 平均，去除細胞數與批次造成的干擾。
  • 盲化 (blinding)：讓分析者事先不知道每張圖的組別，避免人工判讀時的期待偏見。
  • 變異數分析 (ANOVA)：一次比較三組以上的整體檢定，確認有差後再用事後檢定 (Tukey / Fisher LSD) 定位是哪幾組。
  • 配對 t 檢定 (paired t-test)：用於同一個體的兩側比較，先抵銷個體差異再看處理效應。
  • 差異中差異 (difference-of-differences)：比較「兩個落差之間的落差」，用同一個人兩腦區的差抵銷個體整體病理程度的干擾。
  • DEqMS：質譜資料專用的差異表現統計工具，本模塊之外的質譜分析採用它。

方法組合策略

(Synergy & Pipeline) 這篇研究的強大之處在於把「化學工具設計」「無偏篩選」「多層確認」與「三線人／鼠／機制收斂」串成一條環環相扣的證據鏈：

1. 「用不可穿透工具，才能把表型歸因給膜」策略：

  • Step 1: 因神經蛋白酶體與細胞質蛋白酶體共用次單元，基因手段無法區分，故改以親水大基團（PEG／biotin／IgG）令抑制劑不可穿膜。
  • Step 2: 再用 PAMPA-BBB、MDCK-MDR1、胞內代謝物 LC–MS（1 pg 偵測極限）與 GFPu／LC3／bounceback 等一整套正交測定「反覆證明它進不去細胞」。
  • 效果： 解決了「共用次單元無法選擇性剔除」的致命瓶頸——任何 tau 聚集都只能來自膜上那群酶。三種正交骨架（共價 iBEp、可逆 SulfoMG、龐大 IgG-Epox）同時陽性，排除單一化合物的脫靶假象。

2. 「無偏篩選 → 失敗降解過濾器 → 逐層定性」策略：

  • Step 1: TMT 同時定量可溶與不可溶蛋白質體，再套用「不可溶升高但可溶不變」的巧妙過濾器（式篩 4 個蛋白），把訊號從「表現量上升」淨化成「降解失敗導致的聚集」。
  • Step 2: 以免疫墨點、Tau-KO 與失活 scaffold 確認特異性 → 電鏡／免疫金證明是 tau PHF 超微結構 → AT8／ThioS 證明具 AD 磷酸化與 β-摺疊。
  • 效果： 每一層回答上一層無法回答的問題，把「某蛋白變多」一路推進到「內源 tau 形成 AD 樣 PHF」。

3. 「用溶酶體抑制劑解開 epoxomicin 遮蔽之謎」策略：

  • Step 1: 面對「不可穿透 iBEp 誘發聚集、但同時打膜與胞質的 epoxomicin 卻不會」這個看似矛盾的觀察，作者假設胞質抑制觸發自噬／溶酶體代償。
  • Step 2: 以 leupeptin＋epoxomicin 共處理恢復不可溶 tau、且 iBEp＋epox 共處理不產生聚集來驗證。
  • 效果： 完美解釋了為何廣譜抑制反而看不到表型，把一個可能推翻模型的矛盾轉為支持證據。

4. 「內源標記 + 亞細胞差異 Co-IP → 人／鼠證據三角驗證」策略：

  • Step 1: CRISPR 於內源 Psma3 敲入條件式 FLAG，避免過表現假象並取得神經元專一的可純化把手；膜相對細胞質差異 IP–MS 專一撈出 ApoE 與 Lrp1。
  • Step 2: hApoE-KI 小鼠、體外 PC-ApoE 脂顆粒與 RAP 拮抗劑三管齊下建立因果；人腦 BA4/BA7 差異中差異把基因型效應與病理程度解耦。
  • 效果： 讓「ApoE 調控神經蛋白酶體」同時獲得遺傳、生化與人體組織三方支持，而非單一相關性。

5. 「劑量–活性閾值曲線揭示基因型脆弱性」策略：

  • Step 1: 在 hTau/hApoE 雙敲入神經元上做 iBEp 劑量曲線，把不可溶 tau 對「殘餘神經蛋白酶體活性」作圖。
  • Step 2: 得到明確閾值——E4 在僅失去約 20% 活性即聚集、E3 約 60%、E2 約 85%，並用 RAP（表型模擬 E4）驗證。
  • 效果： 把分子發現轉譯成可量化的「蛋白穩態儲備容量」概念，直接對應 ApoE 對 AD 的差異風險與「需二次打擊」模型。

(Takeaways for your research) 如果你未來的研究遇到以下狀況，可以借鑑這篇論文的方法：

  • 選擇性抑制與胞內同源複合體共用次單元的膜蛋白／酵素： 如果你的目標蛋白在膜上與胞內是同一家族、共用催化核心而無法用基因手段區分，可借鑑把親水性大基團（PEG／biotin／甚至整顆 IgG）接到小分子抑制劑上使其「不可穿膜」，並用 PAMPA／MDCK／胞內代謝物 LC–MS 多重驗證不透性，把藥理作用侷限在細胞外露的那一群。
  • 分辨「表現量上升」與「降解失敗」的聚集： 如果你觀察到某擾動導致蛋白聚集卻不確定機制，可借鑑同時定量可溶與不可溶蛋白質體，並專門篩「不可溶升高但可溶不變」的蛋白，直接鎖定降解失敗而非合成增加所致的聚集。
  • 一個表型被更廣譜的擾動「遮蔽」而看似矛盾： 如果窄譜擾動有表型、廣譜擾動反而沒有，可借鑑用下游代償途徑抑制劑（如溶酶體 leupeptin）與共處理實驗拆解代償機制，證明「表型被代償系統掃掉」而非「表型不存在」。
  • 在不引入過表現假象下純化內源複合體並找互作蛋白： 如果你要找某內源複合體的互作體，可借鑑 CRISPR 於內源位點條件式敲入親和標籤（Cre 依賴以取得細胞型專一性），再做「膜相對細胞質」的差異 Co-IP–MS，讓位置專一的真互作蛋白從背景中浮現。
  • 把分子表型連到人類疾病風險但受病理程度干擾： 如果你想宣稱某分子變化由基因型驅動、卻擔心被組織病理程度混淆，可借鑑取同一個體高／低病理腦區的配對樣本並做差異中差異分析，把基因型效應與病理效應在統計上解耦。
  • 論證「風險因子不足以致病、需二次打擊」的閾值模型： 如果你的假說是某風險因子只是降低儲備、需第二個打擊才發病，可借鑑做劑量–殘餘活性閾值曲線並跨基因型比較，量化「儲備容量」差異，再用獨立的第二擾動（如受體拮抗或老化）驗證閾值確實被跨越。

Neuroproteasomes regulate endogenous tau paired helical filament formation in an APOE genotype- and age-dependent manner

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-026-02297-x

Received: 1 January 2025; Accepted: 6 April 2026; Published online: 29 May 2026

Victoria Paradise1,2,8, Kalin D. Konrad-Vicario1,2,8, Chi Nguyen1,2, Nyle A. Sharif1,2, Xiao Wang1,2, Rijuta D. Mukim1,2, Malavika Sabu1,2, Bianca T. Corjuc1,2, Joanna Bafia3, Jack Fu4, Gabriella C. Abstract

In Alzheimer’s disease (AD), endogenous tau undergoes a pathogenic transition to form paired helical filaments (PHFs), but the cellular mechanisms driving this process have been elusive. Here, we identify the neuron-specific plasma membrane proteasome (‘neuroproteasome’) as a critical determinant of tau proteostasis. Selective inhibition of neuroproteasome function rapidly triggers the de novo formation of endogenous, sarkosyl-insoluble tau PHFs in primary neurons and mouse brain, which share key biochemical and ultrastructural features with PHFs from human AD brains. The APOE gene has three isoforms (E2, E3 and E4), with APOE4 being the largest genetic risk factor for AD. Neuroproteasome abundance at the plasma membrane is differentially modulated by ApoE isoforms (E2 > E3 > E4) and declines with age. ApoE4 neurons accumulate tau aggregates following modest neuroproteasome disruption, whereas ApoE2 neurons remain resistant. Our findings delineate a neuron-specific mechanism linking genetic and age-related risk factors to the formation of AD-relevant tau pathology, and position neuroproteasome function as a potential target to preserve proteostasis.

The conversion of normally soluble proteins into aggregated macromolecular assemblies is a pathological hallmark common to many neurodegenerative diseases1,2. A defining feature of AD and a subset of tauopathies is the transformation of endogenous soluble tau into aggregated PHFs that accumulate in defined brain regions and correlate with disease progression3,4. While rare familial forms of tauopathies are driven by tau mutations, this pathological transition in AD occurs in the absence of tau mutation or overexpression3,5. The fundamental cellular mechanisms that can lead to the formation of endogenous PHF tau aggregates in AD have remained elusive.

Neurons rely on intricate protein homeostasis (proteostasis) networks to maintain the proteome and prevent aberrant aggregation. However, proteostatic control weakens during aging, the primary risk factor for AD6. Furthermore, the strongest genetic risk factor for sporadic AD, the ε4 allele of apolipoprotein E (APOE), markedly exacerbates tau pathology compared to the common ε3 and protective ε2 alleles7–9. Despite these strong correlations, little is known mechanistically about how AD risk factors converge to disrupt neuronal proteostasis and promote the formation of tau filaments. Considering ApoE is primarily a glia-derived secreted extracellular factor10, we hypothesized that specialized proteostasis machinery at the neuronal plasma membrane might provide a critical link to intracellular tau stability. We focused on a neuron-specific proteasome complex localized to the neuronal plasma membrane (‘neuroproteasome’)11. While neuroproteasomes are conserved in vertebrates12, their physiological role and relevance to disease remain undefined.

Here, we demonstrate that neuroproteasome dysfunction actively drives the formation of AD-relevant PHF tau aggregates. Using selective membrane-impermeant inhibitors, we show that disrupting this plasma membrane-bound proteasome induces de novo aggregation of endogenous tau in primary neurons and in vivo (wild-type (WT) mice and mice expressing humanized tau). The resulting sarkosyl-insoluble aggregates exhibit key features of human AD PHFs, including characteristic ultrastructure and AD-relevant phosphorylation patterns. We also identify ApoE and its receptor Lrp1 as neuroproteasome interactors and show that neuroproteasome activity and membrane localization are modulated by ApoE isoforms and decline with age—two major risk factors for AD. We provide evidence from human tissues supporting the hypothesis that neuroproteasomes link ApoE genotype to tau proteostasis. Reducing neuroproteasome function lowers the threshold for aggregation in a genotype-specific manner, with ApoE4 neurons exhibiting heightened susceptibility and ApoE2 neurons being relatively protected. Together, our findings define neuroproteasomes as a distinct node of the proteostasis network that integrates genetic and aging-related risk to govern the pathological transition of tau in AD.

Results

Generating selective neuroproteasome inhibitors

Neuroproteasomes share the same essential subunits as cytoplasmic proteasomes, complicating genetic strategies to selectively perturb their localization or function. Instead, to reveal neuroproteasome function, we developed neuroproteasome-specific inhibitors based on epoxomicin, a covalent proteasome inhibitor with no measured off-target effects. The specificity of epoxomicin is based on the unique chemistry of the catalytic threonines of proteasome subunits β1, β2 and β5—the α-amino group of the threonine opens the C-terminal epoxide of epoxomicin, which forms the final inhibitory covalent morpholino adduct13. The catalytic residues of serine and cysteine proteases do not have α-amino groups, thereby rendering epoxomicin specific. Numerous N-terminally modified epoxomicin analogs retain proteasomal specificity without reported off-target effects14,15 and, crucially, epoxomicin only inhibits active proteasomes.

Previous work used N-terminal biotinylated epoxomicin (Biotin-Epox) to target proteasome subunits16. Reasoning that neuroproteasomes are exposed extracellularly11, we hypothesized that introducing a hydrophilic PEG linker between biotin and epoxomicin’s N terminus would drastically reduce cell permeability17, thereby rendering the inhibitor selective for neuroproteasomes. Initial screening identified impermeant biotin-PEG24-epoxomicin (iBEp; Fig. 1a and Extended Data Figs. 1a–g and 2a) as a lead candidate. iBEp selectively targets catalytic proteasome subunits, confirmed by fluorescent labeling with a Fluor-conjugated PEG24-epoxomicin and liquid chromatography–mass spectrometry (LC–MS)-based identification of iBEp-bound targets after streptavidin enrichment in primary mouse neurons and mouse brain (Fig. 1b, Extended Data Fig. 2b and Supplementary Table 1).

We extensively validated iBEp’s cell impermeability using multiple assays. iBEp showed no detectable permeability in the highly sensitive PAMPA-BBB assay18, being at least 2,500-fold less permeable than epoxomicin (Fig. 1c and Extended Data Fig. 2c). We also observed no iBEp permeability in the MDCK-MDR1 assay19 (Extended Data Fig. 2d) or into primary mouse neurons assessed by both biotin detection (Extended Data Fig. 2e,f) and LC–MS-based metabolite profiling (1 pmol and 1 pg limit of detection, respectively; Extended Data Fig. 2e–i). Importantly, iBEp added to primary mouse neurons selectively inhibited proteasomes in the membrane fraction but not the cytosol, confirming its activity at the target location (Fig. 1d and Extended Data Fig. 2g–j). LC–MS analyses also confirmed that iBEp is stable and is not cleaved in mouse brain or in neurons (Extended Data Fig. 2h–k).

Consistent with its impermeability, iBEp did not elicit conventional cellular responses to cytosolic proteasome inhibition, despite being an active inhibitor. Unlike epoxomicin, iBEp treatment did not cause accumulation of the ubiquitin-proteasome reporter GFPu20 (Fig. 1e), induce an increase in LC3-GFP levels21 (an indicator of autophagosome formation; Fig. 1f), trigger the proteasome bounceback response22 (Fig. 1g) or increase total intracellular ubiquitin conjugates or p62 levels23 in primary neurons (Fig. 1h and Extended Data Fig. 2a). Altogether, our extensive characterization of iBEp shows that it is a specific, stable, active and cell-impermeable neuroproteasome inhibitor.

To corroborate these findings with an independent tool, we developed Sulfo-MG132 (SulfoMG), modifying the reversible inhibitor MG132 (ref. 14), with a membrane-impermeant sulfonated biotin linker (Fig. 1i and Extended Data Fig. 3a–d). Physiochemical and cellular assays confirmed SulfoMG is also membrane impermeant, stable and active (Fig. 1j–m and Extended Data Fig. 3e–g). Biotin pulldowns to reveal SulfoMG-bound proteins revealed the catalytic proteasome subunits as well as some off-targets of MG132 (for example, calpains24; Supplementary Table 1). However, since these are all cytosolic targets, and SulfoMG is cell impermeable and less potent than MG132 (Extended Data Fig. 3g,h), we suggest that there are no off-targets of SulfoMG in intact cells.

Neuroproteasome inhibition induces endogenous sarkosyl-insoluble tau aggregates

To investigate the functions of neuroproteasomes in proteostasis, we treated primary mouse neurons with iBEp and identified proteins in detergent-soluble and detergent-insoluble fractions, representing soluble and aggregated species, by quantitative Tandem Mass Tag (TMT) proteomics (Fig. 2a). Neuroproteasome inhibition caused only 52 proteins to become significantly enriched in the sarkosyl-insoluble fraction, compared to over 250 proteins increasing in the soluble fraction (Fig. 2b,c, Extended Data Fig. 4a and Supplementary Table 2). Because of the potential importance of failed protein degradation resulting in protein aggregate formation without changing total protein levels, we identified proteins that showed significant and greater than log2-fold increases in the insoluble fraction with no change in the soluble fraction. This revealed only four proteins: Ckmt1, tau, Taf15 and Hspb1 (Fig. 2d). Notably, aggregated tau is a primary hallmark of AD, while Taf15 and Ckmt1 have been linked to tau tangles25,26.

Based on these data, we sought to test whether neuroproteasome inhibition indeed drives formation of endogenous sarkosyl-insoluble tau aggregates. We first confirmed a substantial increase in sarkosyl-insoluble tau upon iBEp treatment in primary mouse neurons by immunoblot (Fig. 2e). No such signal was seen in neurons obtained from tau-knockout (KO) mice (Extended Data Fig. 4b) or following treatment with the inactive iBEp analog biotin-PEG24-IleIleThr (identical to iBEp but lacking the active site epoxyketone warhead; Extended Data Fig. 4c), confirming specificity. Neuroproteasome inhibition similarly induced sarkosyl-insoluble aggregation of human tau in primary neurons from hTau-knockin (hTau-KI) mice (Fig. 2f and Extended Data Fig. 4d), which express all six human tau isoforms at endogenous levels without mutations27. As an orthogonal means of testing whether neuroproteasome inhibition could induce the accumulation of sarkosyl-insoluble tau, we coupled PEG24-epoxomicin with normal mouse immunoglobulin (IgG), which is too large and hydrophilic to penetrate across the plasma membrane (Fig. 2g). Similarly to iBEp, we found that IgG-Epox induces the accumulation of sarkosyl-insoluble tau in primary WT mouse neurons, compared to IgG-PEG24-IleIleThr (IgG-Epox without epoxyketone warhead) alone (Fig. 2g). Finally, we found that SulfoMG induces similar partitioning of hTau into the sarkosyl-insoluble fraction as iBEp in primary hTau-KI neurons (Fig. 2h).

Neuroproteasome inhibition by iBEp and SulfoMG induces tau aggregation, while total proteasome inhibition with the cell-permeant parent compounds epoxomicin and MG132 does not28,29, despite epoxomicin and MG132 inhibiting both cytosolic and neuroproteasomes. We hypothesized that cytosolic proteasome inhibition induces a compensatory response, which may clear inclusions. Consistent with this, co-treatment of iBEp and epoxomicin together does not induce insoluble tau, compared to iBEp alone (Extended Data Fig. 4e). We considered that cytosolic proteasome inhibition, and not neuroproteasome inhibition, resulted in compensation by the autophagy/lysosomal degradation system28–30 (Fig. 1f,m). Supporting our hypothesis, we found sarkosyl-insoluble tau from neurons co-treated with the lysosomal protease inhibitor leupeptin together with epoxomicin (Fig. 2i). We interpret these findings to mean that the lysosome is involved in clearing aggregates when cytosolic proteasomes are inhibited, thereby explaining why epoxomicin masks neuroproteasome inhibition-induced tau aggregate formation.

Neuroproteasome inhibition-induced tau aggregates are PHFs

Next, we examined whether neuroproteasome inhibition would induce the formation of sarkosyl-insoluble endogenous tau aggregates in vivo. Here, we stereotactically injected iBEp into the mouse hippocampus, a region that displays robust tau pathology in humans with AD and shows some of the earliest pathology in AD31. Based on membrane fractionation of hippocampal tissues, we did not detect permeability of iBEp in vivo (Extended Data Fig. 5a). iBEp injection resulted in a substantial induction of sarkosyl-insoluble tau in hippocampi from hTau-KI mice (Fig. 3a). There was no detectable signal in iBEp-injected hippocampi from tau-KO mice (Extended Data Fig. 5b).

AD-relevant tau aggregates are sarkosyl-insoluble tau PHFs as determined by electron microscopy32. To characterize the insoluble tau formed upon neuroproteasome inhibition, we analyzed sarkosyl-insoluble fractions of hippocampi from mice following 72 h of iBEp injection by electron microscopy. Depending on the staining protocol, we obtained both positively and negatively stained samples (Fig. 3b). Twisted filaments resembling PHFs were readily apparent by negative-stain electron microscopy analyses (Fig. 3b). As a demonstration, nine examples of filament-like structures were chosen from low-magnification images and highlighted, though many more examples are easily observable in these preparations, as seen in low magnification images (Fig. 3d and Extended Data Fig. 5c). These were confirmed to be tau PHFs by immunogold electron microscopy (Fig. 3c). We did not detect any straight filaments in our preparations. No twisted filaments could be detected in samples obtained from tau-KO animals, despite exhaustive searching at low and high magnification (Fig. 3d,e and Extended Data Fig. 5c,d). The maximal width of the tau PHFs formed by neuroproteasome inhibition was 25.9 nm ± 3.3 nm, while the crossover spacing was 87 nm ± 8.8 nm (Extended Data Fig. 5e).

Together, these results establish that neuroproteasome inhibition is sufficient to trigger the formation of PHFs from endogenous tau in the absence of genetic lesions or overexpression.

Neuroproteasome inhibition-induced tau aggregates have AD-like features

Neuroproteasome inhibition induces a shift of sarkosyl-insoluble tau that migrates at a higher molecular weight on an SDS–PAGE gel, at approximately 64 kDa (Fig. 2e–i and Extended Data Figs. 4c,d and 6a). Similar shifts in molecular weight of tau are observed in the AD brain and are a hallmark phenotype of multiple neurodegenerative disorders33, likely reflecting tau phosphorylation. Using quantitative phosphoproteomics we find a time-dependent increase in tau phosphorylation after iBEp treatment of primary mouse neurons at 4 of 26 identified sites, which correspond to human Ser202, Thr205, Thr217 and Ser404 (Fig. 4a and Supplementary Table 3). These sites are established markers of phosphorylated tau in the human AD brain4.

To validate these data, we performed immunostaining for phosphorylated tau in either hTau-KI neurons treated with iBEp or sections from hTau-KI mice stereotactically injected with iBEp. hTau-KI mice were stereotactically injected with iBEp and contralaterally injected with vehicle control. We validated the specificity of our antibodies and approach in both immunocytochemistry (Extended Data Fig. 6b,c) and immunohistochemistry (Extended Data Fig. 6d,e). Tau phosphorylation at Ser202/Thr205 was markedly elevated in iBEp-treated primary hTau-KI neurons (Extended Data Fig. 6f) and in hippocampi from mice injected with iBEp compared to the contralateral dimethylsulfoxide (DMSO) controls (Fig. 4b). We observed AT8+ (phospho-Ser202/Thr205) signal mislocalized to somatodendritic compartments in iBEp-injected hippocampi, which extend into the stratum radiatum and stratum moleculare (Fig. 4b). No tau Ser202/Thr205 phosphorylation was detectable in epoxomicin-treated primary neurons (Extended Data Fig. 6g) or in hTau-KI mice injected with epoxomicin compared to contralateral controls (Extended Data Fig. 6h). We reproduced the increase in mislocalized AT8+ dendrites in hTau-KI mice stereotactically injected with IgG-Epox compared to contralateral IgG-PEG24 controls (Fig. 4c).

Thioflavin S (ThioS) stains β-pleated sheets, including aggregated tau tangles and β-amyloid plaques. We conducted ThioS staining of sections from hTau-KI mice stereotactically injected with iBEp and contralaterally injected with vehicle control. After 72 h, we find a strong increase in ThioS staining from the CA1 region of hippocampi exposed to iBEp relative to contralateral controls (Fig. 4d). ThioS-positive aggregates appear as flame-like inclusions in both the CA1 pyramidal layer and the granule cell layer of the dentate gyrus (Fig. 4d,e and Extended Data Fig. 6i–k). Neuroproteasome inhibition-induced flame-like ThioS-positive aggregates colocalize with phosphorylated tau (AT8) in single z-plane images from neurons in the CA1 and dentate gyrus of the hippocampus (Fig. 4f,g). We also observed thread-like ThioS+ aggregates in the hippocampal neuropil (Fig. 4g and Extended Data Fig. 6i–m). Taken together with the biochemical and electron microscopy analyses, these results establish that neuroproteasome inhibition rapidly induces aggregation of endogenous tau into assemblies that resemble those observed in individuals with sporadic AD.

Neuroproteasomes associate with ApoE

To understand whether neuroproteasomes are part of the mechanism for tau aggregation in neurodegenerative diseases such as AD, we sought to gain insights into how neuroproteasomes are regulated. After extensive screening (Extended Data Fig. 7a–c), we generated a conditional knockin 20S–FLAG transgenic mouse line into the endogenous Psma3 locus (Fig. 5a). We generated mice that expressed FLAG-tagged proteasomes selectively in neurons in a Cre-dependent manner (Extended Data Fig. 7d,e). The FLAG tag does not affect the catalytic activity of the 26S or 20S proteasome relative to controls (Fig. 5b and Extended Data Fig. 7f). Moreover, we found that mice expressing 20S–FLAG appear indistinguishable from WT littermates, confirming that the endogenous FLAG tag on Psma3 is inert. The 20S–FLAG was efficiently incorporated into the neuroproteasome, as determined by surface biotinylation experiments in primary neurons and hippocampal tissue from 20S–FLAG transgenic mice (Fig. 5c and Extended Data Fig. 7g). To identify neuroproteasome interactors, we extracted membrane proteins from plasma membrane-enriched fractions (Extended Data Fig. 7h) from 20S–FLAG/Baf53b-Cre34 mouse brains followed by FLAG affinity isolation to extract neuroproteasomes and TMT-based IP–MS (Fig. 5d). Sixty-four proteins were enriched in the neuroproteasome fraction and depleted in the cytosolic fraction, including enrichment of ApoE and its receptor Lrp1 (Fig. 5d and Supplementary Table 4). We focused on ApoE given that the APOE allele influences the risk for tau aggregation8,9,35. We did not observe other ApoE receptors35, such as ApoER2, VLDLR and LDLR, in our proteomics analysis. We validated these proteomic data by affinity isolating FLAG-tagged neuroproteasomes as well as cytosolic FLAG-tagged proteasomes and observed co-purification of ApoE and Lrp1 with the FLAG-tagged neuroproteasomes (Fig. 5e). We only observed ApoE and Lrp1 after immunoprecipitating against FLAG from Cre-positive membrane fractions and not from membrane fractions of transgene controls with no Cre expression, cytosolic controls from Cre-positve or Cre-negative brains, or in mock immunoprecipitations (IPs; Fig. 5e).

ApoE isoforms differentially modulate neuroproteasome localization

The most significant genetic risk factor for sporadic AD is the ApoE4 isoform of the APOE gene, while the ApoE3 isoform is neutral and the ApoE2 isoform is considered protective10. Given the connection between tau aggregation and ApoE carrier status, we examined whether ApoE isoforms could regulate neuroproteasomes. We obtained human ApoE isoform knockin mice that each express one of three fully humanized ApoE isoforms36. Surface biotinylation and immunoblotting revealed a strong reduction of neuroproteasome surface localization in the hippocampus of ApoE4 KI mice, compared to ApoE3 KI and ApoE2 KI mice (Fig. 6a).

We next tested the role of ApoE receptors in modulating neuroproteasome localization using the well-established pan-ApoE receptor antagonist, receptor-associated protein (RAP)37 (Extended Data Fig. 8a). We incubated primary mouse neurons with purified RAP and measured surface protein levels using surface biotinylation and immunoblotting. Treatment with RAP rapidly decreased the surface localization of Lrp1, without modifying another membrane protein GluR1 (Extended Data Fig. 8b). We also find that RAP reduced the surface abundance of neuroproteasomes (Extended Data Fig. 8b).

ApoE is a lipoprotein that is released in a lipid-bound form by glia in the brain35. Therefore, we determined whether extracellular ApoE could modulate neuroproteasome membrane localization and if ApoE lipidation was important for this modulation. We conjugated recombinant ApoE isoforms with a mixture of POPC and cholesterol (PC) and purified ApoE lipoproteins, referred to as PC-rApoE2, PC-rApoE3 and PC-rApoE4 (Extended Data Fig. 8c), which resemble the endogenous ApoE lipoproteins observed in the human brain38. In primary mouse neurons from 20S–FLAG mice, we found that PC-ApoE4 reduces neuroproteasome membrane localization compared to PC-ApoE3, whereas PC-ApoE2 increases neuroproteasome membrane localization (Fig. 6b). We found no change in neuroproteasome levels with delipidated ApoE particles or lipoparticles alone, nor did we find PC-ApoE-dependent changes in other membrane proteins such as GluN1 (Fig. 6b). We found similar results in primary WT mouse neurons, suggesting that the observed ApoE-isoform-dependent effect on localization is not an artifact of the FLAG epitope tag on neuroproteasomes (Extended Data Fig. 8d).

We next performed surface labeling experiments to determine whether ApoE isoforms modified the subcellular distribution and localization of neuroproteasomes. We observed punctate neuroproteasome localization when feeding anti-FLAG antibodies in primary hippocampal neurons (Fig. 6c and Extended Data Fig. 8e). No staining was observed when feeding with antibodies against MAP2 or secondary antibodies alone (Extended Data Fig. 8e–h) or from neurons not transduced with Cre (Extended Data Fig. 8i). We observed that PC-ApoE2 increases neuroproteasome surface localization by nearly 2-fold and that PC-ApoE4 reduces neuroproteasome surface localization by nearly 3-fold compared to PC vehicle or PC-ApoE3 controls (Fig. 6c). Similarly to our biochemical analyses, delipidated ApoE did not modify neuroproteasome surface localization. These data recapitulate the observations from the hApoE-KI mice and indicate that extracellular lipidated ApoE isoforms can influence neuroproteasome localization.

Neuroproteasomes link ApoE and aging to tau proteostasis

Having established that neuroproteasome function is both critical for tau proteostasis and is directly modulated by ApoE isoforms, we next determined whether these ApoE-dependent effects dictate neuronal vulnerability to tau aggregation. We began by testing whether ApoE-dependent mislocalization of neuroproteasomes is observable in human postmortem AD tissues. We observed no disease-, genotype- or region-dependent changes in the cytosolic proteasome population (Extended Data Fig. 9a). We next monitored surface localization of neuroproteasomes by immunoblotting plasma membrane-enriched fractions from two different brain regions of human brains, Brodmann area 7 (BA7) and BA4, which were obtained from the same individual (Fig. 7a–d). BA7 has substantial amyloid plaque and tau tangle pathology as well as neuronal loss, whereas BA4 has little tau pathology or neurodegeneration, but still has amyloid plaques. The difference between high and low tau pathology regions across ApoE genotypes provides an opportunity to analyze the relationships between ApoE isoform, tau and neuroproteasome levels. We controlled for sample loading both by total protein and then by normalizing against Kv1.2, a protein whose expression has been reproducibly demonstrated as unchanged in the AD brain39–41. We observed that individuals with the APOE ε4/4 genotype exhibited significantly reduced neuroproteasome levels compared to APOE ε3/3 carriers, regardless of region (Fig. 7b). Neuroproteasome levels were lower in APOE ε3/3 individuals with AD compared to APOE ε3/3 controls, consistent with a disease-associated effect (Fig. 7b). When comparing all three groups—unaffected APOE ε3/3, AD APOE ε3/3 and AD APOE ε4/4—neuroproteasome levels were unchanged in BA4 (low tau pathology) between controls and AD APOE ε3/3, but were lower in BA7 (high tau pathology), consistent with an inverse relationship between neuroproteasome levels and tau pathology (Fig. 7b).

To determine the effects of genotype and tau pathology, we performed interaction analyses between region and genotype. These analyses pool all APOE ε3/3 carriers, regardless of disease status. These revealed that neuroproteasome reductions in APOE ε4/4 were present regardless of brain region compared to APOE ε3/3, but were stronger in regions with high tau pathology (Fig. 7c). To more specifically isolate the genotype from the disease-associated effects, we performed a difference-of-differences analysis between regions only from individuals with AD but between genotypes. We found the difference in neuroproteasomes between BA4 and BA7 was ~2-fold greater in APOE ε4/4 individuals (Fig. 7d), indicating a genotype-driven amplification of regional vulnerability. Because BA4 exhibits minimal tau pathology yet shows a genotype-dependent decrease, these findings collectively support that neuroproteasome levels are reduced by APOE ε4/4 independent of local tau burden, but are markedly lower in more pathologically affected regions.

While these findings underscore the impact of APOE genotype, aging represents the largest overall risk factor for AD6. Therefore, we next examined the effect of aging on neuroproteasome levels. We observed a progressive age-dependent reduction in membrane-localized neuroproteasomes in WT mice starting around 12 months (Fig. 7e). While aging thus represents a major risk factor impacting neuroproteasome levels, the striking differential modulation by ApoE isoforms (Figs. 6 and 7a–d) points to a more direct mechanism influencing genotype-specific risk. To determine if this ApoE modulation functionally alters susceptibility to tau pathology, we quantified tau aggregation as a function of neuroproteasome inhibition across a range of iBEp doses in primary neurons from mice expressing human tau alongside ApoE2, ApoE3 or ApoE4. We found no sarkosyl-insoluble tau aggregates without neuroproteasome inhibition in any line (Fig. 7f and Extended Data Fig. 9b). However, after iBEp treatment, tau aggregation exhibited a sharply genotype-dependent relationship to neuroproteasome activity. ApoE4 neurons exhibited insoluble tau accumulation when only ~20% of neuroproteasome activity is lost, whereas ApoE3 and ApoE2 neurons do not cross this threshold until ~60% and ~85% of activity are inhibited, respectively (Fig. 7f and Extended Data Fig. 9c,d). Consistent with this, we found that RAP, which reduces neuroproteasome localization at the plasma membrane similarly to ApoE4, also is not sufficient but renders neurons more susceptible to tau aggregation (Extended Data Fig. 9e). These findings imply that ApoE4 neurons operate with reduced reserve capacity to adapt to proteotoxic insults or age-related decline, whereas ApoE2 neurons possess higher reserves, relative to ApoE3s. The neuroproteasome may, therefore, serve as a critical determinant of neuronal susceptibility to tau pathology in an ApoE genotype-dependent manner.

Discussion

In this study, we identify the neuron-specific plasma membrane proteasome (‘neuroproteasome’) as a critical regulator of tau proteostasis, mechanistically linking it to the major AD risk factors, APOE genotype and aging. We demonstrate that selective neuroproteasome inhibition rapidly drives the de novo formation of endogenous tau into PHF aggregates. Furthermore, we show that ApoE isoforms differentially modulate neuroproteasome function and localization, establishing genotype-specific thresholds for tau aggregation. These findings carry important implications for understanding both tau pathogenesis and ApoE biology in the context of AD.

A central question in AD research is how PHF tau pathology forms. Our results offer one potential insight by identifying neuroproteasome disruption as sufficient to trigger pathological conversion from endogenous, WT tau. The rapid formation of PHFs, together with AD-relevant phosphorylation, suggests neuroproteasome impairment could be an early event rendering neurons vulnerable to subsequent pathogenic cascades. Identifying the relationships between neuroproteasomes and these mechanisms should be a central focus of future work. While the speed at which tau aggregates develop following neuroproteasome inhibition may be surprising given that tau pathology emerges after months in tau overexpression and genetic tauopathy models, there is compelling evidence that individual tangles in these models can form within 24 h in vivo42,43. While acute in vivo neuroproteasome inhibition does not fully replicate the chronic, widespread pathology of end-stage AD, it provides critical proof-of-principle for a direct link between neuroproteasome dysfunction and the formation of PHFs, a key feature of AD-relevant tau pathology. The appearance of pathology in specific neuronal populations might reflect differential underlying vulnerability or cell-to-cell variability in neuroproteasome function in vivo, which is not accurately quantifiable with the current tools available. How neuroproteasome inhibition-induced PHF formation relates to other important aspects of tau pathology, such as hyperphosphorylation, secretion, spreading, seeding and neurodegeneration, are important future directions.

Delineating the pathways by which ApoE isoforms confer dramatically different risks for AD is crucial for developing a mechanistic explanation for AD pathogenesis. Our findings provide a new mechanistic explanation for how APOE genotype influences tau pathology, a link that has been poorly understood. ApoE4-induced reduction of neuroproteasome levels is not sufficient to drive tau aggregation but requires a ‘second hit’ to sufficiently compromise neuroproteasome function under this threshold level (for example, by aging or potentially by other insults blunting neuroproteasome function). In contrast, ApoE2 neurons are afforded protection not only by higher neuroproteasome expression but also by a greater tolerance to its loss—a dual mechanism of resilience. Based on these data, we suggest ApoE isoforms help establish a neuron’s proteostatic reserve capacity by modulating neuroproteasomes. These observations hold in human tissues, where neuroproteasome levels are reduced in APOE ε4/4 carriers in multiple brain regions, and show even further reduction in regions with high tau pathology than those with low tau pathology. Future work should focus on how ApoE influences neuroproteasome localization through lipidation or other mechanisms, as well as the precise structural mechanism by which neuroproteasome membrane localization is modulated.

ApoE is likely one of multiple, yet-to-be-identified genetic mechanisms that can modulate neuroproteasome localization and, consequently, the neuronal capacity for tau proteostasis. The neuroproteasome interactors we found here will be an informative starting place for identifying and studying relevant mechanisms. These studies will likely gain insights into if and how neurons recognize and respond to neuroproteasome perturbations. Determining why neurons do not trigger canonical protective responses to neuroproteasome inhibition, and what if any responses are enacted, will likely be valuable. Overall, our findings highlight the importance of understanding genetic and cell biological networks that regulate neuroproteasomes, and other neuronal-specific principles of proteostasis, to achieve long-term neuronal health. The neuroproteasome thus emerges as a key molecular node integrating both genetic and age-related risk factors and helps explain differential susceptibility to AD pathology. Consequently, strategies aimed at preserving or enhancing neuroproteasome function, or mitigating the negative impact of ApoE4 on this pathway, represent potential new therapeutic avenues for AD and perhaps related tauopathies.

Synthesis of iBEp. Synthesis of Azido-PEG(n)-Epox. Azido-PEG(n)-Epox (where n = 1, 4, 8, 12 or 24) was synthesized using Fmoc solid-phase peptide synthesis, followed by coupling of the epoxy group in solution. All reactions were carried out at room temperature. Solid-phase peptide synthesis was carried out in a capped 6-ml solid-phase extraction tube with a frit and a metal stopcock. The tube was shaken by hand for 1-min washes and rotated on a tube rotator during incubation periods longer than 10 min. A vacuum trap was used to release the solution phase after each deprotecting, coupling and washing step. Washing steps were carried out between all deprotecting and coupling steps.

Around 100–200 mg of Fmoc-Thr(tBu)-Wang resin (ChemPep, 161702) was swelled in 3 ml of dimethylformamide (DMF) for a minimum of 1 h, then Fmoc deprotection was done by incubating the resin in 3 ml of 20% piperidine in DMF for 3 × 10 min, followed by 5 × 1-min washes. Next, 3 eq of Fmoc-Ile-OH (ChemPep, 101001) and 2.9 eq of hexafluorophosphate benzotriazole tetramethyl uronium (HBTU) was dissolved in 3 ml of DMF and added to the resin, followed by addition of 9 eq of N,N-diisopropylethylamine (DIEA). The coupling reaction was carried out for a minimum of 3 h, followed by 5 ×1-min washes. The Fmoc group was deprotected and the second Fmoc-Ile-OH was coupled using the same procedure as described above. Following this, 1.5 eq of Azido-PEG X-acid (X = 1, 4, 24) and 1.5 eq of HBTU was dissolved in 3 ml of DMF and added to the resin, before adding 4.5 eq of DIEA. After an overnight reaction period, the resin was washed with 3 ml DMF for 3 × 1 min, then with 2 ml dichloromethane for 2 × 1 min and left to dry on the vacuum trap for at least 30 min. The resin was then moved to a capped glass vial, and a cleavage solution containing 95% trifluoroacetic acid (TFA), 2.5% triisopropylsilane and 2.5% dichloromethane was added on top of the resin. The cleavage reaction occurred for 2 h with continuous stirring using a magnetic stir bar. Finally, the entire cleavage solution was passed through glass wool to filter out the resin, and the cleaved peptide was precipitated into cold diethyl ether. The precipitate was centrifuged, decanted, reconstituted in 3 ml of water and 100 µl of acetonitrile (ACN), then lyophilized.

The epoxy group was coupled to the Azido-PEG X-Ile-Ile-Thr-OH peptide in solution using the procedure described in ref. 45. In one glass vial (vial A), Azido-PEG X-Ile-Ile-Thr-OH (compound A) was dissolved in 1 ml of tetrahydrofuran (THF). The solution was purged with argon gas. Then, 2 eq of tert-butyldimethylsilyl chloride and 2.2 eq of imidazole was added to compound A in THF. The solution was purged with argon gas and left to run overnight. Next, 1.05 eq of hydroxybenzotriazole, 1.05 eq of HBTU and 3 eq of DIEA was added to vial A. The solution was purged with argon gas and left to react for 30 min. In a separate vial (vial B), 1.05 eq of (S)-2-Amino-4-methyl-1-((R)-2-methyloxiran-2-yl) pentan-1-one 2,2,2-trifluoroacetate (Combi-Blocks) and 3 eq of DIEA was dissolved in 1 ml THF. The solution was purged with argon gas and left to react for 5–10 min. Next, the content of vial B was transferred to vial A under argon gas, and the mixture was allowed to react for 4–5 h. The reaction was then concentrated using the SpeedVac vacuum concentrator. The concentrated mixture was reconstituted in 1 ml of methanol, then injected directly into a high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) system for purification, then lyophilized.

PAMPA conditions. All steps of the PAMPA were carried out according to the pION PAMPA Explorer Manual. The main principle of the assay is the incubation of the compound in a donor chamber (a well in the donor plate) with an aqueous buffer, which is separated from the acceptor chamber (a well in the acceptor plate) with another buffer by a phospholipid or hydrocarbon membrane fixed on filter support. After the test, concentrations in the corresponding donor and acceptor wells are measured and permeability is calculated.

The blood–brain barrier model was simulated using BBB-1 phospholipid mix. Clozapine, chlorpromazine (high permeability) and ranitidine (low permeability) were used as reference compounds. All compounds were tested in triplicates. Prisma HT buffer (pH 7.4) containing 50 µM test compounds (0.5% DMSO) was added into the donor plate wells. Brain sink buffer was added into each well of the acceptor plate. Incubation was done at room temperature for 4 h without stirring. After incubation, samples of the tested compounds were diluted 10-fold with ACN containing internal standard and were analyzed using the HPLC system coupled with a tandem mass spectrometer. Then the apparent permeability coefficient was calculated.

All test articles were detected using the Shimadzu HPLC system including vacuum degasser, gradient pumps, reverse-phase HPLC column, column oven and autosampler. The HPLC system was coupled with a tandem mass spectrometer API 3000 (PE Sciex, LC–MS-1SX-1165). The TurboIonSpray ion source was used in positive ion mode. Acquisition and analysis of the data were performed using Analyst 1.6.3 software (PE Sciex).

(1)

VD – volume of transport buffer in donor compartment (0.3 ml); A – surface area of the lipids in the insert (effective growth area of the insert, 0.3 cm²); t – time of the assay, seconds; Cdon – final concentration of test compound in the donor compartment; Cref – starting concentration of test compound in the donor compartment; εa – apparent filter porosity, equals to 0.76; RM – membrane mass retention, calculated as

$$R_M = \left(1-\frac{C_{\mathrm{don}}+C_{\mathrm{acc}}}{C_{\mathrm{ref}}}\right) \times 100\%$$

MDCK conditions. Canine MDR1 knockout, human MDR1 knockin MDCKII cells (MDR1-MDCKII; Sigma-Aldrich, MTOX1303) were cultured in 75-cm² flasks to 80–90% confluence according to the manufacturer’s recommendations in a humidified atmosphere at 37 °C and 5% CO2. Cells were detached with trypsin/EDTA solution and resuspended in the cell culture medium to a final concentration of 4 × 105 cells per ml. Then, 400 µl of the cell suspension was added to each well of the HTS Multiwell Insert System and 25 ml of prewarmed complete medium was added to the feeder tray. Confluent MDR1-MDCKII monolayers expressing P-gp were obtained after 4–5 days after seeding. Integrity of the cell monolayers was determined by measuring the transepithelial electrical resistance using an epithelial voltammeter (Millipore, MERS 000 01). Mature MDR1-MDCKII cell monolayer exhibited the transepithelial electrical resistance (TEER) values > 100 Ω × cm². The 24-well insert plate was removed from its feeder tray and placed in a new sterile 24-well transport analysis plate. The inserts were washed with PBS after medium aspiration.

To determine the rate of compounds transport in the apical (A)-to-basolateral (B) direction, 300 µl of the test compound dissolved in transport buffer (9.5 g l−1 HBSS and 0.35 g l−1 NaHCO3 with 0.81 mM MgSO4, 1.26 mM CaCl2, 25 mM HEPES, pH adjusted to 7.4) was added into the filter wells; 1,000 µl of transport buffer was added to transport analysis plate wells.

Ketoprofen, atenolol, quinidine and digoxin were used as reference compounds.

To determine transport rates in the basolateral (B)-to-apical (A) direction, 1,000 µl of the test compound solutions was added into the wells of the transport analysis plate, and the wells in the filter plate were filled with 300 µl of buffer (apical compartment). The final concentration of the test compounds was 1 µM.

The effect of the inhibitor on the P-gp-mediated transport of the tested compounds was assessed by determining the bidirectional transport in the presence or absence of cyclosporine A. The MDR1-MDCKII cells were preincubated for 30 min at 37 °C with 10 µM of cyclosporine A in both apical and basolateral compartments. After removal of the preincubation medium, the test compounds (final concentration, 1 µM) with cyclosporine A (10 µM) in transport buffer were added in donor wells, while the receiver wells were filled with the appropriate volume of transport buffer with 10 µM cyclosporine A.

The plates were incubated for 90 min at 37 °C under continuous shaking at 100g. After this, 75-µl aliquots were taken from the donor and receiver compartments for LC–MS/MS analysis. All samples were mixed with two volumes of ACN followed by protein sedimentation by centrifuging at 10,000g for 10 min. Supernatants were analyzed using an HPLC system coupled with a tandem mass spectrometer.

All solutions of test and reference compounds were prepared manually, and further manipulations with the solutions were performed with automation using Opentrons.

$$P_{\mathrm{app}} = \frac{V_A}{\mathrm{Area} \times \mathrm{Time}} \times \frac{[\mathrm{drug}]_{\mathrm{acc}}}{[\mathrm{drug}]_{\mathrm{initial},d}}$$
(2)

To identify the P-gp substrate, the P-gp inhibitor was added to the incubation medium. A decrease of the efflux ratio in the presence of an inhibitor indicates the compound is a P-gp substrate.

MS analysis. Peptide-spectrum matches (PSMs) were adjusted to a 1% FDR. PSM filtering was performed using a linear discriminant analysis. To quantify the TMT-based reporter ions in the datasets, the summed signal-to-noise ratio for each TMT channel was obtained and found the closest matching centroid to the expected mass of the TMT reporter ion (integration tolerance of 0.003 Da). Proteins were quantified by summing reporter ion counts across all matching PSMs. PSMs with poor quality, or isolation specificity less than 0.7, or with TMT reporter summed signal-to-noise ratios that were less than 100 or 150 or had no MS3 spectra were excluded from quantification.

The list of quantified proteins exported from Proteome Discoverer 2.1 was utilized as the input for our differential expression analysis. The raw values were organized in a matrix where each column represented a sample and each row a protein. To normalize the raw expression values, we began by log2 transforming the matrix with a + 1 for computation. Then we median polished the log-transformed values by subtracting the row median from each row, followed by the subtraction of the column median from each column. The resulting normalized expression values for each sample appeared normally distributed and were comparable across samples.

For the detection of differential regulation, we followed the recommendation outline in ref. 50. An empirical Bayes method was used on the normalized matrix to detect differences between the iBEp-treated group compared to DMSO. The empirical Bayes method shrinks individual protein’s sample variance toward a pooled estimate, and creates a more stable and powerful inference in differential protein abundance detection.

For the selection of the colors in the heat map, we carried out feature-scaling of the normalized expression values on a peptide-by-peptide basis. For each gene, this process assigns the largest expression a value of 1, and the smallest expression a value of 0. The remaining values are scaled between 0 and 1 based on where they are relative to the largest and smallest expression values. These were then normalized to DMSO. For instance, a feature-scaled value of 0.5 represents an expression level that is halfway between the lowest expression and the highest expression observed for a gene. In other words, this sample’s expression is 50% of the maximum fold change away from the lowest and the highest expression values at this gene.

From quantitative MS results, proteins were selected for bioinformatic comparisons based on the relative changes in quantities between iBEp and DMSO control treatments. Proteins with greater than a 25% increase in quantities under iBEp treatment over the control were categorized as ‘increased’. The samples were prepared following the RC DC user manual and the absorbance was read at 750 nm on the Molecular Devices SpectraMax iD5 Microplate Reader. Gels were run using the standard western blot protocol either on 10% Criterion TGX gels (Bio-Rad, 5671034) or on 10% gels made in the laboratory. All antibodies were made up in a blocking buffer (5% BSA in 0.1% TBST). Western blots were imaged on a LiCor Odyssey M Imager. Images were quantified using ImageStudio by standard densitometry analysis, which accounts for a large range of signal intensities using quantitative IR secondaries. A rectangle of the same area was drawn for each band for a given gel and signal intensities were output by ImageStudio.

Cellular fractionation for plasma membrane enrichment. Cellular fractionation experiments were performed as previously described11. For cellular fractionation experiments with primary neurons, cultured neurons were lysed in a hypotonic lysis buffer (20 mM Tris pH 7.4, 5 mM NaCl, 2 mM MgCl2, 0.2 mM CaCl2, complete protease inhibitor cocktail tablet (Roche, 11697498001)) supplemented with 0.1% GDN and adjusted to 100 mM NaCl and collected. Nuclei were pelleted at 400g, heavy membranes were pelleted at 5,000g, and the supernatant containing plasma membrane-enriched samples was pelleted at 75,000g for 30 min. Pelleted membranes were washed twice by homogenizing in lysis buffer and re-pelleted. Following two washes, membranes were collected and prepared for SDS–PAGE. For cellular fractionation with brain tissue, whole tissue from mouse brain was homogenized in lysis buffer (20 mM Tris pH 7.4, 100 mM NaCl, 2 mM MgCl2, 0.2 mM CaCl2, complete protease inhibitor cocktail tablet). Nuclei were pelleted at 400g, and the pellet was washed in lysis buffer and re-pelleted. The supernatant containing membranes was brought up to 0.1% GDN, heavy membranes were pelleted at 5,000g, and the supernatant containing plasma membrane-enriched samples was pelleted at 75,000g for 30 min. Pelleted membranes were rinsed and then resuspended and pelleted, and this was repeated three times.

Surface biotinylation and streptavidin pulldown. Surface biotin labeling was performed as previously described11. For surface biotinylation with hApoE-KI mice, brains were obtained from six ApoE2 KI mice (3 male and 3 female), six ApoE3 KI mice (3 male and 3 female) and ApoE4 KI mice (3 male and 3 female) at ~12 months of age. For surface biotinylation with 20S–FLAG mice, four mice were used. Mouse brain tissue was recovered in cutting artificial cerebropsinal fluid (195 mM sucrose, 10 mM NaCl, 15 mM glucose, 26 mM NaHCO3, 2.5 mM KCl, 1.25 mM NaH₂PO₄, 0.5 mM CaCl2 and 1 mM MgCl2, bubbled with carbogen) at 4 °C with light agitation for 30 min before biotin labeling. Mouse brain tissue and cultured neurons were washed in pH 8.0 cold PBSCM (Gibco, 10010023) and incubated in 0.5 mg ml−1 Sulfo-NHS-LC-Biotin (Thermo Fisher) dissolved in PBSCM at 4 °C for 10 min. The labeling reaction was quenched in 50 mM glycine in two sequential 10-min incubations at 4 °C. Mouse brain tissue and cultured neurons were collected and homogenized in RIPA buffer (50 mM Tris pH 7.4, 250 mM NaCl, 1% Triton X-100, 0.5% sodium deoxycholate, 0.1% SDS, 5 mM EDTA, complete protease inhibitor cocktail tablet (Roche, 10010023)). Samples were pelleted at 20,000g for 10 min at 4 °C and the supernatant was taken for streptavidin pulldown. For streptavidin pulldowns, lysate was incubated with high-capacity streptavidin agarose beads (Thermo Fisher, 20353) overnight and washed five times with RIPA buffer before elution in SDS sample buffer supplemented with 25 mM biotin.

LLVY-based proteasome activity assay and ATPase assay. Chymotrypsin-like catalytic activity was assessed using a Suc-LLVY-AMC activity assay. Lysate was collected and diluted in activity assay buffer (25 mM Tris pH 7.4, 2.5 mM MgCl2 and 10 mM NaCl) supplemented with 2 mM ATP. A total of 10 mM Suc-LLVY-AMC (Enzo Life Sciences, BML-P802) was added to the diluted lysate, and proteasomal activity was assessed ±10 µM epoxomicin by measuring fluorescence intensity at 460 nm. ATPase assays were performed using the ATPase Assay Kit (Abcam, ab234055), according to manufacturer instructions.

IP by affinity beads. IP was performed in a batch format. For FLAG IP from the membrane protein extract, ChromoTek DYKDDDDK Fab-Trap Agarose resin (Proteintech, hfa) was equilibrated with LSB buffer. The extract from each brain along with about 75 µl of beads was added to equilibrated beads and incubated with end-to-end rotation. The beads were washed and proteins were eluted with 2× SDS sample loading buffer.

FLAG IP from cytosolic fractions were performed similarly with the following modifications. About 50 µl of beads slurry was equilibrated in the PD buffer (20 mM Tris-HCl, 100 mM NaCl, 2 mM MgCl2, 0.5 mM ATP) for each IP reaction. The IP reaction was then incubated and proteins were eluted with a 2× SDS sample loading buffer. UBL IPs from cytosolic fractions were performed using UBPBio Rapid 26S proteasome Purification kit (UBPBio, J4310). The affinity beads were prepared by combining GST-Ubl with equilibrated glutathione agarose beads slurry for each IP reaction.

RAP purification. Stable RAP expression plasmid was obtained from Kerafast (EMD008) and was transformed into T7 Express lysY Competent Escherichia coli (NEB C3010I). E. coli containing the plasmid were cultured, induced with isopropyl β-D-1-thiogalactopyranoside (Hello Bio, HB3941), and incubated37. Cultures were pelleted and resuspended in resuspension buffer (50 mM Tris-HCl, 50 mM EDTA, 15% sucrose, pH 8.0). Cells were lysed on ice in 1 mg ml−1 lysozyme (Thermo Fisher, 89833) and 1:1,000 DNase (Worthington Biochemical, 9003-98-9). Around 0.02% Triton X-100 (vol/vol) and complete protease inhibitor cocktail tablets (Roche, 04693116001) were mixed into the lysate. Lysate was passed consecutively through 18- and 25-gauge needles and centrifuged. The supernatant was removed and incubated with Glutathione Sepharose resin (Cytiva, 17075601). Beads were packed into a gravity column and washed with five column volumes in buffer A (20 mM HEPES, 100 mM KCl, 0.2 mM EDTA, 5% glycerol and complete protease inhibitor cocktail tablets). RAP-GST was eluted in buffer A with 25 mM glutathione (Fisher Scientific, AAJ6216606). Fractions confirmed by Coomassie were dialyzed in a dialysis buffer (20 mM HEPES, 50 mM NaCl and 5% glycerol at pH 7.4) and incubated with Thrombin beads (Innovative Research, IHUTHRB100UG). Beads were removed and RAP was purified using an AKTA pure with a Superdex 200 increase column (Cytiva, 28990944) in SEC buffer (150 mM NaCl, 0.2 mM EDTA pH 8, and 10% glycerol). Fractions confirmed by Coomassie were concentrated using a 30-kDa molecular-weight-cutoff filter and concentration was assessed using a BCA assay (Thermo Fisher, 23225). RAP was stored in single-use aliquots at −80 °C.

Lipidated ApoE particles. Lipidated ApoE2, ApoE3 and ApoE4 (PC-ApoE) were prepared from recombinant ApoE2, ApoE3 and ApoE4 (AlexoTech, AE-100-50, AE-101-50 and AEN-102-10, respectively) as previously described56. Samples were purified after lipidation using superose 6 increase GL 10/300 columns using buffer A (20 mM phosphate buffer, 50 mM NaCl, pH 7.4) at a flow rate of 0.5 ml min−1. To show negative-stain images of the ApoE isoforms lipidated with POPC and cholesterol, copper grids with carbon film were glow discharged and incubated with 30 µl of sample. Samples were then washed three times with water and then stained with uranyl formate. Grids were blotted with a filter paper and imaged with a JOEL TEM microscope at a magnification of 50,000 (0.212492 nm per pixel).

Antibody feeding. Antibody feeding was performed as previously described57. On DIV13, neurons were treated with 400 nM PC-ApoE. Twenty-four hours later, hippocampal neurons were washed with PBSCM. On ice, hippocampal neurons were treated with mouse anti-FLAG M2 antibodies (1:100 dilution) for treatment conditions, and additionally with Chicken anti-MAP2 antibodies (1:100 dilution) and Rabbit anti-GluR1 antibodies (1:100 dilution) for controls. Critically, all antibody feeding experiments are always performed with negative controls each time, where neurons are fed with anti-MAP2 to ensure no intracellular labeling is observed. This ensures that cultures are healthy—if signal is ever observed using negative controls, samples are no longer considered reliable and are discarded. Antibodies were diluted in PBSCM supplemented with 1% BSA and incubated on live neurons. Antibodies were washed and neurons were rinsed in PBSCM. Neurons were fixed in 4% PFA (Electron Microscopy Sciences, 15710)/4% sucrose and samples were blocked in 1× GDB (0.2 mM NaPO4, 0.2% gelatin, 0.8 M NaCl and 0.1% Triton X-100). Chicken anti-MAP2 primary antibody was diluted as noted in 1× GDB and coverslips treated with PC-ApoE were incubated in primary dilutions. Coverslips were washed, incubated in secondary antibodies diluted at 1:1,000 in 1× GDB and washed again. Neurons were mounted on glass slices and imaged using a CSU-W1 SoRa confocal microscope. Images are representative maximal z-projections of multiple optical sections. Images were quantified using ImageJ by integrated density analysis.

Human samples. Human samples were obtained from MassGeneral Institute for Neurodegenerative Disease. The use of postmortem brain tissue was approved by the Harvard University Institutional Review Board and the Columbia University ADRC Brain Bank and associated Institutional Review Board. Informed consent was obtained from all participants. The authors did not have access to any identifying personal information. AD disease neuropathologic change rating was made using three criteria. Blind assessment for density of neuritic beta amyloid, distribution of beta amyloid plaques and Braak tau stage were conducted using the scoring system recommended by the Consortium to Establish a Registry for Alzheimer’s Disease58, Thal Score59 and Bielchowsky’s silver stain60 along with total tau immunostaining, respectively. According to the Braak tau pathway, the following brain regions were collected from either unaffected ApoE3/3 controls or individuals with Braak V ApoE3/3 or Braak V ApoE4/4. A full list of deidentified information about the participant list is presented in Supplementary Table 5.

We obtained material from BA4 (primary motor area) and BA7 (parietal associative area). We analyzed these regions as a means of untangling how neuroproteasome localization changes in a limited survey of severe tau pathology (BA7) versus less severe tau pathology (BA4) containing regions of the brain. Tissue from BA7 contained severe tangle and amyloid pathology as well as neuronal loss, whereas tissue from BA4 had moderate amyloid pathology but not tangle accumulation or neuronal loss. We fractionated these samples to remove nuclei, mitochondria and cytosol, enriching for plasma membranes and detergent-insoluble proteins. We monitored the success of our fractionation using LLVY-based proteasome activity assays out of the supernatant of our washes and continued washing our plasma membrane preparations until cytosolic proteasomes were undetectable.

The brains were sliced, flash frozen and dissected from BA4 and BA7 to be homogenized in lysis buffer (50 mM HEPES, 0.1 mM CaCl2 and 2 mM MgCl2, 100 mM NaCl) supplemented with fresh 1 mM ATP and 0.1% GDN (Anatrace, GDN101 - GDN). Nuclei, mitochondria and heavy membranes enriched in endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi membranes were pelleted at 5,000g and then 10,000g and washed several times. The supernatant containing the membranes was pelleted at 75,000g, for 30 min and the supernatant was taken as cytosol. Pelleted membranes were washed by homogenizing in lysis buffer and re-pelleted. After each wash, the supernatant was analyzed by LLVY-based proteasome activity assay to assess cleanliness of the prep. Following washes, plasma membrane-enriched and cytosolic fractions were prepared for MS and SDS–PAGE.

Laboratory animalsWildtype mice (C57BL/6J, stock number 027 from Charles River Laboratories) and mice transgenic for 20S-FLAG flox/flox (lines designed by Ramachandran lab, created by Biocytogen, reported in this manuscript), BAF53b Cre (RRID: IMSR_JAX:027826), Tau knockout (RRID: IMSR_JAX:007251), LC3-GFP, hTau KI (obtained from Abid Hussaini through transfer from RIKEN, Saito et al 2019 JBC), ApoE2 KI, ApoE3 KI, and ApoE4 KI (Huynh et al 2019, Mol Neurodeg) were used in this study. Non-sibling 20S-FLAGflox/WT mice were crossed to generate a homozygous 20S-FLAGflox/flox line. 20S-FLAGflox/flox were bred with neuronal Cre driver line BAF53b Cre to generate a 20S-FLAGflox/flox BAF53 line which expresses 20S-flag in neurons. hTau KI mice were crossed with ApoE2 KI, ApoE3 KI, and ApoE4 KI mice and homozygosed to generate homozygous hTau/ApoE2 KI, hTau/ApoE3 KI, and hTau/ApoE4 KI lines. Subsequent generations were confirmed by genotyping. Mice were genotyped using ear punches from weaning-age mice and tail snips from P0-1 pups post-mortem for primary neuron cultures. For the majority of experiments, mice were euthanized with carbon dioxide-induced anoxia and decapitated as a secondary method of euthanasia.
Wild animalsStudy did not involve wild animals
Reporting on sexFor all in vivo experiments, sex was considered a variable and was reported in figures and figure legends. All analyses were sex balanced where possible, only rare in vivo experiments were not due to limitations on transgenic mice available. For primary cultures, sex was not determined for E16 or P0-1 pups prior to dissection, cultures were mixed sex.
Field-collected samplesStudy did not involve samples collected from the field.
Ethics oversightAll animal procedures were performed under protocols compliant and approved by the Institutional Animal Care and Use Committees of Columbia University Medical Center.
