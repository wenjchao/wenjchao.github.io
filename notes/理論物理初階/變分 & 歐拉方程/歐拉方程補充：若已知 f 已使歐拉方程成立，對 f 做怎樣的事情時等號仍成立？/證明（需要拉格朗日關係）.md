# 證明（需要拉格朗日關係）

## 內文
$$
\frac{\partial (f_0(x,y,y')+\frac{d}{dx}f_{add}(x,y))}{\partial y}- \frac{d}{dx} (\frac{\partial f_0(x,y,y')+\frac{d}{dx}f_{add}(x,y)}{\partial y'}) \\= \frac{\partial f_0}{\partial y} +\frac{\partial \frac{d}{dx}f_{add}}{\partial y} - \frac{d}{dx} (\frac{\partial f_0}{\partial y'})-\frac{d}{dx} (\frac{\partial \frac{d}{dx}f_{add}}{\partial y'})\\=\frac{\partial \frac{d}{dx}f_{add}}{\partial y} - \frac{d}{dx} (\frac{\partial \frac{d}{dx}f_{add}}{\partial y'}) \\=\frac{\partial f'_{add}}{\partial y} - \frac{d}{dx} (\frac{\partial f'_{add}}{\partial y'}) \\ = \frac{\partial f'_{add}}{\partial y} - \frac{d}{dx} (\frac{\partial f_{add}}{\partial y}) \\ = 0
$$

- 4 → 5：符號改寫 $\frac{d}{dx}f_{add}(x,y) = f'_{add}(x,y)$
- 5 → 6：拉格朗日關係 (1)（前提是 $f_{add}$ 不顯含 $y'$ ）
- 6 → 7：拉格朗日關係 (2)（前提是 $f_{add}$ 不顯含 $y'$ ）
