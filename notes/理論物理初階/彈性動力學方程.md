# 彈性動力學方程

## 內文
1. 每個質元中所蘊含的動能 $T = \frac{1}{2} m (\frac{\partial u}{\partial t})^2$
   ⇒ **<u>動能密度</u>** $\overline T = \frac{1}{2}\rho(\frac{\partial u}{\partial t})^2$ ，其中 $\rho$ 表示密度
2. 每個質元中所蘊含的彈力位能密度 $\overline U =\frac{1}{2}\lambda\delta^2+\mu(\epsilon_x^2+\epsilon_y^2+\epsilon_z^2 + \frac{\gamma_{xy}^2}{2}+ \frac{\gamma_{yz}^2}{2}+ \frac{\gamma_{xz}^2}{2})$
   因為$\begin{bmatrix} \epsilon_x & \frac{1}{2}\gamma_{xy} & \frac{1}{2}\gamma_{xz} \\ \frac{1}{2}\gamma_{yx} & \epsilon_y & \frac{1}{2}\gamma_{yz} \\ \frac{1}{2}\gamma_{zx} & \frac{1}{2} \gamma_{zy} & \epsilon_z \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x} & \frac{1}{2}(\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial u_x}{\partial y}) & \frac{1}{2}(\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial u_x}{\partial z}) \\ \frac{1}{2}(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_y}{\partial x} ) & \frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y} & \frac{1}{2}(\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_y}{\partial z}) \\ \frac{1}{2}(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial z} + \frac{\partial u_z}{\partial x}) & \frac{1}{2} (\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial z} + \frac{\partial u_z}{\partial y})& \frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z} \end{bmatrix}$，因此可以把位能密度 $\overline U$ 用 u 來表示，寫成以下形式：
   $$
   \overline U =\cfrac{1}{2}\lambda\delta^2+\mu(\epsilon_x^2+\epsilon_y^2+\epsilon_z^2 + \cfrac{\gamma_{xy}^2}{2}+ \cfrac{\gamma_{yz}^2}{2}+ \cfrac{\gamma_{xz}^2}{2}) \\=\cfrac{\lambda}{2}(\epsilon_x+\epsilon_y+\epsilon_z)^2+\mu(\epsilon_x^2+\epsilon_y^2+\epsilon_z^2 + \cfrac{\gamma_{xy}^2}{2}+ \cfrac{\gamma_{yz}^2}{2}+ \cfrac{\gamma_{xz}^2}{2}) \\=(\cfrac{\lambda}{2} + \mu)(\epsilon_x^2+\epsilon_y^2+\epsilon_z^2) +\lambda(\epsilon_x\epsilon_y + \epsilon_y\epsilon_z+ \epsilon_x\epsilon_z) + \cfrac{\mu}{2}(\gamma_{xy}^2 + \gamma_{yz}^2+\gamma_{xz}^2)\\=(\cfrac{\lambda}{2} + \mu)[(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x})^2+(\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y})^2+(\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z})^2]+\lambda(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y}\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z}+ \frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z}) + \cfrac{\mu}{2}[(\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial u_x}{\partial y})^2 + (\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_y}{\partial z})^2+(\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial u_x}{\partial z})^2] \\ = \cfrac{\lambda + \mu}{2}[(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x})^2+(\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y})^2+(\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z})^2]+\lambda(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y}\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z}+ \frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z}) + \cfrac{\mu}{2}[(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x})^2+(\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y})^2+(\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z})^2 +(\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial x})^2 + (\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial y})^2 + (\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial y})^2 +( \frac{\partial u_y}{\partial z})^2+(\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial x})^2 + (\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial z})^2 + 2\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial x} \frac{\partial u_x}{\partial y} + 2\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial y} \frac{\partial u_y}{\partial z}+2\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial z}] \\ = \cfrac{\lambda + \mu}{2}[(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x})^2+(\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y})^2+(\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z})^2]+\lambda(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y}\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z}+ \frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z}) + \cfrac{\mu}{2}[(\nabla u_x)^2 + (\nabla u_y)^2+(\nabla u_z)^2] + \mu(\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial x} \frac{\partial u_x}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_z}{\partial y} \frac{\partial u_y}{\partial z}+\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial z})
   $$
3. 計算出彈性體的拉格朗日量密度：
   $$
   \overline L = \overline T - \overline U \\= \frac{1}{2}\rho[(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial t})^2 + (\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial t})^2 + (\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial t})^2] \\- \frac{\lambda + \mu}{2}[(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x})^2+(\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y})^2+(\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z})^2]\\-\lambda(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y}\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z}+ \frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z}) \\- \frac{\mu}{2}[(\nabla u_x)^2 + (\nabla u_y)^2+(\nabla u_z)^2] \\- \mu(\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial x} \frac{\partial u_x}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_z}{\partial y} \frac{\partial u_y}{\partial z}+\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial z})
   $$
   因為此時的 $u(x,y,z,t)$ 有四個自變量，因此原拉格朗日方程
   $$
   \frac{d}{dt}(\frac{\partial L}{\partial (\frac{\partial q_j}{\partial t})}) - \frac{\partial L}{\partial q_j} = f
   $$
   應該要改寫為
   $$
   \begin{cases} \cfrac{\partial }{\partial x}[\cfrac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x})}] + \cfrac{\partial }{\partial y}[\cfrac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial y})}]+\cfrac{\partial }{\partial z}[\cfrac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial z})}]+\cfrac{\partial }{\partial t}[\cfrac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial t})}]- \cfrac{\partial \overline L}{\partial u_x} = \overline f_x\\  \cfrac{\partial }{\partial x}[\cfrac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial x})}] + \cfrac{\partial }{\partial y}[\cfrac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y})}]+\cfrac{\partial }{\partial z}[\cfrac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial z})}]+\cfrac{\partial }{\partial t}[\cfrac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial t})}]- \cfrac{\partial \overline L}{\partial u_y} = \overline f_y\\ \cfrac{\partial }{\partial x}[\cfrac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial x})}] + \cfrac{\partial }{\partial y}[\cfrac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial y})}]+\cfrac{\partial }{\partial z}[\cfrac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z})}]+\cfrac{\partial }{\partial t}[\cfrac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_z}{\partial t})}]- \cfrac{\partial \overline L}{\partial u_z} = \overline f_z \end{cases}
   $$
   代入 $\overline L$（以第一式 $u_x$ 為例）：
   $$
   \begin{cases} \frac{\partial }{\partial x}[\frac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x})}] = -(\lambda + \mu)(\frac{\partial^2 u_x}{(\partial x)^2})- \lambda \frac{\partial }{\partial x}(\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z})-\mu(\frac{\partial^2 u_x}{(\partial x)^2}) =  -\mu(\frac{\partial^2 u_x}{(\partial x)^2} + \frac{\partial^2 u_x}{(\partial x)^2}) - \lambda \frac{\partial }{\partial x}(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x} +\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z})  \\ \frac{\partial }{\partial y}[\frac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial y})}] = -\mu(\frac{\partial^2 u_x}{(\partial y)^2}) - \mu(\frac{\partial^2 u_x}{\partial x\partial y}) = -\mu(\frac{\partial^2 u_x}{(\partial y)^2} + \frac{\partial^2 u_x}{\partial x\partial y})\\\frac{\partial }{\partial z}[\frac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial z})}] =-\mu(\frac{\partial^2 u_x}{(\partial z)^2}) - \mu(\frac{\partial^2 u_x}{\partial x\partial z}) = -\mu(\frac{\partial^2 u_x}{(\partial z)^2} + \frac{\partial^2 u_x}{\partial x\partial z})\\\frac{\partial }{\partial t}[\frac{\partial \overline L}{\partial (\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial t})}] = \rho\frac{\partial^2 u_x}{(\partial t)^2}\\  - \frac{\partial \overline L}{\partial u_x}  = 0\end{cases}
   $$
   綜合起來：
   $\begin{cases} \rho\frac{\partial^2 u_x}{(\partial t)^2}-\mu(\frac{\partial^2 u_x}{(\partial x)^2} +\frac{\partial^2 u_x}{(\partial y)^2}  + \frac{\partial^2 u_x}{(\partial z)^2} ) - (\lambda + \mu) \frac{\partial }{\partial x}(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x} +\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z}) = \overline f_x\\ \rho\frac{\partial^2 u_y}{(\partial t)^2}-\mu(\frac{\partial^2 u_y}{(\partial x)^2} +\frac{\partial^2 u_y}{(\partial y)^2}  + \frac{\partial^2 u_y}{(\partial z)^2} ) - (\lambda + \mu) \frac{\partial }{\partial y}(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x} +\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z}) = \overline f_y\\\rho\frac{\partial^2 u_z}{(\partial t)^2}-\mu(\frac{\partial^2 u_z}{(\partial x)^2} +\frac{\partial^2 u_z}{(\partial y)^2}  + \frac{\partial^2 u_z}{(\partial z)^2} ) - (\lambda + \mu) \frac{\partial }{\partial z}(\frac{\partial u_x}{\partial x} +\frac{\partial u_y}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_z}{\partial z}) = \overline f_z \end{cases}$
   ⇒ $\begin{cases} \rho\frac{\partial^2 u_x}{(\partial t)^2}=\mu\nabla^2 u_x + (\lambda + \mu) \frac{\partial \delta}{\partial x} + \overline f_x \\\rho\frac{\partial^2 u_y}{(\partial t)^2}=\mu\nabla^2 u_y + (\lambda + \mu) \frac{\partial \delta}{\partial y} + \overline f_y\\\rho\frac{\partial^2 u_z}{(\partial t)^2}=\mu\nabla^2 u_z + (\lambda + \mu) \frac{\partial \delta}{\partial z} + \overline f_z \end{cases}$ ⇒ 將三式綜合成向量形式 ⇒
   $$
   \rho\frac{\partial^2 \vec u}{(\partial t)^2}=\mu\nabla^2 \vec u + (\lambda + \mu) \nabla \delta + \overline f
   $$
   - 注意：也可以寫成 $\rho\frac{\partial^2 \vec u}{(\partial t)^2}=\mu\nabla^2 \vec u + (\lambda + \mu) \nabla (\nabla \cdot \vec u) + \overline f$
   此即為**<u>彈性動力學方程</u>**
