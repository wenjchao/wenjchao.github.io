# 泊松括號

## 內文
對於任意力學量 F 而言，F 均可以用廣義座標及廣義動量來表達，表示成 $F(p,q,t)$

1. Ｆ的全微分展開式： $\frac{dF}{dt} = \frac{\partial F}{\partial t} + \sum_{j} (\frac{\partial F}{\partial p_j}\frac{dp_j}{dt} + \frac{\partial F}{\partial q_j}\frac{dq_j}{dt})$
2. 將 $\frac{dp_j}{dt}、\frac{dq_j}{dt}$用哈密頓正則方程換掉：$\frac{dF}{dt} = \frac{\partial F}{\partial t} + \sum_{j} (\frac{\partial F}{\partial p_j}\frac{\partial H}{\partial q_j} - \frac{\partial F}{\partial q_j}\frac{\partial H}{\partial p_j})$
3. 括號內即為 F 與 H 的泊松括號，記作 $[F,H] = \frac{\partial F}{\partial p_j}\frac{\partial H}{\partial q_j} - \frac{\partial F}{\partial q_j}\frac{\partial H}{\partial p_j}$
   - 原本Ｆ的全微分展開式可以寫成 $\frac{dF}{dt} = \frac{\partial F}{\partial t} +[F,H]$
   - F & H 均可以換掉，即 $[f,g] = \frac{\partial f}{\partial p_j}\frac{\partial g}{\partial q_j} - \frac{\partial f}{\partial q_j}\frac{\partial g}{\partial p_j}$
5. 泊松定理：兩守恆量可以創造新的守恆量
   1. 對於某個守恆量 F，因為 $\frac{dF}{dt} =0$ ⇒ $[F,H] =- \frac{\partial F}{\partial t}$
   2. 對於另一個守恆量 G，因為 $\frac{dG}{dt} =0$ ⇒ $[G,H] =- \frac{\partial G}{\partial t}$

- 在進行正則變換前後，由 (p,q) 定義的泊松括號 [f,g] 會等於由 (P,Q)定義的泊松括號 [f,g]，即 $\frac{\partial f}{\partial p_j}\frac{\partial g}{\partial q_j} - \frac{\partial f}{\partial q_j}\frac{\partial g}{\partial p_j} = \frac{\partial f}{\partial P_j}\frac{\partial g}{\partial Q_j} - \frac{\partial f}{\partial Q_j}\frac{\partial g}{\partial P_j}$
