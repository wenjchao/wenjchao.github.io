# 拉格朗日關係

## 內文
1. 對於 $f(x,y)$ 而言，$\frac{df}{dx} = \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\frac{dy}{dx} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}$，若把 $\frac{df}{dx}$寫成 $f'$， $\frac{dy}{dx}$寫成 $y'$
   ⇒ $f' = \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}y' + \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}$ ⇒ 兩邊對 $y'$ 求偏導數 ⇒
   $$
   \frac{\partial f'}{\partial y'} = \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}
   $$
   - 前提是 $f(x,y)$ 不顯含 $y'$
2. 對於 $f(x,y)$ 而言，$\frac{df}{dx} = \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\frac{dy}{dx} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}$，若把 $\frac{df}{dx}$寫成 $f'$， $\frac{dy}{dx}$寫成 $y'$
   $$
   \frac{\partial}{\partial y} (\frac{df}{dx})\\= \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}(\frac{dy}{dx}) + \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}) \\= \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(\frac{\partial f}{\partial y})(\frac{dy}{dx}) + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}) \\= \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(\frac{\partial f}{\partial y})(\frac{dy}{dx}) + \frac{\partial}{\partial x}(\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}) \\= \frac{d}{dx}(\frac{\partial f}{\partial y})
   $$
   - 同樣前提是 $f(x,y)$ 不顯含 $y'$
