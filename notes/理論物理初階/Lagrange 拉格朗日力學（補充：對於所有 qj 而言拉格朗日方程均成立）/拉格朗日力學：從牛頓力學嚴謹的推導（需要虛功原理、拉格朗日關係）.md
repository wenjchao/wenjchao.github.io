# 拉格朗日力學：從牛頓力學嚴謹的推導（需要虛功原理、拉格朗日關係）

## 內文
1. 從前面的 **<u>[3. 直角座標中的動力學]</u>** 開始：$\vec f_{cart} - m\vec a_{cart} = 0$，此時座標系均為舊的直角坐標系，如果此質點系統有 n 個點，則座標系 old 共有 3n 個座標
2. 根據達朗貝爾原理，把 $𝛿W = \displaystyle\sum_{cart=1}^{3n} ( F_{cart}-m a_{cart})\cdot {\delta s_{cart}} =0$（把向量拆開來寫成 $\sum$各座標 ）改寫成以下形式：
   $$
   𝛿W  = \sum_{cart} ( F_{cart}-m a_{cart})\cdot {\delta  s_{cart}}\\= \displaystyle\sum_{cart}  F_{cart}\cdot {𝛿 s_{cart}} - \displaystyle\sum_{cart} m a_{cart}\cdot{\delta s_{cart}} = 0
   $$
3. 其中第一項 $\displaystyle\sum_{old} F_{old}\cdot {𝛿 s_{old}}= \sum_{new} Q_{new} \delta {q_{new}}$ （這就是廣義力 Q 的定義，詳見虛功原理）
4. 接著處理第二項：
   $$
   -\displaystyle\sum_{cart} m a_{cart}\cdot{ s_{cart}} = -\displaystyle\sum_{cart} m a_{cart}\cdot(\sum_{gen} \frac{\partial s_{cart}}{\partial q_{gen}}\delta q_{gen} )\\= -\sum_{gen}(\displaystyle\sum_{cart} m  \frac{\partial s_{cart}}{\partial q_{gen}}a_{cart})\delta q_{gen} =-\sum_{gen}(\displaystyle\sum_{cart} m  \frac{\partial s_{cart}}{\partial q_{gen}}s''_{cart})\delta q_{gen} \\=-\sum_{gen}(\displaystyle\sum_{cart} m \frac{\partial s_{cart}}{\partial q_{gen}}\frac{d}{dt}(s'_{cart}) )\delta q_{gen} \\ = -\sum_{gen}\delta q_{gen}[\displaystyle\sum_{cart} m \frac{d}{dt} (s'_{cart}\frac{\partial s_{cart}}{\partial q_{gen}}) -\displaystyle\sum_{cart} m s'_{cart}\frac{d}{dt}(\frac{\partial s_{cart}}{\partial q_{gen}}) ]\\= -\sum_{gen}\delta q_{gen}[\displaystyle\sum_{cart}  \frac{d}{dt} (ms'_{cart}\frac{\partial s'_{cart}}{\partial q'_{gen}}) -\displaystyle\sum_{cart}  ms'_{cart}(\frac{\partial s'_{cart}}{\partial q_{gen}})] \\=-  \sum_{gen}\delta q_{gen}[  \frac{d}{dt} \frac{\partial}{\partial q'_{gen}}\displaystyle\sum_{cart}(\frac{1}{2}m{s'_{cart}}^2 ) - (\frac{\partial }{\partial q_{gen}}) \displaystyle\sum_{cart}(\frac{1}{2}m{s'_{cart}}^2 )]\\=   -\sum_{gen}\delta q_{gen}[ \frac{d}{dt} (\frac{\partial T}{\partial q'_{gen}}) - (\frac{\partial T}{\partial q_{gen}}) ]
   $$
   - 3 → 4：分部積分
   - 4 → 5：前半段用拉格朗日關係(1)，後半段用拉格朗日關係(2)（見歐拉方程）
   - 6 → 7：定義系統動能 $T = \sum_{old}(\frac{1}{2}m{s'_{old}}^2 )$
5. 綜合 2.~ 4. 可得
   $$
   𝛿W  = \displaystyle\sum_{cart = 1}^{3n}  F_{cart}\cdot {𝛿 s_{cart}} - \displaystyle\sum_{cart = 1}^{3n} m a_{cart}\cdot{ s_{cart}} \\=\sum_{gen=1}^{a}  Q_{gen} \delta {q_{gen}} -\sum_{gen=1}^{a} \delta q_{gen}[ \frac{d}{dt} (\frac{\partial T}{\partial q'_{gen}}) - (\frac{\partial T}{\partial q_{gen}}) ]\\=\sum_{gen=1}^{a} \delta {q_{gen}}[Q_{gen}  - \frac{d}{dt} (\frac{\partial T}{\partial q'_{gen}}) + (\frac{\partial T}{\partial q_{gen}}) ] =0
   $$
6. 因為現在 $\delta q_{new}$ 之間彼此獨立可以任取，因此對於所有 a 個廣義座標 $\hat{q}_{new} =\{\hat{q_1}、\hat{q_2}......\hat {q_a}\}$ 而言，
   $$
   Q_{gen}  - \frac{d}{dt} (\frac{\partial T}{\partial q'_{gen}}) + (\frac{\partial T}{\partial q_{gen}}) = 0
   $$
7. 其中廣義力 Q 也可以區分為主動力 & 保守力 $Q_{new} = Q_{new(主動)} + Q_{new(保守)}$，同時利用 $Q_{new(保守)}$ 定義位能場 $V$ ：$Q_{new(保守)} = -\frac {\partial V}{\partial q_{new}}$
8. 最後定義拉格朗日量 $L = T - V$

$$
Q_{gen(主動)}  = \frac{d}{dt} (\frac{\partial L}{\partial q'_{gen}}) - (\frac{\partial L}{\partial q_{gen}})
$$

9. 保守系統下，$Q_{new(主動)} =0$
