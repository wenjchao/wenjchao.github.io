# 座標軸轉換關係

## 內文
$\hat{r}=cos(\theta)*\hat{i}+sin(\theta)*\hat{j}$ ， $\hat{\theta}=-sin(\theta)*\hat{i}+cos(\theta)*\hat{j}$

$\hat{i}=cos(\theta)*\hat{r}-sin(\theta)*\hat{\theta}$ ， $\hat{j}=sin(\theta)*\hat{r} +cos(\theta)*\hat{\theta}$

即 $\begin{bmatrix} cos(\theta) & sin(\theta) \\ - sin(\theta) & cos(\theta)\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \hat i\\ \hat j\end{bmatrix} =\begin{bmatrix} \hat r\\ \hat \theta \end{bmatrix}$，$\begin{bmatrix} cos(\theta) & -sin(\theta) \\ sin(\theta) & cos(\theta)\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \hat r\\ \hat \theta \end{bmatrix} =\begin{bmatrix} \hat i\\ \hat j\end{bmatrix}$
