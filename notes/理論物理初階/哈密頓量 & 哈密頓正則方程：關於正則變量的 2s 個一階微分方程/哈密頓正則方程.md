# 哈密頓正則方程

## 內文
1. 對哈密頓量進行全微分展開： $dH(p,q,t) = \sum_{j} (\frac{\partial H}{\partial p_j})dp_j + \sum_{j}(\frac{\partial H}{\partial q_j})dq_j +\frac{\partial H}{\partial t}dt$
2. 根據定義，$dH = d(\sum_{j} p_j\dot q_j -L(q,\dot q,t)) = \sum_{j}\dot p_jdq_j - \sum_{j} \dot q_jdp_j - \frac{\partial L}{\partial t}dt$

⇒ 兩相比較，$\begin{cases}\dot p_j =\frac{\partial H}{\partial q_j}\\\dot q_j =-\frac{\partial H}{\partial p_j}\end{cases}$。此即為 **<u>哈密頓正則方程</u>**，$p_j$ & $q_j$ 則是**<u>相互共軛的正則變量</u>**

- 同時可以看出，$\frac{dH}{dt}= \frac{\partial H}{\partial t} = -\frac{\partial L}{\partial t}$（因為 $\frac{dH}{dt}= \sum_{j}\dot p_j\dot q_j - \sum_{j} \dot q_j\dot p_j - \frac{\partial L}{\partial t}$）
