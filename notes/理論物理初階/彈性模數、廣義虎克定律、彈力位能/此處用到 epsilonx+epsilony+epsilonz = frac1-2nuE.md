# 此處用到 $\epsilon_x+\epsilon_y+\epsilon_z = \frac{1-2\nu}{E} (\sigma_x+\sigma_y+\sigma_z)$﻿

## 內文
將一開始的 $\begin{cases} \epsilon_x =  \frac{\sigma_x}{E} - \nu \frac{\sigma_y}{E} - \nu \frac{\sigma_z}{E}\\ \epsilon_y =  \frac{\sigma_y}{E} - \nu \frac{\sigma_x}{E} - \nu \frac{\sigma_z}{E} \\ \epsilon_z =    \frac{\sigma_z}{E} - \nu \frac{\sigma_x}{E} - \nu \frac{\sigma_y}{E}\end{cases}$ 三式相加即可得此式
