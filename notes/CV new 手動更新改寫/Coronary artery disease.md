# Coronary artery disease

## 摘要
依嚴重度 STEMI > NSTEMI > UA > stable angina（前三者合稱 ACS），各自的 ST／troponin 組合與症狀定義、CCS 分級、hs-troponin 0/1/3 小時流程、NICE 處置、冠狀動脈攝影視角。

## 內文
依嚴重程度分類，分成四階：STEMI > NSTEMI > Unstable angina > Stable angina

其中 STEMI、NSTEMI、Unstable angina 合稱 ACS（acute coronary syndrome）

1. **<u>STEMI</u>**：**<u>ST elevation</u>** + **<u>elevated troponin</u>**

   [[Coronary artery disease/STEMI equivalent|摘要]]
2. **<u>NSTEMI</u>**：~~No ST elevation~~ + **<u>elevated troponin</u>**
3. **<u>Unstable angina</u>**：~~No ST elevation~~ + ~~normal troponin~~
   > Symptom of UA：
   >
   > Prolonged, Rested, New-onset, Progressed, Post-MI angina
   >
   > 1. **<u>Prolonged</u>** (>20 min) chest discomfort **<u>at rest</u>**
   > 2. **<u>New-onset</u>**(<3 months) angina (class II or III)
   > 3. Recent **<u>destabilization</u>** of previously stable angina(Class III)
   >    (crescendo/unstable angina).
   > 4. **<u>Post-acute myocardial infarction</u>** angina
   > 5. [[Coronary artery disease/Canadian Cardiovascular Society classification|扁平]]
4. **<u>Stable angina</u>**：~~No ST elevation~~ + ~~normal troponin~~
   > Symptom of stable angina：
   >
   > retrosternal, exertional,
   >
   > relieved by
   >
   > rest
   >
   > 1. **<u>retrosternal(1)</u>** heaviness with radiation
   >
   >    [[Coronary artery disease/疼痛 radiation 位置|摘要]]
   > 2. provoked by **<u>exertional or emotional stress(2)</u>**
   > 3. relieved by **<u>rest or NTG(3)</u>**
   >
   > - 三項症狀 ≥ 兩項 ⇒ stable angina， ≤ 一項 ⇒ noncardiac schest pain

- Other ACS symptoms
  1. **cold sweating, dyspnea, nausea & vomiting, syncope**
  2. hypotension, pulmonary edema, crackles
  3. epigastric pain
  4. pallor, sweating, or tremor ⇒ precipitating conditions ex: anemia / thyrotoxicosis

> 💡 No ST elevation 也可以是 ST depression / T inversion / Normal sinus rhythm
