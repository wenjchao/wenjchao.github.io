# Heart failure

## 摘要
急性：惡性循環、urgent／immediate phase、congestion × perfusion 的 2×2 評估、Swan-Ganz 數值、2016 ESC 處置。長期：vital sign、lab、echo、NYHA／ACC-AHA／Intermacs 分期、HFrEF／HFmrEF／HFpEF、GDMT（ARNI + BB + SGLT2i + MRA）與劑量、裝置治療、機械循環輔助。

## 內文
### Acute heart failure & acute management

- [[Heart failure/Disease progression|扁平]]
- 惡化因子：飲食中水分鹽分未控制、不規律用藥、Infection、Anemia、酒、藥物、懷孕

1. Initial（**Urgent phase**）：keep vital sign，送 ICU / CCU
   1. **<u>cardiogenic shock</u>**: pharmacological & mechanical support
   2. **<u>respiratory failure</u>**: ventilation support
2. Initial（**Immediate phase**）： etiology survey & specific treatment
   1. acute coronary syndrome
   2. arrythmia
   3. pulmonary embolism
   4. hypertensive crisis
   5. myocardial / valve rupture

### Long-term evaluation & management

1. Vital sign：SpO2 & BP
2. Lab data
   1. **Troponin, NT-proBNP**（不同年齡有差）
   2. Hb, HCT, PT, aPTT, Electrolyte, Thyroid / Renal / Liver function
3. <u>Heart echo</u> for systolic & diastolic dysfunction
