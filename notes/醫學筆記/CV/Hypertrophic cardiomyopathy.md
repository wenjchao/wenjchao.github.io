# Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy

## 摘要
病生理（thick／thin filament 突變）、好發年齡、症狀與徵象（ECG、echo、收縮期雜音）、併發症（猝死、HF、心律不整、中風）、猝死預防、治療原則。

## 內文
[[Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy/圖示]]

### Pathophysiology

1. thick filament mutations (myosin/binding protein), **most common**
2. thin filament mutations (troponin/tropomyosin/actin)
3. AR 或是 LV dilated 都可導致 hypertrophy

### Epidemiology

infancy, childhood, **adolescent(most common)**, Late age(lower risk for HCM-related death)

### Symptoms

1. Dyspnea on exertion / Fatigue / Atypical or anginal chest pain
2. Palpitation
3. Presyncope and syncope, during or immediately following exertion
4. **<u>Sudden cardiac death</u>** <u>⇒ prevention</u>

### Signs

1. Abnormal ECG (left axis deviation, high R voltage in aVL, aVF, V4 - V6, septal Q waves in aVL, I, II)
2. [[Heart Echo|摘要]]：
   1. High intra-LV pressure gradient
   2. Diastolic dysfunction
3. Murmurs：**Systolic**
   1. Left ventricular outflow tract (LVOT) gradients / obstruction ⇒ Systolic murmurs
   2. Mitral regurgitation ⇒ Systolic murmurs


### Related complication

1. **<u>Sudden Death</u>**
2. Heart failure
3. Arrythmias, both ventricular and atrial (especially important due to LV filling impairment)
4. Stroke, in older age and larger left atrial size (even without identified atrial fibrillation)
