# Syncope

## 摘要
Cardiac vs Noncardiac vs Vertigo

## 內文
1. **Cardiac**
   1. **Arrhythmia:** History of CAD, MI, cardiomyopathy? Torsades de pointes?
   2. Left ventricular outflow tract obstruction (LVOTO): in <u>AS</u>, <u>HOCM</u>
2. **Noncardiac:** prodrome for < 30 seconds & recovery within seconds
   1. **Orthostatic hypotension**: 站立 **3 min 內** **SBP 掉 ≥ 20** or **DBP 掉 ≥ 10** mmHg
      1. ANS disorder: Parkinson’s, Lewy body disease, MSA (P+), DM, Amyloidosis
      2. Drug (α-antagonist) / hypovolemia / 剛吃飽
   2. **Vasovagal**
   - 這兩個都 tilt table positive
3. D/D: vertigo
