# 先天代謝型疾病：昏、吐、酸、肝

## 內文
新生兒急性出現四個症狀，要高度懷疑：

1. 餵食情形差，一直嘔吐
2. 昏迷、嗜睡 (**encephalopathy** ex: <u>lethargy</u>, <u>coma</u>, <u>seizure</u>)
3. 代謝性酸血症
4. 肝脾腫脹 或 肝功能異常

注意指摽：Ammonia, BUN(Urea), Glucose, Lactate, Ketone(包含Organic acid & amino acid), Gas(Acid)

1. [[先天代謝型疾病：昏、吐、酸、肝/代謝總表：Amino acid (1,2,3), glucose (4,5,6), lipid|扁平]]
2. amino acidopathy: amino acid 代謝不掉 (氮還在) ⇒ **<u>身體有味道</u>**、**<u>autosomal recessive</u>**

   [[先天代謝型疾病：昏、吐、酸、肝/Phenylketonuria ⇒ 合成不了 tyrosine ／ seretonin ／|摘要]]
   - Homocystinuria, Maple syrup urine disease, Isovaleric acidemia
3. organic acidemia: amino acid 代謝不掉 (胺基酸上沒氮了，剩有機雖酸) ⇒ **<u>NH3 高</u>** 、**<u>autosomal recessive</u>**
   - Methylmalonic acidemia, Propionic Acidemia, Glutaric acidemia

   [[先天代謝型疾病：昏、吐、酸、肝/Organic acidemia 中 NH3 高的原因：抑制 Urea cycle|摘要]]
4. urea cycle disorder: 氮排不掉 ⇒ **<u>NH3 高</u>** (除 OTC 外皆 **<u>autosomal recessive</u>**)
   1. Ornithine transcarbamylase deficiency (OTC deficiency): **<u>X-link</u>**、最常見、沒補助
   2. Citrullinemia

- glycogen storage disease: **<u>autosomal recessive</u>**, 分 11 型 ⇒ <u>低血糖</u>、腦病變、無力

  [[先天代謝型疾病：昏、吐、酸、肝/總圖示|摘要]]
  1. von Gierke’s disease: Glucose 無法變成 G6P (就是不能用) ⇒ **<u>lactic acidosis</u>**, 高尿酸
  2. Pompe disease: 無法分解 glycogen ⇒ 堆積在心臟、肝臟、肌肉

5. galactosemia: **<u>autosomal recessive</u>**, RBC 缺糖 ⇒ <u>hemolysis</u> ⇒ indirect bilirubin
   - 易 E. coli sepsis、白內障、肝脾腫大
6. mitochondrial defect: myopathy 為主，沒補助
7. β-oxidase defect: fatty acid 代謝不了 ⇒ **<u>Hypoketotic hypoglycemia</u>**
   1. HMG CoA lyase deficiency, Primary carnitine deficiency, Carnitine Palmitoyltransferase deficiency, medium / long chain acyl CoA dehydrogenase deficiency
8. 其他健保補助篩檢：
   1. **<u>G6PD deficiency</u>**: **<u>X-link</u>** , <u>NADPH ↓ + oxidative stress</u> ⇒ **Intrinsic hemolytic anemia**
      - histology: bite cells

      [[先天代謝型疾病：昏、吐、酸、肝/不要用 sulfa drug、SU、Hydroxychloroquine、Ciprofloxacin|摘要]]
   2. Congenital adrenal hyperplasia: steroid 代謝問題 (21-)，見 DSD
   2. Congenital hypothyroidism：智能障礙、生長發育遲緩、身材矮小
   3. Holocarboxylase synthetase deficiency: biotin cycle 壞掉
9. 不在健保補助裡的
   1. Lesch-Nyhan syndrome (HGPRT defect) ⇒ hyperuricemia, **<u>X-link</u>**
10. **Lysosomal storage diseases: 除 Fabry & Hunter 外皆** **<u>autosomal recessive</u>** (猶太人多)
    共通特徵：<u>Progressive neurodegeneration</u>、<u>Developmental delay</u>、<u>Hepatosplenomegaly</u>
    1. Tay-Sachs disease: <u>Cherry-red spot on macula</u>、hyperreflexia、聽覺過敏、**<u>沒有 Hepatosplenomegaly</u>** (vs Niemann-Pick)
    2. Fabry disease: **<u>X-link</u>**
       1. Early: peripheral neuropathy (Fabry crises), hypohidrosis (少汗), angiokeratomas
       2. Late: cardiomyopathy, nephropathy
    3. Krabbe disease: Peripheral neuropathy, loss of vision、**<u>沒有 Hepatosplenomegaly</u>**
    4. Gaucher disease: pancytopenia, bone crises (ex: avascular necrosis of femur)
    5. Niemann-Pick disease: <u>Cherry-red spot on macula</u>、Slurred speech(講話很慢)、gelastic cataplexy (大笑時全身無力跌到)
    6. Metachromatic leukodystrophy: Progressive demyelination of the central and peripheral nervous system, Ataxia, Developmental delay, Memory deficits
    7. Mucopolysaccharidoses: Hurler (**<u>autosomal recessive</u>**) & Hunter (**<u>X-link</u>**)
       1. Hurler: <u>skeletal abnormalities</u>, <u>airway obstruction</u>, <u>corneal clouding</u>
       2. Hunter: mild Hurler + <u>aggressive behavior</u>, <u>no corneal clouding</u>
