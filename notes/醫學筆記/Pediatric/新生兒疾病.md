# 新生兒疾病

## 內文
Trisomy 13: Patau, 1y

1. 唇顎裂、腦無裂 (holoprosencephaly)、臍膨出
2. 小眼 / 無眼
3. Ulnar 側多指、Radial bone 發育不良

Trisomy 18: Edwards syndrome, 1y

1. micrognathia (小下巴) 、低位耳
2. clenched hands：食指壓中指、小指壓無名指
3. horseshoe kidneys
4. VSD

Trisomy 21: Down syndrome ⇒ **5** **A**'s

1. **A**dvanced maternal age
2. duodenal **A**tresia
3. **A**trioventricular septal defect (AS、ASD、VSD)
4. **A**ML > **A**LL
5. early onset of **A**lzheimer disease
6. megacolon、低位耳、hypothyroidism

Turner：矮、璞狀頸，早發性卵巢衰竭 ⇒ 無月經、不孕症、FSH / LH 高
