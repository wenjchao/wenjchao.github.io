# Diagnosis criteria: Fever ≥ 5 day + 五中四以上

## 內文
Fever > 5 days + ≥ 4 criterias (1口 2眼 3趾摸脖 4掌 5全身)

1. Oral mucous membrane changes, including erythema and/or fissuring of lips, strawberry tongue, and/or erythema of oropharyngeal mucosa
2. Bilateral bulbar conjunctival injection without exudate
3. Cervical lymphadenopathy (at least one lymph node ≥1.5 cm in diameter, usually unilateral)
4. Peripheral extremity changes, including erythema of palms and/or soles and/or edema of hands and/or feet (acute phase) and/or periungual desquamation (subacute phase)
5. Polymorphous rash (maculopapular, diffuse erythroderma, or erythema multiforme-like)
