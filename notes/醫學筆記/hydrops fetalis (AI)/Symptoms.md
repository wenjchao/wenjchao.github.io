# Symptoms

## 內文
The symptoms of hydrops fetalis depend on the severity of the condition and the underlying cause. Some of the common symptoms include:

- Swelling of the fetus or certain parts of the fetus, such as the abdomen or the scalp
- Abnormal accumulation of fluid in the fetal organs, such as the heart, lungs, or liver
- Low levels of amniotic fluid
- Fetal anemia, which may lead to low fetal heart rate or fetal distress
- Premature birth or stillbirth
