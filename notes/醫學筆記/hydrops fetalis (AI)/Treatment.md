# Treatment

## 內文
The treatment of hydrops fetalis depends on the underlying cause of the condition. In some cases, the condition may resolve on its own, while in other cases, it may require immediate medical intervention. Some of the common treatments for hydrops fetalis include:

- Blood transfusions for fetal anemia
- Medications to treat infections or heart failure in the fetus
- Fetal surgery to remove tumors or correct structural abnormalities
- Early delivery of the baby in severe cases
