# Causes

## 內文
The causes of hydrops fetalis are diverse and can be due to both genetic and non-genetic factors. Some of the possible causes of non-immune hydrops fetalis include:

- Heart or lung abnormalities in the fetus
- Infections, such as parvovirus B19, cytomegalovirus, or toxoplasmosis
- Anemia in the fetus due to blood group incompatibility between the mother and the fetus
- Twin-to-twin transfusion syndrome
- Tumors in the fetus or placenta
- Maternal conditions, such as diabetes or lupus
