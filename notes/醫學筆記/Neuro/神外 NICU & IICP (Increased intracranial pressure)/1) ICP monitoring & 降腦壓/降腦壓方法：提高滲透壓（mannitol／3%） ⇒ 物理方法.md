# 降腦壓方法：提高滲透壓（mannitol/3%） ⇒ 物理方法（抬高/引流/降溫/Sedation） ⇒ 開刀（開腦/切腦）⇒ 長期用 acetazolamide

## 內文
- 注意：Monro-Kellie hypothesis：腦壓由 CSF、腦組織、blood flow 三個部分構成，這三個東西的體積相加不變，其中任何一個體積改變都會影響另外兩個 ( ex: 突然站起時blood flow 變少 )

1. Intravenous mannitol (1 to 1.5 g/kg)
2. 3% hypertonic saline
3. 頭坐高(30º)
   - 注意：⇒
     Avoid :
     1. restrictive neck taping
     2. excessive flexion or rotation of the neck
     3. stimuli induces Valsalva responses(ex: endotracheal suctioning)
4. Drainage (Removal of CSF for hydrocephalus):
   1. ventriculostomy not lumbar drain(induce transtentorial herniation)
   2. 1 to 2 mL/minute(slow), for 2~3 minutes a time, 2~3 minutes intervals.
   3. Contraindication: SAH (precipitate recurrent hemorrhage)
5. CO2 wash out (to a PCO2 of 26 to 30 mmHg) => vasoconstriction => 腦壓降低。但 perfusion 會掉，要小心，**只能當作<u>緊急處理</u>**撐幾分鐘
   - 注意：小心
     traumatic brain injury / acute stroke
     (vasoconstriction ⇒ critical decrease in local cerebral perfusion)
6. Aggressive treatment of fever。如果藥物無法退燒，可以使用物理方法退燒：冰毯，或是伸一根水管插進femoral(水冷式降溫)
7. Sedation, pain control, anticonvulsant therapy if seizure
   - Sedation(ex: Propofol, Barbiturates) reduce metabolic demand & ventilator asynchrony. ☆☆ Keep a secure airway ☆☆
8. 開腦減壓： dura 跟顱骨要一起打開
9. 腦組織切除
10. 長期：<u>acetazolamide</u>
11. 其他需注意事項：
    1. O2 if need
       1. **avoid ICP rise** during intubation (sympathetic laryngeal reflex) ⇒ use paralytic agents/sedation
       2. **hyperventilation for CO2 washout**
    2. Resolve the proximate cause ex: blood clot / tumor / CSF diversion / metabolic disorder
    3. avoid hypotension, especially after sedation. Keep CPP > 60 mmHg & < 120mmHg
    4. Fluid: avoid all free water(NS ok), keep serum > 280 mOsm/L(often 295~305) with **Hypertonic saline bolus** or **Mannitol** or Furosemide
