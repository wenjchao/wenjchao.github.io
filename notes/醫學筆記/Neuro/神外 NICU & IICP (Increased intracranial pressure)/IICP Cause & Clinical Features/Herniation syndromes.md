# Herniation syndromes

## 內文
1. Uncal transtentorial herniation : Ipsilateral fixed and dilated pupil (compression of parasympathetic fibers of CN3) & contralateral motor paralysis (compresses the pyramidal tract)
2. Central transtentorial herniation : Bilateral pinpoint pupils & bilateral Babinski’s signs & increased muscle tone
3. Cerebellotonsillar herniation : pinpoint pupils, flaccid paralysis, and sudden death.
