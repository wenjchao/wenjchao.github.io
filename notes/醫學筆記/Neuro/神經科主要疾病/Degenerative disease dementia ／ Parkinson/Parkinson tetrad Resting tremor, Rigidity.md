# Parkinson tetrad: Resting tremor, Rigidity, Akinesia, Postural instability

## 內文
1. **Resting tremor (eg, “pill rolling”):** A frequency of 4–6 Hz that ↓ with voluntary movement
2. **Rigidity:** “Cogwheeling.”
3. **Akinesia/bradykinesia:** Slowed movements caused by difficulty initiating movements. Festinating gait (small, shuffling steps) without arm swing is also seen.
4. **Postural instability:** Stooped posture, impaired righting reflexes, falls. Can often be a very early finding in Parkinson disease.

其他：Masked facies, memory loss, and micrographia
