# Multiple sclerosis (MS)：多次 + 多處 CNS demyelination

## 內文
Charcot triad: scanning speech, intranuclear ophthalmoplegia, nystagmus

Diagnosis:

1. MRI：McDonald Diagnostic Criteria (多次、多處) 為 Gold standard
2. CSF：<u>IgG ↑ & oligoclonal bands</u>

Treatment:

1. Acute: high-dose IV corticosteroids, plasma exchange
2. Long-term: <mark>Ocrelizumab(anti-CD20), Interferon-β</mark>
3. Symptomatic therapy: baclofen for spasticity, cholinergics for urinary retention, anticholinergics for urinary incontinence, carbamazepine or amitriptyline for painful paresthesias, and antidepressants for clinical depression
