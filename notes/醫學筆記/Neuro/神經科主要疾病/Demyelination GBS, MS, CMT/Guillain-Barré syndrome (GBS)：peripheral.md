# Guillain-Barré syndrome (GBS)：peripheral (包括 CN3~12)

## 內文
Acute inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy

Clinical feature & Diagnosis:

1. post-infection
2. symmetric, ascending paralysis & paresthesias & autonomic dysregulation
3. <u>CSF protein</u> level > 55 mg/dL & no cell
4. <u>nerve conduction velocity ↓</u>
5. may take up to 1 year ⇒ 事前病解很重要
6. 危：respiratory failure ⇒ ICU, 插管

Treatment:

1. <u>Plasmapheresis</u> and <u>IVIG</u> & 復健
2. **No corticosteroid**
