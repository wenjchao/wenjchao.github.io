# Spinal lesion

## 內文
1. [[Spinal lesion/圖|扁平]]
2. Poliomyelitis (不對稱) & <mark>SMA (對稱)</mark> ：**<u>lower motor neuron</u>** (anterior horns)
3. ALS (Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis): <u>l</u>**<u>ower + upper motor neuron</u>** <mark>(eye & sphincter sparing)</mark>⇒ treatment: Riluzole
4. Brown-Séquard hemisection: 一節 spinal cord 半邊壞掉

   [[Spinal lesion/那節 LMN + 無感覺，那節以下同側 UMN + 無觸壓，對側無溫痛|摘要]]
5. Complete occlusion of anterior spinal artery : 與 <u>abdominal aorta surgery</u> 有關，除了 dorsal column 全出事 ⇒ 同側 Lower，同側以下 Upper，對側以下無溫痛
6. Central cord syndrome：<u>hyperextension</u> 造成, sensory & motor, **<u>上肢比下肢嚴重</u>**
7. Syringomyelia (in <u>Chiari malformations</u>)：anterior white commisure (spinothalamic)
8. Vitamin B12 deficiency (<u>**demyelination of large fibers**</u>): dorsal columns, lateral corticospinal tracts, and spinocerebellar tracts (又稱 subacute combined degeneration)
9. Cauda equina syndrome (急症)：disc herniation ⇒ 失禁、會陰麻、腳無 reflex
