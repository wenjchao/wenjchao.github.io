# Stroke location

## 內文
Pons 以下同側 以上異側 重點交換位置：pyamidal dicussation, medial leminscus, pons(corticocerebellar)

Sympathetic 永遠同側 (Horner syndrome)

### Vessel territory

- <u>**ICA 才會到眼睛**</u>；MCA & ACA 都不會

1. Anterior cerebral artery: <u>**內側**</u>
   1. <u>內側 motor & sensory</u>: Contralateral paresis and sensory loss in the leg
   2. <u>Frontal lobe</u>: cognitive or personality changes
   3. <u>Frontal lobe</u>: urinary incontinence
2. Middle cerebral artery: <u>**外側**</u>
   1. <u>外側 motor & sensory</u>: Contralateral paresis and sensory loss in the face and arm
   2. <u>Frontal eye field</u>: gaze (眼睛往有問題那側篇，大同橋異)
   3. <u>視野</u>: homonymous hemianopsia preference toward the side of the lesion
   4. <u>Nondominant hemisphere</u>: hemi-neglect
   5. <u>Dominant hemisphere</u>: aphasia
3. Posterior cerebral artery: <u>**occipital lobe & midbrain**</u>
   1. <u>視野</u>: homonymous hemianopsia <u>with macular sparing</u>
   2. <u>CN5 nucleus</u>: **ipsilateral sensory loss of face**
   3. <u>Medial lemniscus(剛跨來) & spinothalamic tract (早就跨)</u>: **contralateral sensory loss of limbs**
   4. <u>CN8</u>: Vertigo
   5. <u>小腦</u>: **ipsilateral** limb ataxia
4. Basilar artery: **Locked-in syndrome** (corticospinal & corticaobulbar tract)
5. AICA: 介於 PCA & PICA 之間，可能都有
6. Lacunar: hypertension, (DM, hyperlipidemia)
   1. <u>internal capsule</u>: pure motor
   2. <u>internal capsule</u>: dysarthria & clumsy hand
   3. <u>thalamus</u>: pure sensory, thalamic pain syndrome
   4. <u>pons</u>: ataxic hemiparesis

7. PICA / vertebral: lateral 為主
   1. Lateral medulla (**Wallenberg syndrome**):
      1. <u>Spinothalamic tract</u>: Loss of pain and temperature sensation on **contralateral** body
      2. <u>Sympathetic fiber</u>: **Ipsilateral** Horner syndrome
      3. <u>CN5</u>: Loss of pain and temperature sensation on **ipsilateral** face
      4. <u>CN8 nucleus</u>: Vertigo, nystagmus, diplopia, nausea
      5. <u>CN9,10</u>: **Ipsilateral** bulbar weakness (dysphagia, dysarthria)
   2. Medial medulla:
      1. <u>CN12</u>: **Ipsilateral** tongue weakness
      2. <u>medial leminscus</u>: **Contralateral** sensory loss **(本體、輕觸、震動)**
      3. <u>medullary pyramid</u>: **Contralateral** body weakness

8. Carotid artery dissection
   1. Sudden headache, neck pain
   2. Horner syndrome: ptosis (眼睛小), miosis (瞳孔小), 但是有汗
