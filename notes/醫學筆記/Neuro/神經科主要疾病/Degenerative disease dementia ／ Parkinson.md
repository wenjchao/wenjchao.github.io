# Degenerative disease: dementia / Parkinson

## 內文
- 注意：輕度認知障礙 (mild cognitive impairment) vs 失智症 (dementia)：MCI 不會影響日常生活功能

- [[Degenerative disease dementia ／ Parkinson/Spectrum 很大，overlap 很多|扁平]]
- [[Degenerative disease dementia ／ Parkinson/Alzheimer disease 大腦萎縮，記憶力最先變差，藥物|扁平]]
- Vascular dementia: 一直 stroke 一直階梯式變爛
- Frontotemporal dementia (Pick disease): frontotemporal atrophy
- Lewy Body dementia: <u>dementia & motor symptom 一起來</u> (< 1 year 內)
  - Lewy body: α-synuclein
  - 會有**視幻覺** (visual hallucination)
- Parkinson disease: motor 症狀五年後才開始失智，動作慢易跌倒
  - <u>**Levodopa**</u>-induced chorea: 用 levodopa後常見，Dopa 太多一直動

  [[Degenerative disease dementia ／ Parkinson/Parkinson tetrad Resting tremor, Rigidity|摘要]]

  [[Degenerative disease dementia ／ Parkinson/Stages for Parkinson：Start from olfactory or ENS|摘要]]

  [[Degenerative disease dementia ／ Parkinson/Parkinsons Plus|摘要]]
- Huntington disease: CAG triplet repeat。太年輕 / 老的 Huntington 會表現得像 Parkinson
- Rest leg syndrome: 晚上一直想動腳，與 neuropathy, <u>**iron deficiency anemia**</u> 有關。
  - Treatment: dopamine agonists (ropinirole, pramipexole) or gabapentin
- Creutzfeldt-Jakob (Prion diseases) : PrPc (cell) ⇒ PrPSc (scrapie)，腦海綿化，無藥醫。需 biopsy 但 MRI & EEG 可能有用
  - vCJD 是狂牛症，存活期比 sporadic CJD 長，患者較年輕，也較少 myoclonic jerk

Other reversible dementia

- Normal pressure hydrocephalus: ICP 其實會不時上升，只是沒有 IICP sign ⇒ 引流
- hypothyroidism, vitamin B12 deficiency (& large fiber), neurosyphilis
- Essential tremor: 家族遺傳，無時無刻一直抖 ⇒ β-blocker
