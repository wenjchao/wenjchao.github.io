# Secondary headaches: ⇒ neurological exam

## 內文

### Red flags ⚑: First, Worst, Cursed

1. **meningeal sign** (畏光、噁心嘔吐、脖子僵硬)：<u>meningitis</u>? <u>SAH</u>? <u>migraine</u>?
2. fever or rash：meningitis?
3. jaw claudication: temporal arteritis (<u>giant cell arteritis</u>)
4. constitutional symptoms: <u>tumor</u>?
5. focal neurologic signs or symptoms: <u>stroke</u>?
6. <u>IICP</u> sign: Papilledema, Blurred vision, fixed & dilated pupil
