# Lambert-Eaton myasthenic syndrome: Paraneoplastic, 不侵犯臉/眼/呼吸, 越動越有力

## 內文
- 注意：Paraneoplastic autoimmune disorder (SCLC)

1. Pathophysiology: anti-presynaptic calcium channels
2. proximal muscles weakness & autonomic, <u>越動越有力</u>
3. <u>不侵犯眼睛、呼吸、臉</u>
4. Treatment: 3,4-diaminopyridine or guanidine ± pyridostigmine (± corticosteroids / immunosuppressent for non-neoplastic antuimmune disease)
