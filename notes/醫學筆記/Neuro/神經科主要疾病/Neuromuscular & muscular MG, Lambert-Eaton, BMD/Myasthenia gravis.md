# Myasthenia gravis

## 內文
1. Pathophysiology: anti-postsynaptic acetylcholine (ACh) receptors（[[基礎醫學/免疫學/Hypersensitivity 四型|標題|Type II hypersensitivity]]）
2. Association with **thymoma**
3. Clinical features:
   1. ptosis, double vision, dysarthria, dysphagia, proximal muscle weakness
   2. Symptoms typically worsen with fatigue at the end of the day
   3. Myasthenic crisis: respiratory compromise and aspiration
   4. Test: **重複電刺激測試**
4. Treatment:
   1. Anticholinesterase inhibitors (pyridostigmine)
   2. Prednisone, immunosuppressants, plasmapheresis, or IVIG in acute / severe
   3. Resection of thymoma
   4. Avoid drugs affects neuromuscular junction ex: aminoglycosides, fluoroquinolones, mood-stablizer, 麻醉藥
