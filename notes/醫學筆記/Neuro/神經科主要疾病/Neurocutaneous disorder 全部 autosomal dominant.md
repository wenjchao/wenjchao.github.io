# Neurocutaneous disorder: 全部 autosomal dominant

## 內文
1. Neurofibromatosis 1:
   1. **<u>café-au-lait spots</u>** (flat, uniformly hyperpigmented macules)
   2. <u>Lisch nodules</u> (pigmented iris hamartomas)
   3. neurofibromas / Optic glioma / Pheochromocytomas
   4. Intellectual disability, seizure, Bone abnormality
2. Neurofibromatosis 2
   1. <u>Bilateral vestibular schwannomas</u>
   2. neurofibroma, meningioma, glioma, or schwannoma
3. Tuberous sclerosis
   1. Hamartomas in CNS and skin, angiofibromas
   2. ash-leaf spots, shagreen patches
   3. cardiac rhabdomyoma, renal angiomyolipoma
   4. intellectual disability, seizures
   5. mitral regurgitation
4. von Hippel-Lindau disease: Deletion of VHL gene
   1. Hemangioblastomas ⇒ polycythemia
   2. Renal cell carcinoma & Pheochromocytomas
5. Sturge-Weber syndrome: Capillary malformation ⇒ <u>**port-wine stain**</u>, 易 seizures、青光眼
