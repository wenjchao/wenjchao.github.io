### Cranial Nerve

CN2：手放上下左右看Diplopia、Visual field Defect、Pupil light reflex

CN3,4,6：ptosis、eye movement

CN5：牙籤刺臉兩邊、手輕撫兩邊，問感覺一不一樣

CN7：facial expression，笑一笑，抬眉，看對不對稱，大力閉眼，看能不能用手撥開

CN8：Nystagmus、在耳邊搓指

CN9,10：說「啊～」，看中間uvula有沒有偏一邊；Dysatrthria

CN11：Head rotation、shoulder elevation

CN12：Tongue 伸出來，有無對稱？

**Cerebellar & CN8**：Gait、Finger-nose-finger / Heel-Knee-Shin Test

**Meningitis**：Brudzinski’s sign(neck stiffness)、Kernig’s sign(hamstring stiffness)

**Capillary refilling time**

### 6Ds：Brain stem lesion

1. Diplopia：要上下左右都測試，讓他盯著手看。要區分是Binocular diplopia(神經科) 還是 Monocular diplopia(眼科)
2. Dysarthria
3. Dysphagia：<mark>**NG快放**</mark>，不然會嗆咳
4. Dysmetria：測距異常，Finger-nose-finger
5. Dizziness(Vertigo)
6. Drop attack：突然跌倒，可能因為 Cardiogenic syncope、vestibular cause或是seizures。
   比較：因Vestibular cause導致的Drop attacks可能因為跌倒撞到頭造成腦部損傷，但是跌倒前腦袋沒事

### Motor Examination：

1. Muscle power：Deltoid / Elbow flexion+extension / Wrist f+e / grasping / Hip f+e / knee f+e / ankle f+e
2. Muscle tone：Spasticity / Rigidity
3. Tendon reflexes: biceps, triceps, brachioradialis, patellar ligament, achilles tendon

### Sensation:

numbness? light touch(four limbs), vibration(great toe), Touching nose with eyes closed

- 注意：The Romberg test tests proprioception (peripheral nerves and dorsal columns), not for cerebellar function!
