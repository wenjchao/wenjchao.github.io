# Further assessment & workup

## 內文
1. CBC/DC、Electrolyte(Na, K, Ca, Mg)、Liver(AST/ALT/bilirubin/ammonia)、Renal(BUN/Cre)、ABG/Lactate、PT/aPTT(focal lesion prefered)
2. EKG (AF induced stroke, electrolyte emergency, unstable arrythmia)
3. Fever-work-up (CXR) / Toxic-work-up if needed
4. Brain CT / Lumbar puncture(Meningitis/Encephalitis) / EEG(Seizure) if needed
5. other workups: VDRL, Vit B1/B12, T3/T4
