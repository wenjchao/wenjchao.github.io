# Contraindication for TPA

## 內文
1. Hemorrhage on CT or Clinically suspected SAH or Hemorhagic stroke history
2. Combined with trauma or hemorrhage on other site
3. Under anti-coagulation in 48 hours ( or INR>1.7 / PT>15 ) or platelet < 100000
4. SBP > 185 or DBP > 110
5. Multilobar stroke
6. Recent Intracranial surgery or Arterial puncture at uncompressible site
7. Intracranial aneruysm / Arteriovenous malformation
8. Seizures present at onset of stroke
