# Assessment & Management

## 內文
1. Check Airway、Breath、Circulation、O2、IV、Sugar monitor, Stablize Vital Sign
2. Last normal time：不是什麼時候開始無力
3. [[Assessment & Management/NIHSS 評分|扁平]]
5. 照會Neuro Stroke team
6. Immediate laboratory studies：不要影響到TPA, Thrombectomy
   1. Noncontrast Brain CT(確定沒懷孕) / MRI ⇒ intervention
   2. One touch Glucose
   3. O2 Saturation
   4. Blood test ：CBC、Troponin、PT/aPTT
   5. Selective：Liver / Renal / Electrolyte / ABG / Blood Alcohol / Toxicology screen
   6. Follow up：EKG
   7. If fever：UTI(Urine culture), URI(CXR), GI
7. Supportive Treatment Goal (all evidence-based)：SBP<220，DBP<120，發燒壓掉，血糖控制在140-180mg/dl
   1. Dysphagia screen：**吞嚥困難就on NG**
   2. SpO2沒有低於94%就不要給O2
   3. 點滴不要用D5W，用Normal Saline
8. Strategies for prevention：see AHA guideline
