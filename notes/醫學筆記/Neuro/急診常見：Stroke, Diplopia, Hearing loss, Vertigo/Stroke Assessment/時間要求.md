# 時間要求

## 內文
**Since arrival at ED:**

D2D: 10min to see doctor

D2CT complete: 25min to CT

D2CT read: 45min to CT report

D2N: 60min to TPA

**Since Last normal time :**

Maximum 3/4.5 hours for TPA

Maximum 6 hours for IA thrombectomy

Maximum 24 hours for IA thrombectomy on patients with NIHSS > 6

- 注意：Fibrinolytic therapy會造成6.4%腦出血機率（一般人0.3％）
