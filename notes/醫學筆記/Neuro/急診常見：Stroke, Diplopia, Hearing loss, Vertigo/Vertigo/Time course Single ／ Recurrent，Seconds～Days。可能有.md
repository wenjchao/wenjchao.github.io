# Time course : Single / Recurrent，Seconds～Days。

## 摘要

可能有 Constant imbalance 但不可能有 Constant vertigo lasting month (人腦會習慣)，須考慮其他 D/D（注意病人說的一直暈可能是很多次暈）。下面這些只是參考，不一定準。

## 內文

1. Recurrent vertigo lasting under one minute ⇒ BPPV
2. Recurrent episodes last hours, seldom bilateral ⇒ Meniere disease / Vestibular migraine
3. Prolonged, severe episodes, can last for days ⇒ Infarction of the brainstem or cerebellum / Multiple sclerosis
4. Prolonged, severe episodes, can last for days ⇒ Vestibular neuritis
5. Single episode, minutes~hours, could be bilateral ⇒ Transient ischemia of labyrinth or brainstem / Vestibular migraine
