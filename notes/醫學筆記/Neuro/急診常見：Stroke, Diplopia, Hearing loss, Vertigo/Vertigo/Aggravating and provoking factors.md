# Aggravating and provoking factors

## 內文
1. moving head(100% 加重因素)
2. visual vertigo(visuo-vestibular mismatch)：看複雜的花紋 / 大螢幕 會誘發vertigo
3. Provoking history：
   1. Trauma? ⇒ BPPV / Fractures of Temporal bone / Persistent Postural Perceptual Dizziness
   2. Hyperextension injury? ⇒ Vertebral artery dissection(with neck pain) & Brainstem / Labyrinthine ischemia
   3. Viral symptoms? ⇒ Acute vestibular neuritis
   4. Caused by coughing, sneezing, exertion, or loud noises? ⇒ Perilymphatic fistula
