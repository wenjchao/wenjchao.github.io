## 內文
Associated with **Neurologic signs** ( Cranial Nerve, Muscle power, Gait, Finger-nose-finger, diplopia, dysarthria, dysphagia, numbness )

1. Brainstem or Cerebellar infarction and hemorrhage / Multiple sclerosis：**Acute onset & sustained，** History of **HTN, DM, Smoking, Vascular disease**
2. Vestibular migraine：Headache, Aura, Photophobia, Phonophobia
3. Others: Chiari malformation / Episodic ataxia type 2
