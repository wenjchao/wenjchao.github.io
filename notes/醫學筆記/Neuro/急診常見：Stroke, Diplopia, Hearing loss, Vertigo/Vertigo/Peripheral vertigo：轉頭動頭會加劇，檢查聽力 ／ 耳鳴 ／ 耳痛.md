# Peripheral vertigo：轉頭動頭會加劇，檢查聽力 / 耳鳴 / 耳痛

## 內文
Associated with **Audiologic symptoms**: abnormal hearing / tinnitus / ear pain

1. Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo(BPPV)：Episodic，Dix-Hallpike maneuver / Particle repositioning maneuvers
2. Meniere disease：Aural fullness 覺得耳朵充滿水
3. Vestibular neuritis：**Acute onset & sustained**，<u>viral</u> infection?
4. Herpes zoster oticus (Ramsay Hunt syndrome)
5. Aminoglycoside toxicity：Maybe **bilateral**
6. Otitis media
7. Labyrinthine concussion
8. Others: Acoustic neuroma / Recurrent vestibulopathy / Perilymphatic fistula
