# Bell’s palsy (idiopathic facial palsy)：額頭沒事就是中央出事

## 內文
spontaneous peripheral facial paralysis

[[Bell's palsy (idiopathic facial palsy)：額頭沒事就是中央出事/Etiology：nerve inflammation|摘要]]

### Clinical feature

Unilateral facial paralysis (upper + lower portion)，皺紋變少

1. onset: sudden, in hours
2. worsening: from few days～3 weeks
3. improve: in less than 4 months

- 注意：Time course 很重要

Maybe associatied with

1. impairment in taste
2. hyperacusis(聽覺過敏)
3. decreased tearing of the eye
4. additional cranial neuropathy(local viral or inflammatory spread)(\***atypical**)

- 注意：用 lower 或是 upper + lower paralysis 判斷 central 或是 peripheral lesion 大部分時候都是對的，不過也有可能有例外，造成相反的結果（partial lesion of facial nerve / nucleus / tract）

Lab & Image are only for atypical presentation

1. Multiple cranial neuropathies ex: ipsilateral new hearing loss
2. Skin finding ex: rash, vesicles
3. Wrong time course

### Differential Diagnosis

**Stroke** : very sudden onset, forehead-sparing weakness

**Herpes zoster** : burning sensation, ear pain , vesicles

**Lyme disease** : bilateral palsy, in Lyme-endemic areas

**Otitis media, mastoiditis, and cholesteatoma**: 耳鏡進去看

**Guillain-Barré syndrome** : limb weakness / low DTR , CSF protein(albumin)

**HIV / Sarcoidosis / Sjögren syndrome / Tumor**

### Treatment

Short-term course of **oral glucocorticoids** : **prednisone** (60 to 80 mg/day) for one week

Artificial tear drops if tear loss

Antiviral therapy(controversial): valacyclovir
 1000 mg 3 times daily for one week

Prognosis : 70% recover spontaneously, 85% under glucocorticoids
