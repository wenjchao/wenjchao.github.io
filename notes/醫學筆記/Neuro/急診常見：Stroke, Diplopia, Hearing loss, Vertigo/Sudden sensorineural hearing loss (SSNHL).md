# Sudden sensorineural hearing loss (SSNHL)

## 內文
Criteria: Diagnosed when 3/3, Probably when 2/3

1. sensorineural : inner ear or CN8 problem
2. sudden onset: in 3 days
3. loss of 30 dB over 3 frequencies (consecutive, pure-tone)

### Clinical feature

1. 人數統計：1/2 acute hearing loss, 1/2 hearing loss upon awakening, few with rapid progresive hearing loss
2. Sensation of a **blocked ear** or **aural fullness** at first
3. tinnitus: 90%, vertigo: 20~60%

### Evaluation

1. **Distinguish sensorineural from conductive hearing loss:**

   [[Sudden sensorineural hearing loss (SSNHL)/測聽力：搓手指或是whisper test|摘要]]

   [[Sudden sensorineural hearing loss (SSNHL)/sensorineural or conductive Weber and Rinne tests|摘要]]
2. History:
   1. trauma? exposure to loud noise? barotrauma(壓力性損傷)?
   2. vertigo or tinnitus?
   3. ear pain, ear drainage, fever? ⇒ otoscopic(for acute/chronic otitis media)
   4. ototoxic drugs: aminoglycosides, loop diuretics, platinum-based chemotherapy, salicylates
   5. others: eye pain or redness ⇒ Cogan syndrome; exposure to Lyme disease; history or other symptoms consistent with autoimmune disease or vasculitis
3. PE:
   1. external ear: rash, infection, or trauma?
   2. otoscopic: obstructing cerumen or foreign body, AOM/COM (acute/chronic otitis media), perforated tympanic membrane, serous otitis, or cholesteatoma?
   3. NE
4. **Imaging: MRI preferred, no lab indicated**

### Treatment

1. Glucocorticoids: 2週內開始用，prednisone 60 mg orally once daily for 10 days。最久可用6~8週。
