# AKI

## 內文

### Prerenal：水太少 ⇒ 留鈉留水放廢物 ⇒ FeNa < 1%, BUN/Cre >20, Uosm > 500, 超音波看IVC

### Renal (intrinsic) 尿少 + Cast

1. AIN（Acute interstitial nephritis，即ATIN）：**<u>Drugs</u>** (antibiotic, NSAIDs, PPI, sulfa-drugs, diuretics)
2. ATN（Acute tubular necrosis）：Renal **<u>ischemia</u>** (hypotension, shock, sepsis, hemorrhage, HF，即 Prerenal ⇒ Intrinsic) or Toxin (aminoglycosides, cisplatin, radiocontrast, lead)
3. RPGN
4. Vasculitis: **<u>MAHA</u>** (TTP / HUS / DIC / Malignant HTN), ANCA-related vasculitis

### Postrenal：hydronephrosis ± 漲大的膀胱 ⇒ 超音波
