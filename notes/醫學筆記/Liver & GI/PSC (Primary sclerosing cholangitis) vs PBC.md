# PSC (Primary sclerosing cholangitis) vs PBC (Primary biliary cholangitis)

## 內文
兩者皆須考慮 transplant（皆無法治癒）

### PBC (Primary biliary cholangitis)

1. <u>**女多**</u>，40-60歲
2. <u>**AMA**</u> (anti-mitochodrial antibody)、ANA
3. Intra-hepatic，眼瞼上有xanthoma
4. 與 <u>**Autoimmune disease**</u> 相關 ex: RA, Siogren, Systemic sclerosis
5. UDCA 治療改善預後

### PSC (Primary sclerosing cholangitis)

1. <u>**男多**</u>，40歲 & 70歲 雙高峰
2. <u>**P-ANCA**</u>、ANA
3. Intra & Extra-hepatic，ERCP呈多灶性狹窄與珠狀(multifocal structuring and beading)
4. 與 <u>**Cholangiocarcinoma**</u>、Ulcerative colitis 相關（還有其他癌症）
5. UDCA 治療無法改善預後，但可以改善 lab data

- IgG4-related sclerosing cholangitis: features similar to PSC, accompanied by IgG4-related autoimmune pancreatitis
- Autoimmune hepatitis: <u>**ASMA(+) / Anti-LKM1(+)**</u>
