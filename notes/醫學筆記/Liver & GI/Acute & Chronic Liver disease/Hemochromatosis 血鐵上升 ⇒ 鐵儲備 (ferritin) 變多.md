# Hemochromatosis: 血鐵上升 ⇒ 鐵儲備 (ferritin) 變多

## 內文
1. HFE gene defect (hereditary, ⇒ iron GI uptake ↑) or iron overload (secondary, in thalassemiama, sickle-cell anemia, sideroblastic anemia)
2. Serum iron ↑，Serum ferritin ↑，TIBC ↓ (Total iron-binding capacity, 即 transferrin)，Transferrin saturation ↑
3. Related diasease
   1. liver: hepatomegaly, cirrhosis, HCC
   2. pancreas: DM
   3. skin: hyperpigmentation, bronze skin
   4. heart: cardiac siderosis ⇒ arrthmyias, heart failure
