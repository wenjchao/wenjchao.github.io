# Alcoholic liver disesase: AST > ALT，AST & ALT < 500

## 內文
1. Disease course: Alcoholic <u>fatty liver</u> (reversible) ⇒ Alcoholic <u>hepatitis</u> (reversible in mild cases) ⇒ Alcohol-related <u>cirrhosis</u> (irreversible)⇒ <u>HCC</u>
2. Pathophysiology: drgradation of alcohol ⇒ NADH excess ⇒ G3P & fatty acid ↑ ⇒ TG↑ ⇒ Steatohepatitis
3. **<u>AST > ALT</u>、<u>γ-GT↑</u>、<u>Macrocytic anemia</u>** **(non-megaloblastic)、** **<u>Serum ferritin↑</u>**
   - Nonmegaloblastic macrocytic anemia: toxic effects of alcohol & liver disease on bone marrow
4. Treatment: <u>Glucocorticoid (急性期)</u>, Alcohol cessation, Nutritional support (B1, Zn)
5. Complication: sepsis, cirrhosis, AKI, hepatorenal syndrome, psy-related
   - be careful if SIRS ( <u>Fever</u> / <u>HR>90</u> / <u>RR>20 or PaCO2<32</u> / <u>WBC>12k</u> 四取二)
6. Mallory bodies under H&E staining
7. Other organ damage following <u>chronic alcohol use</u>：Gastritis, Malabsorption, Chronic pancreatitis, Wernicke-Korsakoff syndrome
8. <u>Acute alcohol withdrawal</u>: tremor, agitation, tachycardia, alcohol withdrawal seizures, alcohol withdrawal delirium
