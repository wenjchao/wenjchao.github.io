# Precipitating factors of acute liver failure：基本上就是下面這些 hepatitis 分類

## 內文
1. Acute viral hepatitis infection
2. HBV reactivation
3. Drug-induced liver injury
4. Alcoholic hepatitis
5. Vascular disorders ex: Budd-chiari syndrome, ischemic hepatitis
6. Storage disease ex: Wilson disease
7. Pregnancy related: HELLP, acute fatty liver of pregnancy
8. Malignant infiltration: lymphoma, metastases
