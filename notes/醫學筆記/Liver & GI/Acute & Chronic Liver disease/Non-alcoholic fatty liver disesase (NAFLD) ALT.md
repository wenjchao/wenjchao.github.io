# Non-alcoholic fatty liver disesase (NAFLD): ALT > AST，AST & ALT < 500

## 內文
1. NAFLD ⇒ NASH (steatohepatitis) ⇒ NASH cirrhosis ⇒ HCC
2. Etiology: <u>**Obesity**</u>, <u>**Type 2 DM**</u> (metabolic syndrome) ⇒ insulin resistence ⇒ hepatic uptake of fatty acid ⇒ TG accumulation
3. rule out other hepatitis
4. The reversal of the AST/ALT ratio to values > 1 may indicate progression to cirrhosis
