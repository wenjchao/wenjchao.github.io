# Liver enzyme

## 內文
### **Liver Damage：ALT, AST**

1. ALT：L for Liver ⇒ <u>**病毒性肝炎、藥物性肝炎、缺血性肝炎**</u>
2. AST：<u>**酒精性肝炎**</u>、AMI / CVA / Rhabdomyolysis

### Biliary tract / canaliculi damage：ALP, γ-GT

1. ALP (Alkaline phosphatase)：存在 bile canalicular membrane 中，GI / Liver / <u>**Bile**</u> / Kidney / Bone / Placenta / Any tumor 均有關
2. γ-GT(GGT)：存在 bile duct 上皮細胞中，上升慢 ⇒ 慢性<u>**膽道疾病**</u> ex: Cholangiocarcinoma, CBD stone, PBC/PSC, Alcohol, Drug, DM

### Liver function for metabolism：PT / aPTT, Albumin

Liver function for throwing trash：Bilirubin

- 注意：肝炎：ALT & AST，膽的問題：ALP & γ-GT & D-Bilirubin
  肝硬化：PT / aPTT + Albumin + Bilirubin + Hepatic encephalopathy + ascities
