# Polyposis syndromes : 全部會癌化

## 內文
1. Familial adenomatous polyposis (FAP) : <u>**滿滿的 ademona**</u>, mutations in <u>***APC***</u> gene
2. Lynch syndrome (HNPCC, hereditary nonpolyposis colorectal cancer)：<u>**大而少的近端大腸 ademona**</u> (Lynch 1) + <u>endometrial / ovarian</u> / skin cancers (Lynch 2), mutations in <u>***MLH1, MSH2***</u> gene
3. Gardner syndrome: <u>**FAP**</u> + <u>**osseous**</u> and <u>**soft tissue**</u> tumors
4. Turcot syndrome: <u>**FAP or Lynch syndrome**</u> + malignant <u>**CNS**</u> tumor
5. Peutz-Jeghers syndrome: <u>**hamartoma**</u> & <u>breast</u> cancer
6. Juvenile polyposis syndrome: <u>**hamartoma**</u> in children
