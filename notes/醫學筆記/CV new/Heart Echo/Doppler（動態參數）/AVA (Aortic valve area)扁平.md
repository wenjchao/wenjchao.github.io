# AVA (Aortic valve area)扁平

1. [[醫學筆記/CV new/Heart Echo/AVA (Aortic valve area)|扁平]]

2. **<u>V2 max (Aortic valve maximal velocity)</u>**：直接量

3. [[醫學筆記/CV new/Heart Echo/V2 mean|扁平]]

4. **<u>Transvalvular pressure gradient（PG）</u>** = **4\*V^2** ，一樣分 Peak PG & Mean PG

5. Peak PG = 4\*V2 max^2

6. **<u>Mean PG</u>** (Mean aortic valve gradient) 把 PG 積分起來以後取平均
   - Mean PG > 4\*V mean^2 (平方的平均 > 平均的平方)
