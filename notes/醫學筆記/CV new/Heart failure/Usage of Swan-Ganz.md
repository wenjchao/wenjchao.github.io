# Usage of Swan-Ganz

## 摘要
直接量測 CVP、PAP、PCWP（>18 表 congestion）；衍生指標 PAPi（<0.9 表 RV failure）、TPG、DPG、PVR；附 pulmonary hypertension 鑑別補充。

## 內文
![](../圖片/Untitled-31.png)

> Direct measurement

1. CVP (central venous pressure) = RAP
2. PAP(s/d/m, pulmonary artery systolic, diastolic and mean pressure)
3. **<u>PCWP</u>** (pulmonary capillary wedge pressure, 即 LAP, 即 LVEDP, 即 Preload) : if > 18, indicates **<u>congestion (cardiogenic shock)</u>**

> Indices

1. **<u>PAPi</u>** (Pulmonary artery pulsatility index) = (PAPs - PAPd)/RAP : if < 0.9, indicates **<u>RV failure</u>**
2. TPG (Transpulmonary pressure gradient) = PAPm - PCWP
3. DPG (Diastolic pulmonary gradient) = PAPd - PCWP
4. PVR (Pulmonary vascular resistance, Wood Unit) = TPG/CO

> 💡 無關的補充：Pulmonary hypertension D/D
