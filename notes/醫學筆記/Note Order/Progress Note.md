# Progress Note

## 內文
1. Background: Patient, Date, Ward, Time
2. Default condition: Important History of Illness, Reason for Admission, Active & Underlying disease
3. Recent events: Today’s new event, Subjective (病人主觀感受), Objective(Vitals, I/O, BW, Lab, Image, Rash size, Lymph node size, PE)
4. Assessments & Plans: all conditions & past events/efforts/results & future plans for that unsolved problem. **每個 Assessment 都要有應對的 Plans**
