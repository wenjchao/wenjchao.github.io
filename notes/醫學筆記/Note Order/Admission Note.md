# Admission Note

## 內文
1. Basic data
2. Chief complaint: Onset, Location, Duration, Character, Alleviating/Amplifying factors, Severity, Radiating to XXX
3. Present illness: Tell the story, put everything important in
4. Past Medical History & Family History & Social History & TOCC : Something that is not related to the Admission
5. (Birth history / maternal history)
6. Psychosocial assessment / Nutritional screen / Fall risk assessment
7. PE & ROS: 至少 vital sign、Pain score、Consciousness
8. Labs & CXR & Patho & XXX
9. Assessment & Plan: 1 condition(Lab, CXR,…) / complaint / disease / problem + plans for that condition, another Ps for another A

補充

1. 同部門（包括部內轉科、科內教班、進出 ICU）用 On / Off sevice note，不同部用 Discharge / Admission note
2. Discharge note：注意 Co-mobidity & Complication、理學檢查的改變。住院過程言簡意賅
