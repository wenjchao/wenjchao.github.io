# 1. Canadian CT rule

## 內文
1. 不適用者(不適用不代表不能照，只是當初設計rule的時候排除這些人)：
   1. Non-trauma case
   2. under anticoagulation （實務上很可能需要照）
   3. GCS < 13
   4. Age < 16-year-old
   5. Obvious open skull fracture
2. High Risk: Rules out need for neurosurgical intervention
   1. GCS score < 15 at 2 hours after injury
   2. Vomiting ≥ 2 episodes
   3. Suspect open or depressed skull fracture
   4. Any sign of basal skull fracture:
      1. Hemotympanum：鼓室積血，耳鏡進去耳膜內有血
      2. CSF otorrhea / rhinorrhea：<mark>β-2 transferrin</mark> / Glucose test
      3. “Racoon” eyes：periorbital ecchymosis, associated with edema, indicate fracture of <mark>anterior cranial fossa</mark> of the skull
      4. Battle’s sign：mastoid ecchymosis (behind the ear), indicate fracture of <mark>middle cranial fossa</mark> of the skull
   5. Age ≥ 65-year-old
3. Medium Risk: Rules out “clinically important” brain injury (Positive CT's that normally require admission)
   1. Amnesia before impact ≥ 30 min
   2. Dangerous mechanism
      1. pedestrian struck by vehicle
      2. occupant ejected from motor vehicle
      3. fall from elevation ≥ 3 feet or 5 stairs
      4. other dangerous mechanisms
