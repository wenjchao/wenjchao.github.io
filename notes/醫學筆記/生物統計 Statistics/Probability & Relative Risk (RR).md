# Probability & Relative Risk (RR)

## 內文
1. Probability of 有病 given ⊕：$\frac{a}{a+b}$ ，亦即 PPV、precision
   Probability of 有病 given ⊖：$\frac{c}{c+d}$
   Probability of 有病 in all population：$\frac{a+c}{a+b+c+d}$
   - Relative Risk of 有病 to ⊕：$\frac{a/(a+b)}{c/(c+d)}$
2. Probability of 沒病 given ⊕：$\frac{b}{a+b}$
   Probability of 沒病 given ⊖：$\frac{d}{c+d}$ ，亦即 NPV
   Probability of 沒病 in all population：$\frac{b+d}{a+b+c+d}$
