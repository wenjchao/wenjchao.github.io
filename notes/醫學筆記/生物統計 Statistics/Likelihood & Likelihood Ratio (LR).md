# Likelihood & Likelihood Ratio (LR)

## 內文
1. Likelihood ⊕ 有病：$\frac{a}{a+c}$ ，亦即 sensitivity、recall
   Likelihood ⊕ 沒病：$\frac{b}{b+d}$
   Likelihood Ratio ⊕ ：$\frac{a/(a+c)}{b/(b+d)}$
2. Likelihood ⊖ 有病：$\frac{c}{a+c}$
   Likelihood ⊖ 沒病：$\frac{d}{b+d}$ ，亦即 specificity
   Likelihood Ratio ⊖ ：$\frac{c/(a+c)}{d/(b+d)}$
