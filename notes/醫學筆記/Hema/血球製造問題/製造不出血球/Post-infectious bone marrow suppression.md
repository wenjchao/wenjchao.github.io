# Post-infectious bone marrow suppression

## 內文
Post-infectious（mostly viral, ex: EBV）：viral marrow suppression。bacterial sepsis → endotoxin suppression

- Post-infectious neutropenia／thrombocytopenia：感染風險 low，marrow reserve normal／mild decrease（[[醫學筆記/Hema/Neutropenia/Infection risk by type of neutropenia]]）

- 注意：infection → neutropenia？neutropenia → infection？還是無關？

1. Viral：RSV、influenza、parvovirus、EBV、HHV6
2. Bacterial：typhoid、Shigella、tuberculosis、brucellosis、tularemia
3. Rickettsial、malaria
