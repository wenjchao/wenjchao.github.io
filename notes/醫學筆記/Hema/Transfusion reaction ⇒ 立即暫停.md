# Transfusion reaction ⇒ 立即暫停

## 內文
- 注意：輸血前可以給 Diphenhydramine (Vena) ，可以防止 ATR

1. **Acute hemolytic transfusion reaction**(< 24hr): <u>**Type 2**</u> <u>**hypersensitivity**</u> (antigen 攻擊細胞) ⇒
   - 注意：通常是血型不合（ABO or Non-ABO antigen），但也可能是其他因素造成 RBC destruction (mechanical, thermal, or osmolar injuries)
   1. intravascular hemolysis: jaundice, hemoglobinuria, renal failure, dyspnea/tachypnea(因為anemia), DIC
   2. shock: hypotension, tachycardia,其餘 shock 症狀（ex: renal, brain, respiratory distress）
   3. Fever, chills, nausea, flushing
   4. Burning pain at the IV site
   5. Pruritus, urticaria
   - 注意：Lab:
     1. Direct Coomb’s test positive
     2. anemia, thromocytopenia & coagulopathy (if DIC)
     3. jaundice: ↑ indirect bilirubin, ↑ LDH, ↓ haptoglobin
     4. electrolyte: 見Massive transfusion-associated complications
     5. <u>**Repeat blood typing and crossmatching of patient and donor blood samples**</u>
     6. shock & fever & respiratory managenent
2. **<u>Anaphylactic</u>** **/** **<u>Minor allergic</u>** **transfusion reaction**: <u>**Type 1**</u> <u>**hypersensitivity**</u> (IgE ⇒ mast cell) ⇒
   1. <u>Anaphylactic</u> transfusion reaction ⇒ **shock**: hypotension, tachycardia,其餘 shock 症狀（ex: renal, brain, respiratory distress）
      - Treatment: 同 anaphylaxis ⇒ epinephrine, antihistamine, steroid
   2. <u>Minor allergic</u> transfusion reaction ⇒ cutaneous reaction: **Pruritus, urticaria**
      - Treatment: 同 allergic ⇒ **Diphenhydramine** (H1 antihistamine)
3. **Pulmonary transfusion complications** (呼吸都可能會爛掉)
   1. <u>**TRALI**</u> (Transfusion-related acute lung injury): 本來就在肺裡面的 neutrophil 接觸到外來物質 ⇒ **肺部大發炎**，破壞肺泡 & 肺間質 ⇒
      1. pulmonary edema ⇒ <u>ARDS (hypoxia) ⇒ oxygen / ventilator therapy</u>
      2. hypotension(水都到肺裡去了) & tachycardia
      3. fever & peripheral neutrophil count ↓ (都去肺了)
   2. <u>**TACO**</u> (Transfusion-associated circulatory overload): 輸太多液，fluid overload ⇒ **acute heart failure (LVEF ↓)**
      1. pulmonary edema & **pleural effusion** ⇒ <u>ARDS (hypoxia) ⇒ oxygen / ventilator therapy</u>
      2. hypertension / hypervolemia (S3 心音, jugular venous distension, peripheral edema)
4. **Massive transfusion-associated complications**:
   1. <u>hyperkalemia</u>
   2. <u>hypocalcemia</u>: 血包中citrate會蟄合鈣離子
   3. <u>hypothermia</u>
   4. <u>coagulopathy</u>: 所以大輸血要 RBC : FFP : platelet = 1 : 1 : 1
5. **Septic transfusion reaction**: 輸進來的血裡有污染細菌 ⇒ bacteremia
6. **Febrile nonhemolytic transfusion reaction**: most in children
   1. old donor RBC degrade ⇒ cytokine release
   2. refractory fever, bacteria-infection-like signs(ex: ≥ 2 SIRS criteria or qSOFA criteria)
   3. rule out all above transfusion reaction

### 前面都屬於 Acute reaction；以下屬於 delayed reaction

7. **Delayed hemolytic transfusion reaction**(>24hr): minor group antigen ex: Kidd or D (Rh)
   1. memory antibody response（需要時間召喚所以慢） to RBC antigen
   2. gradual, most commonly asymptomatic
   3. symptoms and signs: signs of mild hemolytic anemia (mild fever, jaundice, others signs of anemia)
   4. Direct Coomb’s test positive
8. **Post-transfusion purpura**(5~10 days): women with a history of <u>multiple pregnancies</u> or multiple transfusions
   1. memory antibody response（需要時間召喚所以慢） to platelet antigen
   2. Petechiae, purpura or other clinical features of thrombocytopenia
   3. normal coagulation profile
   4. PF4 antibody immunoassay: to rule out HIT(Heparin-induced thrombocytopenia) or VITT(Vaccine-induced immune thrombotic thrombocytopenia) in exposed patients
   5. treatment: IVIG & steroid(效果不確定)
9. Transfusion-associated graft-versus-host disease: in severely immunocompromised host. Almost always fatal.
