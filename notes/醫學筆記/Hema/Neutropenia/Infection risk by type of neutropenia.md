# Infection risk by type of neutropenia

## 內文
| Diagnosis | Risk | Marrow reserve | T/B immunity | Relation to ANC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chronic benign | Low to no risk | Normal | Normal | No |
| Duffy-null associated neutrophil count (DANC) | Low to no risk | Normal | Normal | No |
| Post infectious | Low to no risk | Normal／mild decrease | Normal | No／Yes |
| Hypersplenism | Low to no risk | Normal／increase | Depends on cause of hypersplenism | No |
| Transient neonatal neutropenia | Moderate risk | Variable | Normal | Yes |
| Post infectious | Moderate risk | Decreased | Normal | No／Yes |
| Drug induced | Moderate risk | Decreased／Normal | Normal | Yes |
| Cyclic | Moderate risk | Variable | Normal | Yes |
| Nutritional | Moderate risk | Variable | Normal／variable | Yes |
| Immune (antibody mediated) | Moderate risk | Normal | Abnormal | No |
| Immune (SLE, CVID) | Moderate risk | Variable | Abnormal T/B | No／Yes |
| LDLGL, PWCA | High risk | Very decreased to absent | Abnormal T cells | Yes |
| Shwachman | High risk | Decreased | Normal／variable | Yes |
| Kostmann | High risk | Decreased | Normal／variable | Yes |
| Drug induced | High risk | Very decreased to absent | Abnormal | Yes |
| Antineoplastic chemotherapy | High risk | Very decreased to absent | Abnormal T/B | Yes |
| Aplastic anemia | High risk | Decreased／absent | Probably abnormal T cells | Yes |
| Marrow infiltration/MDS/leukemia | High risk | Decreased／absent | Probably abnormal | Yes |

ANC: absolute neutrophil count; SLE: systemic lupus; CVID: common variable immunodeficiency; LDLGL: lymphoproliferative disorders of large granular lymphocytes; PWCA: pure white cell aplasia; MDS: myelodysplastic syndrome.
