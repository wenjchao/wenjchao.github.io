# Paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria: complement-mediated, Coombs test (-)

## 內文
1. PIGA mutation ⇒ 細胞外糖基磷脂裝不上去 ⇒ 被 <u>**complement**</u> 攻擊
2. **<u>pancytopenia</u>**, <u>**venous thrombosis**</u> (ex: Budd-Chiari syndrome)
3. intravascular hemolysis ⇒ Pink/red urine in morning
4. Treatment: eculizumab (anti-C5), 不可用 steroid
