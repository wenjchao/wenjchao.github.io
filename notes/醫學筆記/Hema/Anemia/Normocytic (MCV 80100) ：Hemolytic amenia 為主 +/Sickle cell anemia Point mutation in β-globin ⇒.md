# Sickle cell anemia: Point mutation in β-globin ⇒ HbS ⇒ vaso-oclusion in low O2

## 內文
1. vaso-occlusive crises: Low O2 / acidosis ⇒ deoxygenated HbS polymerization ⇒ anemia, avascular necrosis, acute chest syndrome, stroke, 持續勃起
2. splenic infarct ⇒ infection by encapsulated bacteria (ex: salmonella ⇒ osteomyelitis)
3. Acute treatment: hydration (沖掉 vaso-oclusion), 輸血 / 給 folate, hydroxyurea
4. sickle cell trait have resistance to malaria.
