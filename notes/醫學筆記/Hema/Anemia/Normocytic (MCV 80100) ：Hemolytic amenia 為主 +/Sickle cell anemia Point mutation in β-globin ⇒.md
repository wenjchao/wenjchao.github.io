# Sickle cell anemia

## 內文
1. Mechanism: 
   1. Point mutation in β-globin 
   2. ⇒ HbS 
   3. ⇒ vaso-oclusion in low O2

2. Vaso-occlusive crises:
   1. Low O2 / acidosis 
   2. ⇒ deoxygenated HbS polymerization 
   3. ⇒ anemia, avascular necrosis, acute chest syndrome, stroke, 持續勃起

3. splenic infarct ⇒ infection by encapsulated bacteria (ex: salmonella ⇒ osteomyelitis)

- Acute treatment: hydration (沖掉 vaso-oclusion), 輸血 / 給 folate, hydroxyurea
- sickle cell trait have resistance to malaria
