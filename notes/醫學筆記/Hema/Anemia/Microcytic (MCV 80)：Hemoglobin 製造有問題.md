# Microcytic (MCV < 80)

## 摘要
Hemoglobin 製造有問題

## 內文
1. [[Microcytic (MCV 80)：Hemoglobin 製造有問題/Hb = Heme + globin = Iron + protophorphrin +|扁平]]
2. Iron defiency anemia：Iron 不足，RBC count 低
   - Plummer-Vinson syndrome：iron deficiency anemia + esophageal webs + dysphagia
3. Anemia of chronic disease: 有可能 normocytic，iron↓ 但 ferritin↑
   1. CKD 最後會變成 Iron defiency anemia (腸道吸收少了)
4. Sideroblastic anemia & Lead poisoning：protophorphrin 生產有問題
   1. <u>**Lead**</u> poisoning: Lead inhibits ALA dehydratase
   2. <u>**X-linked**</u> sideroblastic anemia: defect in ALA synthase
   3. Other reason: <u>MDS</u>, <u>Vit B6</u> deficiency, <u>isoniazid</u>, <u>linezolid</u>
   4. histology: <u>basophilic stippling</u> of RBCs
   5. Treatment: **<u>Vit B6</u>** (pyridoxine)
5. Thalassemia：Globin 生產有問題，RBC count 稍高
