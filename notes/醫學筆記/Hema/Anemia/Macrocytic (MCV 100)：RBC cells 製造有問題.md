# Macrocytic (MCV > 100)

## 摘要
RBC cells 製造有問題

## 內文
1. Megaloblastic anemia：DNA synthase 出問題

   [[Macrocytic (MCV 100)：RBC cells 製造有問題/⇒ low RPI & pancytopenia & homocystinuria|摘要|扁平]]
   1. Vit B9 (folate) deficiency：no neurologic symptoms

      [[Macrocytic (MCV 100)：RBC cells 製造有問題/原因：OCP、Drug (MTX, TMX, phenytoin)|摘要|扁平]]
   2. Vit B12 (cobalamin) deficiency：neurologic symptoms + methylmalonic acid
      - 給 Folate <u>**會治好 anemia**</u> 但是<u>**不會治好神經學**</u>症狀，要小心
      - 原因：<u>**Pernicious anemia**</u> (<u>Anti-parietal cell Ab</u>)、<u>**Gastrectomy**</u>、<u>**Crohn disease**</u>、<u>pancreatic</u> insufficiency（with animal protein，需要 pancreas 來切）
2. Nonmegaloblastic anemia: Bone marrow disease
   1. chronic <u>**alcohol**</u> overuse、<u>**liver**</u> disease
   2. Genetic: Diamond-Blackfan anemia (pure red cell aplasia，矮、craniofacial & upper extremity abnormalities)
