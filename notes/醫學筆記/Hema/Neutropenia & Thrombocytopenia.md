### 是什麼原因？

Common etiology for isolated neutropenia & thrombocytopenia

1. Post infectious (Mostly viral, ex: EBV)
2. Drug induced
3. Autoimmune（ITP & maternal-alloimmune 都在這裡）
   1. Steroid, IVIG ⇒ Rituximab
   2. 可以做 Direct Coombs test，原理同驗 AIHA（autoimmune hemolytic anemia）的方式
   - 注意：ITP + AIHA ⇒ Evan’s syndrome
4. Bone marrow failure：MDS/MPN、Leukemia/Lymphoma
5. Congenital (genetic)
6. Nutritional neutropenia (in chronically ill children): <u>folate</u>, <u>**B12**</u> <u>⇒ also important for neuro function & anemia</u>
