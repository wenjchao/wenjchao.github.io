# Uptodate: Important clinical signs to indicate good/bad bone marrow reserve

## 內文
<u>**Fever ⇒ Immediate treatment**</u>

1. Periodic fever ⇒ cyclic neutropenia
2. Recurrent fevers ⇒ more serious type of neutropenia, underlying autoimmune disease, or malignancy
3. ESR & CRP: inflammation state
4. [[Uptodate Important clinical signs to indicate/圖：Algorithm for neutropenic fever (Uptodate)|扁平]]

- 注意：Neutropenia 的病人只需要少少細菌就可以致病，所以可能 fever 但一直 culture 都 culture 不出來，需要用 ESR / CRP / Procalcitonin

<u>**Neutropenia with decreased marrow reserve**</u>

1. **Gingivitis**: Frequently present in untreated clinically significant chronic neutropenia. Normal gingiva argues for normal marrow reserve
2. **Mucosal ulcers**: especially ulcers with no evidence of exudate
3. Cramping **abdominal pain** or discomfort: even of a minor nature can indicate intestinal mucosal ulcerations that can serve as a dangerous portal of entry for enteric bacteria.
4. Past history: Unusual or frequent major **infections**, chronic **diarrhea**
5. **Dysmorphia** ⇒ primary marrow failure syndromes

<u>**Neutropenia with normal marrow reserve**</u>

1. **Abscess / Pus / Exudate** : Clear, purulent material anywhere (ear drum, pharyngeal exudate, boil, other abscess)
