# Bone marrow reserve?

## 內文
1. Only 3% of the body's neutrophils are circulating in the peripheral blood
2. ⇒ **bone marrow reserve pool** is the most critical determinant of propensity to infection
3. ⇒ if good **bone marrow reserve**, ANC doesn’t matter; if bad **bone marrow reserve**, ANC matters
4. Bone marrow aspiration / biopsy is the only direct way to evaluate Good OR Bad bone marrow reserve.
