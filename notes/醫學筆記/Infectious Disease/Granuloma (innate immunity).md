# Granuloma (innate immunity)

## 內文
- [[Granuloma (innate immunity)/Mechanism: IL-12 ⇒ Th1 ⇒ IFN-γ ⇒ Macrophage(M1) ⇒ Granuloma|扁平]]
- 同屬 Th1：IL-12 ⇒ Th1 ⇒ **<u>IL-2</u>** ⇒ **<u>cytotoxic T cell</u>** (CD8)
- 對比 Th2：**<u>IL-4 ⇒ Th2</u>** ⇒ IL-4, 5, 10, 13 ⇒ B-cell ⇒ plasma cell ⇒ **<u>antibody</u>**
- IFN-γ 抑制 Th2，IL-10 抑制 Th1 (Th1 & Th2 均為 CD4 T cell)
- Etiology of granuloma:
  1. Caseating granulomas: infection induced

     - [[Granuloma (innate immunity)/Mycobacteria(TB, NTM), Salmonella (intracellular pathogens), Parasites, Protozoa, Fungus (ex: cryptococcus), Tertiary syphilis|扁平]]
  2. Noncaseating granulomas
     1. Foreign body: **<u>implants, gout, dust / mineral inhaled</u>**
        1. asbestosis（石綿肺）
        2. Silicosis（矽肺病，礦場/工地工人吸入矽塵）
        3. Anthracosis / Coal workers’ pneumoconiosis （碳）
        4. Aluminosis（鋁） / Berylliosis（鈹） / Pulmonary siderosis（鐵）
        5. Organ dust toxic syndrome（農場）/ Byssinosis（棉塵症，紡織工人）
     2. Immune-mediated diseases: **<u>Sarcoidosis</u>**, Crohn disease, Primary biliary cholangitis
        - **Sarcoidosis:** CXR ⇒ lambda sign、Ga-67 scan ⇒ panda sign
     3. Vasculitis: **<u>Granulomatous with polyangiitis (c-ANCA), Eosinophilic granulomatous with polyangiitis (p-ANCA),</u>** Giant cell arteritis
  - 注意：Granuloma 會製造 1,25-OH vit D ⇒ 有機率 hypercalcemia
- Disorders
  1. Chronic granulomatous disease (CGD, XR or AR):
     1. congenital deficiency of superoxide production ⇒ impaired to digest microorganisms
     2. **<u>Catalase-positive organisms</u>:** S.aureus, E.coli, Klebsiella, Pseudomonas, Candida, Aspergillus, Serratia, Nocardia
     3. Treatment: **IFN-γ, steroid,** Life-long prophylactic antibiotics / transplant
  2. Chédiak-Higashi syndrome: lysosome-fusion disorder ⇒ **<u>pyogenic</u>** infections: S. pyogenes, S. aureus, Pseudomonas
  3. Leukocyte adhesion deficiency type 1 (LAD1) : integrin defect ⇒ defect in the leukocytic chemotaxis ⇒ **<u>nonsuppurative</u>** bacterial infections（neutrophil 到不了，無法殺敵產生pus）& **<u>Delayed separation of the umbilical cord</u>**
  - 注意：以上三個屬於 Phagocyte disorder
  4. Anti-IFN-γ autoantibodies (Adult-onset immunodeficiency): severe opportunistic infections such as **<u>disseminated nontuberculous mycobacterium</u>**
  5. IL-12 receptor deficiency (T cell disorder, AR): 原理同上，易 mycobacterial infection (& Salmonella)
  6. Complement deficiency (也算是innate): encapulated bacteria (因為需要打洞)
