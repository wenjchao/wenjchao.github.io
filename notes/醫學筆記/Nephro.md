## 內文
osteopenia ⇒ osteoclastic(ALP 高) OR osteoblastic(ALP 正常)

> 💡 Urinary Casts
>
> 1. Transparent hyaline casts: hypovolemia(prerenal AKI)
> 2. Granular “muddy brown” casts: ATN (Acute tubular necrosis, renal AKI)
> 3. WBC casts: AIN (Acute interstitial nephritis)
> 4. RBC casts: GN (Glomerulonephritis)
> 5. Waxy and broad casts: advanced CKD
