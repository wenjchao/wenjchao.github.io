# Increase Empathy Levels：Loving-kindness meditation（慈心冥想?）

## 內文
1. First, the practitioner sends this energy to themselves.
2. Next, they send positive energy to a friend or a close family member – someone who brings them joy.
3. Third, they choose someone that has caused them harm or causes them to feel annoyed or upset.
4. Finally, they send compassionate love and healing energy to their wider community and the world as a whole.
