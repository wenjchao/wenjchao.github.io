# Ethical codes of Counsellors and therapists 治療師守則

## 內文
1. Put clients first and make them your primary concern at all times;
   refrain from allowing personal interests to intrude in their wellbeing
2. Update your skills and knowledge and work in your area of competence
3. Refrain from false portrayal of your qualifications or experience
4. Collaborate with colleagues if required in order to help your client and, if your area of expertise does not cover their needs, refer them
   to another practitioner
5. Value each client as a unique person and decide on how you will work together
6. Display constancy, respect and value your client’s privacy at all times
7. Respect boundaries and keep within the framework of professionalism while displaying warmth and genuineness
8. Explain benefits, costs and features of the counselling therapy clearly
9. Avoid discriminating against clients on the basis of their gender, ethnicity, colour, economic or educational background
