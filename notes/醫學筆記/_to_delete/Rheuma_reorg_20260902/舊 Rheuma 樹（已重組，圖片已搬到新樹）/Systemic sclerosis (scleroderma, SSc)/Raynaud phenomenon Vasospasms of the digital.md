# Raynaud phenomenon: Vasospasms of the digital arteries and arterioles triggered by cold or emotional stress

## 內文
1. **triphasic presentation**: ischemic phase (white) ⇒ hypoxic phase (blue) ⇒ hyperemic phase (red)
2. reversible, for 15-20 min after removal of triggers (can be prolonged & ischemic ulcer in secondary RP if trigger not removed)
3. secondary triggers: drug (β-blocker), smoking, cryoglobulinemia(HCV), cold agglutinin disease, Waldenstrom macroglobulinemia, autoimmune disease
4. treatment: 保溫、CCB(nifedipine)
