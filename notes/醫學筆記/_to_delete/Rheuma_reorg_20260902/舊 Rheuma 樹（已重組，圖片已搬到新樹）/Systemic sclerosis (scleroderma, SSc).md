# Systemic sclerosis (scleroderma, SSc)

## 內文
重點表現：皮膚變厚硬化（先 edematous ⇒ fibrosis），從末端（手指腳趾）往軀幹長：

1. 變厚變硬 & 平順光滑
2. 手指：Limited range of motion
3. 臉：Loss of expression（mask-like）、皺紋變淺、嘴巴變小

- 注意：禁用 steroid，怕 renal crisis

1. Pathophysiology: noninflammatory vasculopathy & inflammatory fibrosis (TGF-β)

   [[Systemic sclerosis (scleroderma, SSc)/Raynaud phenomenon Vasospasms of the digital|摘要]]
2. **<u>Limited SSc</u>** (**<u>anti-centromere</u>**): CREST syndrome
   1. **<u>Years</u>** after Raynaud phenomenon
   2. 皮膚硬化停在四肢 & 臉 & 脖子（**軀幹沒有**）
   3. CREST syndrome: Calcinosis cutis (皮下鈣結晶), Raynaud phenomenon (皮膚遇冷發白), Esophageal hypomotility, Sclerodactyly (硬指症), Telangiectasia (皮膚微血管擴張)

      [[Systemic sclerosis (scleroderma, SSc)/Esophageal hypomotility：smooth muscle|摘要]]
   4. 其他：pulmonary hypertension (10%)
   - 注意：Limited SSc 除發展成 pulmonary hypertension 外，預後均不錯
3. **<u>Diffuse SSc</u>** (**<u>anti-Scl 70</u>**):
   1. **<u>weeks to months</u>** after Raynaud phenomenon
   2. **全身**皮膚硬化
   3. scleroderma renal crisis: <u>medical emergency</u>
      1. 腎動脈血管阻塞 ⇒ 大量 Renin ⇒ 惡性高血壓 ⇒ hypertensive crisis / AKI / MAHA (microangiopathic hemolytic anemia, TTP/HUS/HELLP)
      2. Treatment: ACE inhibitors
   4. interstitial lung disease ⇒ Cyclophosphamide
   - 注意：Diffuse SSc 預後差，最常死於 interstitial lung disease
