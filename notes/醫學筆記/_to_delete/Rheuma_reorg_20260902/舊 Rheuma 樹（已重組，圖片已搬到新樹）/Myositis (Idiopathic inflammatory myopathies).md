# Myositis (Idiopathic inflammatory myopathies): Dermatomyositis (CD4) / Polymyositis (CD8)

## 內文
### Dermatomyositis & Polymyositis 共同特徵

1. **Symmetrical**, **Proximal** weakness (對比：neuropathy 是 distal weakness)
   - Proximal ex: 爬樓梯、梳頭髮、站起來；Distal ex: 扣鈕扣
2. 眼球肌肉、臉部肌肉不受侵犯 (對比：Myasthenia gravis 會影響眼睛)
3. 食道肌肉侵犯多在**<u>上 1/3</u>** (對比：**<u>Systemic sclerosis</u>** 侵犯**<u>下 1/3</u>** 的食道)
4. Morning stiffness (對比：Myasthenia gravis 是活動之後傍晚會累)
5. myositis 診斷要件：Polymyositis 需要 4中4，Dermatomysitis 需要 4中3 + skin rash 3中1
   1. Proximal muscle weakness
   2. Elevated muscle enzyme: **CK** / ASL, LT, LDH, aldolase
   3. biopsy: Muscle fiber damage & Inflammatory cellular infiltrates
   4. abnormal EMG (這條可以用 MRI / autoantibody 取代 )
6. Antibodies survey:
   1. Anti-Jo-1: intersitial lung disease
   2. anti-Mi-2: Dermatomyositis
   3. anti-PM/Scl: Polymyositis + Systemic sclerosis overlapping

   [[Myositis (Idiopathic inflammatory myopathies)/other autoantibodies|摘要]]
7. Treatment: Steroid(小心 steroid myopathy) ⇒ Aza / MMF / MTX ⇒ IVIG ⇒ Rituximab / Cyclosporin
   - Cyclophosphamide for intersitial lung disease

### Special for Dermatomyositis

1. Biopsy: Perimysial inflammation with **<u>CD4+ cell</u>** infiltration (可能還有 B cell)
   - 對比 Polymyositis: Endomysial inflammation with **<u>CD8+ cell</u>** infiltration

   [[Myositis (Idiopathic inflammatory myopathies)/Perimysial vs Endomysial|摘要]]
2. skin rash 3中1：
   1. Heliotrope: 上眼瞼浮腫性紅班
   2. Gottron’s sign: 手指關節伸側角質萎縮性紅斑
   3. 肢體伸側紅斑
   4. 其他不算 criteria 的 skin rash: 前胸 V sign, 後肩頸背 Shawl sign, Mechanical hand (手指粗糙角質化)

   [[Myositis (Idiopathic inflammatory myopathies)/圖|摘要]]
3. **<u>Dermatomyositis 須可能由癌症 (breast & lung) 引發，需 survey</u>**

### Differential diagnosis for myopathy

1. Inflammatory myopathy: Dermatomyositis, Polymyositis, Inclusion body myositis
2. Drug-induced myopathy: steroid, statin, alcohol, cocaine
3. **Polymylagia rheumatica**: **Giant cell arteritis** associated, symmetric pain in the shoulder, neck, and thigh (proximal muscles)
4. Others: Hypothyroid myopathy, fibromyalgia(神經痛，肌肉沒事)
