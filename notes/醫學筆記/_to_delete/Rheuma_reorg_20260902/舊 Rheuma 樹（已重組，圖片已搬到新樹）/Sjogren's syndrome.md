# Sjogren’s syndrome

## 內文
1. Symptoms
   1. **<u>Sicca syndrome</u>: 口乾舌燥 / 眼乾 > 3 個月**、呼吸道 & 消化道 & 陰道 黏膜萎縮
      1. 在所有 Sjogren’s syndrome 病人中，2/3 只有口乾眼乾
      2. Schirmer’s test: 濾紙放眼睛，< 5 mm in 5 min 即為乾眼症
      3. 需排除其他原因 ex: 頸部放療、lymphoma、sarcoidosis、HIV、HCV、Anti-cholinergic drug
   2. Non-erosive arthritis
   3. Interstitial lung disease
   4. RTA type 1 (renal tubular acidosis)
   5. B-cell lymphomas ex: MALT lymphoma
   6. neonatal lupus：SLE + anti-Ro/SSA or anti-La/SSB
   7. Reynaud’s phenomenon
   8. autoimmune hepatitis, primary sclerosing cholangitis
2. Lab
   1. anti-Ro/SSA or anti-La/SSB
   2. minor gland biopsy: lymphocyte infiltration
3. Treatment
   1. 人工淚液 / 口水
   2. oral muscarinic agonist
   3. arthritis: Hydroxychloroquine / MTX
   4. vasculitis: steroid + cyclophosphamide
