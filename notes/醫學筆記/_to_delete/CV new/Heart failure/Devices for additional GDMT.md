# Devices for additional GDMT

## 摘要
CRT（LBBB + QRS 變寬）、ICD（唯一能防猝死）、末期機械循環輔助與移植、瓣膜置換（TAVI > SAVR for high risk）。

## 內文
1. CRT(同步收縮): for LBBB+QRS widening>150 or 120 ms with symptoms
2. ICD: 唯一能prevent sudden death 的方法(amiodarone無效)，特別在low EF (健保不允許primary prevention)
3. 末期: mechanical circulation support & transplant(transplant prognosis: 10-y survival > 60%)
4. Valve replacement for valve disease: TAVI > SAVR for surgical high risk
