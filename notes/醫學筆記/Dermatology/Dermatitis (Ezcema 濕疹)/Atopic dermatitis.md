# Atopic dermatitis

## 內文
診斷的criteria:

Family history

Flexor side（小孩其實常見extensor side)

Chronic

非常癢

形狀：pink patch，無屑，with papules，border不明顯

Candle sign negative

Risk factor: 小孩、青少年、有過敏史、皮膚過於乾燥

Special treatment: 乳液

新藥：anti-Interlukin 4/13 (貴)、Jak inhibitor(貴)(口服)

注意抓痕與傷口(常沿抓痕成直線)，平常就會癢(表面看不出來)，抓了以後才會紅跟破皮

Type I hypersensitivity, associated with other atopic diseases (asthma, allergic rhinitis, food allergies); high serum IgE
