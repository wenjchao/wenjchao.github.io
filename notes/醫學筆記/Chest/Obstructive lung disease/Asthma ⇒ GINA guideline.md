2. 目前 guideline：ICS + LABA 最重要
3. ICS：**<u>Fluticasone</u>**、**<u>Budesonide</u>**
4. 支氣管擴張劑：
   1. SABAs (Short-Acting b2 Agonists ): **<u>Salbutamol</u>**、**<u>Terbutaline</u>**、**<u>Fenoterol</u>**
   2. SAMAs (Short-Acting Antimuscarinic Agents)：**<u>Ipratropium</u>**
   3. LABAs (Long-Acting b2 Agonists)：**<u>Salmeterol</u>**、**<u>Formoterol</u>**
   4. LAMAs (Long-Acting Antimuscarinic Agents)：**<u>Tiotropium</u>**、**<u>Glycopyrronium</u>**
5. 更高階用藥：anti-IgE (omalizumab)、anti-IL5/5R、anti-IL4R
6. 其他急性救命用藥：MgSO4（平滑肌舒張）、Epinephrine
