# COPD ⇒ GOLD guideline

## 內文
1. [[COPD ⇒ GOLD guideline/GOLD guideline|扁平]]
2. Diagnostic criteria: FEV1/FVC < 0.7
3. etiology & pathophysiology: chronic bronchitis(airway) and/or emphysema(alveoli)
3. COPD acute exacerbation: 14天內急性症狀加重（dyspnea / sputum / cough）
4. Treatment: LABA + LAMA (dual bronchodilator) 最重要

   [[COPD ⇒ GOLD guideline/要不要加 ICS：看紅綠燈|摘要]]
   - non-pharmalogical: long term oxygen, 正壓呼吸, 呼吸復健
5. 其他
   1. Pre-COPD: FEV1/FVC > 0.7 但有下呼吸道症狀
      - 注意：可能已經有其他問題(ex: emphysema / gas trapping / hyperinflation / DLCO↓)
   2. PRISm: FEV1/FVC > 0.7 但 Predicted FEV1 or FVC < 80%
   3. Smoking COPD ⇒ 戒菸
   4. Non-smoking COPD
      1. More common in females & younger
      2. Lesser rate of decline in lung function
      3. small airways obstruction, less emphysema
      4. Sputum: neutrophils↓, eosinophil ↑
      5. Similar to smoking COPD: similar defect in macrophage phagocytosis, similar spirometric indices, similar symptoms
