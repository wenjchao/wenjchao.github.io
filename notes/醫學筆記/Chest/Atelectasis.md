# Atelectasis

## 內文
1. Etiology
   1. Obstructive（氣管塞住） ⇒ airway obstruction ex: tumor, foreign body, asthma, infectious bronchitis / pneumonia
   2. Passive（Changes in intrapleural pressure，即肺外的問題，肺張不開）ex: large pleural effusion, pneumothorax, hemothorax
   3. Compressive（肺內的問題，肺張不開）ex: mass, bullae
   4. Adhesive: 缺乏 surfactant
   5. Cicatricial (fibrotic scar) ex: TB, sarcoidosis, 放射線治療
2. CXR: trachea 往哪邊偏？⇒ lesion side 是 collapse 還是脹大
   1. 往白掉的那邊偏：lesion side collapse ex: airway obstruction, pneumonectomy
   2. 沒有偏：原地 consolidation ex: pneumonia
   3. 往正常的那邊偏：lesion side 東西太多脹出來 ex: massive pleural effusion
   - 注意：注意 pneumothorax 有問題的部分是黑掉不是白掉，要反過來
3. Other signs on CXR:
   1. Crowding of the bronchovascular markings. 有/沒有 air bronchogram?
   2. Displacement of the interlobar fissures
   3. Compensatory overinflation of remaining lung
   4. Elevation of hemidiaphragm
