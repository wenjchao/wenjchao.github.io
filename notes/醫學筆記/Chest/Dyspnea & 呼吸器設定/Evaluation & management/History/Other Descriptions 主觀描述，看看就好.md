# Other Descriptions: 主觀描述，看看就好

## 內文
1. air hunger ⇒ COPD / asthma / heart failure / pulmonary embolism/ restricted thoracic motion / any acute hypercapnia
2. mind the chest tightness, might be mild dyspnea ex: asthma
3. increased work of breathing ⇒ COPD / severe asthma / myopathy / pulmonary fibrosis
4. cannot get a deep breath ⇒ COPD
5. sensation of rapid, shallow breathing ⇒ reduced lung compliance
6. suffocation(窒息) ⇒ heart failure
