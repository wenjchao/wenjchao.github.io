# Differential diagnosis: Lung*2 Heart*1 Vascular*1 Stomach*2, Musculoskeletal, Anxiety, Herpes zoster, Anemia

## 內文
1. Lung:
   1. **<u>(Tension) pneumothorax</u>**
   2. **<u>Pulmonary embolism</u>**
   3. Bronchitis, Pneumonia, Pleuritis
2. Heart:
   1. **<u>ACS(Acute Coronary Syndrome)</u>**
   2. pericarditis, cardiomyopathies (<u>Takotsubo</u>, dilated, hypertrophic and restrictive)
   3. Tachyarrhythmias
   4. Acute heart failure
   5. Aortic valve stenosis
   6. Hypertensive emergencies, Coronary spasm, Cardiac trauma
3. Vascular:
   1. **<u>Aortic dissection</u>**
   2. Symptomatic aortic aneurysm
   3. Stroke
4. Stomach :
   1. **<u>GERD</u>**, **<u>PPU</u>**
   2. Esophagus ruptured
   3. Esophageal spasm
   4. Pancreatitis, Cholecystitis
5. Orthopaedic:
   1. **<u>Musculoskeletal disorders</u>**
   2. Chest trauma
   3. Muscle injury/inflammation, Costochondritis, Cervical spine pathologies
6. Others:
   1. **<u>Anxiety disorders</u>**
   2. **<u>Herpes zoster</u>**
   3. **<u>Anemia</u>**
   4. Acute chest syndrome in sickle cell disease
