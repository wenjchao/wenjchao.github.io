# Aortic dissection / aortic aneurysm

## 內文
- Severe, abrupt pain + history of hypertension

2. Sensitive but not specific: 沒中rule out，有中不一定rule in
   1. d-dimer
3. Specific but not sensitive: 都沒中才可以rule out
   1. tearing pain
   2. mediastinal widening
   3. asymmetric pulse

⇒ very critical if hypotension, control BP if hypertension
