# Pericarditis

## 內文
1. associated with other pericardial syndromes: pericardial effusion, cardiac tamponade, constrictive pericarditis
2. etiology: cardiac trauma( & post-MI), infection cause(viral/bacterial/TB), non-infection cause(metastasis, connective tissue disease, uremia), drug
3. **findings: 1 of 3**
   1. left parasternal pericardial friction rub
   2. EKG change: widespread ST-elevation or PR depression
   3. pericardial effusion
4. assessment: ECG & echocardiogram & chest X-ray
   1. blood test: CRP + troponin + BUN(uremia) + CDC/DC +
   2. Diagnostic pericardiocentesis for infection/malignant if needed

   [[Pericarditis/empiric treatment high dose NSAID + colchicine|摘要]]
