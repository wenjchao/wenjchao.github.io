# Esophageal reflux ⇒ PPI

## 內文
1. postprandial(餐後) burning sensation, regurgitation
2. dysphagia, odynophagia(吞嚥痛)
3. water brash(口水很多), acid reflux
4. extraesophageal symptoms (eg, chronic cough, hoarseness, wheezing)
5. 喝咖啡、吃甜食

- **red flag(for gastrointestinal malignancy): gastrointestinal bleeding, anemia, weight loss, persistent vomiting, family history**
