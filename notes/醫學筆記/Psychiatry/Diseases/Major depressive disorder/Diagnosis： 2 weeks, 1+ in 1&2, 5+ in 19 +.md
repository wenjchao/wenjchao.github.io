# Diagnosis：> 2 weeks, 1+ in 1&2, 5+ in 1~9 + functional loss

## 內文
除第九項自殺外，其他症狀需 most of the day, nearly every day

1. **Depressed mood**: may be <u>subjective</u> (sad, empty, hopeless) **OR** <u>observed</u> (tearful) by others
   1. in children and adolescents, can be irritable mood
2. **Loss of** <u>**interest**</u> **/ pleasure** in all activities 喪失興趣
3. **Weight loss or gain** : <u>weight loss/gain</u> **OR** <u>decrease/increase in appetite</u>
   1. in children, may be failure to gain weight as expected
4. **Insomnia or hypersomnia**
5. **Psychomotor agitation or retardation 坐立不安**: Nearly every day and observable by others (not merely subjectively restless or slow)
6. **Fatigue or loss of** <u>**energy**</u> 失去活力
7. **Decreased concentration**
8. **Feeling worthless or excessive/inappropriate guilt**, not only guilt about being sick(MDD)
   1. guilt may be delusional
9. **Thoughts of death/suicide:** Recurrent thoughts of death OR suicidal ideation with/without specific plan OR suicide attempt
