# Differential Diagnosis

## 內文
1. Presistent depressive disorder: 即 dysthymia ，比較像人格特質，比較沒聚焦在drive改變，antidepressant效果讚，MDD+PDD稱為double depression
2. Adjustment disorder with depressive mood：有明確壓力源造成無法解決的、超過負荷能力的壓力，壓力源移除就會改善
3. mood disorder due to another medical condition: ex 癌症造成 食慾差、無精打采等
4. substance/ alcohol/ medication-induced mood disorder：情緒⇒ substance 還是 substance ⇒ 情緒
5. schizoaffective / schizophrenia: bipolar OR depression 只有mania或depression時才有psychotic feature；schizoaffective則需要2 weeks有psychotic feature時無mania或depression
6. MDD with anxious distress 容易偽裝成 anxiety disorder
7. MDD 的無力容易偽裝成 insomnia 導致的無力
