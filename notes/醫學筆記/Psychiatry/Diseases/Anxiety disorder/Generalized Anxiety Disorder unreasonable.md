# Generalized Anxiety Disorder: unreasonable, multi-aspect(什麼都擔心) anxiety for 6 month 以上。

## 內文
1. **Anxiety and Worry**(**3+**, 小孩1+)：<u>Restlessness(坐立不安)</u> / <u>Easily fatigued</u> / <u>Difficulty concentrating</u> / <u>Irritable</u> / T<u>ense muscle</u> / <u>Sleep disturbance</u>
2. **6 months** & <u>**more**</u> days with 3 symptoms <u>**than not**</u>
3. **<u>Function loss</u>**
4. NOT Panic disorder, specific phobia, PTSD, OCD, social anxiety disorder, anorexia nervosa, body dysmorphic disorder, anxiety about delusional beliefs
