# Other Trauma- and stressor-related disorders: Reactive attachment disorder, Disinhibited social engagement disorder, Acute stress disorder, Adjustment disorder, Prolonged grief disorder

## 內文
1. **Acute stress disorder**: PTSD in < 1 month
2. **Adjustment disorder**: Distress & functional loss after identifiable stress, < 3 months, Didn’t match PTSD
3. Childhood social neglect: 這兩個在DSM-4中是同個診斷的不同subtype
   1. **Reactive attachment disorder**: ASD / ID / Depression-like
   2. **Disinhibited social engagement disorder**: ADHD-like
4. **Prolonged grief disorder**: Death of a close individual > 12 months ago, persistent grief nearly everyday for the last month, +3 significant symptoms
