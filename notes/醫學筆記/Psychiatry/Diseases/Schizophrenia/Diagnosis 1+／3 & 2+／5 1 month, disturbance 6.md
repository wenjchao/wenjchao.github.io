# Diagnosis: 1+/3 & 2+/5 > 1 month, disturbance > 6 months

## 內文
### **Criteria A**

1+ in 1~3, 2+ in 1~5

1. Hallucination: 沒有stimuli（刺激）卻有聽到/看到
2. Delusion: content 有問題，false and fixed belief
3. Disorganized speech: process有問題
4. Grossly disorganized or behavior
5. Negative symptoms

### **Criteria B**

**: Functional loss**

Criteria C：signs of the disturbance (include prodrome & residual symptoms) persist for >6 months, with active symptoms (Criteria A) >1 month

### **Criteria D**

**:** NOT delirium, substance/ disease induce
