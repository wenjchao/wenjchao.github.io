# Other Psychotic disorders

## 內文
- **Brief psychotic disorder** < 1 month
- **Scizophreniform**: > 1 month , < 6 months
- **Schizoaffective disorder**: schizo + major mood
  1. <u>major depressive</u> **OR** <u>manic</u> <u>**concurrent (要同時)**</u> with **Criteria A of schizophrenia**(1+ in 1~3, 2+ in 1~5)
  2. Delusions or hallucinations > 2 weeks without major mood episode in lifetime
  3. major mood episode presented for the majority of active and residual portions of the illness
- **Delusional disorder**
- **Catatonia**: 行為異常為主
- **Organic psychosis**: neurological disease, rheuma, endocrine, substance use, anti-viral agent
  1. course: 發病年齡不典型
  2. prodrome 不明顯
  3. symptom: delusion主題奇怪，可能與場景相關
  4. function: 對antipsychosis效果差，但是治好疾病就好了
