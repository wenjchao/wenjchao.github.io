### Disorders:

先排除 schizophrenic disorders

1. **Bipolar I Disorder (1%):** 有manic episode 就算, more severe
2. **Bipolar II Disorder (1%):** hypomanic episode + major depressive episode, more chronic & depressed
   - 注意：Bipolar II 可能之後出現manic episode，診斷變成Bipolar I
3. **Cyclothymic disorder (2.5%):** 2 years, numerous mood swing(**不能符合** mania OR hypomania OR depression 的 criteria), with **<u>functional loss</u>**
