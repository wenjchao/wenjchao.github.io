# Luciferase assay (Day4)

## 內文
Preparation

1. find <u>Dual-Glo Luciferase Reagent</u> & <u>Dual-Glo Stop & Glo Substrate</u> & <u>Dual-Glo Stop & Glo Buffer</u>
2. <u>Dual-Glo Luciferase Reagent</u>: dissolve 6x with MiliQ, prepare 75 µL for each well
3. <u>Dual-Glo Stop & Glo Reagent</u>:
   1. Mix <u>Dual-Glo Stop & Glo Substrate</u> : <u>Dual-Glo Stop & Glo Buffer</u> = 1:100
   2. dissolve the mixture 6x with MiliQ, prepare 50 µL for each well

Procedure

1. suck out all the medium with multichannel, be gentle
2. add <u>Dual-Glo Luciferase Reagent</u> 75 µL for each well
3. incubate on the shaker for 30 mins, till all cells lyse
   - 注意：Check under the luminescence microscope after centrifuge
4. transfer 50 µL of the medium from each well to a white plate
5. measure luminescence (Firefly)
6. Add 50µL of the <u>Dual-Glo Stop & Glo Reagent</u> in each well
7. incubate for 15 minutes
8. measure luminescence (Renilla)
