# For cell suspension (Day1&2)

## 內文
Prepare:

1. DMEM--- & trypsin from the fridge
2. cells in t75 from the incubator
3. small bottle of buffer for counting cells
4. salt

Procedure:

1. check the growth of the cells under microscope
2. suck the medium out from the flask
   - 注意：use fire to make sure the suction tube is sterile
3. wash the flask with the salt (10ml for t75, 5ml for t25),
   - 注意：don’t wash away cells
4. suck the salt out, add trypsin (2ml for t75, 1ml for t25)
5. incubate for 5 mins, check under the microscope
6. add DMEM +++ (8ml for t75, 4ml for t25) to inactivate the trypsin
7. transfer into tubes
8. count cells:
   1. 20 µL of sample into the bottle of buffer
   2. lyse the cells with 3 drops of zapoglobin
   3. Machine: turin it on ⇒ flush 2 times ⇒ measure the buffer first ⇒ measure the cells for 2 times ⇒ flush again
