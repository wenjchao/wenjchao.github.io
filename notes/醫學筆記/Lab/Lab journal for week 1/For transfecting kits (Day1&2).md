# For transfecting kits (Day1&2)

## 內文
Prepare:

1. take the TGR5, MRGPRX4, ADRBR2, GFP, Renilla, PEI(in fridge in radioactive room) from -20℃, wait for them to melt
2. vortex & centrifugate them

Procedure:

1. make Tube1 \*5 & Tube \* 2, incubate for 5 mins
   **Tube 1:**
   1. Control A: DMEM 10\*15 wells = 150µL
   2. Control B: DMEM 10\*15 wells = 150µL
   3. TGR5: TGR5 7.5 + Renilla 7.5 + GFP 1.5 + DMEM 133.5 = 150µL
   4. MRGPRX4: MRGPRX4 7.5 + Renilla 7.5 + GFP 1.5 + DMEM 133.5 = 150µL
   5. ADRBR2: ADRBR2 7.5 + Renilla 7.5 + GFP 1.5 + DMEM 133.5 = 150µL
   Tube 1 for TGR5, MRGPRX4, ADRBR2

   | Title | per well | 15 wells (per subjects) |
   |---|---|---|
   | pDNA(µL) (TGR5/ MRGPRX4/ ADRBR2) | 0.5 | 7.5 |
   | Renilla(µL) | 0.5 | 7.5 |
   | GFP(µL) | 0.1 | 1.5 |
   | DMEM---(µL) | 8.9 | 133.5 |
   **Tube 2:**
   1. Control A: DMEM 10\*15 wells = 150µL
   2. Control B & TGR5 & MRGPRX4 & ADRBR2 (Make the big mix for 63 wells): PEI 12.6 + DMEM 617.4 = 630 µL
      Tube 2 for ControlB, TGR5, MRGPRX4, ADRBR2

      | Title | per well | 15 wells (per subjects) | total (63wells) |
      |---|---|---|---|
      | PEI | 0.2 | 3 | 12.6 |
      | DMEM---(µL) | 9.8 | 147 | 617.4 |
4. add 150 µL from Tube 2(Control A OR big mixture) to each Tube1, incubate for 20 mins
