# DNA electrophoresis

## 內文
1. check the recipe (on the wall, in small/ medium/ large)
   1. buffer: big big bucket at right side, measure the amount using graduated cylinder(量筒)
   2. agarose: weigh the container, press “Tare”, then measure the tare
   3. etidium bromide: toxic, find it in the small black bottle on the desk near the wall
   4. DNA: 7µl water + 3µL dna (400-500ng) + 2µl orange dye, make total 10-12 µL
   5. smart ladder: in the fridge in the room

2. add some buffer in the container(with agarose inside), boil it with microwave, until it is “totally transparent”

3. cool down the liquid with the rest of the buffer, then cool it down through the sink

4. Add etidium bromide, mix, don’t make bubble

5. pour it into the model wait for 30mins

6. WASH the container: With buffer ⇒ Hot water flush
   1. if the gel is solid, boil it

7. After becoming solid, put into the electrophoresis machine with the U-shape container, make sure the buffer is over the gel

8. add DNA & ladder into wells

9. connect to battery, 120V, 25 min
