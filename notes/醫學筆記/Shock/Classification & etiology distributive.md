# Classification & etiology: distributive, cardiogenic, hypovolemic, and obstructive

## 內文
![](圖片/CV-new/Untitled-30.png)

**Distributive shock:** severe **peripheral vasodilatation**

1. Septic shock: 見 Septic work-up
2. Systemic inflammatory response syndrome (SIRS)
3. Anaphylactic shock
4. Neurogenic shock
5. Drug and toxin-induced shock

**Cardiogenic shock: intracardiac** causes of cardiac pump failure ⇒ cardiac output ↓

- 注意：見 CV 中的 Acute heart failure

- 注意：CO(cardiac output) 變少是cardiogenic shock，CO不見得是cardiac arrest

1. Cardiomyopathy
2. arrhythmia: atrial & ventricular, tachyarrhythmias & bradyarrhythmias 都有可能
3. Mechanical: aortic or mitral valve破掉

**Hypovolemic shock:** reduced **intravascular** volume ⇒ cardiac output ↓

1. hemorrhagic shock: blood loss
2. non-hemorrhagic shock: water loss
   1. Gastrointestinal losses: diarrhea, vomiting
   2. Skin losses: heat stroke, burns, Stevens-Johnson syndrome
   3. Renal losses: diuresis(drug-induced or hypoaldosteronism or nephropathies), diabetes insipidus

**Obstructive shock: extracardiac** causes of cardiac pump failure

1. Pulmonary vascular: pulmonary embolism (PE) or severe pulmonary hypertension (PH)
2. Mechanical obstuctive: preload↓，因此很像 Hypovolemic shock
   1. Tension pneumothorax
   2. Pericardial tamponade
   3. Constrictive pericarditis & Restrictive cardiomyopathy
