# Stages of shock

## 內文
1. **Pre-shock** (compensated shock): compensatory tachycardia + peripheral vasoconstriction + hyperlactatemia
2. **Shock:**
   1. symptomatic tachycardia
   2. Tachypnea & dyspnea
   3. hypotension
   4. metabolic acidosis
   5. oliguria
   6. restlessness
   7. cool clammy(濕冷) skin or diaphoresis(發汗)
3. **End-organ dysfunction:**
   1. hypotension : severe and recalcitrant to therapy because of vasoplegia(dilation)
   2. acidemia ⇒ depresses CO
   3. anuria & acute renal failure
   4. worsen hyperlactatemia
   5. restlessness ⇒ obtundation(遲鈍) and coma
