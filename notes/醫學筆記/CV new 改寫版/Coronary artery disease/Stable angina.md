# Stable angina

> Symptom of stable angina：
>
> retrosternal, exertional,
>
> relieved by
>
> rest
>
> 1. **<u>retrosternal(1)</u>** heaviness with radiation
>
>    [[疼痛 radiation 位置|摘要]]
> 2. provoked by **<u>exertional or emotional stress(2)</u>**
> 3. relieved by **<u>rest or NTG(3)</u>**
>
> - 三項症狀 ≥ 兩項 ⇒ stable angina， ≤ 一項 ⇒ noncardiac schest pain
