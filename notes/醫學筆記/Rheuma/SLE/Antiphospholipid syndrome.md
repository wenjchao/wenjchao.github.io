# Antiphospholipid syndrome

## 內文
1. Finding: **DVT(vein), stroke(artery), WBC / RBC / platelet penia**
2. anti-phospholipid ab: **Anticardiolipin / Anti-β2-glycoprotein** antibodies（**<u>需發病5年內、相隔12週驗到兩次</u>**）
3. Lupus anticoagulant activity: **Prolong aPTT & DRVVT**(dilute Russell viper venom time) of <u>blood</u> & <u>blood + normal plasma</u>(含有 clotting factor，看是不是clotting factor deficiency)
4. Treatment: long-term systemic thromboprophylaxis
5. In pregnancy: 易流產、子宮胎盤灌流不足、preeclampsia、eclampsia、premature birth
