# Skin(>85%): acute / subacute / discoid cutaneous lupus erythematosus

## 內文
1. [[Skin(85%) acute ／ subacute ／ discoid cutaneous/圖|扁平]]
2. acute (ACLE):
   1. **<u>Malar rash</u>** (butterfly rash, nasal labial spraing)
   2. maculopapular rash
3. subacute (SCLE): reaction to sunlight (on sun-exposed area, **<u>photosensitivity</u>**) ⇒ non-scarring psorasiform (Annular, Papulosquamous)
4. Discoid(chronic) rash
5. Non-specific manifestations:
   1. **<u>Nonscarring alopecia、oral / nasal ulcer</u>**
   2. Vasculopathies: Vasculitis, Raynaud phenomenon (systemic sclerosis 較多)
   3. Periungual telangiectasia (nail fold 微出血，dermatomyoitis 較多)
