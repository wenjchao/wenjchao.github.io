# 甲摺鏡：early、active、late scleroderma pattern

## 內文
以下是**原圖各照片的標示**，數值不改寫成階段判定門檻。

| 原圖特徵 | Early | Active | Late |
|---|---|---|---|
| Density（每 1 linear mm） | 8 capillaries | 3 capillaries | 1 capillary |
| Dimension | 有 giant capillaries：三個 limbs 均勻增粗，diameter ≥50 μm | 有 giant capillary：三個 limbs 均勻增粗，diameter ≥50 μm | 形狀異常，未測量 |
| Morphology | Hairpin-shaped capillaries | Hairpin-shaped capillaries＋abnormally shaped capillary | Abnormally shaped capillary |
| Haemorrhages | Present | Absent | Absent |
