# SLE

## 內文
1. [[SLE/2019 EULAR／ACR classification criteria for SLE|扁平]]
2. Gene: HLA-DR2 / DR3
3. **<u>Symptoms</u>**
   1. Fever
   2. [[SLE/Skin(85%) acute ／ subacute ／ discoid cutaneous|摘要]]
   3. Joint(>90%): MCP + PIP, 似 RA 但無 bone erosion & defomities
   4. CNS: seizure, psychosis, personality / behavior change (與 anti-ribosomal P 有關)
   5. Hema: **leukopenia** or **hemolytic anemia** or **thrombocytopenia**
   6. [[SLE/Renal (Lupus nephritis) proteinuria & urinary|摘要]]
   7. Serositis: **pleuritis** / **pericarditis** / **endocarditis**(不算 serositis，不在 criteria 內)
4. **<u>Lab</u>**
   1. <u>ANA</u>: sensitive
   2. <u>anti-Sm & anti-dsDNA</u>: specific
      - 注意：anti-dsDNA level 與 disease activity 相關（vasculitis, nephritis）
   3. <u>anti-phospholipid ab</u>: **Antiphospholipid syndrome(APS)**，常與 SLE 一起出現
   4. anti-histone: **Drug-induced lupus**
   5. anti-ribosomal P: **CNS involvement**
   6. anti-Ro/SSA and anti-La/SSB: **Neonatal lupus**
   7. <u>low C3C4</u>
5. **<u>Treatment</u>**
   1. Hydroxychloroquine for all patients
   2. NSAID for mild, low dose glucocorticoid for severe
   3. organ-threatening / life-threatening(ex: **RPGN, hemolysis, systemic vasculitis**): induction ⇒ maintainance
      1. Induction: high-dose glucocorticoid + cyclophosphamide / MMF
      2. Maintainance: cyclophosphamide 要換成 MMF / AZA
6. **<u>Other associated disease</u>**
   1. [[SLE/Antiphospholipid syndrome|摘要]]
   2. Drug-induced lupus: **anti-histone (+)**, anti-Sm & anti-dsDNA 常 (-)

      [[SLE/藥物種類（重點：hydralazine & procainamide）|摘要]]
      - Symptoms: Constitutional symptoms + malar rash + myalgia / polyarthragia
   3. neonatal lupus: **AV block, hepatitis**
      - Pathophysiology: transfer of maternal antibodies (**anti-Ro/SSA and anti-La/SSB**)
   4. **Cardiovascular disease**: Most common cause of death in SLE
      - atherosclerosis ⇒ Coronary artery disease
