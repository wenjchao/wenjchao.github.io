# Osteoarthritis (monoarthritis)

## 內文
### 風險與分布

- Risk factor：age、obesity、occupation、姿勢不良
- knee > hip > hand

### 表現與影像

- Pain in both complete flexion and extension、during or after exertion、relieved with rest
- X-ray：irregular joint space narrowing、osteophytes／spurs、subchondral sclerosis、subchondral cyst

[[OA 結構示意：編號待核對|標題]]

### 治療

1. NSAID → intraarticular steroid → surgery
2. 非藥物治療：weight loss、supportive devices、exercise
