# Osteoarthritis

## 內文
1. Risk factor: Age, Obesity, Occupation, 姿勢不良
2. knee > hip > hand
3. Pain in both complete flexion and extension, **during or after exertion** that is **relieved with rest**
4. [[Osteoarthritis/X-ray Irregular joint space narrowing|扁平]]
5. Treatment: NSAID ⇒ intraarticular steroid ⇒ surgery
6. Non-pharmalogical treatment: **Weight loss, Supportive devices, Exercise**
