# Septic arthritis

## 內文
1. Pathogenisis
   1. <u>Direct contamination</u> : wound (iatrogenic/trauma ⇒ GPC, 破傷風桿菌)、contiguous spread (septic bursitis, osteomyelitis)
   2. <u>Hematogenous</u> : Gonococcal arthritis, Other hematogenous pathogen(Staph, Strep, E.coli, Pseudo, TB/NTM)
   - 注意：Gonococcal arthritis — in sexually active (young) people；multiple joints & multiple skin lesion & tenosynovitis（several tendons 同時發炎）
2. Clinical features: **fever(can be absent)**, **monoarticular** joint pain/swollen/red/warm (common in big joints: knee > hip > wrist > shoulder > ankle > others)
3. Lab: **Arthrocentesis with synovial fluid analysis ⇒ stain & culture**
   1. synovial fluid appearance: often yellow-green and turbid ()
   2. synovial fluid analysis: very high WBC(>50k, neutrophil) + low glucose
   3. Gram stain & culture (& PCR testing for N. gonorrhoeae)
   - 注意：影像用於 (1) foreign body (2) suspect osteomyelitis
4. Treatment: <u>Drain if purulent</u> & <u>antibiotic due to stain + culture</u>
   1. Stain: GPC(Staph/Strep) ⇒ Vanco, GNC(Gonococcus) ⇒ 3rd Cepha, GNB(Pseudo, E.coli) ⇒ Cefepime / Mepem,
   2. Nothing under stain: Risk factors of STD? ⇒ 3rd Cepha
      - 注意：young、健康、sexually active，或有 gonococcal arthritis 特徵（tenosynovitis / dermatitis）
      No risk factor of STD ⇒ Vanco + 3rd Ceph / Cefepime
