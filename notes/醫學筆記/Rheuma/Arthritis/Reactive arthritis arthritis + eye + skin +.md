# Reactive arthritis: arthritis + eye + skin + urethritis

## 內文
1. Postinfectious autoimmune disorder: Chlamydia, **SSYC**(Salmonella, Shigella, Yersinia, Campylobacter) ⇒ **Stool culture + STD testing(PCR)**
2. **asymmetrical** (oligoarthritis) with a **migratory** character, most in lower body (knee)
3. Lab
   1. Blood test
      1. ↑WBC & ↑CRP (non-specific)
      2. No RF & anti-CCP (rheumatoid arthritis)
   2. Arthrocentesis with synovial fluid analysis
      1. No crystals (gout)
      2. No bacteria under stain & culture (septic arthritis)
      3. **PCR or antibody test may find pathogen**
   3. **Stool culture + STD testing(PCR)**
4. Treatment: **Antibiotic + NSAID** / Steroid (for NSAID過敏) / DMARD (for chronic arthritis)
