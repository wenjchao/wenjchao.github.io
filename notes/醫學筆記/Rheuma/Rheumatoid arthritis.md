# Rheumatoid arthritis

## 內文
1. Gene: HLA-DR1 / DR4
2. Risk factor: female, obesity, infection, family history
3. Symptoms:
   1. 關節：Symmetrical pain and swelling of affected joints(**MCP, PIP, No DIP**), also at rest ⇒ **Improves with activity**
      - X-ray：**Bone erosion** & deformity（因發炎導致軟骨流失 ⇒ 關節腔變窄）、關節附近骨質流失
   2. **rheumatoid vasculitis** ⇒ neurovascular disease / gangrene / necrosis ⇒
   3. necrosis ⇒ granulomatous tissue ⇒ **rheumatoid nodule**（extensor surface、近關節區、骨頭凸起處）
   4. interstitial lung disease (pneumoconiosis + RA ⇒ Caplan syndrome), pleuropulmonary nodules
   5. Felty syndorme: arthritis & neutropenia & splenomegaly ⇒ recurrent bacterial infections
4. Diagnosis: <u>anti-CCP</u> & <u>Rheumatoid Factor</u> & Thyroid function test (rule out autoimmune thyroid disease)
   1. anti-CCP: anti-Citrullinated proteins (post-translational modification of arginine in vimentin / type2 collagen) ⇒ type 2 & 3 hypersensitivity
   2. Rheumatoid Factor: anti-Fc portion of IgG ⇒ type 3 hypersensitivity
   - 注意：anti-CCP 與關節破壞程度相關；RF 與關節外表現、預後相關；Serological studies 可能陰性
5. 急性期治療：**Anti-inflammation ⇒ NSAIDs ± low dose steroids**
6. 長期治療: **DMARDs (MTX for first-line) ⇒** Immunosuppresent if DMARDs 均無效
