# 關節液分析

## 內文
### 判讀重點

- 黏滯性越低、WBC 越多、glucose 越低 → 越傾向感染與發炎。
- 結晶性關節炎是急性關節炎的一種；關節液中的結晶是重要鑑別線索。
- 講義另描述尿酸結晶與白血球吞噬現象作為痛風判讀線索；所指的「左圖」未包含在目前截圖中，待核對。

### 黏滯性、WBC 與 glucose

數值依講義原圖轉寫；glucose 欄的單位為「% serum」，Same 沿用原圖。

| 分類 | 情況 | 黏滯性 | WBC/μl | Glucose（% serum） |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | Normal | High | <200 | Same |
| Group 1：noninflammatory | Osteoarthritis | High | <2000 | Same |
| Group 1：noninflammatory | Trauma | High | <2000 | Same |
| Group 2：inflammatory | SLE | Slightly decreased | 0–9000 | Same |
| Group 2：inflammatory | Acute rheumatic fever | Slightly decreased | 0–60000 | Same |
| Group 2：inflammatory | Pseudogout | Low | 50–75000 | Same |
| Group 2：inflammatory | Gout | Low | 100–160000 | Same |
| Group 2：inflammatory | Rheumatoid arthritis | Low | 3000–50000 | 75–100 |
| Group 3：purulent | Tuberculosis | Low | 2500–100000 | 50–75 |
| Group 3：purulent | Bacterial arthritis | Low | 50000–300000 | <50 |

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | Yellow | Translucent | <25 | 0 |

| Trauma | Pink or red | Transparent | <25 | 0 |
| SLE | Yellow | Translucent | <25 | 0 |

| Pseudogout | Yellow or white | Translucent or opaque | 90 | + |
| Gout | Yellow or white | Translucent or opaque | 90 | + |
| Rheumatoid arthritis | Yellow or purulent | Translucent or opaque | 50–75 | 0 |
| Tuberculosis | Purulent | Opaque | 50 | 0 |
| Bacterial arthritis | Purulent | Opaque | >90 | 0 |
