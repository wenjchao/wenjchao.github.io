# Skin and soft tissue infection

## 摘要

3. Cellulitis
   1. **需考慮 MRSA 時：**
      1. 常打針 (ex: 毒品使用者)
      2. Prior MRSA infection / MRSA colonization（身上任一處(ex:鼻子)有MRSA，其他地方有 MRSA 機率大增）
      3. penetrating trauma
   2. **須考慮 cover anerobic**：
      1. DM patient ⇒ Unasyn/Augumentin/Tazocin, carbapenem
      2. bite wound ⇒ Unasyn/Augumentin/Tazocin + clindamycin/metronidazole, Moxifloxacin, doxycycline, carbapenem
   3. **是否有接觸水**： *<u>Aeromonas hydrophila</u>* in water, *<u>Vibrio</u>* in salt water ⇒ + doxycyline / quinolone (嚴重的話都加)
   4. **recurrent cellulitis risk facter**: 香港腳、DM、chronic skin lesion、淋巴回流被阻斷（ex: 乳癌/子宮頸癌/大腸癌手術清除淋巴）
   5. 颱風過後：meliodosis（Burkholderia pseudomallei，類 pseudo，在土壤裡，淹大水時一起出來） ⇒ Ceftazidime
4. Necrotizing Fasciitis **⇒ surgery**
   > 💡 Clinical judgment is the most important element in diagnosis（CT、MRI 太慢）
   >
   > Gold standard 是 needle aspiration
   > 💡 Features suggesting fasciitis
   >
   > 1. Rapid progressing disease
   > 2. Severe pain that seems disproportional to the clinical / cutaneous findings
   > 3. **Systemic toxicity**, often with altered mental status / lethargy
   > 4. **Bullous lesions, skin necrosis or ecchymoses**
   > 5. the hard, wooden feel of the subcutaneous tissue, edema or tenderness extending beyond the area of apparent skin involvement
   > 6. **Crepitus**, indicating gas in the tissues
   > 7. Failure to respond to initial antibiotic therapy
   1. Type I (Polymicrobial)
      1. managment: **<u>immediate surgery</u>** + empirical: <u>Vancomycin or Linezolid or Daptomycin</u>(for GPC) +  <u>Tazocin or carbapenem or [Ceftriaxone + Metronidazole]</u>(for GNB + anaerobic)
   2. Type 2 (Monomicrobial): S. pyogenes（GAS）, Clostridia sp., S. aureus (MRSA), Aeromonas sp., Vibrio vulnificus, Klebsiella sp. (台灣 KP 特多)
   3. Fournier gangrene : necrotizing fasciitis at genital ⇒ 一樣是 surgery
