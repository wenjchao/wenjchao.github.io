# UTI：community PEcK；hospital + Pseudomonas／Enterococcus；Foley + yeast／S. aureus

## 摘要
UTI：社區型以 PEcK 為主；院內加 Pseudomonas／Enterococcus；留置 Foley 再考慮 yeast／S. aureus。另整理尿液檢驗、無症狀菌尿、sterile pyuria、cystitis／pyelonephritis 與其他泌尿道感染。

## 內文
- [[UTI/LUTS-FUN-WISE|扁平]]
- Lab: WBC + WBC esterase + nitrite + bacteria 有 2~3 個就高度懷疑
  > 💡 會驗 WBC esterase 是因為 WBC 可能破掉了看不到
  1. Bacteriuria but not pyuria: 即 asymptomatic bacteriuria
     ⇒ 不用治療，除非懷孕 or 準備接受泌尿科手術 or <u>immunocompromised</u>
  2. Pyuria but not bacteriuria: 長不出來的原因有 已經用過抗生素 or 菌種奇怪 ex: TB, Neisseria

> Uncomplicated UTI: 年輕健康女性，三者全部滿足才算（年輕指育齡）。

1. Uncomplicated cyctitis:
   1. dysuria 排尿疼痛
   2. hematuria
   3. suprapubic pain 膀胱脹痛
   4. fever ***<u>absent</u>***
   5. Storage disorder : <u>FUN</u>
2. Uncomplicated pyelonephritis: flank pain, fever, nausea & vomiting

> Others:

1. Prostatitis (chronic): <u>cystitis symptoms</u> + <u>WISE</u> ⇒ pre + post prostate massage U/C
   > 💡 Acute prostatitis
   >
   > :
   >
   > perineal pain, fever, tenderness on prostate exam
2. Urethritis: <u>cystitis symptoms</u> + <u>urethral discharge</u> ⇒ STD (*Chlamydia trachomatis, Neisseria gonorrhoeae, Trichomonas vaginalis, Mycoplasma genitalium*) ⇒ 3rd cepha(for Neisseria) + Doxycyline / Azithromycin
3. Renal abscess: <u>pyelonephritis symptoms</u> + <u>persistent fever under antibiotic</u>
4. Catheter-associated ⇒ replace foley ⇒ U/C > 1000 after foley removal（不能只看WBC）
