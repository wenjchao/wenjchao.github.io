# Infections in immunocompromised hosts

## 摘要
AIDS／lymphopenia 與其他免疫缺陷的機會性感染，依 CD4、T-cell 功能、骨髓移植及 anti-cytokine autoantibody 分類。

## 內文
Lymphopenia：HIV, DiGeorge, SCID

- in AIDS
  1. CD4 > 200: TB, MRSA
  2. CD4 < 200: PJP, CMV, Cryptococcus, TB/NTM, Talaromyces
