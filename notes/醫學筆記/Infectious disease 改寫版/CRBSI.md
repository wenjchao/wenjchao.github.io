# CRBSI

## 摘要
導管相關血流感染的症狀、導管與周邊血培養診斷條件、移除導管及不適合 lock therapy 的病原。

## 內文
1. S/S: Fever & chills
2. Diagnosis: 兩個都要滿足
   1. postive catheter culture + positive peripheral culture
   2. catheter culture positive 2 hours earlier than peripheral culture
3. remove catheter ⇒ catheter culture
4. 無法 lock therapy：Candida, S.aureus, Pseudomonas aeruginosa, TB & NTM, Drug-resistant GNB (這幾隻殺不乾淨)
